Connect your site to WooCommerce.com

Connecting your WooCommerce store to your WooCommerce.com account enables you to install extensions automatically, receive product updates and support, and manage your subscriptions directly from your site’s WP Admin dashboard.

You are prompted to connect your store during your first purchase on WooCommerce.com. You can also connect at any time from within WP Admin.

Note: WooCommerce.com requires a WordPress.com account
for login — your credentials are shared across both platforms. This
connection does not require Jetpack and is separate from the
WordPress.com connection used by WooPayments, WooCommerce Shipping,
WooCommerce Tax, and Google for WooCommerce.

Why connect your site?

Connecting your site to WooCommerce.com allows you to:

Install extensions and themes automatically from your WooCommerce.com dashboard or from WP Admin, without downloading and uploading .zip files.
Receive updates for your paid Woo Marketplace extensions and themes via the WooCommerce.com Update Manager.
Access support by submitting your store’s System Status Report automatically when creating a support ticket.
View your connected stores on the My Stores page of your WooCommerce.com account.
How to connect your site
From WP Admin
In your site’s WP Admin, go to WooCommerce > Extensions > My Subscriptions.
Click the Connect button.
Connect button is shown when a WooCommerce.com account isn’t connected to your site
If you are not already logged into your WooCommerce.com account, log in when prompted.
Log into WooCommerce.com with your WordPress.com login credentials if you aren’t logged in yet.
Click the Connect your store button to authorize the connection.
Your WooCommerce.com subscriptions and account data sync once you connect your store.

Once connected, your WooCommerce.com subscriptions and account data sync to your site. If you already own paid Woo Marketplace subscriptions, the WooCommerce.com Update Manager installs automatically.

During your first purchase

When you complete your first purchase on WooCommerce.com, you are prompted to connect a store. Enter your store URL and approve the connection. For future purchases, your connected store is available for selection.

From the My subscriptions page on WooCommerce.com
Go to My subscriptions on WooCommerce.com.
Click the Add to store button next to a product.
Choose a connected store, or enter a new store URL to connect.
Approve the connection if prompted.
From the My stores page on WooCommerce.com
Go to My stores on WooCommerce.com
Click the Connect new store button
Enter the Store URL
Insert your store URL – this should usually just be your domain name
Check the details are correct and click the Connect your account button

Use the correct WooCommerce.com account


When connecting your site, use the WooCommerce.com account that holds your Woo Marketplace purchases. Subscriptions are tied to the account used during purchase. If you connect with a different account, your subscriptions do not appear on the site.

To check which account is connected, go to WooCommerce > Extensions > My Subscriptions in your site’s WP Admin. Your connected account’s email address and Gravatar appear at the top of the page.

REST API requirement

To connect your site to WooCommerce.com, your site’s REST API must be accessible.

If the REST API is blocked by a security plugin, firewall, or hosting configuration, the connection may fail. Common causes include:

Security plugins (e.g., Wordfence, Really Simple Security) that restrict or disable REST API access.
Hosting-level firewalls that block /wp-json/ endpoints.
WordPress.com-hosted sites set to “Private” — automatic installation and connection features are not available while a site is private. Set the site to Coming Soon instead. See the WordPress.com privacy settings guide for instructions.

If you are unable to connect, verify that your REST API is accessible and consult your hosting provider or security plugin documentation.

Disconnect and reconnect your site

If you need to switch WooCommerce.com accounts, or if your connection is not working correctly, you can disconnect and reconnect your site.

How to disconnect
In your site’s WP Admin, go to WooCommerce > Extensions > My Subscriptions.
Click the account dropdown (where your Gravatar and email address appear).
Select Disconnect account.
Confirm by clicking the Disconnect button in the pop-over.
How to reconnect

After disconnecting, follow the prompts on the same page to connect your store to WooCommerce.com again (click here for detailed steps). Make sure you log in with the correct WooCommerce.com account — the one that holds your Woo Marketplace purchases.

Troubleshooting
ISSUE	SOLUTION
Subscriptions do not appear on the site	Verify that the correct WooCommerce.com account is connected. Disconnect and reconnect with the account used to purchase the subscriptions.
Connection fails or times out	Ensure your site’s REST API is accessible. Check for security plugins or hosting firewalls blocking /wp-json/ endpoints.
WordPress.com-hosted site cannot connect	If the site is set to “Private,” change it to “Coming Soon” to enable REST API access.
“Connect” button does not respond	Clear your browser cache and try again. If the issue persists, try a different browser or disable browser extensions temporarily.
WooCommerce.com Update Manager does not install automatically	Verify the connection is active, then install the Update Manager manually using the download link.
Installing Jetpack does not resolve fix the connection to WooCommerce.com	The WooCommerce.com store connection does not use Jetpack.

For additional help, see Troubleshoot common issues.
