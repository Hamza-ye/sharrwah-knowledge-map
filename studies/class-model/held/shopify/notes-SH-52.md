# SH-52: Winter '23's Product list, placed by SH-51's rule E18

**What the batch is.** All 98 entries are headings of Winter '23's Product list (`productList/solutions/N/collections/M/products/K`) in Shopify's `editions-page-data`. The Product list repeats the edition's solution items, each under a heading of its own. SH-50 and SH-51 placed those items. SH-51's E18 maps each Product list entry to its item by collection and order, and each entry takes the item's line.

**How it was placed.** The coordinator placed it by script (`method/held_productlist.py`), not by an agent. The rule is mechanical and the items' lines were already checked.
- Each entry takes its item's first line: its placement, capability, support and scope.
- Its terms are the heading's own words, and its reading names the item and the rule.
- An item's further lines aren't copied. A heading takes them only where it names their thing too (E18), and the item holds them already.
- **94 entries are mapped by order,** in collections where the Product list's count matches the items'.
- **4 are mapped by heading,** where the counts differ:
  - 'Shopify Managed Markets' takes 'Introducing Managed Markets' (CM-MKS-003, `shows`). Its collection holds four items and one heading.
  - 'Built for Shopify program' and 'App discoverability' take 'Make your app shine''s App Store line (CM-APP-002, `core;extension-point`).
  - 'Improved app reporting' takes the same item's partners line (CM-PTN-001, `core`). That collection holds one item and three headings.

**What the checker prints.**
- **Cell changes:** the same ones the items made, kept at SH-50's and SH-51's checks. Examples are CM-SHP-012's fulfilment network, CM-ORD-014's reorder and CM-DVT-004's development stores.
- **Notes:** they repeat the items' own (the brand, Shop's `own-service`, and a lead by the word 'tools').
- **New things:** Quantity rules again.

**No new kinds of entry.** Each heading names one item, so nothing here goes beyond E1 to E18.

The checker's last line, for SH-52: `98 of 98 entries placed; 0 problems` (after this file was written).
