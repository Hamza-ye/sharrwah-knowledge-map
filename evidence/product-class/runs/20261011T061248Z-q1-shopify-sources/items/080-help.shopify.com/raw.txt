# Managing customer privacy settings

As a Shopify merchant, it's crucial to prioritize customer privacy and comply with applicable privacy laws. To help you manage your privacy settings and maintain compliance and transparency, you can customize the **Customer privacy** settings in your Shopify admin.

Because privacy laws and regulations vary across different regions, the **Customer privacy** settings lets you customize your privacy settings for each country and region where you sell to.
