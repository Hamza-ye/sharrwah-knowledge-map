Does WooPayments use Stripe Radar?

Yes, WooPayments utilizes Stripe Radar. This provides merchants with protection against risky transactions via Stripe’s machine learning technology.

Most of the Radar rules for WooPayments accounts are managed by our team and cannot be modified by merchants. Please consult our fraud protection documentation for some options on how to customize your protection settings.

We also have some additional recommendations for protecting your store against risk and fraud in our guide to preventing disputes.
