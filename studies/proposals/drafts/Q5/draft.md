## 2. Proposed: names, structure and behaviour

- **Payment method**: in Sharrwah's proposed design, a way a buyer can pay an order, such as a card network, a wallet, pay later, bank transfer or cash on delivery. It is either a **provider method**, which a payment provider processes, or a **manual method**, which the buyer pays to the merchant outside any provider and the merchant records; an order paid by a manual method waits, unpaid, until then. Q5-SH-027, Q5-BC-P0115, Q5-WC-016
  - It draws on the five platforms' split between methods a provider processes and methods the merchant collects, and on the waiting order of Shopify, BigCommerce and WooCommerce. Q5-SH-083, Q5-BC-060, Q5-SA-015, Q5-ZD-P0075, Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-WC-K0037
  - Why: rule 3; an order's payment state depends on its method's kind, so the kind is in the data model from the start. Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-BC-P0115
- **Payment provider**: a company that processes provider methods and pays the money out, recorded once with the methods it carries, the places it serves, its money route and its terms. A method may come from several providers, and a provider may carry several methods. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - It draws on Shopify's cards through "Shopify Payments, a third-party provider, or both together", BigCommerce's alternative methods, "typically offered through a cooperating payment gateway", and Salla's K net, which hangs on Tap. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - Why: rule 2; a provider's arrival or exit changes only its own record and its methods. Q5-SH-022, Q5-SA-K1654
- **Provider integration**: the code through which a provider plugs into Sharrwah, behind one payments interface shared by every provider: a **built-in integration**, which Sharrwah builds and keeps, or a **payments add-on**, which a provider or partner builds against Sharrwah's published interface. Merchants can activate an add-on only once Sharrwah has reviewed it and it reports itself ready, and a new method in it is reviewed again. The interface serves payment on Sharrwah's checkout and on the provider's page. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-K1696, Q5-SH-058
  - It draws on BigCommerce's Embedded Providers, which "integrate directly with the BigCommerce platform"; Shopify's approved payments extensions, its review of new methods and its readiness step; Zid's Open APIs and app review; and the direct and hosted ways of paying of Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-062, Q5-ZD-K0077, Q5-ZD-K0363, Q5-SH-007, Q5-WC-016, Q5-BC-068
  - Why: rule 2, each provider behind its own part; rule 1, one interface for every place, while the providers plugged in differ by place. Q5-SH-K1694, Q5-ZD-K0077
  - Not taken: payment code a merchant adds to its own store, as with WooCommerce's plugins, because a provider offered at a place is a partner relationship Sharrwah answers for (rule 3); WooCommerce itself asks a separate agreement of a partner offering a "Payments Solution". Q5-WC-K0006, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Provider account**: the merchant's own account with a provider, connected with the credentials of an account it holds, or applied for through Sharrwah. The provider decides each application; Sharrwah shows its state: applied, approved, rejected or connected. Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-BC-P0048
  - It draws on Salla's choice between an existing business account and a request the provider may reject; the own account Zid's pay-later providers need; BigCommerce's application and underwriting; and Shopify's apps, where the merchant may "log in or create an account with the payment provider". Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-ZD-P0080, Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SH-053
  - Why: rule 3; whose account receives the money fixes the money route and the contract. An account applied for through Sharrwah may count as one opened through Sharrwah, under the guard (section 5). Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SA-053
- **Place payment list**: Sharrwah's list, for each place it serves, of the providers and methods a store there may activate, each with its conditions: plan, verification, business type and amount limits. A store sees only its place's list, filtered by its conditions. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036
  - It draws on Shopify's provider list by store address, Zid's options by country and Salla's methods kept to stores in Saudi Arabia; and, for conditions, on Salla's and Zid's plans, Salla's verification, Zid's كي نت for institutions and companies only, and Salla's limits by country. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036, Q5-SA-P0068, Q5-ZD-P0150, Q5-SA-K0091, Q5-ZD-K0042, Q5-SA-048
  - Why: rule 1; what differs between places is set up as data, not built apart. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001
  - Not taken: WooCommerce's country list as suggestions only, which leaves the choice to the merchant, because each provider offered at a place is a partner contract (rule 3). Q5-WC-K0054, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Store payment settings**: per store, the methods the merchant has activated from its place's list and, for each, the one provider it goes to. Only staff holding a payments permission change them, a change of provider is kept to the store owner, and each change is logged. Where a store keeps separate markets or currencies, its settings follow them. Q5-SH-K1524, Q5-SH-008, Q5-SH-009
  - It draws on the activation all five show; the payments permissions of Shopify, Salla, Zid and WooCommerce; Shopify's owner-only provider change and one card provider at a time; BigCommerce's change log; and Salla's market selector and BigCommerce's methods per currency. Q5-SH-K0193, Q5-SA-K1509, Q5-ZD-002, Q5-WC-001, Q5-BC-072, Q5-SA-016, Q5-BC-074
  - Why: rule 2; one provider per method tells checkout and refunds which provider holds each payment. Q5-SH-040, Q5-SH-015
  - Not taken: two card gateways at once, which WooCommerce allows though it "does cause a few issues". Q5-WC-031
- **Checkout payment rules**: rules the merchant sets, within its place's list, to hide a method, or to reject an order or hold it for review, by amount, quantity, the buyer's or card's country, customer group, category, shipping method or market. Q5-SA-043, Q5-ZD-013
  - It draws on Salla's قيود الدفع, Zid's restrictions, Shopify's Payment Customization, WooCommerce's cash on delivery by shipping method and BigCommerce's countries per offline method. Q5-SA-046, Q5-ZD-013, Q5-SH-K1761, Q5-WC-004, Q5-BC-P0121
  - Why: rule 3; they are cheap to change, so they come late, as data the checkout reads. Q5-SA-043, Q5-SH-K1761
- **Payment record**: each payment on an order, with its method, provider, the provider's reference, amount, currency, state (awaiting, paid, recorded by the merchant, refunded) and money route. A refund goes back through the provider that took the payment, which stays callable for that after it leaves checkout. Q5-SH-015, Q5-WC-033, Q5-BC-085
  - It draws on BigCommerce's attribution of each order "to the provider that processed it"; Shopify's earlier provider, kept for returns, as in store fake-demo-sharrwah on 30 September 2026 (observed); WooCommerce's six months for refunds and disputes; and Shopify's manual mark as paid. Q5-BC-085, Q5-SH-015, Q5-SH-098, Q5-WC-033, Q5-SH-093
  - Why: rule 3, the data model; a payment that doesn't name its provider and route can't be refunded or reconciled once that provider has gone. Q5-SH-015, Q5-WC-033
- **Money route**: for each provider, and for manual methods, the path the buyer's money takes to the merchant's own bank account. On the **held route**, it waits in a **merchant balance**, held before payout by a payment company in an account opened through Sharrwah, or by Sharrwah itself.
