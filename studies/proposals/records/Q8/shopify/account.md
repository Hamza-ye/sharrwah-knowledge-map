# Q8 on Shopify: what Shopify's material shows

## [1]

- Shopify's Terms of Service describe "a complete commerce platform that enables merchants to unify their commerce activities". They call everything it offers the "Service(s)", and they cover any new features or tools added to the current Services. Q8-SH-052; Q8-SH-054
- The Help Center says Shopify has "several plans to help your business grow at every stage". Q8-SH-001
- Shopify Editions are described as "150+ updates to Shopify, twice a year" and "Everything new across Shopify. Every six months." Q8-SH-064; Q8-SH-065

## [2]

### Services, Additional Services and Beta Services
- The Terms name the Services, in "Online Services" and "POS Services". They also name "Additional Services", among them Shopify Payments, Shopify Capital, Shopify Shipping, point-of-sale services and equipment, and the services of Section 9. Q8-SH-053; Q8-SH-057
- Section 9 terms that the copies hold name Enhanced Services, POS Services, Shopify Shipping, Shopify Email, Shop, Third Party Services, Audiences, Shopify Checkout Extensibility and Agentic Storefronts. Q8-SH-K2130; Q8-SH-K2111; Q8-SH-K1986; Q8-SH-K2047; Q8-SH-K2071; Q8-SH-K2168; Q8-SH-K2054; Q8-SH-K1469; Q8-SH-K2104

### Plans
- The Help Center lists these plans: Starter, Lite, Retail, Basic, Grow, Advanced, Shopify Plus, Shopify for enterprise and Agentic. Q8-SH-002
- The pricing table prices Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus by month and by year. The API object `ShopPlan` "Indicates the plan tier (such as Basic, Advanced, or Plus)". Q8-SH-K2733; Q8-SH-K2734; Q8-SH-K2889; Q8-SH-K2890
- Shopify for enterprise is offered as the full platform or as "modular components": Shop Pay, Checkout and cart component, and Storefront component. Q8-SH-023; Q8-SH-024; Q8-SH-025; Q8-SH-026; Q8-SH-027
- The Agentic plan lets a merchant add products to Shopify Catalog and sell them through Shopify agentic storefronts without a Shopify online store. Q8-SH-016
- In the Flow app's answer on store capture-plus (observed 7 October 2026), the billing plan's public display name has these possible values: Advanced, Agentic, Agentic Enterprise, Basic, Development, Grow, Inactive, Lite, Other, Paused, Plus, Plus Trial, Retail, Shop Component, Staff Business, Starter and Trial. Q8-SH-098

### Organization, stores and the admin
- An Organization holds stores. On Shopify Plus, expansion stores are "additional .myshopify.com stores that fall under the same organization and contract". Dev stores are a further kind of store. Q8-SH-K1254; Q8-SH-K1256; Q8-SH-046; Q8-SH-051
- The admin's sections include Plan, Apps, Sales channels, Markets, Banking, Growth and Analytics. Q8-SH-K1312; Q8-SH-K2163; Q8-SH-K2062; Q8-SH-K1928; Q8-SH-K1791; Q8-SH-K2042; Q8-SH-K2117
- On store capture-plus (observed 7 October 2026), the admin's navigation showed, in this order, Home, Orders, Products, Customers, Growth, Discounts, Content, Markets, Analytics, Sales channels, Online Store, Agentic, Shop, Apps and Capture app. Q8-SH-104
- The same store's settings list showed General, Plan, Payments, Checkout, Customer accounts, Shipping and delivery, Taxes and duties, Locations, Apps, Sales channels, Domains, Customer events, Notifications, Metafields and metaobjects, Languages, Customer privacy and Policies. Q8-SH-103

### Sales channels, Markets and POS
- A sales channel app "connects a merchant's online store to surfaces outside Shopify". Q8-SH-K2063; Q8-SH-K2064
- Markets manage how customers experience a store "based on their location, customer group, retail location, or sales channel". Q8-SH-K1930; Q8-SH-K1931
- Point of Sale, Retail and POS Pro appear as named offers. Q8-SH-K2109; Q8-SH-K2110; Q8-SH-K2750

### Apps, extensions and functions
- Apps are either public (listed or unlisted) or custom. Shopify also makes apps of its own. Q8-SH-028; Q8-SH-029; Q8-SH-033; Q8-SH-K2165
- The Shopify App Store lists built-in features, such as Shopify Balance, Shopify Capital, Managed Markets and B2B, each on its own page. Q8-SH-K2702; Q8-SH-K2703; Q8-SH-K2701; Q8-SH-K2696
- App Store categories appear as unit names in the copies, such as Dropshipping apps, Subscription apps, Accounting apps and Store design apps. Q8-SH-K2363; Q8-SH-K2370; Q8-SH-K2392; Q8-SH-K2400
- Extensions add functionality to areas such as the Online Store, checkout, customer accounts and POS. Functions customize "your store's backend logic". Q8-SH-038; Q8-SH-039; Q8-SH-040; Q8-SH-K2210
- shopify.dev's table "lists all of the available app extensions that you can build". Among them are admin actions and blocks, the channel config extension, App Home UI extensions, checkout UI extensions, Shopify Functions, post-purchase, customer account UI extensions, Flow templates, the payments extension and POS UI extensions. Q8-SH-085; Q8-SH-086; Q8-SH-087; Q8-SH-088; Q8-SH-089; Q8-SH-090; Q8-SH-091; Q8-SH-092; Q8-SH-093; Q8-SH-094; Q8-SH-095; Q8-SH-096
- "An app extension isn't an app. It's a mechanism that lets an app add features to certain defined parts of several Shopify user interfaces." Q8-SH-081

### Entitlements and features in the admin
- Admin routes carry eligibility conditions named "entitlements", "features", "plans", "regions", "betas", "permissions" and "organizationFeatures". The stores' entitlements include `products`, `shopifyFinance.balance`, `shopifyFinance.capital`, `maximumLocations`, `shopifyTax.enabled` and `customDomains`. Q8-SH-K0175; Q8-SH-K0041; Q8-SH-K0191; Q8-SH-K2522; Q8-SH-K2524; Q8-SH-K2533; Q8-SH-K2591; Q8-SH-K2604; Q8-SH-K2578

Among them are unlimited products, discount codes, custom pricing by market, abandoned cart recovery, analytics and finance reports. Bot protection, Combined Listings, Audiences, free expansion stores, custom functions and Plus Certified apps are listed only for Plus. - The Agentic plan has no monthly subscription fee. Five features are not available on it, and products and settings carry over to a paid plan. - Shopify Plus organizations can have ten stores on their contract with no additional cost. Expansion stores share no data by default. Apps are billed per store, and theme licenses are needed for each store. The Plus terms and the 3-year renewal flow are shown only in a Plus store's admin. Q8-SH-050; Q8-SH-047; Q8-SH-048; Q8-SH-049; Q8-SH-K2636; Q8-SH-G0245; Q8-SH-G0246

### By place
- The Terms say "Not all Services or features will be available to all Merchants at all times". They add that Shopify is "under no obligation to make any Services or features available in any jurisdiction". Q8-SH-055
- The contracting party is set by the store's billing address. Q8-SH-K1243; Q8-SH-K1244; Q8-SH-K1247; Q8-SH-K1248
- Shopify Payments may be used only in the Designated Country. Shopify Capital requires a principal place of business and a bank account in the country. The Shopify Balance admin route names the regions US and GB. Q8-SH-K1537; Q8-SH-K1816; Q8-SH-K0041

### How new units are added or extended
- Additional Services can carry their own fees, terms and policies ("Additional Service Terms"). Q8-SH-058
- Apps add features. Public apps undergo Shopify's app review, App Store apps must use a Shopify billing solution, and the Built for Shopify badge is reviewed annually. Q8-SH-028; Q8-SH-029; Q8-SH-K2176; Q8-SH-031; Q8-SH-032
- Most app charges are billed through Shopify. Third Party Services are "solely between you and the applicable third party services provider". Q8-SH-030; Q8-SH-K2169
- A developer must register for a Partner Account and agree to the Partner Program Agreement. Q8-SH-K2183; Q8-SH-K2181
- Custom apps are built for one store and managed in the Dev Dashboard. Q8-SH-033; Q8-SH-034
- Extensions and functions become available when the app is installed and activated. They are activated in the admin area where they are used. Q8-SH-038; Q8-SH-041
- Extensions are versioned with the app. Some, such as post-purchase, Flow templates and the payments extension, need Shopify's review and approval. Extension-only apps install only by custom distribution. Q8-SH-083; Q8-SH-084; Q8-SH-092; Q8-SH-094; Q8-SH-095; Q8-SH-082
- A sales channel is created only by those who "own and operate, or are contracted to represent, a distinct surface". Q8-SH-K2066

### By release stage
- Section 9.10 of the Terms: Shopify may invite merchants to use "pre-release or beta features that are in development and not yet available to all merchants ('Beta Services')". These are not part of the Services, may carry additional terms, and may be discontinued or never released. The additional terms were not found. Q8-SH-059; Q8-SH-060; Q8-SH-061; Q8-SH-062; Q8-SH-063; Q8-SH-N01
- Location rules for order routing are marked "Beta" and are available by request through Shopify Plus Support. Q8-SH-097
- Admin routes gated by named "betas" include product imports, inventory counts, the merchant audit log, the theme editor and WorldFirst setup. The seller-protection terms sit at a path containing "beta". Q8-SH-K0014; Q8-SH-K0166; Q8-SH-K0178; Q8-SH-K0183; Q8-SH-K0199; Q8-SH-K2849
- On Shopify Plus, feature test drives let an organization activate upcoming features on chosen stores before release to all stores, with auto-release timelines. Q8-SH-042; Q8-SH-043; Q8-SH-044; Q8-SH-045
- Feature previews give early access on dev stores, one preview per store. Six are listed, among them `market_driven_shipping`, `physical_inventory_apis` and `ship_and_pickup_in_one_order`. Q8-SH-071; Q8-SH-072; Q8-SH-073; Q8-SH-074; Q8-SH-075; Q8-SH-076; Q8-SH-077; Q8-SH-078; Q8-SH-079
- On the Create store page for a dev store (organization 239421532, observed 7 October 2026), "Test a feature preview on this store" offered previews of "changes to Shopify and APIs before they're generally available". It listed Ship and pickup in one order (available to Plus merchants), Market-driven shipping, both together, Physical inventory, Liquid July '26 changes and Shopify admin's new design. Q8-SH-099; Q8-SH-100
- A backwards-incompatible change reaches merchants' admins nine months after the stable version that brings it. Q8-SH-080
- APIs are released every three months. Each stable version is supported for at least 12 months. The version types are Stable, Release candidate and Unstable. Q8-SH-036; Q8-SH-K2243; Q8-SH-066; Q8-SH-067; Q8-SH-068
- Deprecated parts are announced in the developer changelog. An app that keeps using unsupported resources becomes unsupported and is delisted from the Shopify App Store. Q8-SH-069; Q8-SH-070; Q8-SH-037
- Shopify reserves the right to modify the Services "for any reason, without notice and at any time". Q8-SH-056
