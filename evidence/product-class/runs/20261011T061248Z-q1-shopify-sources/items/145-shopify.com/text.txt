1. Eligibility to Participate in the Program

1.1 Eligibility. In order to participate in the Program, you must at all times maintain a Shopify Payments account in good standing and accept payment in cryptocurrency using the Cryptocurrency Payment Protocol. 2. Earning Credits on Shopify Platform Purchases

2.1. Earning and Calculating Credits. You will earn a Credit when you accept payment for an eligible transaction in cryptocurrency using the Cryptocurrency Payment Protocol. Credits are calculated as a percentage of the total purchase price of eligible purchases that are paid for in cryptocurrency using the Cryptocurrency Payment Protocol. You can view the Credit percentage that applies to you in the Shopify Admin. Credits are calculated at the time of transaction and paid to your Payout Account(s) along with payouts you receive for customer purchases processed through Shopify Payments. If you receive such payouts in cryptocurrency, then you will receive Credit payments in cryptocurrency to the cryptocurrency wallet that you have designated as a Payout Account. If you receive such payouts in fiat currency, then you will receive Credit payments in fiat currency to the financial account that you have designated as a Payout Account.

You are only entitled to receive Credits net of refunds, returns, and other transaction reversals. If a transaction for which you previously received Credits is subsequently subject to a refund, return, or other transaction reversal, then we may withhold or offset future Credit payments, or post a debit entry on your Shopify Payments account statement, to recoup the Credit amount that we provided on the refunded, returned, or reversed transaction.

Unlike traditional cash rewards that maintain a stable value, Cryptocurrencies can increase or decrease in value over short periods of time. This means your Credits might be worth less when you receive or spend them than when you originally earned them. Some Credits may be paid in cryptocurrencies called stablecoins, such as USD Coin. Stablecoins are designed to maintain a stable value (for example, 1 USD Coin should be worth 1 US dollar). However, this stability is not guaranteed. Stablecoins can lose value.

2.2 Restrictions and Recoup of Credits

You are not entitled to earn Credits on transactions that involve violations of these Credit Terms, the Shopify Payments Terms of Service, or any other terms that apply between you and Shopify.
