# The coordinator's rulings on the dry run's review (30 October 2026)

**What this is:** the coordinator's answers to the method questions the review's parts asked, where B-extent.md doesn't settle a case (`briefs/H-review.md`; `held/review-H-R1.md` to `R5b.md`; the join, `review-H.md`, section 5, M-1 to M-14). Each is a reading, the coordinator's, listed for the founder's veto at the next report (`PLAN.md`, section 3). Each goes into H-held.md for the other batches, and into the corrections of BC-1 and ZD-1. The join's D1 (a page's `beta` badge) and D4 (a mark as a quality) are settled within B-extent.md, as the join shows, and are restated here only for the corrections.

## Who does it, and `supports`

- **Maker evidence held outside the entry counts** (M-1; R1, M3). A held statement that names a company as of the platform's group counts wherever it is held: the entry, another source of the batch, or a cell's own maker evidence. The line cites it in `reading`. B-extent: "Maker evidence is a statement naming the company". So BigCommerce's Feedonomics cards are `own-service`, on the terms page's "any of Commerce's affiliates, subsidiaries, including, but not limited to Feedonomics Holdings, LLC".
- **The platform's own list of third parties' things to connect or install** (a channel catalog, a list of tax services, a section for accounting software) is "something the platform provides" (M-3, M-7; R1, M2; R3, F1 and M1). Accounting software at `/admin/apps/accounting` is therefore `extension-point`; the page itself, not held, would show more. A card or row that names a third party's solution is `extension-point`; one that names no one, or only the venue, is `core` by what it shows, with the missing maker noted as a gap; one that names a group company is `own-service`. The venue is the filler.
  - **Refined after WC-3** (30 October): the platform's suggestion to buy or install an extension from its marketplace, naming no maker (WooCommerce's `woocommerce_marketplace_suggestions`), shows the thing: `shows`, as the model already places the same extensions (CM-SEL-001's WooCommerce Subscriptions, CM-SEL-004's Bookings). `core` is for a card of the platform's own catalog of connections, which shows the platform connecting the thing (BigCommerce's channels). A suggestion that names a third party's product is `extension-point` on its capability, with the third party as filler.
- **A list of providers the platform's own words call its integrations is `core`,** each provider its filler (M-4; R2, M1). BigCommerce's tables 1 and 3: "BigCommerce gateway integrations", and "payment gateway integrations" that BigCommerce "supports", each row linked to the platform's own "Connecting with" article.
- **A provider's role is placed only from the entry and its sources** (M-5; R2, M2). A role known only from another platform's cell (instalments, cryptocurrency) is a lead for the second stage, not evidence.
- **A page the platform's navigation lists under its brand at an app's address,** with no maker and no gate stated (Zid Ads, Zid Voice), is `shows` on what the app does, with the missing maker a gap, and `core` on CM-APP-007 as the dashboard's page for an app (M-10; R4, M1).
- **A tooltip's "you can build X with it"** makes the merchant responsible: `shows`. What a plan line "includes" is `core` (M-9; R3, M3).
- **A third party's own feature through the platform's interface** (Avalara's return filing) is `extension-point` on the capability whose thing it is (CM-TAX-006), beside the provider's line (M-8; R3, M2).

## What an entry is, and where its other things go

- **A thing an entry names counts in each capability it names** (M-6; R2, M4; R3, F4). A child's entry whose own words name the parent's thing (its section is the parent's term) takes the parent's line too. That is domain C's rule, "a statement that names several things counts in each".
  - **Refined after the calibration** (30 October; `held/calibration.md`): the child takes the parent's line where it is a kind of the parent's thing, as Bank deposit, under OFFLINE PAYMENT METHODS, is an offline payment method (R2's case). A group the entry only sits under, whose title says where the entry is and not what it is (BigCommerce's `group: General`), gives it no line: the group is placed once, as a container (rule 3). Read as every section's group, the rule gave BigCommerce's settings lines that changed no cell: 7 at `high`, each of the General group's sections on store details (CM-ACC-006), and 14 at `xhigh`, over five groups.
- **A mark is never a quality** (D4, within B-extent.md; R2, M3; R3, F2):
  - a plan's mark of inclusion (a check, "Available", "Included") is the line's scope;
  - a provider's feature mark (Recurring Payments ✔) is a statement of the marked feature's capability: a line there, `shows` unless the row says who does it, the provider its filler.
- **A listing condition** (M-11; R4, M2; R5a; R5b):
  - a condition the platform sets for a store (a named store flag, a kind of store, a store's state such as trial or suspended, a plan) is the entry's scope, in the source's own identifiers. A computed one with no name of its own is named by the module it comes from (`use-is-ngo-store`), marked as a reading;
  - a condition on the store's own data (the blog turned on, an app installed) is one store's data, and goes in `reading`.
- **A conditional title is a statement of its own** (M-14; R4, M5): the platform states the page under each condition, and the condition is its scope. Zid's charity-store titles (Donations, Donation opportunities, Donors) go to the second stage, for CM-SEL-007.
- **A release badge** (D1, within B-extent.md; R4, M6; R5a, F4): a page the platform lists with a badge, `new` or `beta`, is offered to the store: `core`, with the badge in `reading`. Only a flag stated as not released (`released: false`, `is_experimental: true`) is `shows`. H-held.md had listed "a beta" among flags not released, which reads a listed page's badge as a flag.
- **A container** goes on the capability its title or its group names, as the cells' readings show; failing that, on the capability whose page its own path opens; failing both, `new:<its title>` with `core`, since it is the platform's own admin place, and the coordinator decides it with the other new things. It takes a second line where its own page holds another thing. A section whose title names only a grouping is placed the same way (M-13; the join's D3; R4, F6 and M4; R5a, F3).
- **BigCommerce's Extensions aren't CM-APP-011's** (M-2; R1, M1). The terms define an Extension as "a service developed by a third party but implemented and provided by Commerce"; CM-APP-011 is apps the platform's company makes. The five held lines take `new:Extensions` with `shows`, no place of its own. The model's own CM-APP-011 BigCommerce cell rests on the same homonym: its `own-service` from BC-S-0428 and BC-S-0432 becomes `shows`, and CM-APP-011's count drops by one platform.

## What an agent may read

- **Beyond the entry's item and its source,** an agent may read, to clarify an entry only: the run steps the platform's ledger or `index/runs.tsv` joins to it, the locales, and the model's cells (R3, F3; R4, F1, M3). So may the source page around a segment, and the answers `index/` names. A reading that rests on one cites it at its file (`<run_id>/steps/<NNN>`), and quotes it exactly. Scope, terms, filler and quality still come from the entry and its source (the join's FX-1).
- **What a run step or a locale states that no entry holds** isn't placed by the held pass (M-12; R4, M3; the join's D6). It goes in the notes, as a lead for the second stage, which reads held pages as statements for the cells that hold nothing. So R5a's scope "Saudi Arabia" for line 91, read from the Mokafaa page, isn't taken; the Mokafaa and Qitaf rates, Zid's three settings toggles (CM-INV-004, CM-MKT-023, CM-ORD-014), the feed's named platforms and Zid Insights' trial go to the second stage.
