# Shopify Tax pricing

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Shopify Tax pricing depends on when your store was created. For stores that were created <a href="/en/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/pricing#price-old">before May 13, 2026</a>, Shopify Tax is free until you reach an annual threshold of sales. For stores that were created <a href="/en/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/pricing#price-new">on or after May 13, 2026</a>, Shopify Tax is free until you reach a lifetime threshold of sales.</p>
<p>Make sure that you review the correct pricing for your store.</p>
</aside>

You can use Shopify Tax to automatically calculate and collect sales tax on your orders. Shopify Tax is free to use until your store reaches a sales threshold, after which a per-transaction fee applies based on your store's location and Shopify plan. You can review your Shopify Tax fees in the [billing section](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/billing) of your Shopify admin.

## Shopify Tax fees {#fees}

Fees for tax calculations apply if your store's global sales meet or exceed the free tier threshold. If your store's sales are below the threshold, then there are no charges for tax calculations.

The following types of sales count towards the threshold for tax calculations:

* Sales from the online store to any destination country
* Sales from [other channels](/manual/online-sales-channels) that you've connected to Shopify (for example, Facebook or Instagram)
* Sales made in person through [Shopify Point of Sale](/manual/sell-in-person)
* Sales made through [Shop](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop)
* Sales of gift cards
* Sales that include non-taxable products
* Sales made through all payment gateways
* Taxes and shipping fees on sales

If your store's sales are equal to or above the fee threshold, then a percentage fee applies to transactions with calculated tax. The fee applies to orders from regions (states, provinces, or countries) where you have tax collection activated, including orders where no tax is charged due to product exemptions. The fee doesn't apply to the following orders:

* Orders from regions (states, provinces, or countries) where you don't collect tax
* Orders imported from outside Shopify

This fee is charged to you with your subscription invoice for Shopify, or in a bill if you exceed your [billing threshold](/manual/your-account/manage-billing/managing-your-bills/viewing-your-bills/billing-cycles-thresholds#about-billing-thresholds). It isn't charged to your customers, and isn't displayed on their invoice. To review your tax transaction fees, go to [**Billing**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/billing) in your Shopify admin, where you can access and export your bills.

## Shopify Tax pricing for stores created before May 13, 2026 {#price-old}

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>The following pricing information applies to stores that were created before May 13, 2026, and have less than $25,000,000 USD in sales per year. If your store was created after this date, then refer to <a href="/en/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/pricing#price-new">Shopify Tax pricing for stores created on or after May 13, 2026</a>. If your annual sales are above $25,000,000 USD, or the equivalent in your local currency, then contact your Customer Success Manager (CSM) for pricing.</p>
</aside>

All stores selling to the United States, Canada, European Union, and United Kingdom can use Shopify Tax without charge until a certain annual threshold of global sales is reached on your store in your local currency. This threshold applies to each store individually. If you have more than one store, then each store has its own threshold for the amount of sales for free each calendar year. After a store exceeds the threshold, fees are applied up to certain maximums per region you collect sales tax in.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Fees are charged only in regions (states, provinces, or countries) where you&#39;ve activated tax collection.</p>
</aside>

For example, you have a store in the United States on the Shopify Plus plan. You collect tax in the United States and in Canada. The following applies to your store:

* Shopify Tax is free to use until your store reaches $100,000 USD in sales in a single calendar year.
* After the threshold is reached, there's a 0.25% fee for US orders where tax is collected, and a 0.15% fee for Canadian orders where tax is collected.
* Fees per order are capped at $0.99 USD.
* Fees for US orders are capped at $5,000 USD per year.
* Fees for Canadian orders are capped at $5,000 USD per year.
