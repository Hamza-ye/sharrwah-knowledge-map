About us

It is electronic financial account that enables all customers to transfer money “ Cash” to electronic money to perform money and purchases transfer  ,Top-up prepaid mobile, Bills payment, Cash in/ Cash out money , bills creating and payment and do all the government payments such as ( water , electricity , internet …..etc) through agents network and service points to facilitate, quicken and simplify financial transactions .

From its starting point, Cash Wallet has aimed to provide the electronic financial services to individuals and small, medium and large companies in addition to governments institution and organizations, and to develop wide  services and electronic solutions to manage various financial transactions in immediate ,fast ,secure and effective manner.

Cash Wallet gives to companies , organizations and governmental institutions the variety of solutions and services such as electronic money distribution service and payment of salaries to a large number of beneficiaries  and employees throughout Yemen ,in addition to the possibility of adding many services to the Wallet in future like ( online shopping ,electronic commerce ) ,and  link a global cards( Master , Visa…etc) with  the possibility of International Transfers  across the largest global transfers companies like (Money Gram or Western Union).

Cash Wallet has adopted the innovation way for the development of electronic payments services and that meets the needs of customers which include a payment and earning money by QR and other solutions that the company will provide in future like ( NFC and smart devices ) at most service points ,aiming to provide a modern technological environment to all segments of society ,member ,institution and organizations …..etc.

Cash Wallet is not only the electronic payment company but today is the largest company in Yemen through a network of agents that have around 1500 service agents throughout Yemen, and it includes all the services that the client needs.
