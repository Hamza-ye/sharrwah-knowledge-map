Main advantages
What is gifting?
Gifting lets your customers buy a product on behalf of someone else, add a personalized message and a branded gift card, and have it delivered as a gift.
Gift add-ons for every occasion
Customize the card design and fields, and let customers write their message or upload their own design.
Do I have full control over gifting?
You decide where gifting appears across the store, which card fields customers can fill in, and which products and categories are available as gifts.
