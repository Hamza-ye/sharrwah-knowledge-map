As part of our commitment to your privacy and security, we are transparent about the data we collect about you and how we use that data. We also offer you the option to opt out of some of our tracking.

EU/UK Marketing Banner

If you visit WooCommerce.com from within the EU or UK, we display a banner that describes some of our tracking and gives you granular choices about how your data can be used. Use the toggles within this banner to set your tracking preferences.

US sale opt-outs

If you reside in certain US states, you may have the right to opt out of having your data sold. Automattic does not directly sell personally identifiable information about its users. However, we sometimes share information with third parties who help us with our advertising, marketing, and analytics — as described in our Privacy Policy. Residents of these states may opt out by clicking the “Do not sell/share my personal information” links found in the footers of our sites.

Other tracking

There is some other tracking on WooCommerce.com that is not currently possible for you to opt out of. Most of this tracking is related to data that’s required to provide you with our service. For example, a cookie is needed for the site shopping cart to properly function.

You can find detailed descriptions of the data we collect about you, and how we use it, in our Privacy Policy. You can also find details about the cookies we use on WooCommerce.com in our Cookie Policy, which describes options for blocking this kind of tracking.
