About this app
arrow_forward
Floosak Wallet is your go-to digital wallet for secure and seamless money management in Yemen. With Floosak, you can send money transfers, withdraw funds, pay for commercial purchases, withdraw cash from ATMs, and enjoy digital payment and entertainment services. Whether you need to send money via Floosak Network, Akwa Money Network, or other transfer networks, Floosak provides the most reliable and efficient financial solution at your fingertips.

Key Features of Floosak Wallet:
1. Money Transfers Within Yemen
Send money instantly using Floosak Network, Akwa Money Network, or other local transfer services.
Secure and fast transactions to family, friends, or business partners.
Available 24/7 with instant notifications for each transaction.
2. Withdraw Money Easily
ATM withdrawals allow you to get cash whenever needed, using Floosak-supported ATMs.
3. Pay for Commercial Purchases
Make cashless payments at stores, restaurants, supermarkets, and online platforms.
Simply scan a QR Code or use digital payment options at point-of-sale (POS) terminals.
4. Entertainment & Digital Services
Recharge mobile credit, pay for internet, electricity, and water bills directly from the app.
Subscribe to entertainment services, top up gaming accounts, and renew digital subscriptions with ease.
5. Easy & Secure Money Management
Store your money safely in your digital wallet, ensuring quick access anytime, anywhere.
Track and manage your transaction history with an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
Advanced encryption technology for secure financial data protection.

Why Choose Floosak Wallet?
✅ Instant and secure money transfers within Yemen.
✅ Cash withdrawal via ATMs for convenient access to funds.
✅ Easy commercial payments at physical and online stores.
✅ Comprehensive financial management via a secure digital wallet.
✅ Available 24/7, eliminating the need to visit banks or transfer agents.

How to Use Floosak Wallet?
1️⃣ Download the app – Install Floosak Wallet on my mobile from Google Play.
2️⃣ Create an account – Register using your phone number and complete the verification process.
3️⃣ Add funds – Top up your wallet via authorized service providers or direct transfers.
4️⃣ Start using Floosak – Send money, pay bills, withdraw cash, and access entertainment services effortlessly.
