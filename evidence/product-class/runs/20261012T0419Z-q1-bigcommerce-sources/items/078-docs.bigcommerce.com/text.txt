# Closed Betas

> **Note**
>
> These features are in closed beta and are not available by default. To request access, contact [BigCommerce support](https://support.bigcommerce.com). The functionality documented here will not work unless your store has been approved for the beta program.

This list provides direct access to the documentation for our current closed beta programs. While this documentation exists and is accessible, the features they represent are limited availability, pending approval of the engineering teams managing them.

Some of these betas may have moved to general availability. If so, they will be documented in the appropriate area in the general developer documentation.

* [Checkout Extensions](/developer/docs/beta/checkout-extensions/overview) - Extend and enhance your store's checkout experience.
* [Team Permissions](/developer/docs/beta/team-permissions/overview) - Group-based access control with reusable user groups, policies, and per-store targets.
* [Page Widgets API](/developer/docs/beta/page-widgets/overview) - Develop more tailored experiences with page widgets that provide unique design and behavior.
* [Powered By](/developer/docs/beta/powered-by/api-documentation) - Build custom experiences for multiple store control panels under a single brand.
* [Product Recommendations API](/developer/docs/beta/product-recommendations/overview) - Improve and manage product recommendations on your storefront.
* [Webhooks](/developer/docs/beta/webhooks/overview) - Extensions to standard webhook functionality.
