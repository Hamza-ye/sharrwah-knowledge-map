About Jawali
Jawali.. my financial companion
Jawali:
It is an electronic wallet that enables you to carry out your financial transactions
(Purchases - Payment - Transfer) in an easy and secure way via mobile without the need to have a bank account.

It is a service provided by WeCash that allows you to send and receive money transfers, pay and receive collective payments such as salaries and benefits, pay the value of purchases, collect the value of sales and dues, charge mobile balance, pay internet and landline bills, fees and other services, and also enables you to withdraw and deposit money in cash to and from Your account in this service is through any authorized agent throughout Yemen or through any branch of the partners (Al-Najm - Al-Imtiaz - Yemen Express)



Conditions for subscribing to Jawali service:
• An active mobile phone number.
• A valid identity card for Yemenis, and a valid passport and residency for non-Yemenis.

 

:Create an account in Jawali service
You can open your financial account in the Jawali service through the Jawali application or from any service agent. Opening the account in the service requires the required data and uploading the necessary documents for registration and activation of the account.
• Click on create an account.
• Enter the required data.
• Upload the registration documents (personal card for Yemenis - passport for non-Yemenis).
• You will receive a one-time PUK recovery code at the beginning of registration, through which you can enter the application.
• You will receive a text message containing the activation code.
• To activate the account, take a selfie with the ID upon registration and call the Contact Center at 8000444, or visit the nearest service agent and provide him with the activation code and ID document for verification.
