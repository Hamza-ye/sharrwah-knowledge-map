SabaCash is an electronic money service, an alternative to paper money, provided by SabaFon Company in partnership with CAC Islamic Bank, which enables you to conduct your financial transactions via the mobile phone easily, quickly, and securely, such as transferring funds, paying the value of your purchases, paying utility bills such as landline and internet bills and recharging mobile phone balance. And others and the service can be used through an Internet connection through a dedicated application or without an Internet connection, and the service can be used via the USSD communication protocol for SabaFon numbers only.
Saba Cash subscriber does not need to use cash or credit cards to complete payments/purchases or transfers. As your phone number is your online account number through which you can pay for purchases and transfers
The Sabacash application provides you with a mobile phone recharge service for all mobile phone operators in Yemen, whether for the payment of the billing system or the prepaid system:
Pay bills, charge balance, and purchase packages (SabaFon, YOU, Yemen Mobile)
Recharge Y
ADSL home internet payment
Paying the landline
Payment of water and electricity

Icons available on sabaCash:
1- Send money
2 -Recharge balance
3 -Buy packages
4- Cash withdrawal
5 -Pay to merchant
6 -Pay bills
7 -Entertainment
8 -Communicate and support

You can also top up your wallet account by depositing cash through a SabaCash agent.
