# Shopify Payments

[Shopify Payments](https://www.shopify.com/payments) is the simplest way to accept payments online. It eliminates the hassle of setting up a third-party payment provider or merchant account and having to enter the credentials into Shopify. With Shopify Payments you're automatically set up to accept all major payment methods as soon as you create your Shopify store.

If you already use a direct third-party payment provider, then you can also [use Shopify Payments alongside your existing provider](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/using-shopify-payments-with-a-third-party-provider) to offer additional payment methods such as Shop Pay and local payment methods, and to recover failed transactions. Eligible United States stores that use Shopify Payments and Shop Pay can offer [health savings account (HSA) and flexible spending account (FSA) payments with Shop Pay](/manual/payments/shop-pay/hsa-fsa-payments) for qualifying products.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Before you use Shopify Payments, review the following requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li>Shopify Payments is only available to online stores in <a href="/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries">certain countries and regions</a>. If you operate a retail store, then check the <a href="/en/manual/sell-in-person/getting-started/setup-payment-method/shopify-payments-pos#supported">list of countries that support Shopify Payments for in-person selling</a>.</li>
<li>To use Shopify Payments, you must secure your account with <a href="/en/manual/your-account/logging-in/secure-sign-in">two-step authentication</a>. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/privacy-and-security/account-security">account security</a>. If you deactivate two-step authentication in the future, then you expose your account and financial information to potential criminal attacks.</li>
</ul>
</aside>


When you use [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments), you aren't charged third-party transaction fees for orders that are processed through Shopify Payments, Shop Pay, and Shop Pay Installments, and you aren't charged transaction fees on manual payment methods such as cash, cash on delivery (COD), and bank transfers. If you elect to use Shopify Payments as your primary payment provider, then PayPal transactions are exempt from third-party transaction fees.

If you use Shopify Payments alongside a third-party provider, then transaction fees apply. Learn more about [using Shopify Payments with a third-party provider](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/using-shopify-payments-with-a-third-party-provider).

You can use the [Shopify Payments activity report](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/payouts-activity-report) to review a reconciled breakdown of your Shopify Payments balance activity, fees, and payouts.
