WooAds Policy
Information and data we collect from your store

To use WooAds, you authorize us to access the data from your ad platform APIs about your ad campaigns that is necessary for us to provide you with the WooAds service.

We only use the data we retrieve from your connected ad accounts to provide you with the WooAds service and to improve our product for you. We do not share this data with any third-party service or product.

In order to sync this data between your store and WooAds, we make use of a data sync module from Jetpack. This collects and stores additional information about your site, which you can read about here. If you do not currently have Jetpack installed, activating WooAds will not install Jetpack.

You can read more about how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
