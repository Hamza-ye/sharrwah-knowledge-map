## B. What I want Sharrwah to make possible

This is my intent, to be tested against the five platforms. None of it is a decision. For each item, the work shows me how the platforms handle it, in their own terms. It then proposes Sharrwah's names, structure and behaviour, for me to accept or change.

1. **One platform, set up per market.** Adding a market, a currency or a provider shouldn't need a redesign.
2. **Rules land as availability, not as design.** A market's law, sanctions and licences, and my choice of providers, should mostly decide what is available there, not how Sharrwah is built. Where a market needs something the design can't carry, it comes to me.
3. **Conditions on what a seller can do.** Sharrwah can require things before a seller does certain things, such as before a store goes public or before it takes payment.
4. **Selling without Sharrwah touching the money.** A seller can sell with manual payment, arranged between seller and buyer, with no money passing through Sharrwah.
5. **Sharrwah chooses which payment methods are offered,** by its own criteria, per market. Methods may come from different kinds of providers, and through apps.
6. **A seller anywhere can register,** even where Sharrwah hasn't set up a market, subject to who may register.
7. **Currency.** My expectation, for the work to confirm or correct, is that the platforms' norm is one store currency. Whatever the norm proves to be is my starting point. How far Sharrwah lets currencies be set or changed is decided when the design gets there. The rial's two editions are not part of the core.
8. **Built gradually, in parts,** each part fitting the same overall model, drawn from the best of what the five platforms do.
