About WeCash

WeCash is a Yemeni electronic payment and financial services company, launched in 2021, focused on delivering digital financial solutions and mobile money services across the country. The company operates primarily through its flagship product, Jawali e-wallet, which enables users to send and receive money, make payments, and manage financial transactions digitally without needing a traditional bank account.

WeCash plays an important role in promoting financial inclusion in Yemen, providing accessible financial tools to individuals, businesses, NGOs, and government entities. It leverages a wide agent and remittance network, allowing users to easily perform cash-in and cash-out transactions nationwide.

SERVICES

Digital Wallet (Jawali): Allows users to store money electronically and perform financial transactions via mobile.
Money Transfers: Enables sending and receiving local remittances quickly and securely.
Bill & Service Payments: Supports payment for utilities, telecom, and other services.
Merchant Payments: Allows users to pay for goods and services through digital channels.
Cash-In / Cash-Out Services: Enables deposits and withdrawals through a wide agent network.
Bulk Payments: Supports salary payments, NGO disbursements, and financial aid distribution.
Mobile Top-Ups: Allows recharge of airtime and telecom services.

FAQs
I want to start a lending business but don't know where to begin. Can this help?

Absolutely. Use the Lenders directory as competitive research to see who operates in your market and what they offer. Use the Providers directory to understand infrastructure needs: identity verification, payments, credit bureaus, etc. Most lenders here showing "Powered by Lendsqr" launched in 2-8 weeks using our digital lending infrastructure instead. You get a pre-built loan management system and provider integrations already done.



What if I have a complaint about a lender listed here?

Email support@lendsqr.com with clear evidence. If the lender is powered by Lendsqr, our compliance and support teams get alerted immediately. If it’s a third-party lender, we escalate to them. Listings can be suspended if we find harmful or unethical practices.

What is Project X and how does it work?

Project X is a free, public-facing directory of lenders and providers powered by Lendsqr. It brings together over 1,900 lenders across Nigeria and other African markets in one place, so borrowers can search, compare, and apply for loans without visiting multiple websites.

All you have to do is use the filters to narrow results by country, loan amount, tenor, or borrower type, then click through to apply directly with the lender of your choice.

Are the lenders on this site licensed and regulated?

Yes, the lenders on Project X are licensed and regulated. Project X features loan products from lenders operating on the Lendsqr platform. Lendsqr operates as a cloud platform specifically for licensed lenders, and any lender offering loans through the system must hold the required regulatory licence to issue loans.

Depending on the type of institution they are, these lenders hold licences to operate as Fintechs, Money Lenders, Cooperatives, Microfinance Banks, Finance Houses, or other recognised financial entities.

How do I apply for a loan through this directory?

Project X does not offer loans directly — it is a discovery platform that helps you find the right lender. Once you find a lender that suits your needs, the application process is seamless. Here is how it works:

Visit the Project X website, browse the available loan offers, and click "Get this loan" on your preferred option
In the application modal that appears, input your desired loan amount and tenor to view the loan summary
Provide your personal, employment, education, BVN, and bank account information
The system will instantly run a scoring process and present you with a loan offer alongside a repayment breakdown
Input and verify your card details, then accept the loan offer
The system will automatically create your user account with that lender, log the loan request, and display a confirmation message
You will receive a link to log in directly to that lender's website while your loan request is forwarded to the lender for final approval
What is the difference between "Find a Loan" and "Find a Lender"?

They serve two different purposes. Find a Loan allows borrowers to browse a variety of top loan offers from different lenders on the Project X landing page, or use the loan filter to narrow down options based on a specific loan amount and repayment tenor.

Find a Lender allows borrowers to use the search bar to look up a specific lender by name, leading to a dedicated page that displays the lender's contact information, website, and all the specific loan products they currently offer.

If you are not sure where to start, Find a Loan is the better entry point.

Is my personal information safe when I apply?

Project X does not collect or store any personal or financial information from borrowers. When you click Apply and move to a lender's platform, your information is handled directly by that lender under their own privacy policy. We recommend only applying through lenders that have a clearly visible privacy policy on their website or app.

I've been rejected by banks. Will lenders here consider me?

Yes, lenders on this platform (especially those powered by Lendsqr) may still consider your application even if you have been rejected by traditional banks. Every lender here sets their own unique rules and risk acceptance criteria using an automated decision engine called Oraculi.

Instead of relying solely on traditional credit history, many of these lenders use alternative data such as your bank statement transaction patterns to get a fairer and more complete picture of your financial health.

Because each lender has different requirements and risk models, a rejection from a traditional bank does not automatically mean you will be declined here. A large number of digital lenders on the platform also offer loans without collateral and cater specifically to salary earners, small business owners, and first-time borrowers.

Use the "Best For" filter to find lenders suited to your situation.




How do I get my lending company listed for free?

Getting listed on Project X is free and straightforward. If you are already a Lendsqr-powered lender, your company is automatically listed on the directory as part of your account. If you are not yet on Lendsqr, you can sign up for a free account at https://lendsqr.com/nigeria and your lender profile will be created as part of the onboarding process. For other lenders who wish to be listed independently, contact the Lendsqr team directly via the website.

How do I get my loan company listed?

Getting listed on Project X is free. If you are a lender, simply sign up on the Lendsqr platform at https://lendsqr.com/nigeria, once your account is set up and your loan products are active, they will automatically be showcased on Project X without any additional action required.

If you are an independent lender or a service provider offering payments, identity verification, credit scoring, core banking, or similar services and would like to be listed, reach out directly to the team at marketing@lendsqr.com with your company information, loan products, countries served (for lenders) or service type (for providers).

What is the difference between a Lendsqr-powered lender and an Other Lender?

A Lendsqr-powered lender runs their lending operations directly on the Lendsqr platform: their loan products, borrower management, and disbursements are all managed through Lendsqr's infrastructure. These lenders carry a Verified by Lendsqr badge on their profile.

An Other Lender is a lending company operating independently that has been listed in the directory for borrower visibility, but does not use Lendsqr as their operating platform. Both types are visible to borrowers, but Lendsqr-powered lenders have been more directly verified through the onboarding process.
