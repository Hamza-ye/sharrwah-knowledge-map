# Q6 on Salla: what Salla's material shows

## [1]

### Sign-up (التسجيل)

- The sign-up page asks for the merchant's name, email, mobile number and password. Pressing (تسجيل) accepts "سياسة الاستخدام على منصة سلة", and a code sent to the phone verifies the number. Q6-SA-001, Q6-SA-002, Q6-SA-K1992
- The UAE and Kuwait articles add a choice of language at the sign-up page and then ask for the same fields. Q6-SA-003
- In the onboarding, "🌍دولة المتجر" asks from which country the store is registered, "because that determines the government documents". The step "🎯الكيان القانوني" asks for the store's legal entity. Q6-SA-K0083, Q6-SA-K1989, Q6-SA-K0088, Q6-SA-K2523
- The dashboard's sign-up strings say "اختيارك للدولة يحدِّد المتطلبات القانونية اللازمة لمتجرك." / "Your country selection sets the legal requirements for your store." Q6-SA-008
- For an individual (فرد), the UAE onboarding asks for the status of "متطلباتك النظامية للبيع في الإمارات". The Kuwait article carries the same sentence, with الإمارات. Q6-SA-005
- What the country step lists, and what it connects to, is not held. Q6-SA-G0162

### Who may register: the usage agreement (اتفاقية الاستخدام)

- "( التاجر )" is defined as every merchant who registers on Salla to create a store, "سواءً كان شخص طبيعي أو معنوي". Q6-SA-014
- Under المادة الثانية – أهلية التاجر القانونية, the merchant must have legal capacity and be at least eighteen, "كما هو معتبر في القوانين والأنظمة المرعية بالمملكة العربية السعودية". Q6-SA-011
- A merchant under eighteen may trade with proof of the guardian's consent, such as صك الولاية. Q6-SA-012
- An establishment, company or other legal form that registers must itself have legal capacity. Q6-SA-013
- Under المادة الرابعة, every person with legal capacity may create a store. The store must not breach the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and Salla keeps the right to refuse to register a store that does not comply with them or with the agreement. Q6-SA-015, Q6-SA-016
- A person whose membership or store Salla has cancelled, or a court has ordered cancelled, may not use Salla as a merchant. Q6-SA-017
- The merchant acknowledges having met, before registering, every procedure and condition required by the official bodies in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Q6-SA-018
- Every store wishing to register must have "سجل تجاري قائم أو وثيقة عمل حر". It must also have the licences its activity needs, "كحساب أبشر وحساب بنكي في أحد البنوك السعودية ورقم جوال سعودي". Q6-SA-019
- An individual merchant provides national ID and freelance-document data. An establishment, company, charity or legal entity provides its commercial registration and any documents Salla asks for. Q6-SA-020, Q6-SA-021
- Under المادة السابعة عشرة, Salla may suspend a store or cancel membership in listed cases, among them "إذا لم يكن بإمكان منصة سلّة توثيق أي من معلومات المستخدم المقدمة إليها". Q6-SA-022
- The agreement served at salla.com/ar-ae/terms opens with the same company, registered in Saudi Arabia. It carries the same clause requiring Absher, a Saudi bank account and a Saudi mobile number. Q6-SA-023, Q6-SA-024
- Other agreements name شركة تطبيق سلة لتقنية المعلومات and شركة منصة سلة للاتصالات وتقنية المعلومات. The footer names شركة سلة القابضة. Q6-SA-K2507, Q6-SA-K2510, Q6-SA-K2516, Q6-SA-K2512
- Which Salla entity contracts per market (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait), and the rule that decides it, were not found. Q6-SA-G0213, Q6-SA-G0214
- No UAE- or Kuwait-specific eligibility clause was found in the agreement. Q6-SA-N02

### Store verification in Saudi Arabia (توثيق المتجر)

- Verification is "المحطة الأخيرة لتدشين متجرك". Q6-SA-027
- The owner's national ID or iqama must be valid and match Absher. An SMS from Absher goes to the owner's registered number. Q6-SA-025, Q6-SA-K0089
- Entity types and documents: فرد, a freelance document (وثيقة العمل الحر), "للمواطنين السعوديين فقط"; مؤسسة - شركة, a valid commercial registration, "للمواطنين والمقيمين"; جمعية خيرية, its registration certificate. Q6-SA-026, Q6-SA-K0084
- The table "المستند المطلوب حسب نوع كيانك" gives the same entities, with "مَن يستحقه" (Saudi citizens only; citizens and residents; licensed charities) and the bank account for each. Q6-SA-030, Q6-SA-031, Q6-SA-032
- The checklist asks for an ID or iqama, the owner's mobile registered in Absher, the freelance document or commercial registration, a stamped IBAN certificate, and clear images. Q6-SA-033
- The individual's bank account must match the owner's ID or freelance document. A company's or establishment's account must be a business account in its name, linked to its registration. Q6-SA-029
- The FAQ says an individual may use a personal or business account, and an establishment must use a business account in its name. Q6-SA-040
- Foreign merchant: "يمكن للتاجر الأجنبي إنشاء متجره على سلة سواء بصفته شركة أو مؤسسة". To complete verification a commercial registration must be issued, and it must be "سجلاً تجارياً سعودياً". Q6-SA-035
- One owner may verify several stores with the same data, with no upper limit on the number of stores. Q6-SA-038
- A waqf institution may appoint a legal representative as the store's user. Q6-SA-041
- Salla calls verification "مبادرة من شركة سلة". It does not replace verification with government bodies. Q6-SA-039
- For stores on باقة بيسك, verification is "متطلب أساسي للبدء في عملية البيع". Q6-SA-036
- Without verification, payment and settlement operations are suspended until it is completed. Q6-SA-037
- Verification enables e-payment gateways, shipping services and "كامل خصائص منصة سلة دون قيود". Q6-SA-028, Q6-SA-034
- Verification takes 24 to 48 hours, and Salla's team reviews each request. Q6-SA-042, Q6-SA-K0087
- The dashboard holds a permission for شهادة توثيق منصة الأعمال. Salla's FAQ calls the Business Platform e-commerce certificate mandatory under Ministry of Commerce rules. Q6-SA-K0079, Q6-SA-K0093, Q6-SA-K2519
- The flag kyc_new_workflow lists the countries AE, SA, KW, QA, OM and BH. Q6-SA-K1988

### Store verification in the UAE and Kuwait

- In the UAE, verification is among the last steps to prepare a store "للعمل في الإمارات" and leads to activating e-payments. Q6-SA-K0090
- UAE documents: a valid trade licence; Emirates ID and passport for each owner, or an alternative ID for some licence types; a TRN only if VAT-registered. Q6-SA-043, Q6-SA-044, Q6-SA-045
- The Emirates ID must start with 784. Q6-SA-046
- A licence issued on an alternative ID or passport is accepted with that document. Q6-SA-047
- Accepted UAE licences are E-Trade, mainland (DED), free zone, professional and freelance permit, "قد يتم الرفض او القبول بناء على تقييم الحالة". Q6-SA-048, Q6-SA-K0085
- The bank account accepted depends on the licence and the number of owners. It must be at a UAE bank, chosen from the supported UAE banks, and in AED. Q6-SA-049, Q6-SA-050, Q6-SA-K1564
- Kuwait asks for the 12-digit Kuwaiti civil number, the civil card and passport for each owner and manager, a commercial-register extract with beneficial owners, and a business bank account at a Kuwaiti bank in KWD. Q6-SA-051, Q6-SA-052, Q6-SA-K0086, Q6-SA-K1565, Q6-SA-K1566
- Salla Payments in the UAE and Kuwait requires a Plus, Pro or Special plan and completed verification. The UAE requirements add activation by Salla's team. Q6-SA-K1522, Q6-SA-K1526, Q6-SA-K1523, Q6-SA-K1528
- Fees and settlement follow the payment services contract (عقد خدمات الدفع) signed during verification. The contract's parties and where it is signed were not found. Q6-SA-K2015, Q6-SA-K2017, Q6-SA-G0176, Q6-SA-G0177
