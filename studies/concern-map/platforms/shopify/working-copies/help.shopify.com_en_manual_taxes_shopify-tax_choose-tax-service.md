# Choose a tax service

If your store is located in the United States, then you can select [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index), [Manual Tax](/manual/taxes/manual-tax-settings), or use a [third-party tax service provider](/manual/taxes/tax-apps) if you need specialized solutions. If your store is located in Canada, the European Union, or the United Kingdom, then you can select [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index), [Basic Tax](/manual/taxes/registration), [Manual Tax](/manual/taxes/manual-tax-settings), or use a [third-party tax service provider](/manual/taxes/tax-apps), depending on which tax services are available to your store.

Each calendar year, Shopify Tax is free until you reach the following thresholds in global GMV per store:

* $100,000 USD for stores located in the United States
* €100,000 EUR for stores located in the European Union
* £100,000 GBP for stores located in the United Kingdom
* $100,000 CAD for stores located in Canada
* $100,000 USD for stores located in other regions

After you reach the threshold, standard rates apply to eligible orders in the regions where you collect tax.

In Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union, Basic Tax is available only for regions where your store was set up to collect tax before May 13, 2026, and isn't available if your store currently uses Manual Tax in the region. Availability is determined separately for each region. Stores that already use Basic Tax in these regions can continue to use it without interruption. Basic Tax remains available to new and existing stores in all other supported regions.

In the United States, stores can't newly enroll in Basic Tax as of July 14, 2025.

Review the following table to determine which tax service is best for your needs.

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>Try to keep your tax services consistent. If you&#39;re using Manual Tax with Basic Tax or Shopify Tax in the European Union and United Kingdom, then you might be unable to properly collect duties.</p>
</aside>

## Choose your tax service {#choose-tax-service}

After you review the features and considerations for each tax service, you can choose the service that you want to use.

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>In the United States, Canada, the European Union, and the United Kingdom, changing your tax service can be permanent. If Basic Tax isn&#39;t available to your store for a region, then you can&#39;t switch back to it after you save a different tax service for that region.</p>
</aside>

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > [**Taxes and duties**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/taxes).


2. Optional: If you want to use a [third-party tax service provider](/manual/taxes/tax-apps), then find the tax app that you want to use in the **Tax apps** section, and then click **Install**.
3. Click **United States**, **Canada**, **European Union**, or **United Kingdom**, depending on which region you want to select a tax service for.
4. In the **Tax service** section, click <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg" alt="Horizontal menu" class="admin-icon" />, and then click the option in the dialog box. Depending on your current tax service, this is labeled **Upgrade service**, **Switch service**, or **Downgrade service**.
5. Select the tax service that you want to use.
6. Click **Save**.

### Registration-based tax collection {#registration-based}

Taxes are automatically collected for eligible states and countries states where you have a registered tax liability.

### Rate calculation using product categories {#product-category-rates}

Tax exemptions and modifications are calculated automatically based on the [category of the product](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/product-categories-tax).

### Automatic recommendations for product categories {#product-category-recommendations}

Product categories are [automatically suggested](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/product-categories-tax#automatic-suggestions) for your products.

### Managed Markets {#markets-pro}

[Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets/overview) is supported on Shopify Tax and Basic Tax. You can't use Manual Tax with Managed Markets.

### Rooftop accuracy (United States only) {#rooftop-accuracy}

Rooftop accuracy means that taxes are calculated precisely according to street address, rather than being limited to ZIP codes. This feature is included only in [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index).

### Support for state fees (United States only) {#state-fees}

Some states collect tax-like fees. For example, in Colorado, orders containing taxable goods that are delivered by a motor vehicle in Colorado are subject to a non-refundable retail delivery fee. This feature is included only in [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index).

### Tax holidays (United States only) {#tax-holidays}

Special tax rates that apply on specific dates are automatically calculated. For example, Texas has a sales tax holiday from August 5-7 each year. This feature is included only in [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index).

### Proportional shipping tax {#proportional-tax}

[Proportional shipping tax](/manual/taxes/shipping-tax#proportional-shipping-tax) refers to the tax charged on shipping being calculated in proportion to the tax charged on products. Proportionally taxed shipping considers the tax rate on line items and the line item value to charge the weighted average percentage to shipping, which is a requirement in the European Union and the United Kingdom.

### Tax reports {#tax-reports}

Allows you to [access reports](/manual/taxes/tax-reports) that display the amount of taxes you've collected.

### Liability insights {#liability-insights}

Automatic suggestions and insights on your potential tax liabilities based on where you ship and sell to. This feature is included only in [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/index). Tax liability insights are available for the following countries and regions:

* The [United States](/manual/taxes/us/us-tax-liability)
* The [European Union](/manual/taxes/eu/eu-tax-liability)
* The [United Kingdom](/manual/taxes/uk/uk-tax-liability)
* [Canada](/manual/taxes/canada/canada-tax-liability#st-liable) 

### Advanced reports (United States and Canada only) {#advanced-reports}

Advanced tax reports have additional details about the amount of tax you collect, and can be used to help you file with tax authorities.

### B2B tax exemptions {#b2b-tax-exemptions}

Allows you to sell licensed products that are tax-exempt.

### Customer tax exemptions {#customer-tax-exemptions}

Allows you to designate that [specific customers are tax-exempt](/manual/taxes/tax-overrides#set-up-tax-exempt-customers).

### Product and shipping tax overrides {#tax-overrides}

[Assign product tax overrides](/manual/taxes/tax-overrides#product-tax-override) or [shipping tax overrides](/manual/taxes/tax-overrides#apply-tax-override) to apply specific tax rates that override any other tax calculations for selected products or shipping destinations. Overrides can only be applied at the state level or country level if you use Manual Tax.

### Collecting tax in states with no state sales tax (United States only) {#states-no-tax}

Allows you to collect tax in states that have no overall tax rate. For example, if you're in New Hampshire and you sell food that's prepared and consumed on-site, then you might need to apply a 9% tax rate.

### Generate and display VAT invoices (European Union and United Kingdom only) {#vat-invoices}

Allows you to generate and display VAT invoices. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, VAT invoices are required for VAT-exempt distance sales. VAT invoices are displayed on the order confirmation page for customers, and on the order page for you to access and download. In order to use invoicing capabilities, you must activate VAT invoices.

### Add custom logo to VAT invoices (European Union and United Kingdom only) {#custom-logo}

Allows you to generate and display VAT invoices with a custom logo that you upload.
