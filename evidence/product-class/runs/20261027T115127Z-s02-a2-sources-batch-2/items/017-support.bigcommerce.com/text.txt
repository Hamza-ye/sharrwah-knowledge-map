The Settings page is a centralized location for many key functions of your store. From here, you can configure and manage multiple features ranging from your store’s basic information, to payment and shipping methods, up through more advanced tools, such as API accounts.

This article provides an overview of the groupings and individual sections of the Settings page, as well as links to related articles containing more detail.

 
Requirements
To view and manage your store’s settings, the Manage Settings permission must be enabled on your user account.
Depending on the area being managed, you may need additional permissions. See the relevant linked articles for specific requirements.

STORE PROFILE

Provide basic information about your business, such as the store name, contact information, default country, and the default storefront language under Settings › Store profile. For more information, see Store Profile Settings.

 

Look different? Stores with multiple languages enabled configure their default country and storefront language under Settings › Localization. For more information, see Localization Settings.

LOCALIZATION SETTINGS

Stores with multiple languages enabled configure their default country, storefront language, physical dimensions, and date and timezone under Settings › Localization. For more information, see Localization Settings.

CURRENCIES

Configure which currencies appear on your store, their exchange rates, storefront channels, and display settings under Settings › Currencies. For instructions on enabling currencies in your store, see Managing Currencies. To learn more about how multicurrency works in your store, see Using Multiple Currencies.

PAYMENTS

Manage payment integration connections, offline payment methods, and select digital wallets under Settings › Payments.

For more information, see:

Manage Buy Online, Pick up in Store (BOPIS) physical location details under Settings › Locations. For more information, see Multi-Location.

PICK UP METHODS

Configure Buy Online, Pick up in Store (BOPIS) pick up methods and assign to locations under Settings › Pick up methods. For more information, see Multi-Location.

Enter your shipping origin address as well as configure and manage shipping zones, methods, and rates under Settings › Shipping.

WEBSITE

Configure your physical dimension settings, default SEO metadata, sitewide HTTPS, and robots.txt file under Settings › Website.

Physical Dimension Settings

Physical dimension settings allow you to specify the measurement units your store uses for product weight and volume, choose the display style for decimals, and assign a factoring dimension for calculating shipping costs. For more information, see Physical Dimension Settings and the Shipping Calculator.

 

Look different? Stores with multiple languages enabled configure their physical dimensions under Settings › Localization. For more information, see Localization Settings.

Search Engine Optimization

Under Search engine optimization settings, enter the SEO metadata for your store’s homepage as well as your custom domain’s www redirect preference. For more information, see SEO Fields.

 

Multi-Storefront: If you have Multi-Storefront (MSF) enabled on your store, the storefront SEO settings found in Channel Manager will take priority and override global SEO store settings. This allows each storefront to have its own SEO strategy.

HTTPS

By default, BigCommerce stores have sitewide HTTPS already enabled. For more information, see Sitewide HTTPS.

Search Engine Robots

The robots.txt file is a tool that helps improve your SEO and reduce server load by providing instructions to search engine and AI bot crawlers on when and where they can crawl your site. For more information, see Robots.txt File.

DISPLAY

Configure your storefront, control panel, category, and product display settings under Settings › Display.

Display Settings

Manage the general display behavior of multiple areas of the storefront, such as the cart page, customer account pages, and product listings. For more information, see Display Settings.

Control Panel

Configure the display options for the control panel text editor and product thumbnails in your product list.

For more information, see:

Using the WYSIWYG Editor
Product List

Category Settings

The Category Settings section allows you to manage which products are displayed on category pages, how they are sorted, and the display depth in your store’s navigation menu. For more information, see the “Category Settings” section of Product Categories.

Product Settings

Configure options for product attributes, including pricing presentation, SKU, weight, customer rating visibility, and more. For more information, see Product Display Settings.

IMAGE

Image settings allows you to set image sizes and related settings for Blueprint themes. To navigate to your store's Image settings go to Settings › Images. For a complete list of Blueprint image settings, see Blueprint Themes.

 

Don't see the Images tabs? Stencil themes control product image sizes in Page Builder. Stores with a Stencil theme enabled won't see the Images tab.

SHARE

Under Settings › Share, manage the displayed social media icons on product and blog pages as well as enable or disable Pinterest Rich Pins.

For more information, see:

Adding Social Media Links
Advertising with Pinterest
 

Looking to connect to a sales or marketing channel? Channel Manager allows you to connect and manage different sales and marketing channels. For more details, including the available options, see Selling Everywhere with Channel Manager.

DATE & TIMEZONE

Manage the internal time tracking used in your store's control panel under Settings › Date & Timezone. These settings affect time-related features, such as order timestamps and promotion start or end dates. For more information, see Date and Timezone Settings.

 

Look different? Stores with multiple languages enabled configure their date and timezone under Settings › Localization. For more information, see Localization Settings.

URL STRUCTURE

Adjust your store’s URL structures for products, categories, and web pages under Settings › URL Structure. For more information, see the “URL Structure” section of SEO Fields.

SEARCH

Configure the built-in storefront search results sort order and search logic under Settings › Search. For more information see Configuring Store Search.

Stores using Stencil themes can customize the search results page appearance in Page Builder. Additional options are also available for themes based on Cornerstone.

 

Stores using a legacy Blueprint theme will see additional search display settings. For more information on these settings, see Blueprint Themes.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Manage various aspects of your store such as shopper password complexity, inactivity thresholds, reCAPTCHA, and privacy under Settings › Security & Privacy.

For a detailed overview of the security settings available for your store, see Security and Privacy Settings.

MISCELLANEOUS

Various additional store settings can be accessed under Settings › Miscellaneous.

Email Settings

Manage several email related features, including product reviews, order invoice forwarding, custom SMTP settings, system notification address, and Abandoned Cart Saver.

For information on the supported features, see:

Managing Reviews
Order Notifications
Email in BigCommerce
Using the Abandoned Cart Saver

Advanced Store Settings

Enable and disable cart and checkout availability on your storefront using the Allow Purchasing checkbox under Advanced Store Settings. For more information on product purchasability and availability, see Managing Product Availability.

Order Settings

Set your store’s starting order number, controlling the values of the next and subsequent orders. For more information, see Understanding Order IDs.

Product review rate limiting

Manage the allowed frequency of customer product reviews. For more information, see Managing Reviews.

ACCOUNTING

Connect your store with select accounting integrations to sync order, product, and customer data under Settings › Accounting.

DATA SOLUTIONS

Connect and manage storefront tracking services, data warehouses, business intelligence platforms, and customer data platforms under Settings › Data solutions. For more information, see Data Solutions.

CHECKOUT

Configure checkout-related settings such as the checkout type, customer account behavior, checkout fields display, and the order confirmation page experience under Settings › Checkout. For more information, see Optimized One-Page Checkout.

ACCOUNT SIGN UP FORM

Manage the signup and address form fields that display on your store’s checkout and customer account pages under Settings › Account sign up form. For more information, see Adding and Editing Fields in the Account Signup Form.

EXPORT TEMPLATES

View the built-in export templates and manage custom templates used by the legacy Import/Export tool under Settings › Export templates. For more information, see Legacy Product Import/Export | Exporting Your Products.

STORE LOGS

Access logging of storefront and control panel events under Settings › Store logs. For more information, see Using Store Logs.

FILE ACCESS (WEBDAV)

View your user account’s WebDAV login information under Settings › File access (WebDAV). For more information, see File Access (WebDAV).

USERS

Manage control panel user accounts and permissions under Settings › Users. For more information, see User Roles and Permissions.

EARLY ACCESS

Features that are available for all stores that may not yet be development complete can appear under Settings › Early access. See the feature’s dedicated article for more information.

FAQ

If a setting is not available in the control panel, can I adjust it somewhere else?

For Stencil themes, there may be a setting found within the Theme Styles menu of Page Builder. Note that some desired changes may require theme customization. To connect with a developer, visit the BigCommerce Partner Directory, or submit a request directly to a small business agency.
