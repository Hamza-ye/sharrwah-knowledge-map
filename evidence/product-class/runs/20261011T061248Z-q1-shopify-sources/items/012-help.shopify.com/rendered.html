# Shopify Bill Pay

<aside class="note note-app"><p><img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/app-store/listing_images/1d80b691267c557c7cf32bfa7db969ae/icon/CJfm4PLImIADEAE=.png" alt="Shopify Bill Pay app icon"> <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/bill-pay">Shopify Bill Pay</a>: Install Shopify Bill Pay from the Shopify App Store.</p>
</aside>

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Shopify Bill Pay is available only to merchants in the United States. Emails originating from the <code>@shopify-billpay.melio.com</code> domain are legitimate.</p>
</aside>

You can use Shopify Bill Pay to pay vendors, business phone bills, freelancers, or any service that invoices your business.

Shopify Bill Pay gives you the flexibility to pay bills how you want, and still let your vendors receive payments in the way they expect. For domestic payments, you can pay any bill using a bank transfer, business credit or debit card, or your Shopify Balance account. When you pay a bill your vendor receives a bank transfer payment, or a paper check in the mail. For international payments, you can pay bills in USD using a bank account or your Shopify Balance account, and your vendor is paid through a wire transfer.

After you install Shopify Bill Pay, you can give your vendors a unique email address to email bills to you, and the bills are automatically added to your unpaid bills in Shopify Bill Pay. You can also add bills manually, or have them sync automatically with [QuickBooks online](/manual/finance/shopify-bill-pay/setting-up-shopify-bill-pay#connect-quickbooks).
