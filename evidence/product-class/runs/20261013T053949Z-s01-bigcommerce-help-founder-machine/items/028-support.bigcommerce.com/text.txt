Getting Started with BigCommerce
CHAPTER 5:
Payments

To view this video in fullscreen, visit Payments.




Accepting payment for goods and services is a core function of every online store.

In this guide, we will introduce you to our streamlined checkout experience, look at how currencies work and how to manage them, and showcase all the different ways you can receive payments from your customers.

 
Checkout

The built-in BigCommerce checkout page is optimized for conversion and provides a streamlined experience for customers.

Responsive design - The checkout page automatically adjusts to the device viewing it, so it is optimized at all times for mobile, tablet and desktop viewing.
Persistent Cart - Logged-in customers can access their in-progress cart from any device.
Autocomplete for Addresses - This feature is powered by Google Maps and helps shoppers to enter their billing and shipping addresses faster, easier, and with greater accuracy. See Google Autocomplete for Addresses for more information.
Stored payment methods - Customers can securely save their credit card and PayPal credentials for quick access during the next time they check out. See Stored Payment Methods for more information.
Guest shoppers - You can require shoppers to create an account to check out, or allow for guest checkout. You can even automatically create accounts for guest shoppers, where they only need to set a password to access their provisional store account. See Managing Customer Accounts and Customer Storefront Accounts for more information on customer account features.
Passwordless login - Reduce customers’ frustration and the friction caused by forgotten passwords by providing them with a one-time login link sent by request to their account email.
Customizable form fields - Add, remove, and make address fields required during customer account creation with the built-in account signup form editor. See Adding and Editing Fields in the Account Signup Form.
 
Currency

With BigCommerce, you can control which currencies are shown on the storefront, and which ones can be used for payment.

Set up multiple display and transactional currencies.
To allow shoppers to check out with a currency, you must first set up a payment provider that supports it.
Maintain price expectations throughout the storefront experience and encourage conversion by allowing shoppers to browse and transact in their native currency.
Offering local currencies allows shoppers to avoid currency conversion fees that can be charged by their financial institution.
Order details include the currency the order was transacted in, giving you greater transparency and insights into your customers.

See Managing Currencies to learn more.

 
Payments

Shoppers are more likely to complete their purchase when they are able to pay with their preferred payment method.

BigCommerce supports over 60 online payment integrations; serving 230 countries and over 140 currencies. See Available Payment Gateways for a comprehensive list.
Shoppers can pay with credit or debit cards, digital wallets like PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay.
Accelerated Checkout solutions like Fastlane by PayPal and Link by Stripe offer a fast, passwordless checkout experience for shoppers.
Several integrations also allow you to offer local payment methods, like Alipay, Boleto Bancario, Sofort, Tilopay and SEPA. Learn more in our Guide to Alternative Payment Methods.
Offer offline payment methods, such as “Pay in Store”, Money Order, or Bank Transfer.
