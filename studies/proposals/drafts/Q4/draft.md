# Q4: Orders whose payment doesn't pass through the platform, a draft proposal

## 1. How the platforms handle it

### [1]

- All five let a seller take orders that the buyer pays outside the platform. They match on role (basis 4), each in its own words, all in stated pages: with a Shopify manual payment method the seller "can arrange to receive their payment in a way that works best for them"; with Salla's bank transfer "يقوم العميل بتحويل مبلغ الطلب مباشرة من حسابه البنكي إلى حسابك"; Zid shows the merchant's bank account at payment "لتمكين العملاء من الدفع عن طريق التحويل البنكي"; with WooCommerce's direct bank transfer "the payment process is manual and happens outside your WooCommerce powered store"; BigCommerce's offline payment methods "do not require a credit card or a traditional payment gateway". Q4-SH-K1737, Q4-SA-P0099, Q4-ZD-K0124, Q4-WC-002, Q4-BC-P0114
- WooCommerce and BigCommerce share the term offline payment method (basis 1), and Salla and Zid the terms التحويل البنكي and الدفع عند الاستلام (basis 1), all in stated pages. Q4-WC-002, Q4-BC-P0114, Q4-SA-P0098, Q4-ZD-K0098
- Cash on delivery collected by the seller's own carrier keeps the money out of Salla: with a carrier outside Salla's shipping labels "لا تتولى سلة تحصيل مبالغ الدفع عند الاستلام في هذه الحالة", and the amounts are settled "مباشرة بين التاجر وشركة الشحن". WooCommerce's cash on delivery "lets you take payments at the time of delivery", BigCommerce's lets customers "pay you with cash", and Shopify lists it among the common types of manual payments (role, basis 4, all stated). Q4-SA-P0091, Q4-SA-P0092, Q4-WC-004, Q4-BC-P0117, Q4-SH-003
- Orders the seller creates take such payments too (role, basis 4, all stated): a Salla manual order takes bank transfer with the receipt uploaded, or cash on delivery with its commission; Zid's تفعيل الدفع اليدوي lets the merchant "إنشاء وتنفيذ الطلبات دون الحاجة إلى وجود عملية دفع إلكترونية فعلية"; BigCommerce's manual orders "can reference an offline payment such as cash or check". Q4-SA-007, Q4-ZD-001, Q4-BC-020
- In their terms (official pages), Shopify tells the seller any contract of sale is "directly and only between you and the customer"; Salla's agreement says "ليس هناك أي علاقة لمنصة سلة بطريقة الدفع عند الاستلام", and lets Salla require that payments be made directly between merchant and consumer through their own bank accounts; Zid says it is "ليست طرفاً في العلاقة بين المستهلك والتاجر", which names no one in the role. Q4-SH-K1491, Q4-SA-P0080, Q4-SA-P0081, Q4-ZD-029

### [2]

- No one term covers all five. Shopify says manual payment methods, with cash on delivery, money orders and bank transfers as common types; WooCommerce says offline payment method; BigCommerce's offline payment methods are Bank deposit, Cash on delivery, Check, Money order and Pay in store; Salla and Zid name each method, as التحويل البنكي and الدفع عند الاستلام. Q4-SH-003, Q4-WC-002, Q4-BC-006, Q4-SA-P0099, Q4-SA-P0098, Q4-ZD-K0098
- The seller can name a method (role, basis 4, both stated): Shopify's custom manual payment method takes a Custom payment method name, with Cash on Delivery (COD), Bank Deposit, Money Order and other names reserved; each BigCommerce offline method takes a Display Name shown at checkout. Q4-SH-007, Q4-BC-007

### [3]

- **Recording the payment.** Four platforms have the seller record by hand a payment received outside them (role, basis 4): Shopify's Mark as paid, "if you have accepted payment outside of Shopify"; Zid's Bulk Payment Status Update in its plan comparison (shipped); WooCommerce's offline methods "require you to manually verify the payment was processed" before the seller changes the status; BigCommerce's merchant moves the order on "After receiving the payment". Q4-SH-012, Q4-ZD-K0208, Q4-WC-018, Q4-BC-P0115
- Shopify and BigCommerce keep that payment as a transaction on the order (role, basis 4, both stated): `orderMarkAsPaid` records "a payment transaction for the outstanding amount"; BigCommerce's Transactions API creates records "for payments that were processed externally". WooCommerce's order carries `date_paid` and `transaction_id` (shipped). Q4-SH-039, Q4-BC-K0100, Q4-WC-043
- **Proof of payment.** In Salla's dashboard text (recorded session, 12 October 2026) the merchant picks the bank receipt image on the order (اختر صورة الإيصال البنكي), and one storefront's checkout (store.rasasi.com.sa, 9 October 2026) asked the buyer to "Upload your bank transfer receipt". How a merchant reviews a receipt and confirms the payment, and whether Salla takes part, was not in what was read. Q4-SA-015, Q4-SA-074, Q4-SA-N02
- **Payment status.** Shopify and Zid keep payment apart from the order's progress (role, basis 4, both stated): an order made with a Shopify manual payment method gets the Payment pending status; Zid's order has a payment status (pending, paid, failed, refunded) beside an order status (new, preparing, indelivery, delivered and others). Q4-SH-016, Q4-ZD-016, Q4-ZD-015
- WooCommerce, BigCommerce and Salla carry payment in the one order status (role, basis 4). WooCommerce sets cash-on-delivery orders to Processing "until payment is collected", though Processing means "Payment has been received (paid)"; BigCommerce places offline-paid orders in Awaiting Payment (both stated). Salla's dashboard text (recorded session, 12 October 2026) shows قيد المراجعة on the confirmation page "عند الدفع عبر التحويل البنكي أو الدفع عند الاستلام", and its help offers to set cash-on-delivery orders to تم التنفيذ at once, "لتجنب إشكالية سوء فهم العميل لحالة" that status. Q4-WC-015, Q4-WC-019, Q4-BC-P0115, Q4-SA-018, Q4-SA-045
- **Time to pay** (role, basis 4, all stated). Salla's merchant can turn off the bank-transfer مهلة الدفع, so that the customer must transfer and attach the receipt on completing the order; WooCommerce's Hold Stock "automatically cancels eligible Pending payment orders created through checkout after the configured time limit"; Shopify lets the seller add payment terms, after which an unpaid order becomes Overdue. Q4-SA-047, Q4-WC-039, Q4-SH-014
- **Refunds.** WooCommerce and BigCommerce record a refund that the seller makes outside them (role, basis 4, both stated): a WooCommerce order can reach Refunded "without the customer’s funds having been returned"; recording an offline refund in BigCommerce is "an optional manual process". When a refund can't go through Salla, the merchant transfers the amount to the customer's bank account or adds it to the customer's wallet in the store. Q4-WC-026, Q4-BC-018, Q4-SA-009
- **Fees.** Shopify and BigCommerce differ (both stated): Shopify says "you aren't charged transaction fees on manual payment methods such as cash, cash on delivery (COD), and bank transfers"; BigCommerce charges its Open Payment Provider fee on orders "processed using offline payment methods" by stores on self-serve plans, while Performance plan stores under contracted terms are exempt. Its Pricing article (official page) bills the fee "monthly in arrears", "as a separate line item on your invoice". Q4-SH-K1740, Q4-BC-012, Q4-BC-P0125
- For a bank-transfer order, Salla takes a Salla label's cost from the merchant's balance, not from the order, and asks for a top-up when it is short; Zid takes a return label's cost from محفظة زد. Q4-SA-013, Q4-ZD-K0581
- **Reports.** Zid's analytics give "نسب استخدام طرق الدفع في متجرك", and Salla's dashboard text (recorded session, 12 October 2026) the distribution of payments by payment method: a match on role (basis 4) across stated and observed sources. With WooCommerce's default Date Paid, "orders without a paid date are excluded". Q4-ZD-022, Q4-SA-049, Q4-WC-032

### [4]

- **By place.** Shopify and Salla limit methods by the seller's country (role, basis 4, both stated): Shopify offers email money transfers only to merchants in Canada (not checked on Zid); Salla's help says its STC pay, الدفع عن التسليم and حوالة بنكية are "مخصصة حالياً للمتاجر في المملكة العربية السعودية فقط" (29 September 2026). Q4-SH-027, Q4-SA-054
- Salla's records disagree on cash on delivery in the UAE: that help limits it to Saudi stores, while its UAE article (12 September 2026) says "يمكنك تفعيل الدفع عند الاستلام". The proposal rests on neither for the UAE, only on availability being set per country. Q4-SA-054, Q4-SA-K1530
- BigCommerce leaves place to the merchant, who checks the Available Countries of each offline method; a Zid bank account's IBAN must start with SA or AE. Q4-BC-P0121, Q4-ZD-006
- **By plan.** Salla offers bank transfer and cash on delivery on its basic plan, where "طرق الدفع الإلكترونية فهي متاحة ضمن الباقات المدفوعة فقط" (stated); Zid's plan comparison (shipped) offers Bank Transfer Payment on all four plans. Q4-SA-P0099, Q4-SA-P0068, Q4-ZD-K0126
- **The seller's conditions.** Salla's قيود الدفع and Zid's قيود الشحن و الدفع, rules the seller sets to hide a method, match in part on term (basis 1, both stated). Salla's can hide cash on delivery above a cart value; Zid's, from its growth plan up, act on cart value, products, weight, customer group, the customer's location and the customer's cancelled orders, against "طلبات الدفع عند الاستلام الوهمية". In Salla's dashboard text (recorded session, 12 October 2026), cash on delivery can also be hidden from "العملاء المحظورين في متاجر سلة الأخرى". Q4-SA-055, Q4-ZD-026, Q4-ZD-K0357, Q4-SA-029
- **By carrier, courier or shipping method** (role, basis 4, all stated). On Salla "يمكن تفعيل أو تعطيل الدفع عند الاستلام بشكل مستقل لكل مندوب أو تسعيرة"; Zid's cash on delivery is turned on per carrier; WooCommerce's merchant chooses "which shipping methods and rates will offer COD". Q4-SA-P0199, Q4-ZD-K0111, Q4-WC-033
- **Who may record** (role, basis 4, both stated). Shopify's orderMarkAsPaid needs the mark_orders_as_paid permission, and BigCommerce's Manual payment option the Finalize Manual Orders using Manual Payments permission. Q4-SH-042, Q4-BC-022

### [5]

- **Salla.** With بوليصات سلة, cash on delivery is collected through the carriers ("يتم تحصيلها من خلال شركات الشحن") and "يتم تحويلها لاحقًا إلى حساب المتجر وفق دورة التحويل المحددة", on "يومي الأحد والأربعاء لجميع شركات الشحن ضمن بوليصات سلة" (stated). In its dashboard text (recorded session, 8 October 2026), "تحوِّل شركة الشحن المبلغ إلى سلة". Q4-SA-P0038, Q4-SA-P0093, Q4-SA-P0042
- Who collects and holds those amounts before payout was not in what was read. Salla's usage agreement (6 October 2026) says "ليس هناك أي علاقة لمنصة سلة بطريقة الدفع عند الاستلام", while its help (7 October 2026) describes the dues reaching the store's account: records that may disagree. The proposal rests on the help for the flow, and on neither for who holds the money. Q4-SA-G0117, Q4-SA-P0080, Q4-SA-P0038
- In the UAE, cash on delivery isn't tracked in Salla's wallet; the merchant follows the amounts through a carrier under an outside contract, or its own courier (not checked on Zid). Q4-SA-K1602
- **Zid.** Its terms define cash on delivery under زد شب as a service in which "تقوم زد بتحصيل المبلغ نيابة عن التاجر من العميل عند التسليم"; a separate fee is deducted from the amounts collected before they reach محفظة التاجر, and the merchant "لا يستحق أي مبالغ مباشرة من شركات الشحن أو العملاء". Q4-ZD-K0122, Q4-ZD-K0117, Q4-ZD-K0119, Q4-ZD-K0123
- Zid's records disagree on timing: its terms (August 2026) transfer the dues twice each calendar month; a help answer last updated 20 July 2023 says within two working weeks. The proposal rests on neither: the timing is a collection service's own setting. Q4-ZD-K0117, Q4-ZD-K0121
- Salla and Zid match on role (basis 4): cash on delivery taken through the platform's own shipping service reaches the seller on the platform's cycle, into the store's account on Salla and, after Zid's fee, into the merchant's wallet on Zid. Salla's evidence is its help (stated), Zid's its terms (official page). Q4-SA-P0038, Q4-ZD-K0117, Q4-ZD-K0119
- **WooCommerce.** A cash-on-delivery order can be paid by card at delivery through WooPayments' In-Person Payments, which marks the order Complete at payment, and WooPayments "pays out money from incoming orders into your linked bank account or debit card". Q4-WC-036, Q4-WC-046
- **Shopify and BigCommerce.** A service of either that collects cash on delivery for the seller was not in what was read. For sellers in Italy, Shopify's Help Center points to App Store apps related to COD. Q4-SH-N01, Q4-BC-N16, Q4-SH-046

## 2. Proposed: names, structure and behaviour

- **Seller-collected payment method**: Sharrwah would offer ways of paying in which the buyer pays the seller, or someone the seller engages such as its own courier, outside Sharrwah, with no money passing through Sharrwah or a payment service it provides. Sharrwah would show the method, keep the order, and record what the seller says it received or refunded. Q4-SH-K1737, Q4-WC-002, Q4-BC-P0114
  - Built-in kinds, each off until the seller turns it on: **bank transfer to the seller**; **cash on delivery**, collected by the seller's courier or carrier; **payment at pickup**, at the seller's branch or store; and a **seller-named method** with the seller's own name. They draw on BigCommerce's five offline methods, Pay in store among them, Salla's and Zid's bank transfer and cash on delivery, and Shopify's custom manual payment method. Q4-BC-006, Q4-SA-P0099, Q4-SA-P0098, Q4-ZD-K0098, Q4-SH-007
  - Each carries instructions shown to the buyer on the confirmation page and in the order notification; Shopify's show on the order confirmation page. Q4-SH-006
  - The same methods serve orders the seller creates, the payment being recorded as the order is made, as Salla's manual orders, Zid's تفعيل الدفع اليدوي and BigCommerce's manual orders allow. Q4-SA-007, Q4-ZD-001, Q4-BC-020
  - Why: rule 1 (built once, set up for each place): which kinds a place offers is set up in the **Availability rule**, as Salla limits methods by the seller's country; rule 2 (hide what is likely to change): each kind sits behind one interface (name, instructions, availability, how its payment is recorded), so a kind one place needs is added without changing orders. Q4-SA-054
  - Alternative not taken: cash on delivery only as a seller-named method, as Shopify's Help Center has sellers in Italy set it up. Cash on delivery is tied to couriers, carriers and shipping methods on Salla, Zid and WooCommerce, which a seller-named method can't carry (rule 2). Q4-SH-045, Q4-SA-P0199, Q4-WC-033
- **Receiving account**: the seller's own account into which buyers transfer, shown with **bank transfer to the seller**. A store may hold several, each shown or hidden, and pausing the method keeps them. Sharrwah would store and show it, and never pay into it. Q4-ZD-004, Q4-ZD-007
  - Why: rule 1. The fields that identify an account (on Salla: bank, holder, account number, IBAN) are set per place: Zid's IBAN must start with SA or AE, and Salla's نظام الحوالات السريع identifier is for Saudi stores only. Q4-SA-004, Q4-ZD-006, Q4-SA-005
  - It is kept apart from any account Sharrwah might pay a seller into, which belongs to an arrangement under the guard; Salla's help, too, names the bank account within payment methods as the one "لاستقبال المدفوعات من عملائك عبر التحويل البنكي". Q4-SA-P0100
  - Alternative not taken: account details only as free-text instructions, as in Shopify's payment instructions. A structured account can be checked against its place's format (rule 1). Q4-SH-006
- **Payment status**: each order would carry a payment status apart from its fulfilment status: awaiting payment, awaiting review, partially paid, paid, partially refunded, refunded or voided, and overdue once a due date passes. It follows from the order's **Payment record** and **Refund record** entries; only voiding is set directly. Q4-SH-016, Q4-ZD-016, Q4-ZD-015
  - It draws on Shopify's and Zid's separate payment status, Shopify's Overdue after payment terms, and Salla's قيد المراجعة for bank-transfer and cash-on-delivery orders. Q4-SH-014, Q4-SA-018
  - Why: rule 3 (effort follows risk), as the data model is expensive to change after launch. A cash-on-delivery order is delivered before it is paid; carrying both in one status puts WooCommerce's in Processing, which means paid, and led Salla to offer a setting so customers don't misread تم التنفيذ. Q4-WC-015, Q4-WC-019, Q4-SA-045
  - Alternative not taken: one order status that carries payment, as on WooCommerce, BigCommerce and Salla, for that reason. Q4-BC-P0115
- **Payment record**: an entry on the order for each payment the seller says it received outside Sharrwah: amount, currency, method, the date received, who entered it, and an optional reference and proof. Each entry states that no money passed through Sharrwah. Marking an order paid writes one entry for the amount outstanding. Q4-SH-039, Q4-BC-K0100, Q4-WC-043
  - Proof: the buyer, at checkout or later, or the seller can attach a receipt; the order then awaits review until the seller confirms, and Sharrwah doesn't judge the proof. It draws on Salla's receipt upload; how Salla's merchant reviews a receipt was not in what was read, so the confirming step is Sharrwah's own proposal. Q4-SA-015, Q4-SA-074, Q4-SA-N02
  - Only staff with a permission for it may enter payments, as on Shopify and BigCommerce. Q4-SH-042, Q4-BC-022
  - Why: rule 3. Entries rather than a paid flag let part payments, several receipts and corrections come without a change of model; Shopify records part payments on its Shopify Plus plan. Q4-SH-022
  - Alternative not taken: a status change alone, as on WooCommerce and BigCommerce, or Zid's Bulk Payment Status Update. Reports need the amount and date received: WooCommerce's analytics count by Date Paid, and Shopify asks the seller to correct the amount received "For reporting purposes". Q4-WC-032, Q4-SH-026, Q4-ZD-K0208
- **Payment window**: per method, an optional time for the buyer to pay after ordering; when it ends, the order shows overdue or is cancelled with its stock released, as the seller chooses. Rule 3 puts only the due date in the data model now and leaves the rest to the build. Q4-SA-047, Q4-WC-039, Q4-SH-014
- **Refund record**: a refund of a seller-collected payment is made by the seller outside Sharrwah (to the buyer's bank account, in cash, or as credit in the store where it offers one) and entered on the order: amount, route, date and who entered it. Sharrwah's screens would say the refund was recorded, not that it was sent. Q4-BC-018, Q4-SA-009
  - Why: rule 3 (money flows): no Sharrwah balance exists for such an order to refund from, and a WooCommerce order can reach Refunded "without the customer’s funds having been returned", which this wording avoids. Q4-WC-026
- **Availability rule**: whether a seller-collected method shows to a buyer is decided at three levels: Sharrwah, per place, sets which kinds stores there may offer; the seller, per store, turns each on and sets conditions (order value, weight, products, customer group, the buyer's area, the buyer's cancelled orders, shipping method or courier); and a courier or carrier decides whether it collects cash at all. Q4-SA-054, Q4-ZD-026, Q4-SA-066
  - It draws on Salla's and Zid's seller-set restrictions, and on cash on delivery set per courier, carrier or shipping method on Salla, Zid and WooCommerce. Q4-SA-055, Q4-SA-P0199, Q4-ZD-K0111, Q4-WC-033
  - Why: rule 1 for the place level, which is set up, not built apart; rule 2 for the conditions, which sit behind one rule so that a new condition changes no method. Q4-SA-054
  - Alternative not taken: BigCommerce's merchant-set Available Countries as the only control of place. Salla's limit by the seller's country is one a seller can't set (rule 1); the buyer's area stays among the seller's conditions. Q4-BC-P0121, Q4-SA-054
  - Not proposed: hiding cash on delivery from buyers banned in other stores, as Salla can, which shares a buyer's record across sellers; it is a company decision (section 7). Q4-SA-029
- **Seller-collected order charge**: if Sharrwah charges for orders paid outside it, the charge would be set per plan and billed to the seller apart from any order, never taken from an order's money, which Sharrwah doesn't hold. Whether to charge at all is a company decision. Q4-BC-012, Q4-BC-P0125, Q4-SH-K1740
  - Why: rule 3. A billing route not tied to proceeds belongs in the data model now, as BigCommerce bills its fee monthly as a separate line; the amount can wait. Q4-BC-P0125
  - Alternative not taken: taking such a charge, such as a label's cost, from a balance the seller tops up, as Salla and Zid do. A prepaid balance would hold sellers' money through Sharrwah, so it is left to the founder's decision under the guard. Q4-SA-013, Q4-ZD-K0581
- **Payment reports**: reports would count seller-collected orders by method and by the amount and date recorded. Rule 3 leaves them to the build; they need only the **Payment record**. Q4-SA-049, Q4-ZD-022, Q4-WC-032
- **Payment events**: Sharrwah's API would let another system, such as a bank feed, an invoicing tool or a courier, enter a payment or refund on an order, and would tell apps when a payment status changes. Why: rule 2, as banks and couriers differ by place and change, so they plug in behind the API. Q4-SH-039, Q4-BC-K0100, Q4-ZD-K0206
  - It draws on Shopify's `orderMarkAsPaid`, a Flow template on store capture-plus (7 October 2026) that marks an order paid "When your bank, invoicing or payment system confirms a payment through your webhook", BigCommerce's Transactions API and Zid's payment-status webhook. Q4-SH-039, Q4-SH-057
- **Collection-on-delivery service**: a service in which a courier or carrier working with Sharrwah collects cash at delivery, and the seller receives it after the service's deductions, on the service's schedule. The design would give it a part of its own. Where it holds sellers' money through Sharrwah, or through a payment company in an account opened through Sharrwah, it goes live anywhere only by the founder's decision. Q4-SA-P0038, Q4-ZD-K0122
  - It draws on Salla's label carriers, whose cash reaches the store's account on a set cycle, Zid's service collecting "نيابة عن التاجر" into محفظة التاجر after a separate fee, and WooPayments' card payment at delivery. Q4-SA-P0093, Q4-ZD-K0122, Q4-ZD-K0119, Q4-WC-036
  - Its payments are not seller-collected: the service, not the seller, would write the order's entry, as Zid's merchant "لا يستحق أي مبالغ مباشرة من شركات الشحن أو العملاء". Q4-ZD-K0123
  - Why: rule 4 (the last responsible moment), because who holds the cash is unsettled (section 5); rule 2, its own part. A service of this kind of Shopify's or BigCommerce's own was not in what was read. Q4-SH-N01, Q4-BC-N16
  - **Cash on delivery** collected by the seller's own courier or carrier, settled between them as on Salla, needs none of this and doesn't wait for the decision. Q4-SA-P0092

## 3. What can plug in, what is set per what, and who sets it

- **Plugs in:** a kind of **Seller-collected payment method** that one place needs, added by Sharrwah behind the method's interface, as Shopify offers email money transfers only to merchants in Canada (not checked on Zid). Q4-SH-027
- **Plugs in:** apps that add to a method, as Shopify's Help Center points to App Store apps related to COD. Q4-SH-046
- **Plugs in:** other systems that enter payments through **Payment events**. Q4-SH-057, Q4-BC-K0100
- **Plugs in:** couriers and carriers that collect cash for the seller, each turned on for cash on delivery on its own. Q4-SA-P0199, Q4-ZD-K0111
- **Plugs in:** a **Collection-on-delivery service** provider, only by the founder's decision. Q4-SA-P0042, Q4-ZD-K0122
- **Set per place:** the kinds offered, and a **Receiving account**'s fields. Q4-SA-054, Q4-ZD-006
- **Set per plan:** whether a store gets the methods, which conditions it may set, and any **Seller-collected order charge**: Salla offers bank transfer on its basic plan, Zid's restrictions start at its growth plan, and BigCommerce's fee applies on self-serve plans. Q4-SA-P0099, Q4-ZD-K0357, Q4-BC-012
- **Set per store and method:** on or off, name, instructions, accounts and conditions. Q4-BC-007, Q4-ZD-007
- **Set per courier, carrier or shipping method:** whether cash on delivery is offered. Q4-ZD-K0111, Q4-WC-033
- **Set per order:** payment and refund entries, proof and a due date. Q4-SA-015, Q4-SH-014
- **Set per staff member:** the permission to enter payments. Q4-SH-042
- **Set by Sharrwah:** the kinds per place, plan entitlements, any charge, and, only by the founder's decision, a collection service. Q4-SA-054, Q4-BC-012
- **Set by the seller:** methods, instructions, accounts, conditions, and payment and refund entries. Q4-ZD-007, Q4-WC-018
- **Set by the courier or carrier:** whether it collects cash, and how it settles with the seller; on Salla "تختلف طرق تسوية مبالغ الدفع عند الإستلام بحسب سياسة شركة الشحن", and cash on delivery must be turned on in the carrier's settings. Q4-SA-P0202, Q4-SA-066
- **Set by the buyer:** the method chosen, and any proof attached. Q4-SA-074
- **Set by a collection service's provider:** its schedule and fees. Q4-ZD-K0117, Q4-ZD-K0119

## 4. Parts, in the order they can be built

1. **Seller-collected payment method** with its kinds, and the place level of the **Availability rule**. Needs the design's places.
2. **Receiving account** and each method's set-up in a store. Needs part 1.
3. **Payment status** and **Payment record**, with proof and the permission to enter payments. Needs the design's orders and staff roles.
4. Checkout and confirmation for the **Seller-collected payment method**: its instructions and **Receiving account** shown to the buyer, and the buyer's proof for the **Payment record**. Needs parts 1 to 3.
5. **Refund record**. Needs part 3.
6. **Availability rule** at the store and courier levels. Needs parts 1 and 2, and the design's couriers and shipping methods.
7. **Payment window**. Needs part 3.

## 5. What the design must keep open

- Who holds the cash a **Collection-on-delivery service** collects, between delivery and the seller: Sharrwah, a payment company, or the carrier paying the seller directly. Salla's carriers transfer to Salla and Zid collects on the merchant's behalf, but in what capacity Salla holds the amounts was not in what was read. The design keeps the service in a part of its own, with its own kind of entry, and gives no other part a balance of sellers' money. Settled by the founder's decision under the guard, with the company decisions on carriers and licences (section 7). Q4-SA-P0042, Q4-ZD-K0122, Q4-SA-G0117
- Whether orders paid outside Sharrwah bear a charge, on which plans, and how it is billed. The design keeps a charge line billed apart from orders. Settled by the company decision on charges. Q4-SH-K1740, Q4-BC-012
- The fields of a **Receiving account** in each place. The design keeps them per place. Settled by each place's banking facts (Yemen's in section 6). Q4-ZD-006, Q4-SA-005
- The currency of an amount received. Shopify converts it at the live rate and asks the seller to correct it for reporting; on BigCommerce offline methods take additional currencies; at one of Yemen's areas a domestic sale is paid in rials only (section 6). The design keeps amount and currency on each entry, unconverted. Settled by the design's currency model. Q4-SH-026, Q4-BC-029, YA-21 (rial only @ the internationally recognized government's area, as of 2026-04-24)
- Whether a rial amount must name its zone. Transfer networks charged up to 180 percent to move money from Aden to Sana'a, to cover the gap between the two prints of the rial, and a payment service's API tells old-rial from new-rial customers. The design keeps each entry's currency able to tell the two apart. Settled by the design's currency model, and by whether Yemen is one place or several for payments. YA-56 (Up to 180% @ Sana'a and Aden, as of 2026-07-10), YK-452 (by currency zone, old, new or all @ E-Pay (Kuraimi Islamic Microfinance Bank)'s service, as of 2026-10-10)
- Whether **bank transfer to the seller** and its **Receiving account** are offered per buyer area within one place, since a transfer between Yemen's areas can cost the buyer much of the amount. The design keeps the **Availability rule**'s buyer-area condition able to show a different account by the buyer's area. Settled by what a place is for payments in Yemen. YA-56 (Up to 180% @ Sana'a and Aden, as of 2026-07-10)
- Whether a buyer's record in one store may change what another store offers that buyer. The design keeps buyer conditions within one store. Settled by the company decision in section 7. Q4-SA-029
- Sharrwah's position in a sale paid outside it. The design nowhere assumes Sharrwah is a party to such a sale. Settled by the founder with a lawyer (section 7). Q4-SH-K1491, Q4-ZD-029

## 6. Yemen: what its evidence allows, forbids or doesn't show

- **Cash on delivery** is allowed and in use: the store offers it inside Sana'a, a condition by the buyer's area that the **Availability rule** lets a seller set. YA-128 (available inside Sana'a @ Dakhm store's service, as of 2026-10-10)
- **Bank transfer to the seller** is allowed: the same store takes payment by money transfer or into its Kuraimi accounts. Paying by money transfer through a transfer network would be added for Yemen as a kind behind the **Seller-collected payment method**'s interface (section 3), or by a seller as a **seller-named method**. YA-131 (accepted, by money transfer or to the store's Kuraimi accounts @ Dakhm store's service, as of 2026-10-10)
- **Receiving account**: an IBAN field is allowed where IBAN is in use; and since wallets take merchants' payments by QR code or point-of-sale number, a receiving account for a wallet would hold such a number, not a bank's. Both fit fields set per place (section 2). YK-36 (IBAN in use @ the internationally recognized government's area, as of 2026-10-10), YA-108 (by QR code or point-of-sale number @ ONE Cash's service, as of 2026-10-10)
- Yemen's evidence doesn't show whether IBAN is in use at the Houthis' area, on which a **Receiving account**'s fields there depend; a Sana'a-based bank's account format, or the Sana'a central bank's rule on it, would show it.
- The **Payment record**'s proof is allowed: a buyer proves a payment by sending the seller a photo of the payment notice, which the proof and the seller's confirming step provide for. YK-173 (a photo of the payment notice on WhatsApp @ Dakhm store's service, as of 2026-10-10)
- **Payment events** are allowed: an app on the merchant's phone matches the buyer's own transfer, so another system can confirm a payment the buyer made straight to the seller and enter it on the order. YA-112 (the buyer's own transfer, matched by an app on the merchant's phone @ YemenPay's service, as of 2026-08-22)
- A courier that collects money from recipients for its business accounts allows **cash on delivery** collected by a courier the seller engages. YK-203 (available to business accounts @ Torod's service, as of 2026-10-10)
- Yemen's evidence doesn't show how or when such a courier pays the merchant, or whether collecting cash for sellers, by Sharrwah or a partner, needs a licence; a **Collection-on-delivery service**'s set-up for Yemen depends on both. A courier's terms for business accounts, and each central bank's payment-services rules read for collecting cash, would show them.
- A domestic sale is paid in rials only at the internationally recognized government's area, which forbids there, as of that date, a **Seller-collected payment method** taking another currency for such a sale. YA-21 (rial only @ the internationally recognized government's area, as of 2026-04-24)
- Rials differ by zone: moving money from Aden to Sana'a cost up to 180 percent through transfer networks, and a payment service's API sorts customers by old or new rials. This adds two items to section 5: a rial amount's zone, and accounts per buyer area. YA-56 (Up to 180% @ Sana'a and Aden, as of 2026-07-10), YK-452 (by currency zone, old, new or all @ E-Pay (Kuraimi Islamic Microfinance Bank)'s service, as of 2026-10-10)

## 7. Company decisions it raises

- Sharrwah's position in sales paid outside it: will its agreement say the sale is between seller and buyer only, and that Sharrwah has no part in cash on delivery or bank transfer, as Shopify's, Salla's and Zid's terms say of themselves? Q4-SH-K1491, Q4-SA-P0080, Q4-ZD-029

- Will Sharrwah charge for orders paid outside it, on which plans, and billed how? Q4-SH-K1740, Q4-BC-012
- May a buyer's cash-on-delivery history in one store change what other stores offer that buyer? Q4-SA-029
- Which couriers and carriers will Sharrwah work with for cash on delivery, and on what terms? Q4-SA-P0093, Q4-ZD-K0111
