# Managing business entities

A business entity is the registered business, or the individual, that your store operates as. Your business entity is used to verify your business, and it determines the information that you need to provide for financial services such as Shopify Payments.

As your business grows, your business structure might change. When your business changes, you need to update the business details required for your type of business. For example, if you expand your business into [international markets](/manual/international), then you need to [provide information for Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/account-setup) for the markets that you're expanding into.

You can manage your store's business information from the **Business details** section on the **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) page of your Shopify admin. If you have access to organizations, then you can manage and add new business entities from the [**Organization**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-details) page of your Shopify admin.

## Considerations

Review the following considerations for managing business entities from your Shopify admin:

* You can sell products from multiple business entities on a single store. Learn more about [managing international markets](/manual/markets/getting-started/overview).
* The information required for your entity depends on the country where your store is operating from. Refer to the list of requirements for each [supported country](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries) for Shopify Payments.
* Each time that a user reveals sensitive information related to your business entity, the action is logged in the **User management activity log** on the **Users** > [**Security**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-account/security) page of your Shopify admin settings. Learn more about the [**User management activity log**](/manual/your-account/users/security/user-management-activity-log).
* When selling with business entities, you need to [add a new market](/manual/markets/getting-started/set-up-markets#create-market) for each entity.
* Business entity information is sent to assessors to verify your business.
* Adding new business entities or making changes might require additional verification.
* Government identifiers, such as tax IDs, business registration numbers, and Social Security Numbers (SSNs), must be unique across your organization. Archived or historical business entities can still prevent the same government identifier from being added to another entity.
* If your store uses financial services such as [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments), [Shopify Balance](/manual/finance/shopify-balance), [Shopify Capital](/manual/finance/shopify-capital), or [Shopify Credit](/manual/finance/shopify-credit), then you need to deactivate all financial services before you can change your business entity.
* The country of your billing address must match the country of one of your active business entities. You select the entity when you change your billing address, and the company name on your bills is filled in from that entity's name, which you can edit. Learn more about [managing your Shopify billing address](/manual/your-account/manage-billing/paying-your-bills/managing-payments/billing-address).

## User permission requirements {#permissions}

The following users can view and manage business entities:

* The [organization owner](/manual/your-account/users/roles/roles-managed-by-shopify#owner-roles)
* Users with the [**Organization administrator**](/manual/your-account/users/roles/roles-managed-by-shopify#organization-administrator) organization role
* Users whose roles include permissions to view and manage business entities

If your store uses financial services such as [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments), [Shopify Balance](/manual/finance/shopify-balance), [Shopify Capital](/manual/finance/shopify-capital), or [Shopify Credit](/manual/finance/shopify-credit), then you need to deactivate all financial services before you can change your store's country in your Shopify admin. Only the [store owner](/manual/your-account/users/roles/roles-managed-by-shopify#owner-roles) can deactivate Shopify Payments.

## Adding a business entity {#add-entity}

You can add a new business entity from **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) > **Business details** in your Shopify admin, or from **Settings** > [**Organization**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-details) if you're part of an organization. You need to include the country that your business is operating from, and how the business is registered.

The information that you need to add to your business entity depends on the following factors:

* The country that your business is operating from
* Whether you're selling as an individual or have a registered business
* If your business is registered, then the type of business that you're operating

After you create a business entity, you can edit the business entity to add additional details, such as persons and required documentation for your business type.

## Managing business entities {#manage-business-entities}

Eligible users can view and manage your organization's business entities from the **Settings** > [**Organization**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-details) page of your Shopify admin. You can view and manage a store's business entities from **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) in the **Business details** section.

Your business entity must include the following information:

* The country your business is operating from
* The type of business that you're operating
* The details of your business, such as registered business name and address
* Any individuals who ultimately own or control the business
* Supporting documentation, including government identification, for the business and individuals associated with the business

The information that you add to your business entity in your Shopify admin must match your legal documentation and identification exactly. For example, if your supporting documentation lists your business name as "Example123 Limited", then enter <kbd>Example123 Limited</kbd> in the form.

<aside class="note note-information"><h4>Tip</h4><p>You can <a href="/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/searching-filtering-views#searching-and-filtering-list-items-in-your-views">search</a> through your list of business entities, as well as <a href="/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/searching-filtering-views#sort-view">sort</a> them by name or country. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/searching-filtering-views">searching and filtering your lists in the Shopify admin</a>.</p>
</aside>

### Edit business country {#edit-business-country}

The address of your store is used to determine your store's location. Changing the store country creates a new business entity and might affect the following details:

* Your subscription fees and billing currency
* The local language and currency for your store
* Your tax and duty obligations
* Shipping availability
* Your access to Shopify products and services

The [Shopify Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms) require you to maintain an accurate location based on your current jurisdiction. Your store address updates to match your new business entity address and country.

### Archive a business entity {#archive-entity}

You can archive a business entity from your **Organization** settings. Archived entities are read-only, and can't be used for stores, [markets](/manual/international), payments, or other Shopify services such as [Shopify Balance](/manual/finance/shopify-balance) or [Shopify Capital](/manual/finance/shopify-capital). You can't archive a business entity that's currently in use by stores, or when there's a pending payout.

You can unarchive a business entity from the **Archived** view in your business entities list at any time.

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > [**Organization**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-details).

2. In the **Business entities** section, click the name of the business that you want to archive.
3. Click **Archive**.
4. In the **Archive business entity** dialog, click **Archive**.

To unarchive an archived business entity, click the name of the business that you want to unarchive, and then click **Actions** > **Unarchive**.

You can set a default business entity for a store from the **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) page of your Shopify admin.

## Manage people

If your business isn't an individual business, then add any individuals who ultimately own or control the business, including the following types of people:

* **Account representative**: Assign one account representative who is a verified person with authority to make financial decisions for your business. This person might be a business owner, executive, or director.
* **Equity owners**: Add all owners with 25% or more equity.
* Directors of the business.

You can view and manage people associated with your business in the **People** section within a business entity.

If your business type is **Individual business** or **Sole proprietorship**, then the business owner is assigned as the account representative by default. The **People** section isn't displayed in your Shopify admin for these business types. You can manage your personal information from the **Business details** section of the business entity details page instead.

### Relationship to business details

When you add a person to a business entity, you need to add their personal information, such as name and date of birth, contact information, and residential address. You also need to define the person's relationship to the business. You can select one or more of the following roles for each person:

* **Account representative**
  * You can only add one account representative to an entity.
* **Executive**
* **Director**
* **Equity owner**
  * If you select **Equity owner**, then enter the **Equity percentage**. The total equity percentage for all people in the entity can't exceed a total of 100 percent.

## Managing government identifications

Government identification might be required for both your business and for people in your business.

The identification that's needed and the accepted types of identification depend on the country where your store is based and your business type. Learn more about [general requirements](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/account-setup) and [country-specific information requirements for Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries).

After you select a country and government identifier type, enter the identification number in the field that matches the requirements for your selection.

### Troubleshoot a government identification that's already in use {#government-id-already-in-use}

A government identification, such as a tax ID, business registration number, or Social Security Number (SSN), can be saved only once in your organization. If you receive an error that the government identification is already in use, then the same identification might already be saved on another business entity or person in your organization, including an archived or historical business entity.

To resolve the error, review the business entity or person that already uses the government identification. If the error message includes a link to the conflicting business entity, then click the link to open the entity details.

Depending on your business details, you can do one of the following actions:

* If the existing business entity is the correct entity for your store, then [switch your store's default business entity](/manual/your-account/business-entities#set-business-entity) to that entity.
* If the government identification is saved on the wrong business entity or person, then [edit](/manual/your-account/business-entities#edit-government-identification) or [delete](/manual/your-account/business-entities#delete-government-identification) the existing government identification, and then add it to the correct entity.
* If the business entity is no longer used, then remove any government identification that's no longer required before you [archive the business entity](/manual/your-account/business-entities#archive-entity).

If you can't access the business entity that uses the government identification, then [contact Shopify Support](/manual/your-account/contact-shopify-support).
