**13 October 2026.** These are the pilot's gaps that survived gap-closing (A2) and the absence audits (A3), where nothing held or reachable from here can close them. The subject is who holds the merchant's funds between the buyer's payment and the merchant's payout.

**How to save.** Use SingleFile from a normal browser, as was done for Yemen. Put the files in `evidence/product-class/blocked-evidence-founder/`. SingleFile writes the address and save time into each file, so the names can stay as it makes them. Captured accounts are synthetic, so nothing in them needs masking.

## 2. Salla: the payment services contract, from a store's dashboard

**Why you.** It needs a store going through verification. The held help articles name it as what payments are settled under, but no held or public page gives its text. It is the only source pointed to for:
- the party that settles Salla Payments funds (S01-SA-G03);
- the entity, licence and account they are held under (G01);
- any reserve or security interest (G04 to G06).

**What to save.** In the merchant dashboard, open store verification (توثيق المتجر, https://s.salla.sa/payments/kyc). Save:
- the page that presents the payment services contract (عقد خدمات الدفع);
- the contract itself, if it opens separately.

If the dashboard shows it per country (Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the UAE), save each one you can reach.

## 3. Zid: Zid Pay's provider and the wallet's bank, from a store's dashboard

**Why you.** No public or held page names them. The Zid Pay terms refer to "the service provider" without naming it.

In the store dashboard (https://web.zid.sa/), save:
- the Zid Pay activation page (المدفوعات > تفعيل الدفع الالكتروني), including the provider named in the Nafath request, which closes gaps S01-ZD-G01 and G13;
- the wallet details page (المالية > المحفظة > تفاصيل المحفظة): the bank and account holder behind the wallet's IBAN, which closes G02 and G14.

Optional, if reachable, the activation forms under المدفوعات > بوابات الدفع الأُخرى (G06, G11) and المدفوعات > الدفع لاحقاً (G07, G17).

## If a page can't be reached

Say which one, and the gap stays recorded as it is. Questions to the platforms' merchant support are a fallback for what these pages don't answer, such as reserves, security interests, and the complete list of countries. A written reply from the platform can be saved as evidence. A summary of a call can't.
