# Q7: Currencies, a draft proposal

## 1. How the platforms handle it

### [1]

**Prices held.**

- All five hold a seller's own prices in one currency per store. Shopify's store currency is the one "for pricing products and running reports"; Zid's العملة الأساسية is "العملة التي تم إدخال أسعار المنتجات باستخدامها"; WooCommerce "uses it to display product prices and record new orders" of its base currency, and allows "one base currency per store"; BigCommerce's default currency is "assigned during store creation, is always a transactional currency"; Salla's seller sets العملة الحالية للمتجر. Shopify, Zid, WooCommerce and BigCommerce match on role (basis 4), each in its own words. Salla's words read don't say its prices are entered in that currency, so its match is partial. All are help or docs pages (stated). Q7-SH-K1935 Q7-ZD-002 Q7-WC-K0747 Q7-WC-K0745 Q7-BC-013 Q7-SA-001
- Reports run in that currency: Shopify's only in the store currency; Salla's convert every amount to العملة الأساسية للمتجر "باستخدام سعر الصرف وقت إنشاء الطلب"; BigCommerce's analytics show in the default currency as approximate values. They match on role (basis 4). Q7-SH-074 Q7-SA-009 Q7-BC-027
- Below the store, prices can be held in further currencies. Shopify's API gives each price list its own currency; Zid sets a price for each market and channel "وبعملة السوق نفسها"; BigCommerce's fixed prices need "a price record for each currency"; Salla's merchant enters prices for each market or lets them follow the base price and the rate. WooCommerce's reading didn't show groups of countries below the store with their own currency and prices. Q7-SH-130 Q7-ZD-019 Q7-BC-044 Q7-SA-005 Q7-WC-N16
- Zid passes an amount to payment providers "in the smallest currency unit", with a "Currency code in ISO 4217 format." Shopify carries an amount as a MoneyBag of `shopMoney`, in the shop's base currency, and `presentmentMoney`, in the customer's selected currency. WooCommerce records an order's currency "in ISO format". Not checked on Salla, for Zid's amount unit (Q7-ZD-024). Q7-ZD-024 Q7-ZD-025 Q7-SH-123 Q7-WC-055

**Shown to buyers.**

- Shopify, Zid and Salla set the shown currency for each market, and match on role (basis 4): Shopify's currency customization "determines what currency is displayed to customers in that market"; Zid's عملة السوق is the currency "لعرض أسعار المنتجات داخل هذا السوق"; Salla's merchant is told "اختر العملة الافتراضية والعملات الأخرى المدعومة للسوق". BigCommerce sets a default currency for each storefront. WooCommerce keeps one list for the store, its "Enabled currencies". Q7-SH-076 Q7-ZD-K0236 Q7-SA-K1977 Q7-BC-039 Q7-WC-013
- The buyer's place picks the currency first, and the buyer may then pick another. Salla's default shown currency "تعتمد على موقع العميل الجغرافي"; WooCommerce can "attempt to geolocate your customers"; BigCommerce shows a currency when "a visitor's IP address matches a supported country"; Zid's plans list "Geo-Based Currency Settings". Salla's buyer picks from a list beside the cart, and BigCommerce's can switch to any currency "enabled on the store level". Q7-SA-K1957 Q7-WC-015 Q7-BC-028 Q7-ZD-K0299 Q7-SA-K1956 Q7-BC-040
- Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce use the same term, "presentment currency" (basis 1), partly for different things, so the match is partial. Shopify's is the customer's currency of the amount paid, "also called the presentment currency"; WooCommerce's customer currency is shown and is "the currency they are charged in"; BigCommerce's Active Currency is one "that the shopper might or might not use to complete a transaction". Q7-SH-105 Q7-WC-002 Q7-WC-003 Q7-BC-002

**Paid by buyers.**

- On every platform the currency shown and the currency charged can differ, and what the payment provider supports decides the charge. They match on role (basis 4): Q7-SH-K1938 Q7-BC-014 Q7-WC-029 Q7-ZD-P0149 Q7-SA-K1963
  - Shopify: only stores with Shopify Payments or Adyen "as the primary gateway can process payments in a customer's local currency"; otherwise "the price will be converted to your store's default currency, and your customer will be charged in that currency". Q7-SH-K1937 Q7-SH-K1938
  - BigCommerce: for a currency to be transactional, "a payment gateway that supports the currency must be enabled". "Display currencies transact in your store's default currency", and checkout shows "a note indicating the actual currency and amount to be charged". Q7-BC-005 Q7-BC-014 Q7-BC-004
  - WooCommerce: to let buyers pay in another currency "you will need to add a multi-currency extension/plugin"; WooPayments' customer currency is the one charged, while the Currency Converter Widget "changes the display of prices only". Q7-WC-K0751 Q7-WC-002 Q7-WC-029
  - Zid: with مدفوعات زد the buyer sees the chosen currency, but "في مرحلة الدفع النهائي سيتم الدفع بالعملة الأساسية للمتجر", except كي نت, paid in Kuwaiti dinar (help page of 3 August 2026). Zid's Zid Pay help (6 August 2026) says خاصية العملات allows "استقبال المدفوعات من عملات مختلفة في زد باي", and that "المبلغ المدفوع خاضع لتحويل سعر الصرف لبوابة الدفع المفعلة". The two pages aren't reconciled in what was read. The proposal rests on neither for the currency Zid charges. Q7-ZD-P0149 Q7-ZD-004 Q7-ZD-K0301
  - Salla: "عند تفعيل التاجر لبوابة دفع سلة، يتم استقبال جميع المدفوعات بالريال السعودي" (stated). In a run on store real-merchant (12 October 2026, observed), an order's total was in SAR, with an exchange rate of 1 from SAR to SAR. Q7-SA-K1963 Q7-SA-020
- Within one provider, support differs by payment method: Shopify marks credit card checkout "Fully supported" and other methods "Unsupported and prices convert to shop currency"; WooCommerce shows a local method only when "the customer billing country and the customer currency meet the requirements"; BigCommerce's local methods depend on the store's "country and currency combination". Q7-SH-089 Q7-SH-099 Q7-WC-036 Q7-BC-018
- Each order records its currency, and four record the store's currency or a rate beside it. They match on role (basis 4): Shopify's `currencyCode` and `presentmentCurrencyCode`; Zid's "the currency in which the order was placed and paid", with the store currency and its exchange rate; BigCommerce's `currency_code`, `default_currency_code` and `currency_exchange_rate`; Salla's `exchange_rate` with `base_currency`, `exchange_currency` and `rate`; WooCommerce's single "Currency the order was created with, in ISO format." **Paid out to the seller.**

- Shopify and WooCommerce use the same term, "payout currency" (basis 1), with the same definition (basis 3): Shopify's is "the currency that Shopify Payments uses to deposit money into your bank account", and WooCommerce's "the currency in which funds are paid out into your bank account". WooCommerce also calls it the "settlement currency", BigCommerce's term (basis 1), which BigCommerce defines as "The currency the merchant gets paid out to their bank account" (basis 3). Q7-SH-004 Q7-WC-004 Q7-WC-005 Q7-BC-P0014
- It can differ from the currency paid, and the provider converts. Shopify applies a rate and fee "at the time of payment capture", the fee charged "in your payout currency"; WooCommerce converts a payment that matches none of the seller's bank accounts "into your default currency"; on BigCommerce "the merchant has to pay a conversion fee, and the conversion rate used is outside of BigCommerce's scope". Q7-SH-013 Q7-SH-K1950 Q7-WC-K0737 Q7-BC-P0015
- Some pay out in several currencies. With Shopify's Multi-Currency Payouts, "orders paid for in that currency are paid out to that bank account"; on plans without it, "all payouts are converted to your domestic currency". WooCommerce may pay "funds in different currencies paid out into separate bank accounts"; BigCommerce allows "a different Stripe merchant account per each currency configured in the control panel". Salla's reading didn't show payouts in more than one currency. Q7-SH-P0155 Q7-SH-057 Q7-WC-K0742 Q7-BC-P0104 Q7-SA-N02
- Salla ties the payout to the country's banks and currency: "الحساب لدى بنك إماراتي و بعملة الدرهم الإماراتي" in the UAE, "الحساب لدى بنك كويتي وبعملة الدينار الكويتي" in Kuwait. In the run on store real-merchant (12 October 2026, observed), the latest payout was 5171.76 in SAR. Q7-SA-K1964 Q7-SA-K1965 Q7-SA-023
- Zid states its deposit minimum as "١٠٠ ريال" in Saudi Arabia and "١ دينار كويتي" in Kuwait, but the pages read don't name the currency of the deposit itself. Q7-ZD-K0025 Q7-ZD-K0029 Q7-ZD-N02

### [2]

- The store currency is set for the store, by the seller, with a default from the store's country. Zid asks the seller to choose it "بناء على عملة دولتك", and its API gives each currency a "Country associated with the selected currency."; WooCommerce's onboarding sets `woocommerce_currency` from the country's `currency_code` (shipped code); BigCommerce's default currency "is assigned based on your location". Zid, WooCommerce and BigCommerce match on role (basis 4). Shopify's seller selects from options whose full list wasn't in what was read, and Salla's reading didn't show whether the store currency is tied to the store's country. Q7-ZD-001 Q7-ZD-007 Q7-WC-054 Q7-BC-029 Q7-SH-N03 Q7-SA-N05

Not checked on Salla, for Shopify's market defaults (Q7-SH-077 Q7-SH-079). Q7-SH-079 Q7-SH-077 Q7-ZD-020 Q7-BC-030

Q7-ZD-K0235 Q7-SA-033 Q7-SH-K2790
