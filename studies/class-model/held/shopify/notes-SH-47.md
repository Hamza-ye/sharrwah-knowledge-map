## Rules added for the rendered form (R47.n), for the coordinator to check and SH-48 and SH-49 to keep

- **R47.1, section headings.** An `h2` entry (Agentic, Sidekick, Online, Merchandising, Retail) is the page's section, a heading of the platform's public site. Where its title names a capability (Agentic: CM-AIA-005, whose cell holds the admin's Agentic section; Sidekick: CM-AIA-002) or one part of the platform (Online: the online store, CM-SFR-001; Retail: POS, CM-POS-001), it is a container on that capability, `core` (M-13; the ruling after SA-2 and SA-5; the ruling after SA-28's 'a title that names a capability or one part of the platform'). A title naming only a grouping over several capabilities is `out`, each card placed on its own (the ruling after SA-28). The opening's list of sections (Agentic 01 to Developer 11) is the page's table of contents: it takes no line, each section being placed at its own heading.
- **R47.2, a rendered card.** A card is an `article` (`data-component-name="product"`, its `id` a handle). Its `h3` is the title and its `p` the description, as a page-data card's (the batch message). Some cards have no paragraph: their `h3` holds the whole sentence with its links, read whole as title and description. An inline link carries a hidden tooltip (`role="tooltip" hidden`: 'Learn more', 'Read help doc', 'Get app', 'Try it', 'Read dev docs', 'Read article'), which the rendering joins to the link's text: it is the link's CTA, read by its address (the digest's rule 53), and a quote stops at the link (the digest's rule 5). A `div` after the CTA is the media's alt text, read with the card, with no line of its own (the digest's rule 1). 'Notes' with `[n]` marks are the card's footnotes, part of the entry (the brief's rule 1); a note that links another card (`#<handle>`) names that card's thing, placed at its own entry. A `figcaption` stat is a claim (the digest's rules 21 and 55). 'Copy prompt' and 'Back to navigation' are UI strings.
- **R47.3, an outside AI assistant linked to the store.** A card naming an outside AI assistant that works on the store through the platform's connector or plugin, as its help link shows (help.shopify.com's `ai-powered-tools/connecting-ai-tools/`: the Shopify connector for Grok, for Gemini, for Muse, for Claude, the plugins for ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot), is CM-AIA-004 `core`, the assistant its filler. A third party's own product built on the connector (Grok Bot's AI teammates) adds `extension-point`. A card that names the assistant at work on the store with no connector named and no held link (Product sourcing in agents) is `shows`.
- **R47.4, Shop Pay in buyers' agents.** Shop Pay in a buyer's AI agent is Shop Pay apart from the platform's checkout: CM-PAY-014 `own-service` (the digest's rule 11), the agents its venues as fillers. Buyers buying through those agents is CM-AIA-005 `extension-point`, `core;extension-point` where the card links the platform's Agentic Storefronts. A note offering the tools for adding Shop Pay to any agent adds `extension-point` on CM-PAY-014.
