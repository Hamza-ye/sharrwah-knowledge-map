## 2. Proposed: names, structure and behaviour

- **Payment method**: in Sharrwah's proposed design, a way a buyer can pay an order, such as a card network, a wallet, pay later, bank transfer or cash on delivery: either a **provider method**, which a payment provider processes, or a **manual method**, which the buyer pays to the merchant outside any provider and the merchant records; an order paid by a manual method waits, unpaid, until then. Q5-SH-027, Q5-BC-P0115, Q5-WC-016
  - It draws on the five platforms' split between provider and manual methods, and on the waiting order of Shopify, BigCommerce and WooCommerce. Q5-SH-083, Q5-BC-060, Q5-SA-015, Q5-ZD-P0075, Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-WC-K0037
  - Why: rule 3; an order's payment state depends on its method's kind, so the kind is in the data model from the start. Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-BC-P0115
- **Payment provider**: a company that processes provider methods and pays the money out, recorded once with the methods it carries, the places it serves, its money route and its terms. A method may come from several providers, a provider carry several methods. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - It draws on Shopify's cards through "Shopify Payments, a third-party provider, or both together", BigCommerce's alternative methods offered "through a cooperating payment gateway", and Salla's K net, which hangs on Tap. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - Why: rule 2; a provider's arrival or exit changes only its own record and its methods. Q5-SH-022, Q5-SA-K1654
- **Provider integration**: the code through which a provider plugs into Sharrwah, behind one payments interface: a **built-in integration**, which Sharrwah builds and keeps, or a **payments add-on**, which a provider or partner builds to Sharrwah's published interface, and which merchants can activate only once Sharrwah has reviewed it and it reports itself ready, a new method in it being reviewed again. The interface serves payment on Sharrwah's checkout and on the provider's page. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-K1696, Q5-SH-058
  - It draws on BigCommerce's Embedded Providers; Shopify's approved payments extensions, review of new methods and readiness step; Zid's Open APIs and app review; and the direct and hosted payment of Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-062, Q5-ZD-K0077, Q5-ZD-K0363, Q5-SH-007, Q5-WC-016, Q5-BC-068
  - Why: rule 2, each provider behind its own part; rule 1, one interface for every place. Q5-SH-K1694, Q5-ZD-K0077
  - Not taken: payment code a merchant adds to its own store, as with WooCommerce's plugins, because a provider offered at a place is a partner relationship Sharrwah answers for (rule 3); WooCommerce itself asks a separate agreement of a partner offering a "Payments Solution". Q5-WC-K0006, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Provider account**: the merchant's own account with a provider, connected with the credentials of an account it holds, or applied for through Sharrwah. The provider decides each application; Sharrwah shows its state: applied, approved, rejected or connected. Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-BC-P0048
  - It draws on Salla's choice between an existing account and a request the provider may reject, the own account Zid's pay-later providers need, BigCommerce's application and underwriting, and Shopify's apps, where the merchant may "log in or create an account with the payment provider". Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-ZD-P0080, Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SH-053
  - Why: rule 3; whose account receives the money fixes the money route and the contract. An account applied for through Sharrwah may count as one opened through Sharrwah, under the guard (section 5). Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SA-053
- **Place payment list**: Sharrwah's list, for each place it serves, of the providers and methods a store there may activate, each with its conditions: plan, verification, business type and amount limits. A store sees only its place's list, filtered by its conditions. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036
  - It draws on Shopify's, Zid's and Salla's methods by the store's address or country, and, for conditions, on their plans, Salla's verification, Zid's كي نت for institutions and companies only, and Salla's limits by country. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036, Q5-SA-P0068, Q5-ZD-P0150, Q5-SA-K0091, Q5-ZD-K0042, Q5-SA-048
  - Why: rule 1; what differs between places is set up as data, not built apart. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001
  - Not taken: WooCommerce's country list as suggestions only, which leaves the choice to the merchant, because each provider offered at a place is a partner contract (rule 3). Q5-WC-K0054, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Store payment settings**: per store, the methods the merchant has activated from its place's list and, for each, the one provider it goes to; changed only by staff holding a payments permission, a change of provider kept to the store owner, and each change logged. Where a store keeps separate markets or currencies, its settings follow them. Q5-SH-K1524, Q5-SH-008, Q5-SH-009
  - It draws on the activation and payments permissions the platforms show, Shopify's owner-only provider change and one card provider at a time, BigCommerce's change log, Salla's market selector and BigCommerce's methods per currency. Q5-SH-K0193, Q5-SA-K1509, Q5-BC-072, Q5-SA-016, Q5-BC-074
  - Why: rule 2; one provider per method tells checkout and refunds which provider holds each payment. Q5-SH-040, Q5-SH-015
  - Not taken: two card gateways at once, which WooCommerce allows though it "does cause a few issues". Q5-WC-031
- **Checkout payment rules**: rules the merchant sets, within its place's list, to hide a method, or to reject an order or hold it for review, by amount, quantity, the buyer's or card's country, customer group, category, shipping method or market. Q5-SA-043, Q5-ZD-013
  - It draws on Salla's قيود الدفع, Zid's restrictions, Shopify's Payment Customization, WooCommerce's cash on delivery by shipping method and BigCommerce's countries per offline method. Q5-SA-046, Q5-ZD-013, Q5-SH-K1761, Q5-WC-004, Q5-BC-P0121
  - Why: rule 3; they are cheap to change, so they come late, as data the checkout reads. Q5-SA-043, Q5-SH-K1761
- **Payment record**: each payment on an order, with its method, provider, the provider's reference, amount, currency, state (awaiting, paid, recorded by the merchant, refunded) and money route. A refund goes back through the provider that took the payment, which stays callable for that after it leaves checkout. Q5-SH-015, Q5-WC-033, Q5-BC-085
  - It draws on BigCommerce's attribution of each order "to the provider that processed it", Shopify's earlier provider kept for returns (and store fake-demo-sharrwah on 30 September 2026, observed), WooCommerce's six months, and Shopify's manual mark as paid. Q5-BC-085, Q5-SH-015, Q5-SH-098, Q5-WC-033, Q5-SH-093
  - Why: rule 3, the data model; a payment that doesn't name its provider and route can't be refunded or reconciled once that provider has gone. Q5-SH-015, Q5-WC-033
- **Money route**: for each provider, and for manual methods, the path the buyer's money takes to the merchant's own bank account. On the **direct route**, it goes to the merchant's own account with the provider, or straight from buyer to merchant; Sharrwah never holds it, and its records show the payment, not the payout. On the **held route**, it waits in a **merchant balance**, held before payout by a payment company in an account opened through Sharrwah, or by Sharrwah itself. This proposal provides for the held route, in its data model and as a later part; under the founder's guard, it goes live nowhere without the founder's decision. Q5-BC-P0089, Q5-SA-K1639, Q5-SA-P0006
  - It draws on the direct route the platforms show for gateways the merchant connects, pay-later providers that pay the store, and bank transfer, and on the held route of their own services. Q5-SH-P0050, Q5-ZD-P0107, Q5-ZD-P0077, Q5-SA-P0099, Q5-WC-P0024, Q5-ZD-P0024
  - Cash on delivery collected through a carrier Sharrwah contracts would pass through Sharrwah, as it passes through Salla and Zid: a held route. Cash the merchant collects is direct. Q5-SA-P0042, Q5-ZD-P0070, Q5-SA-P0092
  - Why: rule 3, money flows are expensive to change after launch, and what a platform's records hold differs by route, so every payment carries its route from the start; rule 4, whether a held route goes live stays open. Q5-SA-A0002, Q5-SH-026
  - Not taken, for the first build: an own service that shuts other card gateways out once active, as Shopify's and Zid's do, since it would be a held route. Q5-SH-008, Q5-ZD-P0109
- **Merchant balance**, with its payouts, on the held route only, and live only by the founder's decision: a ledger, per merchant, holder and currency, of credits from payments and debits for fees, refunds, chargebacks, reserves and other deductions, each debit naming the party it pays and the merchant's authorization for it; and payouts of the amount available to the merchant's own bank account, given at verification, on a schedule. Q5-ZD-P0024, Q5-SA-P0055, Q5-WC-P0124
  - It draws on the platforms' own balances and their deductions and reserves, Salla's label holds under the merchant's authorization, and the payout account Salla takes at verification. Q5-SH-P0028, Q5-WC-P0124, Q5-ZD-P0011, Q5-SA-P0126, Q5-SA-P0055
  - Why: rule 3, the ledger's shape is expensive to change; rule 2, a deduction plugs into the ledger without touching orders. Q5-SA-P0126, Q5-ZD-P0061
  - Where the platforms differ, one balance with credits folded in (Shopify's Shop Cash; WooPayments' FROD bucket) against several wallets (Salla's observed store; Zid's wallet), typed entries in one ledger can show either; the choice is kept open. Q5-SH-074, Q5-WC-042, Q5-SA-075, Q5-ZD-P0067
