Credit Card

It is a credit card granted to customers who have accounts with SBYB, where the customer can issue this type of card after conducting a credit study by the branch in which the customer’s account was opened. This card is issued with a monthly ceiling determined by the customer in advance. The card is used in the process of cash withdrawal, payment locally and globally from various points of sale, as well as shopping and payment online. The amounts used are recorded on the account linked to the card on a monthly basis. This type of card is issued for an annual issuance fee that varies according to the type of card.

 

Types of credit cards issued by SBYB:

- Gold MasterCard:

 MasterCard credit card issued with an annual issuance fee to a segment of high-net-worth, high-income customers, or owners of businesses or sole proprietorships with relatively high financial capabilities. The cardholder is granted a monthly limit starting at $5,000 or more, after a credit study is conducted and the required cash security deposited into the customer's account.

 It is used for cash withdrawals through ATMs, purchases at point-of-sale (POS) stores locally and internationally, and online shopping and payments.

Silver MasterCard:

MasterCard credit card issued with an annual issuance fee to a segment of high-net-worth, middle-income customers, as well as business owners and sole proprietorships with relatively high financial capabilities. The cardholder is granted a monthly limit of less than $5,000 after a credit study is conducted and the cash security covering the required limit is deposited into the customer's account.

It is used for cash withdrawals through ATMs, purchases through points of sale at merchants locally and internationally, and for shopping and paying online.

 

Advantages of credit cards (gold and silver):

• Pay for purchases at points of sale worldwide.

• Withdraw cash from a large number of ATMs worldwide.

• Pay online.

• Issue additional cards for family members.

• Card limits are granted based on the customer's credit standing and in accordance with the terms and conditions for issuing this type of card.

How to obtain credit cards:

- Open a bank account.

- Submit a credit card application, specifying the card type (Silver or Gold).

- Deposit the required security deposit on the card into the customer's account.

- The card will be issued within two to three weeks from the application date.
