22. ONE Cash

Description: Mobile money solutions
Headquarters: Yemen
Date of Establishment: 2018
Founders: Ahmed N. Hayel Saeed, Mohamed N. Hayel Saeed

ONE Cash provides mobile payment and money transfer services to individuals and businesses across Yemen. With more than 700,000 active customers, ONE Cash facilitated 14.7 million transactions worth $2.5 billion in 2024. Its app had been downloaded over 1.4 million times as of December 2024. ONE Cash has a network of more than 15,000 agents. It is owned by the Hayel Saeed Anam Group (HSA Group). The company was established in 2018 but launched in December 2022.
