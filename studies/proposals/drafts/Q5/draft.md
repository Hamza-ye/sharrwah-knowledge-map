## 2. Proposed: names, structure and behaviour

- **Payment method**: in Sharrwah's proposed design, a way a buyer can pay an order, such as a card network, a wallet, pay later, bank transfer or cash on delivery. It is either a **provider method**, which a payment provider processes, or a **manual method**, which the buyer pays to the merchant outside any provider and the merchant records. An order paid by a manual method waits, unpaid, until the merchant records the payment. Q5-SH-027, Q5-BC-P0115, Q5-WC-016
  - It draws on all five platforms' split between methods a provider processes and methods the merchant collects, and on Shopify's, BigCommerce's and WooCommerce's waiting order. Q5-SH-083, Q5-BC-060, Q5-SA-015, Q5-ZD-P0075, Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-WC-K0037
  - Why: rule 3. An order's payment state depends on its method's kind, so the kind belongs in the data model from the start. Q5-SH-K1736, Q5-BC-P0115
- **Payment provider**: a company that processes provider methods and pays the money out, recorded once with the methods it carries, the places it serves, its money route and its terms. A method may come from several providers, and a provider may carry several methods. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - It draws on Shopify's cards through "Shopify Payments, a third-party provider, or both together", on BigCommerce's alternative methods, "typically offered through a cooperating payment gateway", and on Salla's K net, which hangs on Tap. Q5-SH-001, Q5-BC-079, Q5-SA-K1654
  - Why: rule 2. A provider's arrival or exit changes only its own record and the methods it carries. Q5-SH-022, Q5-SA-K1654
- **Provider integration**: the code through which a provider plugs into Sharrwah, behind one payments interface shared by every provider. It is either a **built-in integration**, which Sharrwah builds and keeps, or a **payments add-on**, which a provider or partner builds against Sharrwah's published interface. Merchants can activate an add-on only after Sharrwah has reviewed it and it reports itself ready, and a new method added to it is reviewed again. The interface serves payment on Sharrwah's own checkout and payment on the provider's page. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-K1696, Q5-SH-058
  - It draws on BigCommerce's Embedded Providers, which "integrate directly with the BigCommerce platform"; on Shopify's approved payments extensions, its review of new methods and its readiness step; on Zid's Open APIs and app review; and on Shopify's direct and external providers, WooCommerce's Direct type and BigCommerce's hosted gateways. Q5-BC-105, Q5-SH-062, Q5-ZD-K0077, Q5-ZD-K0363, Q5-SH-007, Q5-WC-016, Q5-BC-068
  - Why: rule 2, each provider behind its own part; and rule 1, one interface for every place, while the providers plugged in differ by place. Q5-SH-K1694, Q5-ZD-K0077
  - Not taken: payment code a merchant adds to its own store, as WooCommerce's plugins are added, because a provider offered at a place is a partner relationship Sharrwah answers for (rule 3); WooCommerce itself asks a separate agreement of a partner offering a "Payments Solution" in its Marketplace. Q5-WC-K0006, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Provider account**: the merchant's own account with a provider, which the merchant connects by entering the credentials of an account it holds, or applies for through Sharrwah. The provider decides each application; Sharrwah shows its state: applied, approved, rejected or connected. Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-BC-P0048
  - It draws on Salla's choice between an existing business account and a request, which the provider answers and may reject; on the own account Zid's pay-later providers need; on BigCommerce's application and underwriting; and on Shopify's apps, where the merchant may "log in or create an account with the payment provider". Q5-SA-053, Q5-SA-057, Q5-ZD-P0080, Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SH-053
  - Why: rule 3. Whose account receives the money fixes the money route and the contract, both expensive to change. An account applied for through Sharrwah may count as one opened through Sharrwah, under the founder's guard; section 5 keeps that open. Q5-BC-P0048, Q5-SA-053
- **Place payment list**: Sharrwah's list, for each place it serves, of the providers and methods a store there may activate, each entry with its conditions: the store's plan, its verification, its business type, and amount limits. A store sees only its place's list, filtered by its conditions. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036
  - It draws on Shopify's provider list filtered by the store's address, Zid's options by country and Salla's methods kept to stores in Saudi Arabia; and, as conditions, on Salla's and Zid's plans, Salla's verification, Zid's كي نت for institutions and companies only, and Salla's limits set by country. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001, Q5-SA-036, Q5-SA-P0068, Q5-ZD-P0150, Q5-SA-K0091, Q5-ZD-K0042, Q5-SA-048
  - Why: rule 1. What differs between places is set up as data, not built apart. Q5-SH-012, Q5-ZD-001
  - Not taken: WooCommerce's list by country as suggestions only, which leaves the choice to the merchant, because each provider offered at a place is a partner contract (rule 3). Q5-WC-K0054, Q5-WC-K0353
- **Store payment settings**: per store, the methods the merchant has activated from its place's list and, for each method, the one provider it goes to. Only staff holding a payments permission change them, a change of provider is kept to the store owner, and each change is logged. Where a store keeps separate markets or currencies, its settings follow them. Q5-SH-K1524, Q5-SH-008, Q5-SH-009
  - It draws on the activation all five show; on Shopify's `MANAGE_PAYMENTS_SETTINGS`, Salla's `payment_methods_activation`, Zid's staff right and WooCommerce's `manage_woocommerce`; on Shopify's owner-only provider change and its one card provider at a time; on BigCommerce's change log; and on Salla's market selector and BigCommerce's methods per currency. Q5-SH-K0193, Q5-SA-K1509, Q5-ZD-002, Q5-WC-001, Q5-BC-072, Q5-SA-016, Q5-BC-074
  - Why: rule 2. One provider per method tells checkout and refunds which provider holds each payment, and keeps a change of provider to one setting. Q5-SH-040, Q5-SH-015
  - Not taken: two card gateways at once, which WooCommerce allows while its own page says it "does cause a few issues". Q5-WC-031
- **Checkout payment rules**: rules the merchant sets, within its place's list, to hide a method, or to reject an order or hold it for review, by order amount, quantity, the buyer's or card's country, customer group, product category, shipping method or market. Q5-SA-043, Q5-ZD-013
