# Q3. Placing the rows

**Status:** waits for Q1 and Q2, and for the founder's go.

The founder agreed this question on 10 October 2026, together with Q1 and Q2. It places the existing question rows against the concern map. The founder's concern behind it is that these questions are time bombs: if they aren't sorted by the engineering found in the product class, they will explode now or later.

## The question, as agreed

> Place every row against the map, extended by the product gaps from the Yemen test:
> 1. For each row, record the concern or concerns it belongs to, who answers it, and when.
> 2. Flag each time bomb by type:
>    - it bundles concerns placed differently;
>    - it's asked of the wrong party;
>    - it's treated as blocking build when it only blocks release, or the reverse;
>    - it phrases a market fact as a product question.
> 3. List the concerns on the map that no row asks about. Those are the questions nobody has asked yet.
> 4. Leave unclear rows unresolved for the founder. Nothing gets settled that the evidence didn't settle.

## What the rows are

The rows are not Sharrwah's requirements. Agents wrote them after reading `observed-sh` and `observed-sa`, recording gaps they perceived at the time in the product-class evidence and in the platforms' visible behaviour.

When they were written, observed-sa was small. Most of its citations are not in the current captures, or have changed. Both observed repositories hold more now than they did then.

The rows go into this question as inputs to place, labelled as agent-generated. Their citations are not trusted.
