▸ How do I ship customers' orders?
We provide shipping and delivery solutions suited to the Syrian market through:
Tarabot for shipping
Mersal for delivery inside Syria
And you can track your orders and manage shipping easily from your store.
▸ Is there a commission on sales?
No, there's no commission on your sales. All the profits from your store's sales are entirely yours.
▸ How do I receive customers' payments?
You can receive your payments electronically through Bemira for e-payments, in a secure and easy way that helps you manage your sales smoothly.
▸ How does a customer buy from my store?
The customer enters your store, browses the products, selects the right product, and completes the order in simple, easy steps. Then payment and delivery are completed according to the options available in your store.
▸ Do I need a commercial registration to sign up with Zid Syria?
No, a commercial registration isn't required to start and open your online store.
But having one helps build customer trust in your brand and gives your store more professionalism.
▸ Can I customize my store's link?
Yes, you can customize your online store's link and choose a name suitable for your brand, so it's easier for customers to remember and reach it.
