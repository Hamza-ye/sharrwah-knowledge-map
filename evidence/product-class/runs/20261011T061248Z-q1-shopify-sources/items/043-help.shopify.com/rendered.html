# Shopify Marketplace Connect app

<aside class="note note-app"><p><img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/app-store/listing_images/1edab14682438f4dafa44e78f0df0497/icon/COr1r-TOrIADEAE=.png" alt="Shopify Marketplace Connect app icon"> <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/marketplace-connect">Shopify Marketplace Connect</a>: Install Shopify Marketplace Connect on the Shopify App Store.</p>
</aside>

Using the [Shopify Marketplace Connect app](https://apps.shopify.com/marketplace-connect)(formerly Codisto), you can connect your product catalog to multiple marketplaces, and you can manage and sync all your orders, listings, and inventory in your Shopify admin. Changes made to products and orders in your online store are automatically synced across your marketplaces.

You can connect your Shopify store to the following marketplaces using Shopify Marketplace Connect:

* Amazon
* Target Plus (United States only)
* Walmart (United States only)
* eBay

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>New connections to Etsy can&#39;t be made at this time with the Shopify Marketplace Connect app. If you&#39;re already selling on Etsy through Marketplace Connect, then you can continue to use the app.</p>
</aside>

You can sell in any region, and in any currency available on your chosen marketplace. Marketplace Connect provides built-in currency conversion, which ensures that your prices display in the local currency of each marketplace that you connect to.

For additional support, you can use the [chat feature in the Marketplace Connect app](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-marketplace-connect).
