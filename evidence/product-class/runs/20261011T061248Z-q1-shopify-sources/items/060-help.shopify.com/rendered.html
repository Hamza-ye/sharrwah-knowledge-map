# Local payment methods

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>To offer local payment methods in countries outside your business location, ensure those countries are set up as markets. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/international/payments">international payments</a>.</p>
</aside>

You can activate local payment methods with Shopify Payments to offer customers region-specific ways to pay at checkout. Each method is available only when a customer is in a supported country and selects an eligible currency. Orders paid using local payment methods are included in your Shopify Payments payouts alongside credit and debit card transactions.

You must deactivate any conflicting [additional payment gateways](/manual/payments/additional-payment-methods/deactivate-payment-methods) before you can activate local payment methods.

Some local payment methods are available in early access, and might not be available to every store or every customer yet. Learn more about [early access local payment methods](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/managed-payment-methods#early-access-local-payment-methods).

### Flexible payment options

Flexible payment methods give customers more ways to spread or delay the cost of their purchase.

* [Klarna](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/klarna) is available to customers across a range of European countries and lets them pay immediately, by invoice, or in installments.
* [Meses sin intereses](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/installments-with-msi) lets eligible customers in Mexico split purchases into monthly installments with no interest.

### Mobile wallet and app-based payments

Mobile wallet and app-based payment methods let customers authorize their payment using a digital wallet or mobile app during checkout:

* [Alipay](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/alipay) is available to customers with an Alipay account.
* [BLIK](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/blik) is available to customers in Poland.
* [GrabPay](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/grabpay) is available in early access for eligible stores in Singapore to accept SGD payments from customers in Singapore.
* [MB WAY](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/mb-way) is available to customers in Portugal.
* [MobilePay](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/mobilepay) is available to customers in Denmark or Finland.
* [Satispay](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/satispay) is available to customers in Italy.
* [Swish](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/swish) is available to customers in Sweden.
* [TWINT](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/twint) is available to customers in Switzerland.
* [WeChat Pay](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/wechat-pay) is available to customers with a WeChat Pay account.

### Cash and voucher payments

Cash and voucher payment methods let customers place an order online and complete payment later using payment instructions, such as a barcode or reference number. [OXXO](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/oxxo) is available to customers in Mexico.

### Bank transfer and direct debit payments

Bank transfer and direct debit methods let customers pay directly from their bank account at checkout.

* [Bancontact](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/bancontact) is available to customers in Belgium.
* [iDEAL | Wero](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/ideal) is available to customers in the Netherlands.
* [EPS](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/eps) is available to customers in Austria who pay in euros.
* [Przelewy24](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/przelewy24) is available to customers in Poland.
* [ACH Direct Debit](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/ach) is available to customers in the United States for B2B orders.
* [Multibanco](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/local-payment-methods/multibanco) is available to customers in Portugal.
