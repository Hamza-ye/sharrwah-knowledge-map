WooPayments: In-Person Payments

With WooPayments’ In-Person Payments feature, your customers can pay via tap, swipe, chip card, or mobile wallet payments, all using our mobile app and a simple card reader or a device that supports Tap to Pay on iPhone or Android.

In-Person Payments is available to qualified merchants based in Australia, Canada, Finland, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Singapore, the United Kingdom, and the United States using iPhone, iPad, or Android devices for non-subscription products. Support for subscriptions is coming soon!

Running a busy store counter? Try WooCommerce Point of Sale (POS) mode in the mobile app for fast checkout on a tablet with the same card readers. Support for phones, including Tap to Pay, is rolling out.

Currencies:

Card readers and Tap to Pay on iPhone/Android can only collect payments in the default currency for each country.

United States

Our Getting Started Guide contains a detailed walkthrough of all the features. Our Quick Start guide for the M2 card reader can help you get up and running quickly if time is a priority.

If you don’t want to purchase a separate reader, you may be able to use Tap to Pay on iPhone or Android to take payments instead.

Please also take note of our card readers’ return policy.

All other supported countries

First, you will need a card reader. For merchants in Canada, Finland, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Singapore, and the United Kingdom, we have the WisePad 3.

Our Getting Started Guide contains a detailed walkthrough of all the features. Our Quick Start guide for non-U.S. merchants can help you get up and running quickly, if time is a priority.

Please also take note of our card readers’ return policy.

Frequently asked questions
Can I take my card reader to another country for a convention or pop-up?

No, live Terminal payments must be processed in the same country as the merchant’s account and associated reader location.

Can customers with foreign-issued cards pay on my card reader?

Yes, as long as the merchant is using the reader in the supported country for their account and charging in the supported local currency. See WooPayments: Fees for International Payments and Currency Conversion charges

Still experiencing issues?

If you’re using the Jetpack plugin to connect the app to your site, make sure that the Jetpack connection is active and working correctly. You can verify some known issues or try reconnecting your site.

If you’re still having difficulty, please fill out our contact form to reach our support team from within the app by going to Menu > Settings > Help & Support > Contact Support.
