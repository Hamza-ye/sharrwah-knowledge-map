Top payment methods in Yemen: A guide on best ways to pay

Wondering what the most popular ways to pay are in Yemen? Explore the top payment options and choose wisely.
Yemen, with a population of around 41.77 million people, has a GDP of approximately 17.40 billion making it the 136th largest economy in the world. The official currency of Yemen is the Yemeni Rial (YER)
The economy has been devastated by civil war and is heavily reliant on humanitarian aid. Its formal economy, once based on oil and agriculture, is largely non-functional, with limited trade conducted through neighboring states.

What is the main payment method in Yemen?

Due to the ongoing conflict and the collapse of the formal banking infrastructure, the only viable and trusted payment method in Yemen is physical cash, the Rial. Informal, trust-based money transfer networks known as "hawala" are critical for moving money domestically and for receiving remittances. While mobile money services exist, they are fragmented and face immense operational challenges.
