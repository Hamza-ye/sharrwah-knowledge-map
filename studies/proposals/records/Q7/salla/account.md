### The store's currency
- **differs** (term): Salla's store sets العملة الحالية للمتجر under مركز الإعدادات > عام > العملات, with other currencies turned on and added there. Store performance reports convert every amount to العملة الأساسية للمتجر at the exchange rate when the order was created. Sorted against Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-001 ("In the help, the seller chooses"); Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1934 ("Shopify names a store currency"); Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-074 ("Reports can be viewed only"). Q7-SA-001 Q7-SA-K1955 Q7-SA-009
- **differs** (term): in the runs, a store's currencies page shows "Store currency Saudi Riyal (SAR)" (store real-merchant, 12 October 2026, whose payments answer gives `"merchant_type":"KSA"`) and "عملة المتجر الحالية ... ريال سعودي ( ر.س )" (store store-sherawya, 8 October 2026). The same real-merchant step's `GET /admin/v2/currencies` lists the store's four currencies, each with `status` "enabled" and an `amount`: UAE Dirham 0.977568, Bahraini Dinar 0.100561, Saudi Riyal 1, US Dollar 0.266186. Sorted against Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1934 ("Shopify names a store currency"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-005 ("The Merchant API returns"). Q7-SA-017 Q7-SA-019 Q7-SA-034 Q7-SA-035
- **differs** (role): the currencies a store could add, as `GET /admin/v2/currencies/available` answered it (store demo, 9 October 2026, and store real-merchant), are 31 items, each a country with its `currency_code`, among them SA SAR, AE AED, KW KWD, BH BHD, QA QAR and YE YER. Sorted against Zid [2]: Q7-ZD-007 ("The store's currency is set at the store level"). Q7-SA-026 Q7-SA-027 Q7-SA-028 Q7-SA-029 Q7-SA-030 Q7-SA-031

Sorted against Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-005 ("The Merchant API returns"); Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K2893 ("In the Admin GraphQL API"); Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-002 ("Shopify names a payout currency"). ### Currency shown to buyers
- **differs** (role): the buyer picks one of the store's currencies from the list beside the cart, and product prices then show in that currency. The default currency shown depends on the buyer's location, not on the store settings' default, so prices may show in a local currency automatically. Sorted against Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0300 ("With مدفوعات زد active and other currencies"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0299 ("On the compare-plans page"); Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1941 ("A market's currency is the one displayed"). Q7-SA-K1956 Q7-SA-002 Q7-SA-K1957 Q7-SA-003
- **differs** (role): in a store with several markets, the money values of offers are shown and applied in the buyer's active market currency. A store with one market keeps them in the store's currency. Sorted against Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-111 ("Reports can be viewed only"). Q7-SA-010 Q7-SA-011

- **differs** (role): payments go first to رصيد المدفوعات الإلكترونية, which holds the store's sales proceeds until they are transferred. Amounts below the minimum (100 riyals, or 250 dirhams in the UAE) stay in that balance. It is separate from رصيد المتجر, used for Salla's subscriptions and services; money can't move from the first to the second. Withdrawals from the store wallet are capped at 5,000 Saudi riyals a day. The published pages read don't name the currency of either balance.
