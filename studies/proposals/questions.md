# The questions agents are given (draft, 18 October 2026)

**Status: draft, for the check before the founder's go on the plan change.**

The founder's section B (`founder-messages/2026-10-18-g1-answer.md`) states what the founder wants Sharrwah to make possible. Its items are the questions the work answers. Agents get each one only as worded below, in neutral words. The founder's working words never become names or categories that agents record under.

**Rules for the wording:**
- **It asks what the platform says and does,** in its own words and terms. It doesn't say what Sharrwah wants, or what the answer should look like.
- **It fixes no level and no setter.** It never says where something is set (a store, a market, an account, a plan) or who sets it. The agent records what the platform shows, at the level and with the setter the platform names.
- **It uses no category of ours.** "Market", "availability", "conditions", "manual payment" and "parts" are the founder's working words. The questions use plain descriptions of what to look for instead, and name no platform's term either.
- **It names what to read, not what to find.** The brief gives each run its reading list. The question says what bears on it, so the run can tell what to record.

**How a run uses its question:**
- it records every statement in the platform's material that bears on the question, in the platform's own words, scoped as the source scopes it;
- what it doesn't find in what it read it records as "not in what was read", listing what it read. It records something as absent only from a complete list the platform publishes;
- it doesn't answer the question in its own words. The answer is the coordinator's proposal, later.

## Q1. Places a store sells in or operates from

The founder's item: B1, "one platform, set up per market".

> When a seller sells to, or operates from, more than one place (as the platform names a place: a country, a region, a market, or otherwise), what can differ between those places, and what stays the same? For each thing that can differ: what does the platform call it, where is it set and by whom, as the platform shows it, and what does the platform say adding a new place, a new currency or a new payment provider involves?

## Q2. What a place's rules and the platform's own choices change for a seller there

The founder's item: B2, "rules land as availability, not as design".

> How do the laws, sanctions and licensing requirements of a place, and the platform's own choice of partners and providers, show in what a seller can use or do there? Record what the platform offers, restricts or withholds by place, the reasons it gives, and where it meets such a requirement some other way, for example through its terms, through a separate company, or through its product.

## Q3. What a seller must do or meet before doing particular things

The founder's item: B3, "conditions on what a seller can do".

> What must a seller do, provide or meet before the platform lets them do particular things? Examples are opening the store to buyers, taking payments, receiving payouts, or selling particular products, but record any such requirement the platform states. For each: what it requires, who sets it, when it is checked, and what the seller can and can't do while it isn't met.

## Q4. Orders whose payment doesn't pass through the platform

The founder's item: B4, "selling without Sharrwah touching the money".

> Can a seller take orders whose payment is arranged directly between seller and buyer, with no money passing through the platform or a payment service it provides? What does the platform call this? For such orders, what does the platform do and not do: recording the payment, the order's status, refunds, fees and reports? What limits or conditions apply?

## Q5. Which payment methods a seller can offer, and who decides

The founder's item: B5, "Sharrwah chooses which payment methods are offered".

> Which payment methods can a seller offer buyers, and how is that decided? What does the platform itself decide, by what criteria, and where is that set, as the platform shows it? What can the seller choose? Through which kinds of providers, and through which kinds of apps or add-ons, do payment methods come? Where the money from each method goes before it reaches the seller, record what the platform says.

The last sentence carries the deferred money decisions (the pilot's Decisions 1 to 14) into the work. The pilot's records answer most of it already, and are reused.

## Q6. Who can register as a seller, and from where

The founder's item: B6, "a seller anywhere can register".

> Who can register as a seller, from which places, and with what requirements? What can a seller do when they are based in, or sell from, a place the platform doesn't fully support, and how does the platform say so?

## Q7. Currencies

The founder's item: B7, "currency".

> In what currency or currencies does a store hold its prices, show prices to buyers, take payments, and receive payouts? What decides each of these? What can be set or changed later, by whom, and with what effect on what is already recorded?

The founder expects one store currency to be the platforms' norm, and asks the work to confirm or correct that. The question doesn't say so, so as not to lead the agent.

## Q8. How the platform is divided into parts

The founder's item: B8, "built gradually, in parts".

> How does the platform divide what it offers into parts, as it names and organizes them itself? What does each part depend on, what do the parts share, and how are parts added or extended, for example through apps, extensions, channels or plans?

Much of this comes from the areas list (step R1) and what is recorded. A run records only what those don't show.

## What isn't a question

- **The guard** on arrangements that hold sellers' money is a decision of the founder's (`founder-decisions.tsv`, G1). The money proposals say what the design provides for. Whether any such arrangement goes live is the founder's to decide.
- **Company decisions** are where the company is based, which providers and partners Sharrwah works with, any licence, and who may register. They aren't questions agents are given. The work collects the evidence that bears on each, and the coordinator brings each to the founder in plain words, with a recommendation and whether it needs a lawyer.
