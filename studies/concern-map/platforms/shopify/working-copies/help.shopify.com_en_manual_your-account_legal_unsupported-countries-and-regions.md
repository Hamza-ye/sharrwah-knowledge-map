# Unsupported countries and regions

Shopify is unable to support business involving certain regions due to local regulatory requirements, commercial partnerships, and/or other pertinent factors.

The following list contains the countries and regions that aren’t supported by Shopify:

* Cuba
* Iran
* North Korea
* Syria
* The Crimea region of Ukraine, and the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People’s Republics in Ukraine

The following restrictions apply to all users of Shopify:

* Individuals and businesses within unsupported countries and regions are prohibited from creating a Shopify account.
* Access to storefronts and accounts is prohibited within unsupported countries and regions.
* Products originating from unsupported countries and regions are prohibited.
* Orders shipping to unsupported countries and regions are prohibited.
