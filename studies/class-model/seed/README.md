# The seed: the Knowledge Map's index, at `e4d5cee`

**What this folder is:** the starting index for the class's model, taken from the founder's Knowledge Map as the repository held it at commit [`e4d5cee`](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/tree/e4d5cee3aaea9a12b3e27c1e641075a01d88052c/register): its register `register/km-questions.tsv` and its page `register/km-artifact-1791419535-9835.html`. The founder gave it as a seed on 29 October 2026 (`reports/intent-review.md`, section 8).

**It is a seed, not evidence and not decisions.** It was written on 8 October 2026, from two platforms (Shopify and Salla), before the concern map existed.
- Its labels are Shopify's and Salla's only.
- Its questions speak of one "target market", with Saudi examples. Those questions are of kind `world`: each market's setup, asked when that market's slot is filled.
- Its "founder" lane and its "decisions as ADRs owned by the founder" aren't current. Under the founder's intent of 29 October, INT-02, INT-03, MTH-01, MTH-04 and ENG-02 are engineering's, and INT-05 is set aside.
- Its evidence, pointers and status columns are left out: they point at commits from before the evidence repositories were reorganised.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `km-areas.tsv` | The 41 areas in eight domains, each with Shopify's and Salla's own groupings, from the page's Areas tab |
| `km-questions.tsv` | The 179 questions: id, area, kind (class, slot, world, build, intent, method), facet, question and when it is needed |
| `km-source-types.tsv` | The 14 source types: what each is authoritative for, what it can't tell, and how it is graded. "Practitioners" stays out: merchants are never asked (`founder-decisions.tsv`, 24 October) |
