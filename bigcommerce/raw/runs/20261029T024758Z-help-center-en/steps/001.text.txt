BigCommerce supports a broad range of payment gateway integrations, serving numerous countries and currencies.
Select gateways support Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) like Alipay, Klarna, and SEPA along with leading digital wallets such as Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. These options allow you to offer increased payment flexibility and popular regional methods.
Accelerated Checkout solutions, Fastlane by PayPal and Stripe Link, provide shoppers with low-friction, passwordless checkout experiences that reduce cart abandonment and improve conversion.
Embedded Payment Providers
Processing payments through BigCommerce gateway integrations on the Embedded Payment Provider listdo not incur Open Payment Provider fees. Note that custom integrations to processors on the Embedded Payment Provider list, including those connected via third-party apps, will incur Open Payment Provider fees.
See each gateway’s documentation for details such as requirements, limitations, setup instructions, supported countries and currencies, and additional features.

Alternative Payment Methods and Digital Wallets
Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) are payment options that often do not require a major credit card scheme such as Visa or Mastercard. Shoppers in many regions prefer local APMs at checkout, making them a valuable tool for international selling.
Offered through a supporting payment gateway, APMs can include payment types such as:
bank transfers
“buy now, pay later” (BNPL)
cash payments
digital wallets
direct debit
mobile payments
Digital wallet APMs allow shoppers to complete purchases by signing into a third-party account, bypassing the need to create a separate account or re-enter payment information. This streamlines the checkout experience, boosts conversion rates, and provides a better mobile shopping experience.
Note that the Amazon Pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay digital wallets have a unique setup experience and are found under the Digital Wallets section of Settings › Payments.
For a full list of available APMs, digital wallets, and their supported gateways, see Introduction to Alternative Payment Methods.
Accelerated Checkout Solutions
Accelerated Checkout solutions remove friction from checkout by offering a passwordless checkout experience. When using an Accelerated Checkout, shoppers are prompted to securely save their information with the provider.
Once they have an account, shoppers receive a one-time passcode to their mobile device whenever they enter their email address at checkout, and the Accelerated Checkout solution automatically fills in their address and payment information for a single-click checkout.
Since Accelerated Checkout profiles are supported on any online store using the solution, offering this payment option can assure shoppers that their payments will be speedy and secure. This can encourage repeat purchases and increase your conversion rate.
The following Accelerated Checkout solutions are available in BigCommerce:
Stripe Link — Supported by the Stripe payment gateways; offer a streamlined checkout experience for any shopper with a Link profile.
Fastlane by PayPal — Fastlane by PayPal provides merchants with a fast, passwordless checkout solution for guest shoppers.
FAQ
Where do I manage my funds?
You will control and direct funds in your merchant account with your merchant service provider. Usually, this is where you will connect to your bank account or perform refunds external to BigCommerce.
What is a hosted payment gateway?
Hosted payment gateways navigate shoppers away from your checkout to the payment service provider’s page. After entering their payment details and completing the transaction, they are redirected back to your storefront.
Hosted payment gateways:
Afterpay
Amazon Pay
BitPay
Klarna
Mercado Pago
Opayo
Optty
Serve First
Sezzle
Note that hosted gateways are not compatible with Embedded Checkout on headless storefronts. In addition, most APMs and digital wallets do not support Embedded Checkout as they redirect shoppers to a hosted payment page.
APMs and digital wallets that support Embedded Checkout:
Google Pay
PayPal Pay Later
Do any payment gateways support Level 2 and Level 3 data?
Yes, several gateways support the use of Level 2 and Level 3 data. For more information, see Using Level 2 and Level 3 Data in BigCommerce.
Do any payment gateways support the selling of regulated products, such as firearms, e-cigs, or CBD?
The following gateways may allow the sale of regulated items. Before selling any items, check with your payment gateway to make sure your products are permitted in their terms of service.
Bankful
NMI
Sezzle
USA ePay*
Worldpay
*You must use Paysafe as your Merchant Services Provider (MSP).
Are there other payment processors outside of payment gateways?
Yes, you can find additional payment processors on our App Marketplace. Note that Open Payment Provider fees may apply. For more details, see About BigCommerce Pricing.
How will I know if changes are made to my payment methods in the control panel?
When a payment method is enabled, disabled, or has settings updated, a notification will appear in the control panel dashboard.
You can also see records of changes to your payment methods in the Staff Action Logs.
