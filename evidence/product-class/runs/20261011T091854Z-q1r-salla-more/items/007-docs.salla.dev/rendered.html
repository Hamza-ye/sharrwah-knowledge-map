# Overview

The Report Creator is a powerful new feature in the Salla Partners Portal that enables developers to build custom analytical reports to be displayed on the Salla store dashboard. 

Developers can choose from a variety of visualizations and bind them to their own data sources to display different metrics which are then displayed in the merchant dashboard.



## Report Builder Tool Impact

With the Report Creator, you can build tailored analytics using live merchant data. That showcases your insights through custom charts, tables, and metrics. 



* Add your custom reports to the store’s main reports dashboard
* Create reports tailored to specific industries or store needs
* Help merchants stay engaged with tools they use every day
* Offer reporting as a paid feature in your app or service
* Make your Salla app stand out with powerful visual insights
* Build reports once and use them across many stores
* Position yourself as a trusted partner who delivers real data value
