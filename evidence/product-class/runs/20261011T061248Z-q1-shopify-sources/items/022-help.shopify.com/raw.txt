# Shopify Capital merchant cash advance for merchants in the United Kingdom

In the United Kingdom, Shopify Capital offers merchant cash advances. This type of funding involves selling a portion of your future sales, referred to as [receivables](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary#receivables). Shopify provides merchant cash advances in the United Kingdom through our partner YouLend under the receivables purchase agreement. If your application for funding is accepted, then Shopify provides you with a lump sum of money for a fixed fee. This lump sum is known as the **purchase price**, and the fixed fee is known as the **purchase percentage**.

Under the receivables purchase agreement, you remit a percentage of your daily sales, known as the purchase percentage, until the total amount, known as the **termination amount**, is remitted. The termination amount and the remittance rate depend on your risk profile.

For example, Shopify Capital through its partner, YouLend might purchase £5,000 GBP of purchased receivables for £5,650 GBP remitted from your store's daily sales. You receive £5,000 GBP into your business bank account, and Shopify Capital receives 10% of your store's gross daily sales until the total £5,650 GBP is remitted. You have the option to [remit all or some of the remaining balance](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/united-kingdom#remit-remaining-balance) in a single lump sum. This is considered a repurchase of the receivables under your agreement.

If your remittances are paused or fail, then you may be in default of your merchant cash advance agreement.

## Shopify Capital UK remittance deadline

There is no deadline for remitting the total to Shopify Capital. The daily [remittance](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary/#remittance) amount in GBP is determined by your store's daily sales, because the remittance rate is a percentage of your store's daily sales. The remittance amount is automatically debited from your business bank account.

## Apply for a Shopify Capital merchant cash advance (United Kingdom) {#request-a-merchant-cash-advance}

If you're eligible to apply for a merchant cash advance (known as a _purchase of receivables_ in the United Kingdom), then you can view your funding options and request a merchant cash advance directly on the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance) page of your Shopify admin. Funding isn't guaranteed, and an offer to apply for funding might expire at any time.

You can customize the funding that you want to apply for, based on available funding options. The terms of each option include the amount received, the total to remit that Shopify Capital will buy, and the remittance rate that is the percentage of your daily sales that will be deducted and remitted to Shopify Capital, and they will adjust automatically based on the Shopify Capital amount but can't be customized independently. After you apply for funding, you can't change the amount.

After you apply for a merchant cash advance, your request is reviewed within 1-3 business days, but it might take longer in some cases. If your request is approved, then your merchant cash advance is transferred to your business bank account.

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance).

2. In the **Accounts** section, click **Capital** and then click **Offers**.
3. In the **Choose funding offer** section, customize your funding application based on available options, and review the relevant terms. Click **Apply now** under the option that you want to apply to.
4. Verify your identity and business details for Shopify Capital.
5. In the **Review and accept terms** dialog, click **Receivables Purchase Agreement**, and then review the terms of the agreement carefully before submitting your application.
6. Select **Change my payments provider to Shopify Payments** to change your remittance provider to Shopify Payments.

After you receive the merchant cash advance, the daily remittance based on your store's daily sales begins within 2 business days.

## Updating your bank account for Shopify Payments payouts (United Kingdom)

If you want to change your bank account for Shopify Payments payouts and your remittances after you accept an amount, then you need to contact Shopify Support to complete a new direct debit agreement.

## Requesting another Shopify Capital merchant cash advance (United Kingdom)

You may qualify for a second merchant cash advance before you have remitted an existing merchant cash advance in full. If you're eligible for a second merchant cash advance, then new funding options are displayed on the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance) page in your Shopify admin.

## Keeping track of your merchant cash advance remittances (United Kingdom)

After you receive your merchant cash advance, you can track the remaining balance of your total to [remit](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary/#remit) (the termination amount), from the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finances) page of your Shopify admin under **Accounts** > **Capital**.

From the **Capital** page, you can view the following remittance information:

* the remaining balance
* the amount that you've remitted
* the percentage of the total that is remitted
* the transfer history

You can view each day's sales revenue in the **Total sales** column of the transfer history section. You can view how much is remitted to Shopify Capital in the **Amount** column.

### Download your balance letter

You can download a balance letter from the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finances) page of your Shopify admin. You can use the balance letter to show your bank that you no longer have any financial obligation or what your current balance is.

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance).

2. In the **Accounts** section, click **Capital**.
3. Click **View all transactions**.
4. Click **Export**, and then click **Balance letter**.
5. Save the balance letter to your device.

## Remit the remaining balance of your merchant cash advance (United Kingdom) {#remit-remaining-balance}

You have the option to remit any amount over £1 GBP against your advance, or the full remaining balance in one lump sum. The remittance is debited from the same business bank account that's tied to your Shopify account. The cost of this financing, known as the **termination amount**, isn't reduced when you remit your entire balance, and remittance doesn't guarantee eligibility for additional financing.

When you make a lump sum remittance, it might take up to 3 business days to process the lump sum remittance. You'll receive an email confirming that the lump sum remittance has been processed and is now complete. However, your regular remittances will still be processed as usual during this time.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Under your agreement, this is a repurchase of your receivables. Even if you remit your entire balance, the purchase price remains the same. Making a remittance doesn&#39;t automatically qualify you for more financing.</p>
</aside>

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance).

2. In the **Accounts** section, click **Capital**.
3. Click **Make a payment**.
4. Choose the amount that you want to remit:
   * To make a manual remittance, enter the amount in the **Custom amount** field.
   * To remit the full amount, click **Full remaining balance**.
5. Click **Next step** and review the amount to be remitted.
6. If you're sure that you want to continue with the remittance, then check the authorization box and click **Make a payment**.

## Calculating merchant cash advance remittance (United Kingdom) {#understanding-remittance}

Every day that you make sales, a fixed percentage (the [remittance rate](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary/#remittance-rate) also known as the purchased percentage in the Receivables Purchase Agreement) is applied to the gross amount of your sales to calculate your [remittance](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary/#remittance) (the amount payable under the Receivables Purchase Agreement). On days with higher sales, your remittance will be larger. On days with lower sales, your remittance will be smaller.

Your sales includes all sales that are associated with your Shopify account and the services that are provided under your account. This includes sales on your Shopify online store, at your retail locations, from any [sales channels](/manual/online-sales-channels) that you have, and through any apps, such as [Shopify Marketplace Connect](https://apps.shopify.com/marketplace-connect) and [Shopify Collective](https://www.shopify.com/collective).

Daily sales revenue includes all completed orders, regardless of the order source, the payment processing method that was used, or whether the order was later refunded or canceled. It also includes all charges on orders, such as shipping and handling, as well as any applicable sales taxes, such as duty and value-added taxes.

When calculating remittance, test orders that you place when Shopify Payments is in [test mode](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/testing-shopify-payments) aren't included in your store's daily sales revenue. If you place an order without test mode activated, then the order is included in your store's sales revenue for the day's remittance calculation.

You'll continue to make these remittances until Shopify Capital has received the full agreed-upon amount of your future sales revenue, known as the termination amount. Each remittance is automatically debited from your bank account and remitted to Shopify in accordance with your Pre-Authorized Debit Agreement. If there are no sales on a particular day, then no remittance will be processed for that day.

## Understanding pre-debit notification emails

If you're repaying Shopify Capital in the UK or EU, you'll receive daily email notifications from `shopify-capital-developers@shopify.com` before each payment is collected. The notification email subject line will say "Debit initiated for Stripe RE Shopify Capital UK".

The Bankers' Automated Clearing System (BACS) Direct Debit regulations require these notifications, and you can't deactivate them. These are operational emails, not marketing emails.

## Secure your Shopify Capital account {#secure-your-account}

If your Shopify account has been compromised and you use Shopify Capital merchant cash advances, then you must take immediate action to protect your finances and report fraudulent activity.

[Contact Shopify Support](/manual/your-account/contact-shopify-support) immediately if you identify unauthorized Shopify Capital merchant cash advance activity. Remittances begin as soon as funding is disbursed, so time is critical. You can’t report Shopify Capital fraud in your Shopify admin.

#### Steps:

1. Review your merchant cash advance payment details for unauthorized funding.  
2. If you identify unauthorized advances or changes to your remittance settings, then contact Shopify Support immediately.   
3. Verify that the bank account connected to your Shopify Capital account hasn't been changed without authorization. Shopify Capital loan repayments are debited from any of the following accounts:  
   * The bank account connected to Shopify Payments.  
   * Your Shopify Balance account if it's set as your payout account.
   * The external bank account that you set up in your Shopify Capital settings (for non-Shopify Payments users).

### Examples of fraudulent activity

The following types of activity might indicate that your Shopify Capital account has been compromised:

- **Unauthorized funding**: Advance funds requested or accepted without your knowledge.
- **Unauthorized remittance setting changes**: Unauthorized changes to your remittance rate or remittance settings.
- **Unauthorized bank account changes**: Unauthorized changes to the bank account used for Shopify Capital merchant cash advance debits.
