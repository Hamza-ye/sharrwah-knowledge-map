# Multi-currency

To sell in multiple currencies, you need to use Shopify Payments in your online store and activate [international sales tools](/manual/international).

With [international sales tools](/manual/international), you can offer a localized experience to your international customers by displaying [prices in their local currency](/manual/international/pricing). You can also add them by using a [third-party geolocation app](https://apps.shopify.com/search?q=geolocation) from the Shopify App Store to include a country selector on your storefront.

Developers setting up a custom storefront with [Headless commerce](/manual/custom-storefronts#headless-commerce) can refer to the Shopify Developer Documentation to [create a country selector](https://shopify.dev/docs/custom-storefronts/hydrogen/markets/country-selector).
