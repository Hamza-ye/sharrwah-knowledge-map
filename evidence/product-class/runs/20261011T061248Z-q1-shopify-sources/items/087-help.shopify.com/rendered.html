# Setting up Shopify Payments for POS

[Shopify Payments](https://www.shopify.com/payments) is the simplest way to accept payments on Shopify POS. It eliminates the hassle of setting up a third-party payment provider or merchant account and having to enter the credentials in manually. After you set up Shopify Payments in your Shopify admin, your customers can make payment card payments in your store with Shopify POS and a [supported card reader](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/card-readers).

## Requirements for activating Shopify Payments in Shopify POS {#requirements}

Before you can activate Shopify Payments in Shopify POS, you must meet the following requirements:

* Your store is in a [supported country](/manual/sell-in-person/getting-started/setup-payment-method/shopify-payments-pos#supported) to set up Shopify payments and meets the [bank account requirements](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries) for your country.
* Your store is [eligible to set up Shopfiy Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/eligibility#prohibited-businesses).
* You've [set up Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/account-setup) in your Shopify admin.
* You've secured your account by setting up [two-step authentication](/manual/your-account/logging-in/secure-sign-in).

## Activate Shopify Payments in Shopify POS

If you've set up [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding) for your store, then you can activate it in Shopify POS and accept payment card payments in person.

#### Steps:

1. From Shopify POS, tap <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg" alt="Horizontal menu" class="admin-icon" /> > **Settings** > **Payments**.
2. Confirm that the **Credit/debit** option is activated.

If you change banks or if your bank account details change, then you can [edit your bank account information](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/configuring-shopify-payments#edit-bank-account-information) in your Shopify admin.
