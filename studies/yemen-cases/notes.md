### How conditions were sized (rule 2)

- The rate in the exchange market, the rate a central bank sets, caps or publishes, and rates quoted by data providers are separate conditions, and the US dollar and the Saudi riyal are separate. A row giving both currencies stays with the US dollar condition (YQ-E600), and a row giving a fixed and a black-market rate stays with the official rate (YQ-E599).
- Lists are kept apart from single acts: which wallets or services exist, and the list a wallet offers, are separate from whether a buyer can do one act through a named service.
- Acts are kept apart where one place can offer one and not the other: paying at shops, online, bills, and person to person; cash in and out at agents, and at ATMs; opening an account remotely, and where.
- Measures of one thing are kept apart where two can hold at once: the share of people with an account, the share of women with one, the gap between men and women, and the number of accounts; mobile data speed and fixed broadband speed; e-money's share of payment value and how widely buyers pay electronically; through which channel public salaries are paid, and whether they are paid.
- Where electronic payment is available and how widely buyers use it are separate, as are how far payment is in cash and how familiar buyers are with electronic payment.
- Licence revocations and suspensions are states of the conditions on whom parties may deal with (which banks, which exchange companies), as they bar dealing with the party named.
- A row stating two things is in the group of its main statement: YQ-D214 (staff and suppliers) with how businesses pay staff; YQ-B048 (all adults and women) with the share of people with an account; YQ-E467 (Yemenia's stop and the Houthis' channel rule) with whether tickets bought in Sana'a are issued and honoured.

### Problems with the method

- **Bodies with no cited tie.** Chains join a body to its governing body only where a cited source ties it to a place in the chain. The Prime Minister, the House of Representatives, the Tax Authority, the Zakat Authority and the Civil Status Authority in Sana'a, and the Ministry of Trade and Industry in Aden, have none, and seatless bodies join neither side; conditions they share with a governing body will show that side in more than one part.
- **Products of one party.** Before step 2b's product rule, a party's several products would have shared its place (Kuraimi's M Floos, Kuraimi Jawal, Hasseb, Kuraimi Express and ePay; CAC Bank's Mobile Money and Mobile Money Business). Step 2b now takes a row that names one product to `<product> (<party>)'s service` and adds those places; step 2c groups them.
- **Lists that grow.** Where a state is a list (which banks or exchange companies a party may deal with, who is under sanctions, which providers are licensed: YG-041, YG-044, YG-046, YG-047, YG-224), today is the latest-dated list at each place. A later decision that names only the parties it adds replaces the earlier list as today.
- **Precision ties.** A row dated only by year ties with every row of that year, so two sources that state different things in the same year are disputed: in YG-508, YQ-D212 (2026) and YQ-E804 (February 2026) at Yemen.
- **One source, several statements.** Some sources give several different statements for one place and date, in one condition: YQ-E166, YQ-E214 and YQ-E223 (Yemen, March 2022, YG-511); YQ-E706 and YQ-E707 (Yemen, 14 September 2023, YG-551). Unless step 2b labels them alike, the build will show them disputed.
- **Rule and practice.** Step 2b puts a rule at its authority's area and what a source reports at the area it names, so one axis can show a rule and a report about the same ground as two parts, as with Starlink (YG-559): banned in the Sana'a side's rules and sold in the government's areas.
- **Rows that state two things** sit in one group, by their main statement; pass 2 adds no row, so their second statement has no place of its own on its axis.

### Problems with the brief

- Fixed: rule 5's chains, which the checker first refused (a grouping only to a place grouped with itself, on one source), and translated seats, which it first counted as a change of seat. Both are now accepted, and the groupings they allow are added above.
- Rule 4 asks for one service per party, but the rows name parties inconsistently (a brand, a bank, an operator: Cash and Tamkeen; Floosak, Yemen Kuwait Bank and Quality Connect; SabaCash, SabaFon and CAC Islamic Bank). `places.tsv` lists the services under the names the rows use, with the operator in the note; step 2b may need to add others, and step 2c groups them.

### How the fixes were made

- **Splits.** A split keeps the group's id for one part, and each other part takes a new id, from YG-562 to YG-728 (167 new groups). Under rule 2's new sub-bullets:
  - **lists that grow:** each decision, circular or designation that names only what it adds is its own condition, named by its source and what it adds (YG-024, YG-041, YG-044, YG-046, YG-047, YG-224, YG-402);
  - **instruments in force together:** each law, regulation, circular or instruction is its own condition, unless one states the other's rule at more detail (YG-080, YG-191, YG-194, YG-202, YG-225, YG-236, YG-371, YG-408, YG-414, YG-415, YG-422, YG-446);
  - **parts of one source:** a party's pages that state different facts on one date are split where each row states one of them (YG-166).
- **Labels.** Rows that state one thing at different detail take the label of what both state, and the rest goes in `note`. Where a party's pages, or one source's rows, each list their own items, lists that hold together, the rows take one label and each list stays in `note` (for example YG-030, YG-163, YG-212, YG-243, YG-280, YG-331).
- **Places.** A row's place was changed only where step 2b's place rule allows it: to an authority's area for a rule that authority sets, or to a named party's service. The edits are in `pass2/states-<k>.tsv`, and each row's `group_id` equals its group in `groups.tsv`.
- **One group per row.** Where a suggested split would put one row in two conditions, because the row names several problems, requirements or facts, the row stays with its main statement and the rest goes in its `note`. Each case is given under its group.
