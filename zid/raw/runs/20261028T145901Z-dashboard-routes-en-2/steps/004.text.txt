Tips on syntax
User-agent: Defines which bot the rule applies to (e.g., * for all bots, Googlebot for Google).
Disallow: Tells the bot not to crawl a specific page or directory.
Allow: Overrides a Disallow rule, granting access to a specific page or path.
Sitemap: Provides the location of your XML sitemap.
#: Lines starting with # are ignored by bots.
