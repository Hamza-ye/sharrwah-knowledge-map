Choosing a checkout configuration that matches your business needs can help enhance the shopper experience and improve conversion rates. Depending on your storefront setup, there are several different checkout configurations options available to you.

Use this article to guide you and your development team in selecting the best configuration for your store’s checkout environment.

 
Checkout Comparison

This section compares the various checkout configurations available for storefronts, and highlights the benefits and potential limits associated with each checkout option.

We recommend determining which type of storefront you will be using (Stencil versus headless or composable), before reviewing the available checkout configurations so you can select the one that best matches your store’s technical requirements.

FAQ

Do I need a developer?

A developer is highly recommended if you want to explore custom checkouts or headless storefronts. You can find a list of BigCommerce certified developers in our Partner Directory.

Are the different checkout configurations limited to certain plans?

No, the checkout configurations listed above are available to all store plans.

Why does the Redirected and Embedded checkout URL need to be a subdomain of the storefront root domain?

Shoppers will encounter security errors and the checkout page will not load properly if the headless storefront domain doesn’t match the BigCommerce storefront domain.

You can configure your store’s checkout domain in Channel Manager or by upserting the URL via API.

What checkout configurations are available for stores using Multi-Storefront?

Stores with more than one storefront can use any of the checkout configurations listed above. In addition, several checkout settings can be configured uniquely per storefront.

Can I control the www redirect for my headless channel's unique checkout URL?

Yes, you can manage the setting via the Storefront SEO API endpoint.
