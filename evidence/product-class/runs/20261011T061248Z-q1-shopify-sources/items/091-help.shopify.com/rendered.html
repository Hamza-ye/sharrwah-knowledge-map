If Basic Tax isn&#39;t available for a region, then you can use <a href="/en/manual/taxes/shopify-tax">Shopify Tax</a> or <a href="/en/manual/taxes/manual-tax-settings">manual tax settings</a>. ## About Basic Tax

The Basic Tax service is available for certain regions only. This guide applies to you if your store is based in one of the following regions:

* Norway
* Switzerland
* Australia
* New Zealand
* Singapore

The Basic Tax service requires you to input a tax registration number to calculate your taxes. You can get this number from your local tax authority.

## Limitations of Basic Tax {#limitations}

The following features are not available in Basic Tax:

* [Automatic rate calculations using product categories](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#product-category-rates)
* [Automatic recommendations for product category](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#product-category-recommendations)
* [Rooftop accuracy](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#rooftop-accuracy)
* [Support for state fees](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#state-fees)
* [Tax holidays](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#tax-holidays)
* [Tax liability insights](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#liability-insights)
* [Advanced reports](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#advanced-reports)
* [Collecting tax in states with no state sales tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service#states-no-tax)

If you require any of these features, then [review the table of tax services](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/choose-tax-service) to determine which tax service is best for your needs.

## Before setting up taxes {#before-basic}

If you're new to Shopify, then you need to determine whether you need to charge sales tax before you start selling. You might need to register with multiple tax agencies. After that, you can set up your taxes in Shopify to help ensure that you charge the correct rates.

Before you add tax registrations on **Settings** > [**Taxes and duties**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/taxes) in your Shopify admin, review the following considerations:

* You need to determine whether to charge sales tax. If you're not sure, then consult with tax authorities or a tax professional. For a summary of state tax rules in the EU or UK, refer to the [EU tax reference](/manual/taxes/eu).
* If you need to charge sales tax in one or more regions or jurisdictions, then you need to contact each of the relevant agencies and register with them. The process varies depending on where your business is based and where you sell, and on the individual government requirements. For links to various tax agencies, refer to the [EU tax reference](/manual/taxes/eu).
* If your inventory is fulfilled from a location that's different from the address that you set in your Shopify admin at **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general), then you need to [create a location](/manual/inventory-and-locations/locations/setup) for your business to ensure that your taxes rates are correct. If the address that you set in **Settings** > [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) > **Store contact details** section of your Shopify admin is different from the address that you set as a [location](/manual/inventory-and-locations/locations/setup), then the location address is used to determine _nexus_, which is a physical or economic connection to a region. The **Store contact details** address isn't used if you have created a location.

## Managing Your Taxes

After you set up your tax regions, you can [manage your tax settings](/manual/taxes/registration/manage) including your registrations, tax overrides and exemptions, and tax calculations.

You can [create tax overrides](/manual/taxes/us/us-tax-manage#override-taxes) for products where the default tax rates don't apply, or shipping rates for specific regions.
