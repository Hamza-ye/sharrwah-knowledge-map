🚨 Critical Update: New Shipping Pricing Structure
December 25, 2024

 


As part of Zid’s commitment to enhancing our partners' experience and supporting their growth within our modern retail ecosystem, we are implementing a new pricing structure effective January 1, 2025. These updates are designed to create more uniformity and provide added incentives for our valued partners.
​

New Pricing Structure:

Unified Transactional Fees:

Shipping Apps: 0.70 SAR per transaction

Fulfillment Apps: 0.50 SAR per transaction

Fulfillment & Shipping Apps integrated with ZidShip: 0.30 SAR per transaction ( Fulfillment Apps to be communicated by Apr 2025)

Incentives:

An exclusive Affiliate programme for partners on 30% cashback ( T&C apply).

 

 

General Terms & Conditions:

The new price structure will be effective for those couriers signing the new agreement

The new price structure will be effective and forceable on all apps by April 1st, 2025.

Apps with no response by June 1, 2025, will be subject to deactivation.

ZID invoices will be released monthly, and based on order/transaction issuance.

Settlement of any outstanding dues is required prior to implementing the new terms and signing the updated contract.

Payment delays and/or dues may result in app deactivation.

To take advantage of these new terms, please reach out to us at [logistics@zid.sa] to receive the amended contract for your review and signature.
We look forward to continuing our partnership and supporting your success with Zid.
​

Warm regards,
Zid Logistics Team
