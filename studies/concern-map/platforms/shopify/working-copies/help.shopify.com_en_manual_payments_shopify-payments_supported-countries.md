# Supported countries for Shopify Payments

You can use Shopify Payments to accept payments at checkout if your business is located in one of the supported countries listed on this page. Each country page provides information about bank account and business verification requirements, accepted payment methods, and payout details.

Before you activate Shopify Payments, ensure that your business type is eligible and that you have the required documentation for your country. You might need to provide personal identification, proof of address, and business registration documents as part of the verification process.

If your country isn't listed, or if Shopify Payments doesn't support your business category, then you need to use a [third-party payment provider](/manual/payments/third-party-providers). Refer to the Shopify Payments [Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/legal) to determine whether your business category is supported.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>If you operate a retail store, then refer to the <a href="/en/manual/sell-in-person/getting-started/setup-payment-method/shopify-payments-pos#supported">list of countries that support Shopify Payments for in-person selling</a>.</p>
</aside>
