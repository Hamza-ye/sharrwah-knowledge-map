Jaib is an electronic wallet that works with innovative and intelligent technologies that facilitate various financial transactions through an application that is downloaded to your phone to save your money and use it for electronic payments for purchases, to recharge your balance, to make financial transfers, and any other financial transactions. Therefore, Jaib is an electronic cash service, an alternative to cash and the problems of currency damage and other problems that individuals suffer from in their financial transactions. Jaib provides services that meet all your basic and entertainment needs, most notably: Financial transfer service: Transfer your money anytime and anywhere through Jaib wallet. For more information: Jaib allows you to send financial transfers to your friends who are subscribed to the wallet from your place with the push of a button, and it also allows you to transfer money between your accounts in different currencies within the wallet. Transfer to a subscriber: With this service, you can transfer your financial transfers from your account in Jaib to the account of another person who has an account in Jaib wallet. Transfer between my accounts: With this service, you can transfer your money in different currencies between your different accounts in Jaib wallet. Bill payment: With the convenience and security of Jaib wallet, you get rid of waiting lines and pay for your various services Jaib wallet allows you to pay for different services easily, securely, and comfortably from your home and at any time, such as paying for internet and fixed phone bills and other services. Recharge your balance: Jaib provides a service to recharge your mobile balance or that of one of your acquaintances, whether for the package system, prepaid, or billing system for all local telecommunications networks. Purchase payment service: Easily, securely, and as an alternative to cash, pay for your many purchases through Jaib wallet. For more information: Anyone who is subscribed to Jaib wallet can replace cash payment with electronic payment through Jaib wallet for all their purchases from commercial, service, restaurant, and cafe shops easily and comfortably, by scanning the QR code or entering the point of sale number. Cash withdrawal service: Withdraw your cash from Jaib agents and service points located everywhere. For more information: It allows you to withdraw your cash through Jaib agents located in different provinces after feeding your account through Jaib agents as well or service points or withdrawing your financial transfers from different networks to your account in Jaib wallet. Entertainment services: Jaib wallet offers a range of entertainment services that make it easy for users to interact with games and various entertainment platforms. Other: Another list on the Jaib application interface enables you to know the latest operations you have performed and a statement of your financial transactions in the wallet. * Download the app now * Get rid of the hassle of cash payment, and facilitate your various financial transactions electronically with complete security.
