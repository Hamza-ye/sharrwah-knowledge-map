# Viewing your financial information in the Shopify admin

Shopify Finance is a suite of products that can help you manage and grow your business and includes [Shopify Balance](/manual/finance/shopify-balance/index), [Shopify Credit](/manual/finance/shopify-credit/index), [Shopify Capital](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/index), [Shopify Bill Pay](/manual/finance/shopify-bill-pay/index), and [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax). The finance suite is built into your Shopify admin, and is connected to your sales performance to streamline your business and financial operations.

To manage and grow your business you need to understand your store's finances. You can review your earnings, spending, and any repayments you owe in one location from the **Finance** page of your Shopify admin and the Shopify app.
