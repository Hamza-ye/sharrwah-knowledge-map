Instant payouts with WooPayments

WooPayments provides a way for eligible accounts to request an instant payout of some or all of the account balance. After requesting an instant payout, the money will arrive within minutes instead of days. Instant payouts can be initiated at any time.

Instant payouts are subject to additional fees. Review our fees.

NOTE: Participation in optional WooPayments features, such as instant payouts, is at the sole discretion of the WooCommerce team. Your account’s eligibility may differ from the below requirements.

Eligibility requirements

To be eligible to request an instant payout, your account must meet the following criteria:

Based in the U.S.
More than 90 days old.
Processed more than $1000 during the last 90 days.
Under 1% of transactions disputed in the last 90 days.
Use either of the following to receive payouts:
A verified bank account, or
A debit card

When you first meet the eligibility requirements for instant payouts, you will receive an email notification from us about it.

NOTE: The requirements above are evaluated on an ongoing basis. Thus, it’s possible to become eligible, but then later become ineligible. If this applies to you, we’ll show a notice about it in your dashboard. To remain eligible, do your best to continuously meet the requirements stated above.

Request an instant payout

To request an instant payout:

In your store’s WP Admin dashboard, go to Payments > Overview.
Click the Get [amount] now button in the top section.
View details about your instant payout, such as:
The amount you have available for instant payout.
What the net payout amount will be after instant payout fees are deducted.
Click Pay out now to initiate the instant payout.

If the Pay out available funds button is visible in Payments > Overview but appears disabled, then the balance may not be within the payout limits explained below.

NOTE: Instant payouts are defined by the payout amount and are not linked to specific transactions. As a result, transaction details for instant payouts are unavailable.

Payout limits

Unlike scheduled payouts, there is both a minimum and maximum amount that can be processed through an instant payout.

The minimum instant payout amount is $50 USD.
The maximum instant payout amount can be up to $1,000 USD.

If the minimum instant payout amount has not been reached, or if the maximum limit has been exceeded, the Payout available funds button will be disabled.

NOTE: Only one instant payout can be requested every 24 hours.

Instant payout accounts

Instant payouts can be made to a verified bank account or a debit card. This section focuses on how to add these payout methods to your WooPayments account.

NOTE: If multiple accounts are in use for payouts, instant payouts will only be paid out to the default account.

Add a verified bank account

Merchants who are otherwise eligible can add a verified bank account to receive instant payouts. Bank account verification uses a combination of automated and manual processes to confirm that the bank account is tied to the individual or organization that owns the WooPayments account.

To add a verified bank account, follow these instructions. When you go to enter your bank account information, you must use the direct bank connection feature — entering your account details manually will not verify your bank account.

If verification of your bank account cannot be completed, you may be asked to provide one of the following documents to help verify it:

A bank account statement
A bank letterhead
A voided check

Once this information is provided, it will be manually reviewed by our partners at Stripe to ensure that the bank details match those on the WooPayments account. This process may take several days.

Add a debit card

To add a debit card, follow these instructions to change your payout method, being sure to use a debit card instead of a bank account.

Bank support of instant payouts

Nearly 6,000 banks in the US support instant payouts. You can find information about bank support for instant payouts below.
