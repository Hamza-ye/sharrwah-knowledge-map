What is Stripe Payments on BigCommerce?

Modernize your BigCommerce checkout with Stripe's Optimized Checkout Suite, adding 30+ local and alternative payment methods, ACH vaulting and Adaptive Pricing. Shoppers see the price in their local currency across 150+ countries and businesses see cross-border revenue grow by an average of 17.8%.

Stripe OCS Payment

Modernize your BigCommerce checkout with Stripe OCS Payment Element, adding 30+ local and alternative payment methods, ACH vaulting, and multi-currency support. Get dynamic payment-method optimization without redesigning your checkout. See an 11.9% revenue uplift on average using Stripe’s latest optimizations.

Link

Integrate Link, Stripe’s saved payment wallet and top-of-checkout button that enables one-tap repurchases, supports guest and logged-in flows, and Instant Bank Payments to speed checkout up to 3x faster than non-Link customers while driving repeat conversions.

Stripe Radar

Take the guesswork out of fraud prevention with Stripe Radar's machine-learning signals and rule controls. While global fraud increased 15%, Radar users saw fraud decline by 17%.

Authorization Boost

Recover revenue lost to false declines with Authorization Boost, Stripe's AI retries accidentally declined payments in real time and automatically matches each authorization request to increase approval rates. On average, businesses recover 1 in 5 false declines that would otherwise have walked away.
