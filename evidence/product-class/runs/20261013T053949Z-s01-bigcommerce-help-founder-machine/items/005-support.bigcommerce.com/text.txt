Braintree is a PayPal payment solution that lets your customers pay for their orders with their debit/credit card or PayPal account.

Braintree offers several features to optimize checkout conversion and grow your business:

local payment methods and PayPal Pay Later to expand your global customer base and encourage larger purchases
Fastlane by PayPal provides guest shoppers a fast, passwordless checkout solution
stored credit cards and PayPal accounts
real-time Account Updater to automatically refresh stored card information when it expires
personalized transaction descriptors to improve payment recognition and customer trust
risk management tools to prevent fraud and minimize chargebacks
Level 2 and 3 data collection for commercial transactions
 
Requirements
Your store’s checkout type must be Optimized One-Page Checkout or Custom Checkout.
Your store must be located in a supported country.
Your store must have a supported currency as an available transactional currency.
You must have a Braintree account or a PayPal business account.
To set up and manage payment methods, the Manage Payments and Manage Settings permissions must be enabled on your user account.
 
Getting Your Braintree Account Credentials

Prior to connecting Braintree in the BigCommerce control panel, you will need to contact the Braintree Sales team to create a Braintree account. If you're signing up with your personal PayPal account, you will need to upgrade it to a business account first. However, we recommend using a different email address to keep your business account separate from your personal PayPal account.

Once created, log in to your Braintree account. Click on the gear icon in the upper right and select API from the drop-down menu. 

Under the API Keys section, copy the Public Key and Private Key as they will be needed for enabling Braintree in BigCommerce. Note that you will need to click View to access your private key. If no API keys appear, click Generate New API Key.

Next, click the Business tab in the upper left.

Copy your Merchant ID, as well as the Merchant Account ID for the currency you will be using with Braintree.

For more information on API keys, the differences between the merchant ID and merchant account ID, and where to find them in your Braintree control panel, see Braintree’s Important Gateway Credentials.

 
Setting up Braintree

To connect Braintree with your store, go to Settings › Payments, and select a supported currency from the drop-down list. Under the Embedded tab, expand Online Payment Methods and click Set up next to Braintree.

Enter the credentials copied from your Braintree account into their respective Merchant ID, Public Key, Private Key, and Merchant account ID fields. If you are setting up Braintree for multiple currencies, ensure that your Merchant account ID is from the correct currency configuration.

You must also enter your Public Key and Private Key in the Notifications endpoint fields.

Click Save to begin accepting payments.

Configuring Braintree with Multiple Currencies

If you want to offer Braintree’s payments across multiple currencies, you will need to create a new merchant account ID in your Braintree account. If you offer both credit cards and PayPal, you will need to ensure that your account is configured to accept the new currency for both payment methods.

After setting up merchant accounts IDs for each currency you want to offer, enable the currencies in your BigCommerce control panel. When you connect Braintree with an additional currency, use the merchant account ID associated with that currency in your Braintree account.

 

Using Multi-Storefront? You can request to opt into using Multi-Storefront Payment Methods to configure multiple payment configurations per provider, per currency. For more information, see Multi-Storefront Payment Methods.

PayPal Pay Later
Requirements
Pay Later button
Customizing Pay Later messaging

PayPal Pay Later lets customers buy now and pay over time. This feature means that the shopper will owe the money to PayPal while you, the merchant, always get paid. PayPay Pay Later also includes messaging banners that inform your customers of the installment options available at checkout. This can encourage larger purchases and increase your average order value (AOV).

Note that PayPal Pay Later is displayed as PayPal Pay in 4 for AU stores, PayPal Pay in 4X for FR stores, PayPal Pay in 3 for UK, IT, and ES stores, and PayPal Ratenzahlung for DE stores.

Note that some activities involving PayPal Pay Later in the United Kingdom are regulated. Before activating PayPal Pay Later buttons on your website, contact PayPal Business customer support or your PayPal Customer Success Manager for more information and to obtain the 4-digit code to unlock PayPal Pay Later for your store.
Your store must use a supported country and transactional currency combination:
CA — CAD
UK — GBP
US — USD
All other countries — AUD, CAD, CHF, CZK, DKK, EUR, GBP, HKD, HUF, ILS, JPY, MXN, NOK, NZD, PHP, PLN, RUB, SEK, SGD, THB, TWD, or USD
You must authorize your storefront to enable and customize Pay Later messaging.

See PayPal’s documentation for each country and product’s order value range.
PAY LATER BUTTON

When Enable Pay Later is checked, a PayPal Pay Later button will display on product, cart, and checkout pages allowing your shoppers to quickly pay using Pay Later. While the dedicated button offers a convenient option, shoppers can also select PayPal Pay Later at checkout.

If you are using Cornerstone 6.13.0 or newer, you can adjust the color, shape, and text of the PayPal Pay Later button on storefront product pages, cart page, the top of the checkout page, and the payment step of checkout. These settings can be found in Page Builder under Theme Styles › Payment buttons.

If you are using a different theme, check your theme’s documentation to see if it supports payment button customization. 

CUSTOMIZING PAY LATER MESSAGING

In the preview pane, view changes to your banners in real-time for desktops and mobile devices, and in dark mode.

Note that the banner location in the preview pane provides a demonstration of how the banner may look on a specific page. The actual location will vary, depending on your theme.

 

Not seeing the PayPal Pay Later messaging settings? If you do not meet all of the eligibility requirements, the messaging settings will not be displayed. Instead, you will see a list of requirements that still need to be met on your store before this feature will be available for use.

 
Setting up Webhooks

Account Updater, ACH Direct Debit, Alternative Payment Methods, and Braintree fraud rules require webhooks to ensure proper functionality.

 

Check user permissions in Braintree. To set up webhooks, you must have the Manage Webhooks (Add/Edit/Delete) permission enabled in your Braintree user account.

To create a webhook for your Braintree configuration, go to Settings › Payments in the BigCommerce control panel and click the Braintree tab. Scroll down to the Notifications endpoint section and copy the Notification URL.

 

Note: If you are setting up Braintree for multiple currencies or using Multi-Storefront Payment Methods, each configuration will have a unique Notification URL.

Next, log into your Braintree Control Panel and click the gear icon in the upper right. Select API from the drop-down menu, then click the Webhooks tab in the upper left.

Click Create New Webhook, then on the next page paste the copied Notification URL in the Destination URL field. Under Notifications, check the boxes next to the following events*:

For Account Updater:
Account Updater Daily Report (optional)
For ACH Direct Debit:
Transaction Settlement Declined
Transaction Settled
For APMs:
Local Payment Completed
Local Payment Reversed
Local Payment Funded
Local Payment Expired
For fraud rules:
Transaction Reviewed

*Some events are only available after the associated feature has been enabled or added to your Braintree account. See each feature’s setup details for more information.

When done, click Create Webhook. Next, click the Keys tab and copy your Public Key and Private Key.

Return to the BigCommerce control panel Braintree tab. Scroll down to the Notifications endpoint section and paste the Public Key in the Notification public key field and the Private Key in the Notification private key field. 

Save your changes.

 
Braintree Account Updater

Account Updater is an optional paid service offered by Braintree that automatically updates stored card information, such as new numbers, new expiration dates, and closed accounts.

This can be useful for stores that offer subscriptions, as it ensures that recurring transactions won't fail due to outdated stored card information and customers won't need to contact the store to update their payment details.

To enable Account Updater:

you must contact Braintree support.
you must have Allow logged-in customers to store their credit/debit cards checked in your Braintree settings.
Your store must be on a Growth plan or higher.
you can optionally set up a webhook with the Account Updater Daily Report event.
 
Dynamic Descriptors
Enabling dynamic descriptors
BigCommerce settings

Dynamic descriptors are an abbreviated business identifier used to explain charges or payments on a customer’s bank statement. Having personalized descriptors can improve recognition of transactions with your business and reduce the likelihood of chargebacks.

A customer's bank will determine exactly how your business's descriptors appear on customer statements, but may appear like this:

BCSTORE*FINECOLLCTBLES 555-123-1234 BCSTORE.COM $100.00

ENABLING DYNAMIC DESCRIPTORS

Contact Braintree to enable dynamic descriptors on your Braintree account. Braintree will also provide you with your processor's dynamic descriptor requirements, such as the maximum character length or supported character types.

Once your Braintree account supports dynamic descriptors, contact our support team to activate the descriptor fields in your BigCommerce control panel.

 

Be careful! Transactions will fail if you enter descriptors in the BigCommerce control panel without Dynamic Descriptors enabled in your Braintree account, or if your descriptor does not meet the processor's character requirements or limitations.

BIGCOMMERCE SETTINGS

Once our support team has enabled Dynamic Descriptors, go to Settings › Payments and click on the Braintree tab. Scroll down to Dynamic descriptors and fill out the descriptor fields.

 

Your processor may have different descriptor requirements from the ones listed in Braintree settings. If you are unsure about your processor's requirements, contact Braintree.

AVS AND CVV RULES

Address Verification System (AVS) and Card Verification Value (CVV) rules confirm that the address information or CVV included with a transaction matches what the issuing bank has on file for the associated card, ensuring that only authorized card users are able to make purchases. Note that AVS and CVV rules only apply to credit card purchases.

To configure your AVS or CVV rules, log into your Braintree Control Panel. Click the gear icon in the upper right and select Fraud Management from the drop-down menu. On the next page under Basic, click Options next to AVS or CVV.

To learn more, see Braintree’s AVS and CVV Rules.

CARD VERIFICATION

Card verification ensures that the credit card number provided is associated with a valid, open account and can be stored in the Braintree Vault and charged successfully. Note that card verification only applies to credit and debit card payments.

When Card Verification is enabled, Braintree verifies credit cards by running either a $0 or $1 authorization and then automatically voiding it. For most processors and card brands, transactions are initially tried with a $0 authorization. If $0 authorizations are not supported, a $1 authorization will be performed automatically if the processor returns one of the following response codes:

2015 (Transaction Not Allowed)
2019 (Invalid Transaction)
2023 (Processor Does Not Support This Feature)
2027 (Set Up Error - Amount)
2048 (Invalid Amount)

An additional setting, Card Verification - Retry All Failed $0, allows you to retry all failed $0 authorizations as a $1 authorization, regardless of the response code returned by the processor.

You can enable both card verification settings in your Braintree Control Panel. Click the gear icon in the upper right and select Account Settings from the drop-down menu. On the next page, scroll down to Vaulting and click the toggle next to Card Verification and/or Card Verification - Retry All Failed $0.

To learn more, see Braintree’s Card Verification.

DUPLICATE TRANSACTION CHECKING

Duplicate transaction checking prevents transaction requests from accidentally processing more than once if a customer refreshes your checkout page or clicks your buy button multiple times.

Enabled by default, this feature rejects transactions if another successful transaction has been completed with the same payment method, order ID, and amount within the last 30 seconds.

You can change the timeframe or disable the feature in your Braintree Control Panel. Click the gear icon in the upper right and select Account Settings from the drop-down menu. On the next page, scroll down to Transactions and click the toggle next to Duplicate Transaction Checking to disable it, or click Options to update the timeframe. You can enter a value up to 3600 seconds (1 hour).

To learn more, see Braintree’s Duplicate Transaction Checking.

FRAUD PROTECTION ADVANCED

Braintree’s Fraud Protection Advanced is a premium fraud management tool that applies machine learning to evaluate card transactions and help protect your business from payment fraud. Note that additional fees apply.

Fraud Protection Advanced:

includes all basic Fraud Protection features.
offers customized risk filters with expanded variables.
provides block, review, and allow list management.
offers optimized manual review investigations and robust reporting.

To apply for Fraud Protection Advanced, log into your Braintree Control Panel. Click the gear icon in the upper right and select Fraud Management from the drop-down menu. On the next page, scroll down to Premium and click Apply next to Fraud Protection Advanced.

To learn more, see Braintree’s Fraud Protection Advanced.

RISK THRESHOLD RULES

Risk threshold rules, also known as velocity checks, are designed to detect and prevent carding attacks. They are triggered when certain customer information is passed through Braintree multiple times within a designated time period.

Rules can be configured to either send you a notification email or automatically reject verifications or transactions. Note that risk threshold rules only apply to credit card and certain Google Pay transactions.

To manage your risk threshold rules, log into your Braintree Control Panel. Click the gear icon in the upper right and select Fraud Management from the drop-down menu. On the next page under Basic, click Options next to Risk Thresholds.

To learn more, see Braintree’s Risk Threshold Rules.

ADDITIONAL FEATURES

Is delayed or manual capture supported?

Yes. When the Authorize only transaction type is selected, manual capture of payments is supported.

Are recurring or subscription payments supported?

Braintree supports recurring payments with stored payment methods via subscription billing apps or a custom development using the Payments API. Additionally, you can enable PayPal Pay Later to give your shoppers the ability to pay over time.

Is multicurrency supported?

Yes. Braintree supports the use of multiple currencies. For more information, see Configuring Braintree with Multiple Currencies.

Can I have more than one store associated with my Braintree account?

No, each store needs to be associated with its own Braintree account.

Can I use multiple Braintree merchant account IDs with BigCommerce?

Yes, you can use multiple merchant account IDs to configure Braintree across multiple currencies.

If you are using Multi-Storefront, you can also configure a single Braintree account for multiple channels per currency. There is no limit to the amount of Braintree configurations that can be created per currency.

Each Braintree configuration has its own unique notification URL that is used for enabling ACH Direct Debit and Account Updater.

Yes, you can control how the PayPal, PayPal Pay Later, and Venmo buttons appear using Page Builder if your store meets the following requirements:

Braintree must be enabled in your store.
Your storefront must be using a compatible theme (like Cornerstone).
Your store must be using Optimized One-Page Checkout.

What are "Smart Buttons"?

When PayPal is enabled, the shopper is automatically shown additional payment methods that are best suited to them, based on the device they are using and the geographical region they are in. For example, shoppers using a mobile device with Venmo set up and with USD as their selected currency will see the option to pay with Venmo, and shoppers in supported countries are shown the option for PayPal Pay Later.

How do I add Smart Buttons to my product pages?

If you are using a theme based on Cornerstone 6.6.1 or newer, you can enable payment buttons for PayPal and Venmo on the product page by checking the box next to Show quick payment buttons in the Products tab under Display settings.

If you are using a different theme, or you have customized your Cornerstone theme files, your theme will require additional customization in order to support product page payment buttons.

Can I place Smart Buttons at the top of my checkout page?

You can place Smart Buttons at the top of checkout by checking the box next to Show wallet buttons at the top of checkout in your Optimized-One Page Checkout settings.

What is the customer experience with PayPal’s package tracking service?

Customers who use the PayPal app can track packages in the app and receive push notifications with live shipping updates. This will be provided automatically for customers who shop at your store and pay with PayPal. Customers can also track packages manually by entering a tracking number.

Can I turn off package tracking and still sync order details, to retain benefits like shorter hold times and seller protection eligibility?

Yes. Package tracking is a separate, optional feature that can be turned toggled off and on without disabling post order syncing.

Can I display PayPal Pay Later messaging banners on my storefront without enabling the Pay Later button?

Yes. The Pay Later button and Pay Later messaging are separate features that operate independently of each other. While the button provides a quick and easy way to access Pay Later, shoppers can still choose the option at checkout.

TRANSACTIONS

How long until a transaction's funds are transferred to my bank?

Funds should appear in your bank account 2-5 days after the transaction has settled. For more details on funding and settlement timelines, see Braintree's Get Paid.

Will I or my customers receive an additional email or invoice from Braintree?

Braintree allows you to send additional email notifications, but this functionality is disabled by default as BigCommerce sends an order confirmation email to customers automatically. To enable email receipts, log into your Braintree Control Panel, click the gear icon in the upper right, and select Account Settings from the drop-down menu. On the next page, scroll down to Transactions and follow the instructions under Email Receipts. 

For more details on BigCommerce email notifications, see Customer Order Notifications.

REFUNDS

How soon after a transaction can I perform a refund?

As soon as the transaction has settled, usually within 24 hours, you can issue a refund. For instructions on how to issue a refund in your control panel, see Processing Refunds.

Is there an amount of time after which I cannot perform a refund?

There is no time limit for credit card transactions. For PayPal transactions, PayPal requires refunds to be issued within 60 days, but this can be changed by contacting PayPal.

PAYMENT DISPUTES

Depending on the payment method, disputes are managed in either the Braintree Control Panel or PayPal Resolution Center. For more information, see Braintree’s Chargebacks and Retrievals.

TROUBLESHOOTING AND SUPPORT

Why did my customer receive an error when trying to pay?

If a customer receives an error at checkout, the BigCommerce store logs may contain additional information. 

Success and error messages from Braintree have a different Module name based on the payment method used at checkout:

Card payments — “Credit Card”
PayPal Checkout — “Braintree (PayPal)”
Digital wallets, ACH, and APMs — “Braintree (Payment Method Name)”

If an error code is present, you can look it up in the list of Authorization Decline Codes to see why the error occurred. If you need additional assistance, contact our support team.

Why is Venmo not available in my store after enabling it in Braintree Settings?

If you have connected Braintree using a partner merchant account, Venmo will be unavailable on your storefront. You can confirm this in your Braintree Control Panel by clicking the gear icon, selecting Account Settings from the drop-down list, and scrolling down to Venmo under Payment Methods.

For more information about changing your merchant account to support Venmo, contact Braintree support.

Why is my customer’s order missing after they completed a transaction with an alternative payment method?

This can occur if you have not set up a webhook for local payment notifications in your merchant account. See Enabling Alternative Payment Methods for instructions on configuring the proper webhook to prevent order syncing errors.

Why are the PayPal Pay Later messaging settings not appearing in the control panel?

If you are using a theme that does not support PayPal Pay Later messaging banners, such as an older Stencil theme, Blueprint theme, or headless storefront, your payment gateway’s settings will not display the messaging settings.

Why aren’t my guest customers able to see the PayPal Checkout, PayPal Pay Later button, and Pay Later messaging on the cart page?

If the PayPal Checkout, Pay Later button, and messaging are missing for guest customers only, it could be due to your store’s customer settings in Settings › Checkout. Guest customers will not be able to access PayPal from the cart if either Customers must have or create an account to check out or Require existing accounts to sign in is enabled. See Optimized One-Page Checkout for more information.

What does the "provider_configuration_error, message: 2026 Invalid Merchant ID" error message mean?

This error indicates that the customer’s bank declined the transaction. Customers should be able to complete checkout using a different card. In some cases, the specific card type may not be enabled for your Braintree account.

For more information regarding this error, contact Braintree support.

How do I contact Braintree’s support team?

For assistance, reach out to Braintree support on their Get Help page.
