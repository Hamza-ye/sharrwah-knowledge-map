# Pass B: Sana'a-zone payment operators, source search

Search date: 2026-10-10. Companion file: `B_operators.tsv` (99 rows; 90 reachable from this container, 9 not reachable).

Column notes:
- `source_year` is the publication year shown on the page. For app-store listings it is the year of the listing's "last updated" date. Operator pages whose only date is a copyright footer are marked "undated", and their `date_on_page` shows the footer.
- Rows dated before 2023 are labelled HISTORICAL in `description`.
- No phone numbers, account numbers, wallet numbers or individuals' names were recorded. Business names that contain a family name were kept only where they are the registered business name.

## What I looked for but could not find
- A 2023–2026 list of wallets or e-money providers licensed by the Sana'a central bank. Its site (`centralbank.gov.ye`) resets connections from here and its newest Wayback capture is Dec 2022. The only lists found are HISTORICAL: the Sana'a statement quoted by Aden on 26 Feb 2020 (`cby-ye.com/news/1`) and the Dec 2019 Sana'a decision naming wallet agents. The 2024 ymp.ye directory lists 11 wallets but is not a licence list.
- A Sana'a central bank notice after June 2025 that lifts, confirms or changes the order on Al-Kuraimi.
- Dated evidence after June 2025 that exchange houses or Kuraimi Express agents in Sana'a deal with Al-Kuraimi again. The only post-order item is the Hakikah fact-check of 22 Jun 2025, which says dealings with exchangers and Express agents were stopped.
- Kuraimi posts that name Sana'a for cash pickup. The posts say "all branches", and two 2026 mFloos posts give shop addresses "جوار فرع الكريمي" in Sana'a. A July 2026 Al-Araby article mentions a Kuraimi ATM withdrawal cap in a passage on Sana'a.
- Official Kuraimi E-Pay developer documentation or merchant terms. The already-cited GitHub README refers to a "Supplier Integration Guide and Technical Specification" but links no official copy.
- Published merchant fee schedules (merchant discount rates) for any wallet. Cash Pay's page states no fee. SabaCash's Aug 2026 offer mentions only a merchant commission. Kuraimi posts say buyer-side Hasib payments are free.
- Jawali's merchant QR terms. Its request form (`apply_qr.php`) sits behind a JavaScript cookie challenge, which was not bypassed.
- Jaib merchant or business pages. Every path under `e-jaib.com/pages/` returns the same template, including a nonexistent path.
- Floosak's own site (`floosak.com`, Cloudflare 522) and the Yemen Kuwait Bank pages (403).
- Courier or delivery-company terms for collecting cash on delivery on behalf of online sellers (remittance timing, fees, liability). Only consumer-facing "pay on delivery" statements were found (Tawseel One, Wagbat, Etlobni). Sellers name the transport offices they ship through (مكتب جوال، المقطري، المختض، مكتب النور للنقل), but none of these offices has a site.
- Yemen Post's "بريدي تحاصيل" collections page. post.ye fails TLS verification from here and returns 503 over HTTP.
- Operator sites for these names on the Aden ban list: الدولي موني، ريال موبايل، الريال الإلكتروني، المتكاملة. Also for two wallets named by a 2026 seller app: نجاز، مواصلات.
- The name and launch date of the Sana'a unified transfer network. The reports found date from 12 Feb 2025 and describe it as planned.
- Content of public Facebook, Instagram and TikTok business pages. These could not be read from here, so sellers' social pages were not counted.
- Escrow or trust-account products (PAY-65) at any wallet or bank serving the zone.
