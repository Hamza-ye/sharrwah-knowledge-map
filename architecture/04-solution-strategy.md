# 4. Solution strategy

**One platform for many markets.** What is built is the same in every market. A market sets values configured for it, and its facts are Context that tests the design rather than shaping it (`METHOD.md` section 3a; the founder's F-00, and G1's B1, B2 and B7).

**Money.** At launch, Sharrwah holds no sellers' money. Each payment method leaves it where its own arrangement puts it: with a provider in the seller's own account, with a courier until it settles, or with the seller (D-001, *proposed*, waiting on the founder's line). The words are defined in section 12. Payments sit behind one narrow boundary (D-002, *proposed*). An arrangement in which Sharrwah or a processor holds the money can be added behind that boundary later, and switched on in a market only by the founder.

**What is owed** is recorded apart from who holds the money. There are two ledgers, one per owner (D-003, *proposed*).
