# Third-party transaction fees on your Shopify bills

Third-party transaction fees cover the cost for Shopify to provide a secure checkout platform and integrate with external payment providers. Transaction fees vary depending on the [Shopify plan](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) that you choose, and are charged in the following circumstances:

* When you use a [third-party payment provider](/manual/payments/third-party-providers) to accept customer payments.
* When an order includes [store credit](/manual/customers/store-credit) or [gift cards](/manual/products/gift-card-products) as a payment method, on Shopify stores created on or after May 12, 2025. For example, if your store uses Shopify Payments and a customer pays for an order using both a credit card and $10 CAD of store credit, then you're charged third-party transaction fees only on the $10 CAD of store credit.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>For Shopify stores created on or after May 12, 2025, orders that include <a href="/en/manual/customers/store-credit">store credit</a> or <a href="/en/manual/products/gift-card-products">gift cards</a> as a payment method are charged third-party transaction fees on the amount paid for using store credit or gift cards. Store credit and gift card transaction fees don&#39;t apply to Shopify stores created before May 12, 2025.</p>
</aside>

When you use [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments), you aren't charged third-party transaction fees for orders that are processed using the following payment methods:

* Shopify Payments
* Shop Pay
* Shop Pay Installments
* Manual payment methods such as cash, cash on delivery (COD), and bank transfers.

If you elect to use Shopify Payments as your primary payment provider, then PayPal transactions are exempt from third-party transaction fees.


How your account is billed for third-party transaction fees depends on your store's transaction volume, any recurring app charges, and the payment provider that you use.

Transaction fees don't apply for POS orders or for [manual payment methods](/manual/payments/manual-payments), which include cash on delivery (COD), bank deposits, checks, test orders, and draft orders marked as paid or pending. Third-party transaction fees aren't returned to you when you issue an order refund.

Transaction fees are in addition to processing fees charged by your third-party payment provider.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>The <em>Advanced Cash on Delivery</em> app is no longer available from the Shopify App Store. You can use the standard Cash on Delivery option in your <a href="/en/manual/payments/manual-payments#deactivate-a-manual-payment-method">payment settings</a>.</p>
</aside>

## Calculate your third-party transaction fees

Shopify uses this formula to calculate third-party transaction fees for a 30-day period:

```html

[(cost of products - discounts) + tax + shipping charges] x rate

```

The rate for third-party transaction charges varies depending on your [pricing plan](localized-pricing-page).

## View a breakdown of your third-party transaction fees

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > [**Billing**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/billing).

2. Click the bill that you want to view in the **Past bills** section.
3. If a bill includes transaction fees, then the **Transaction fees** section displays the total fees for orders within specific date ranges.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>To view third-party transaction fees for stores on the Plus plan, in your Shopify admin, go to the <a href="https://admin.shopify.com/settings/organization-billing"><strong>Billing</strong> page for your organization</a>, and then <a href="/en/manual/organization-settings/billing/view-bills#export-bill-history">export the bill</a> that you want to review.</p>
</aside>
