The store owner is the primary user for your entire BigCommerce account. It is the first user account created when you start a new store and is granted all administrative permissions. Only the store owner can access the Account Dashboard to manage account information and pay invoices.

 
Requirements
To change the store owner user, you must be logged in as the current store owner.
 
Permissions Exclusive to the Store Owner

Only the store owner can:

access all areas of the control panel and can change all settings.
access your BigCommerce billing and account details.
access your support PIN.
change your store's subscription plan.
purchase new storefront seats.
purchase a domain or SSL certificate directly from BigCommerce.
purchase themes in the Themes Marketplace.
assign high-risk permissions to other users.
access a preview code to allow non-users to preview a store before it is launched.
 
Determining Which User is the Store Owner

Unless you've changed it, the store owner email address is the same address you used to create your trial store and BigCommerce account.

If you are not sure if you are the store owner, go to Settings › Users. The store owner's email address will be listed as owner under User Role.

If you don't see Settings or Users in the navigation, your user account has not been granted permission to access those areas and is not the store owner user role.

 
Changing the Store Owner User
 

Are you transferring ownership of your store? See the “Transferring Ownership” section of The Account Dashboard for additional considerations before following the instructions below.

If you are selling your store or changing permissions within your company, you may need to transfer the store owner role to a different user. Go to Settings › Users, click the Action menu (⋯) next to the user you want to assign as store owner, and select Edit. In the User Role drop-down, select Store Owner.

Confirm the ownership change by entering a verification code in the pop-up window. This code can be found in an email sent to the current store owner's email address. Check your inbox for an email from from accounts@bigcommerce.com with the subject line, You have received a new verification code.

If you do not see the verification email in your inbox, check your spam folder, or click Resend code to have another sent to you.

After entering the verification code, click Confirm and save your changes to complete the ownership transfer. Note that after three unsuccessful verification attempts, you will be logged out of the control panel and locked out of your account. If this happens, follow the steps in Unlocking Your Account to log back into the control panel.

To ensure that permissions are updated, both the previous owner and new owner should log out of the control panel by clicking the Profile icon, and selecting Log Out. Then, log back in to your user account again to view the control panel with your new user role.

 
FAQ

How do I reclaim store ownership after transferring to a developer or agency?

 

We do not recommend assigning store ownership to a third party. To give a developer or agency access to your store, create a user account for them and assign only the permissions they need to perform their tasks.

If you assigned an external developer or agency partner as your store’s owner for customization or setup assistance, have them follow the instructions in Changing the Store Owner User to reassign you as the store owner user.

If they are unresponsive, contact our support team to request a store owner transfer. You will be asked to verify ownership with the Primary Contact information in your Account Dashboard.
