## Problems with the brief and the scripts, for the coordinator before the other twelve batches

1. **The checker's `wrong time` drops engineering whenever another answerer has a fixed time.** `others = when_of(non-model answerers)` leaves engineering out. So for `model;engineering;founder` with engineering `before build` and `needed_by` `model` or `slice`, the checker demands `wrong time`, though the brief's rule (the earliest `when` of the answerers other than the model) gives `before build` and no disagreement. My batch didn't hit this, because STF-05's engineering part is `later`, but other batches will.
2. **The checker's `bundles` ignores engineering's time.** A `model;engineering` row whose engineering part is `later` has different answerers' times, which is a bundle by the brief. The checker neither requires nor rejects it there. I added `bundles` to ONB-01 and ONB-04 by hand. Batches will differ here unless engineering's time is a column, or the brief says to add it.
3. **"A row that names neither asks both" sends almost every row to engineering.** 13 of my 16 rows name neither. Engineering's `when` (architecture decision or build detail) is the agent's own call, and it drives `wrong time` and `bundles` while the checker can't check it. Batches will diverge on it.
4. **`slice` against engineering's `later`.** NEEDED reads `slice` as before build, while engineering's `later` for a build detail means "when the area comes up", which is about when its first slice comes. So ONB-01 and ONB-04 (and STF-05 in part) get a `wrong time` that is arguably an artefact of the mapping, not a real mismatch.
5. **Engineering rulings already in force have no place in the brief.** S-02's D1, D3, E3 and E4 are engineering's rulings with no veto (founder-decisions, 30 October). They partly answer ACC-01, ACC-04 and ONB-01. The brief only provides for decisions in force answering a whole row (`beyond the class`, `founder`). I cited them in `basis` (for example `S-02 S02-D1`) and left the rows `covered`.
6. **`class_slice.py key` needs the full key** (`SH-C-024/decides/SH-S-5200`). A bare platform statement id prints `NOT FOUND`, while `q3.py where` takes bare ids. Saying so in the brief would save a step.
7. **`class_slice.py model` prints each cell's terms and placement, but not the statements it cites or its readings.** The brief says it gives "cells and readings". To see what a cell holds, I had to run `q3.py where` on search results.
8. **A subject without a register.** The brief asks for "its id and its register row". S-07 and S-08 have no register yet, so their `basis` names the subject's `subjects.tsv` question or S-02's moved row (S02-M4).
9. **`q3.py slice --area X --from 1 --to 0`** printed every row of the area (a `--to` of 0 reads as unset). It's harmless, but it loads a large output.
10. **STF-02 is framed for two vocabularies** ("the two"), while the model holds five platforms. No time-bomb type fits this, so it is only noted under the row.
