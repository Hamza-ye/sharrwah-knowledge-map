WooCommerce Shipping & Tax
Important notes
In versions 3.0.2 and higher, the WooCommerce Shipping & Tax extension was renamed to “WooCommerce Tax” for new installs and “WooCommerce Tax (previously WooCommerce Shipping & Tax)” for existing installs. This is solely a visual change to the name, as the code remains the same in both cases.
WooCommerce Tax is designed to remove the work of building and updating custom tax rate tables by automating tax calculations for supported countries.
Legacy shipping label functionality is still available when this extension is used on its own but is limited to USPS and DHL Express. For additional carriers (UPS and FedEx) and features, we recommend using the new WooCommerce Shipping plugin for shipping labels instead.
The new WooCommerce Shipping extension and WooCommerce Tax are compatible and can be used together. If both extensions are active on your site simultaneously, WooCommerce Shipping will take over the shipping label functionality (disabling the legacy shipping label UI) and WooCommerce Tax will provide access to the “Automated Taxes” feature.
Requirements
WooCommerce: version 3.0.0+
WordPress: version 4.5+

WooCommerce Shipping & Tax helps get your store ready to sell as quickly as possible. You create your products — we take care of tax calculation, payment processing setup, and shipping label printing!

Collect accurate taxes at checkout

Opt for Automated Taxes — tax rates are set up automatically; no need to enter them manually.

Print shipping labels for USPS and DHL at a discount

Offer lower rates on shipping. Create ready-to-print shipping labels for USPS and DHL directly in WooCommerce and take advantage of our special discount. Labels cannot be generated for Canada Post at this time.

Note: You will also require an extension for USPS and/or DHL if you want to display live rates to customers in the cart and on checkout pages. This extension only facilitates the purchase and printing of shipping labels.

Installation
Install WooCommerce Shipping & Tax.
First, visit WordPress.org to download WooCommerce Shipping & Tax.
Next, in your store’s WP Admin dashboard, head to Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin > Choose File. Select the .zip file you downloaded from WordPress.org and click Install Now.
Alternatively, head to Plugins > Add New in your store’s WP Admin dashboard and search for WooCommerce Shipping & Tax. Click the Install Now and Activate buttons.
Connect your site to WordPress.com:
Follow the prompt to connect your WordPress.com account (this is the same account you use to log into WooCommerce.com).
Once you are directed back to your site, a Setup complete banner will be displayed.
