Aden Cash Wallet

The Aden Cash electronic wallet is a registered trademark provided by Aden Islamic Bank for Microfinance under the license of the Central Bank of Yemen. It is designed with high efficiency to keep pace with technological development and to achieve financial inclusion by enabling customers to use their funds and execute financial transactions via their mobile phones, benefiting from diverse services in a secure and convenient manner.
