# Shipping insurance for your shipping labels

If you ship orders from the United States and you buy shipping labels, then you can protect your orders by purchasing shipping insurance for up to $5,000 USD of insurance coverage. Shipping insurance protects your shipments against loss, theft, or damage.

<aside class="note note-information"><h4>Tip</h4><p>If you’re on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan and use <a href="/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments">Shopify Payments</a> and/or <a href="/en/manual/international/managed-markets">Managed Markets</a>, then Shopify automatically includes up to $200 USD of shipping insurance on each eligible shipment at no extra cost. </p>
<p>If you use <a href="/en/manual/international/managed-markets">Managed Markets</a> and your store is on the Basic plan, then insurance isn’t included, but you can <a href="/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-insurance#purchase-shipping-insurance">purchase shipping insurance</a> for your orders.</p>
</aside>

You can insure your shipment when [buying shipping labels](/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/buying-labels/buying-labels) and submit your claims directly from the Shopify admin. [Shipsurance](https://www.shipsurance.com/) is the insurance administrator for coverage that you buy through Shopify.

For information about filing shipping carrier claims for lost or damaged shipments, refer to [shipping carrier claims](/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/shipping-claims).

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Shipping insurance is only available in specific geographic regions. Check the requirements for your location.</p>
</aside>

## Eligibility for shipping insurance {#eligibility}

You need to meet the following requirements to be eligible for shipping insurance:

* Your shipment needs to be shipped from the United States.
* You need to buy a shipping label in [shipping labels](/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/).
* Your products need to comply with [Shipsurance’s Terms of Service](https://www.shipsurance.com/shopify/terms_t).

To receive up to $200 USD of included shipping insurance on each eligible label at no cost, you also need to meet the following requirements:

* Have [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments) activated on your store.
* To receive included shipping insurance on labels purchased in Shopify, your store needs to be on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan.
* To receive included shipping insurance on labels purchased through [Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets), your store needs to be on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan and Managed Markets must be activated.

## Included shipping insurance {#free-insurance}

If you have [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments) activated and your store is on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan, then up to $200 USD of shipping insurance is automatically included on each eligible label that you purchase in Shopify at no extra cost.

If you use [Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets) and your store is on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan, then labels that you purchase for Managed Markets orders also include up to $200 USD of shipping insurance at no extra cost. If you use [Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets) and your store is on the Basic plan, then insurance isn’t included, but you can [purchase shipping insurance](/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-insurance#purchase-shipping-insurance) for your orders.

Included shipping insurance doesn’t stack or combine across different services. Each eligible shipment is covered for up to $200 USD in total included insurance.

When you purchase a shipping label, the included shipping insurance displays in the **SHIPPING INSURANCE** section. If your order's value is over $200 USD, then you can purchase insurance for the full shipment value or add a custom amount.

### Confirm that a shipping label has included insurance {#confirm-label-insurance-inclusion}

1. From your Shopify admin, click [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).
2. Click the order that you want to purchase a shipping label for.
3. In the **Unfulfilled** section, click **Create shipping label**.
4. In the **SHIPPING INSURANCE** section, confirm that shipping insurance is included.
5. Optional: If you want to purchase additional insurance, then select **Insure full shipment value** or **Insure custom amount**. Learn more about [_Shipping insurance premium rates_](#insurance-premiums).

## Buying shipping insurance {#purchase-shipping-insurance}

If you're not eligible for the [included shipping insurance](#free-insurance), then you can enter the amount of shipping insurance coverage that you want to add to your shipment in **SHIPPING INSURANCE** section. Before you buy shipping insurance, review the following considerations:

* You can buy shipping insurance in increments of $100 USD. For example, if you want to insure your items valued at $250 USD total and you're using international shipping that cost $20.50 USD, then you can add insurance for $270.50 USD to your shipping label. The cost of the insurance premium is $1.29 USD x 3 (per $100 USD of coverage), for a total of $3.87 USD.
* Your shipping insurance and shipping label charges are listed separately on the [**Billing**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/billing) page in the Shopify admin.
* You can cancel shipping insurance by [voiding the unused shipping label](/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/managing-labels/voiding-labels) up to 30 days after the label purchase. After you cancel the insurance, you can't submit a claim anymore and you receive a shipping insurance premium credit. This credit can be used only for future shipping insurance purchases.
* **USPS First Class Mail** shipping service isn't eligible for insurance.
* Shipments sent using UPS services to destinations in Puerto Rico aren't eligible for insurance.

### Purchase shipping insurance {#purchase-insurance}

1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).


2. Click the order that you want to purchase a shipping label for.
3. In the **Unfulfilled** section, click **Create shipping label**.
4. In the **SHIPPING INSURANCE** section, enter the amount of insurance coverage.

## Submitting a shipping insurance claim {#file-insurance-claim}

You can submit a shipping insurance claim for lost, damaged, and stolen shipments directly in your Shopify admin. Before you submit a shipping insurance claim, review the following considerations:

* If shipping insurance was purchased or included for a shipping label that also has coverage provided by the carrier, then any claims for lost, damaged, or stolen shipments should be filed with Shipsurance and not the carrier.
* Additional shipping insurance either included or purchased separately for a shipping label does not stack or combine with coverage provided by the carrier.
* You can submit an insurance claim for most shipments 24 hours after an order has shipped and up to 120 days from the shipping date.

## Getting support for an open claim {#support-for-open-claim}

Shopify doesn't support insurance claims. If you purchased shipping insurance and you need help, then you need to contact Shipsurance support directly:

* by email at [shopifyclaims@shipsurance.com](shopifyclaims@shipsurance.com)
* by phone at 1-866-852-9956
