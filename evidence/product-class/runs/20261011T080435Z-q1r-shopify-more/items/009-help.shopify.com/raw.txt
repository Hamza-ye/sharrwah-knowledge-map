# Cash drawers for Shopify POS

You can accept cash payments in your retail store and provide another way for customers to buy your products. If you accept cash for purchases in your retail store, then you might need a secure and reliable cash drawer. A cash drawer lets you securely store any physical currency you accept in your store and keep track of money received from customers.

## Requirements to use a cash drawer with Shopify POS {#requirements}

To use this hardware with your Shopify store, you need to meet the following requirements:

* You use Shopify POS.
* You have a compatible iPad running iPadOS 15.1 or higher, an iPhone running iOS 15.1 or higher, or a compatible Android device running Android 10.0 or higher.


<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>To open the cash drawer automatically after a transaction, you&#39;ll need a supported automatic-opening setup. Most cash drawers need to connect to a <a href="/en/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/receipt-printers">compatible receipt printer</a>. If your compatible receipt printer connects by USB through the Shopify POS Hub, then set up the receipt printer first using the instructions for your printer model.</p>
</aside>

### iOS permission requirements {#ios-15-permission-requirements}

If you update your device to iOS 15.1 or higher and use third-party Shopify apps, then you might need to activate **Cross-Website tracking** in your iOS settings. **Cross-Website tracking** lets third-party Shopify apps that you install in your Shopify admin stay connected in Shopify Inbox, Shopify Point of Sale, and the Shopify app.

To activate **Cross-Website tracking**, complete the following steps:

1. On your iOS device, open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Shopify**, **Shopify POS**, or **Shopify Inbox**.
3. Activate **Allow Cross-Website Tracking**.

## Installing the cash drawer

Connect the cash drawer to the receipt printer using the cash drawer cable provided. The larger end of the cable plugs into the cash drawer, and the smaller end of the cable plugs into the receipt printer.

For standard printer-kick cash drawers, if you connect your receipt printer by USB using the [Shopify POS Hub](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/pos-hub), then connect your cash drawer to the receipt printer as usual.

### Connect a cash drawer when using Shopify POS Hub {#pos-hub}

When you use Shopify POS Hub, how you connect a cash drawer depends on the drawer model:

* Supported USB cash drawer models listed in [Supported peripherals for the Shopify POS Hub](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/pos-hub#supported-peripherals) can connect directly to a USB port on the POS Hub.
* Standard printer-kick cash drawers connect to a compatible receipt printer. Connect the receipt printer to the POS Hub, and then connect the cash drawer to the receipt printer.

#### Connect a cash drawer through a receipt printer

1. Before you connect your hardware to the Shopify POS Hub, set up the POS Hub:
   1. Plug the Shopify POS Hub power cord into a power source.
   2. Connect the Shopify POS Hub's USB-C or Lightning cable to your POS device.
   3. Plug the other end of the Shopify POS Hub's USB-C or Lightning cable into the tablet port on the bottom of the Shopify POS Hub. Don't use any other USB-C port on the Shopify POS Hub for the tablet cable.
   4. Keep Shopify POS open. Supported USB hardware that you connect to the Shopify POS Hub is added automatically in Shopify POS.


2. Set up your compatible receipt printer using the instructions for your printer model, and connect the printer to a USB port on the Shopify POS Hub when prompted.
3. Confirm that the receipt printer displays as connected in Shopify POS.
4. Connect the larger end of the cash drawer cable to the cash drawer.
5. Connect the smaller end of the cash drawer cable to the receipt printer.
6. Test the cash drawer connection.

## Opening the cash drawer

You can configure Shopify POS to open the cash drawer automatically after each cash transaction, or you can open it manually at any time.

<aside class="note note-staff-permissions"><h4>Staff permissions</h4><p>To open a cash drawer from Shopify POS, staff members must have the <strong>Open drawer</strong> permission. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/staff-management/understanding-pos-staff-management">staff permissions</a>.</p>
</aside>

### Open the cash drawer manually

You can open a cash drawer manually with any of the following methods:

* From Shopify POS, tap **<img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/RegisterIcon.svg" alt="Register" class="admin-icon" />** > **Open drawer**. This option is available only when a [register session](/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/cash-register-management/register-sessions-in-shopify-pos) is open.
* From the [smart grid](/manual/sell-in-person/getting-started/smart-grid), tap the **Open drawer** tile.
* Use the physical keys included with the cash drawer.

### Open the cash drawer automatically

The cash drawer connects to your device through the receipt printer and must be on the same wireless network.

In stores with multiple cash drawers, automatic opening only works when the device is connected to its [default printer](/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/receipt-management/set-default-receipt-printer). If the default printer isn't available, then the receipt prints from another printer. Cash drawers connected to the secondary printer won't open. This ensures cash drawers only open at the expected location.

#### Steps:

1. From Shopify POS, tap the <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg" alt="Horizontal menu" class="admin-icon" /> icon.


2. Tap **Settings** > **Payments**.
3. Tap **Payment Options**, and then activate **Drawer auto-open**.

### Require a reason to open

To require staff to select a reason each time the drawer is opened manually, you can [turn on **Reason codes**](/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/cash-register-management/register-sessions-in-shopify-admin) in the [**Cash management**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/point-of-sale-channel/settings/cash-management) setting in your Shopify admin. This helps track cash handling throughout the day.


## Test the connection to the cash drawer

When you've set up your [receipt printer](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/receipt-printers/) and your [cash drawer](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/cash-drawer/), you can check that the connection between them is working.

#### Steps:

1. From Shopify POS, tap the <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/ConnectivityIcon.svg" alt="Connectivity Icon" class="admin-icon" /> icon.


2. In the **Hardware** section, tap the printer that you want to test.
3. Tap **Test cash drawer**.

## Common issues

To avoid common problems, make sure your printer is set up correctly in the following ways:

* The cash drawer's cable is plugged into the receipt printer.
* The receipt printer has paper.
* The receipt printer is properly paired with the iPad, iPhone, or Android tablet. The pairing instructions for your receipt printer depends on [which receipt printer model](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/receipt-printers) you have.
* The receipt printer is set as the default printer for the device. If a receipt prints on a different connected printer because the default printer isn't available, then no cash drawers open automatically. This is a security behavior to prevent cash drawers from opening unexpectedly in a different part of the store.
* You're using the cash drawer cable that you received in the hardware package to connect the cash drawer to the printer.

If you tap **No Sale** on the products and cart screen and the cash drawer doesn't open, then this is likely a problem with the cash drawer cable. Contact our [support team](https://www.shopify.com/contact).

### Troubleshoot Shopify POS Hub connection issues

If your POS hardware doesn't connect through the Shopify POS Hub, then complete the [Shopify POS Hub troubleshooting steps](/manual/sell-in-person/hardware/pos-hub#troubleshooting).
