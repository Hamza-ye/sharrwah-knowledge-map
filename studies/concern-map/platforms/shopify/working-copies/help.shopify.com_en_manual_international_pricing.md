# Pricing in local currencies

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Only stores using Shopify Payments with access to <a href="/en/manual/checkout-settings/customize-checkout-configurations/one-page-checkout">one-page checkout</a> can use all functionalities of this feature. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/international/pricing/limitations">limitations for using local pricing</a>.</p>
</aside>

When you sell to markets, your customers can view prices in your store, pay for their orders at checkout, and receive refunds in their local currency. By default, prices are [converted automatically](/manual/international/pricing/exchange-rates#auto-convert) using the current market exchange rate, but you can [set exchange rates manually](/manual/international/pricing/exchange-rates#manual-exchange-rate-conversions).

You can customize your local pricing for each market by [rounding prices](/manual/international/pricing/rounding). You can also control the prices in different markets using [price percentage adjustments](/manual/markets/customizations/catalogs), and by [setting the product prices by country or region](/manual/markets/customizations/catalogs).

If you sell in local currencies, then you need to have a [country selector](/manual/international/geolocation) so that customers can select their local country or region and your store displays their local currency.
