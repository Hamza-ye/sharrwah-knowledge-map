# Q2 on Shopify: account

## [1]

**Unsupported countries and regions, and sanctions.** Shopify lists Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea region and the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics in Ukraine as unsupported. In those countries and regions it prohibits creating an account and accessing storefronts and accounts. It also prohibits products that originate from them and orders that ship to them. Q2-SH-K2271; Q2-SH-K2272; Q2-SH-K2273; Q2-SH-K2274; Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-SH-K2276; Q2-SH-K2277; Q2-SH-K2278; Q2-SH-K2279; Q2-SH-K2285; Q2-SH-A0002
- The Payments Services may not be used by a resident of a sanctioned country or region embargoed by the United States, Canada or the European Union. Under the Payment Facilitator Terms, the seller and its representatives and owners may not be subject to trade sanctions. Q2-SH-K2268; Q2-SH-P0048; Q2-SH-K2269; Q2-SH-K2280; Q2-SH-G0216; Q2-SH-G0217
- The partner terms require that a Partner is not resident or located in an Unsupported Region. App Developers warrant an export control classification of "EAR99". Q2-SH-K2281; Q2-SH-K2282

**Shopify Payments, by Designated Country.** A business must be located in its Designated Country, and the Payments Services may only be used there. Shopify Payments is offered in the supported countries the page lists. A business in a country that isn't listed uses a third-party payment provider. Q2-SH-K1536; Q2-SH-K1537; Q2-SH-K1535; Q2-SH-P0096; Q2-SH-P0097; Q2-SH-A0002
- For each Designated Country, Shopify names the Payment Processor(s) whose terms the seller accepts: Stripe entities by country, PayPal, Adyen, AIHK Markets Limited for mainland China, and a Bridge entity in all regions. Shopify may change the Payment Processors. Q2-SH-K1563; Q2-SH-K1566; Q2-SH-K1567; Q2-SH-K1568; Q2-SH-K1569; Q2-SH-K1570; Q2-SH-K1571; Q2-SH-K1572; Q2-SH-K1573; Q2-SH-K1574; Q2-SH-K1575; Q2-SH-K1576; Q2-SH-K1577; Q2-SH-K1578; Q2-SH-K1561; Q2-SH-P0112; Q2-SH-P0113; Q2-SH-P0114; Q2-SH-P0115; Q2-SH-P0116; Q2-SH-P0117; Q2-SH-P0118; Q2-SH-P0119; Q2-SH-P0120
- In the EU / EEA and the United Kingdom, a Shopify affiliate acts as Payment Facilitator, with Adyen N.V. as Acquirer. In Canada, Shopify Payment Activities Inc. is registered under the Retail Payment Activities Act. Q2-SH-K1603; Q2-SH-K1607; Q2-SH-K1608; Q2-SH-K1611; Q2-SH-P0110; Q2-SH-K1614; Q2-SH-K1615; Q2-SH-A0003
- Further limits: Shopify Payments isn't available in French Overseas Territories or in US territories other than Puerto Rico. Some countries, such as Japan, limit payout options, and some set a minimum payout amount. Q2-SH-P0147; Q2-SH-P0146; Q2-SH-K1632; Q2-SH-K1635
- In France, Shopify Payments carries requirements that are in place to comply with regulations and to prevent financial crimes. Q2-SH-038
- Prohibited and Restricted Businesses come from law, Payment Network Rules, Shopify, or a Payment Processor's Financial Services Providers. The list varies by region. Q2-SH-K2262; Q2-SH-K2265; Q2-SH-K1541; Q2-SH-P0045; Q2-SH-P0046

**Hemp and regulated products.** Shopify Payments doesn't support hemp, CBD or THC products. Shopify has partnered with third-party providers that do, and approval is at each provider's discretion. A store removed from Shopify Payments for selling hemp may stay on Shopify with another provider. Q2-SH-001; Q2-SH-002; Q2-SH-003; Q2-SH-004
- US merchants selling hemp, and Canadian merchants selling cannabis, must agree to requirements set by Shopify. Hemp sellers get no SMS notifications, and can't use POS hardware that requires Shopify Payments. Q2-SH-005; Q2-SH-006; Q2-SH-007
- Shopify leaves to the seller compliance with the law where it does business, and with recalls. Q2-SH-K2257; Q2-SH-K2256; Q2-SH-K2288; Q2-SH-K2289; Q2-SH-K2293

**Managed Markets (Global-e).** Managed Markets is only available to merchants based in the continental United States, and to certain stores in Canada and the United Kingdom. Its requirements include Shopify Payments and a store currency that is the default currency of the business's country. Q2-SH-K1956; Q2-SH-K1957; Q2-SH-P0103; Q2-SH-011; Q2-SH-012; Q2-SH-013; Q2-SH-N02
- It doesn't support some countries or regions, and currencies. DDU shipping remains only where DDP isn't supported. Shopify Protect for Managed Markets is US only. It doesn't support multiple business entities in a single store. Q2-SH-010; Q2-SH-014; Q2-SH-015; Q2-SH-016
- It hides a product from customers in a destination country where the product is prohibited, and caps order values for Argentina, Brazil, China, Indonesia and Mexico. Q2-SH-019; Q2-SH-021
- It applies compliant EU cancellation and return policies automatically, the same for all orders into the EU. A seller can turn this off at Settings > Customer accounts. Q2-SH-008; Q2-SH-017; Q2-SH-018
- Shopify's own return and cancellation rules help meet the EU electronic withdrawal function requirement. Q2-SH-009

- Shop Pay Installments is offered in the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom, under a Merchant Agreement with Affirm. Q2-SH-K1688; Q2-SH-K1690; Q2-SH-P0041
- Local payment methods are activated by the regions where a seller sells. Q2-SH-K1741; Q2-SH-K1743; Q2-SH-K1745
- Cryptocurrency needs a Shopify Payments account in the United States, Canada, Mexico or Hong Kong. The seller verifies that it is acceptable in their region. Q2-SH-K1751; Q2-SH-K1750
- Shop Cash names Hong Kong, Mexico and the UAE as exceptions. Q2-SH-K1759
- Paying in a customer's local currency needs Shopify Payments or Adyen as the primary gateway. A checkout restriction changes the currency to match the shipping destination. Q2-SH-K1937; Q2-SH-K1940; Q2-SH-026
- Standalone Stripe isn't available in Shopify Payments regions. Q2-SH-K1528
- Third-party transaction fees apply when a seller uses providers other than Shopify Payments. Q2-SH-K1337; Q2-SH-K1334
- Multiple business entities require Shopify Payments. Q2-SH-K1264
- Selling in the EU and UK, Shopify classifies card transactions as international. Q2-SH-037

- Shopify Balance is only for merchants with a physical residential address in the US or Puerto Rico. It runs through Stripe, Celtic Bank and partner banks. Q2-SH-K1793; Q2-SH-K1794; Q2-SH-K2680; Q2-SH-K1806
- Shopify Capital lenders differ by country: WebBank in the United States, YouLend in the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain and Germany, and Banking Circle S.A. with YouLend SAS in France. Q2-SH-K1815; Q2-SH-K1816; Q2-SH-K1819; Q2-SH-K1825; Q2-SH-K1838; Q2-SH-K1843; Q2-SH-K1848; Q2-SH-K1849; Q2-SH-K1850; Q2-SH-G0119; Q2-SH-G0121
- Shopify Bill Pay is US only, through Melio. Q2-SH-K1852; Q2-SH-K1853
- In the United Kingdom, BACS Direct Debit notifications can't be deactivated. Q2-SH-K1827
- Shopify Audiences is offered to Plus stores in the United States or Canada. Q2-SH-K2053

- Which tax services a seller can use depends on where the business is located. Shopify Tax covers destinations in the US, EU, UK and Canada. Automated filing is US only, through Sovos, and is closed to new enrollment. Q2-SH-K1865; Q2-SH-K1867; Q2-SH-K1909; Q2-SH-K1907; Q2-SH-K1912
- VAT validation needs Shopify Tax in the EU or UK. Q2-SH-K1891
- In the US, Form 1099-K is filed with the IRS. Q2-SH-K1856
- Saudi Arabia and the UAE have VAT on Shopify's bill. Q2-SH-K1329; Q2-SH-K1330

**Point of Sale, by country.** Local requirements apply to selling in person. A return or exchange in France, Spain, Italy, Germany or Sweden must be processed by the same legal entity, with no exceptions in France. Q2-SH-042; Q2-SH-043; Q2-SH-044
- Receipts meet supported regulated markets by default. In 16 listed European markets, a VAT summary always shows. Italy's receipts are managed by the Epson RT printer. Q2-SH-039; Q2-SH-045; Q2-SH-040
- Tax registration numbers on receipts need Basic Tax or Shopify Tax. Q2-SH-046; Q2-SH-047
- Germany: KassenSichV and TSE. Q2-SH-K2975
- Spain: Fiscalization and the Comply app (Veri*Factu). Bundles can't be sold, and the admin shows a banner. Q2-SH-049; Q2-SH-050; Q2-SH-051; Q2-SH-052
- France: Marque NF525 through Infocert and the Comply app. Sales are blocked without Comply. Certification covers stores with Shopify Payments. No offline transactions, and receipt content can't be changed. Q2-SH-053; Q2-SH-054; Q2-SH-055; Q2-SH-057; Q2-SH-041
- Singapore: POS has no GTO reporting, and third parties provide it. Q2-SH-048
- POS must use the same payment processor as online. Interac can't be taken offline in Canada. In-person processing is limited to countries where Shopify Payments supports it. POS Equipment terms apply by country. Q2-SH-K2930; Q2-SH-K1784; Q2-SH-K1788; Q2-SH-K1783; Q2-SH-K2112

- Cookie banner and data-sharing settings are managed per region, while Shopify Network Intelligence is global. Privacy settings can be customized per country and region. Q2-SH-027; Q2-SH-028; Q2-SH-K2134
- EEA event data isn't used for personalized advertising without Google Consent Mode v2 and valid consent. Q2-SH-029
- Address fields and tax fields vary by customer country. Email marketing consent is preselected by regions Shopify recommends. Q2-SH-031; Q2-SH-032; Q2-SH-033
- Compare-at price display for EEA customers depends on whether the primary market is in the EEA. Q2-SH-024; Q2-SH-025
- `.at` and `.fr` domain contact information can't be updated from the admin. EU domain extensions may require identity verification. Q2-SH-034; Q2-SH-035
- Shopify Shipping and shipping insurance vary by location and carrier. Q2-SH-K1987; Q2-SH-K1989; Q2-SH-K2000; Q2-SH-K1998

**Which company contracts with the seller.** The Shopify Contracting Party depends on the billing address. Shopify Inc. and Shopify Technology (USA) Inc. contract for the United States or Canada. Shopify Commerce Singapore Pte. Ltd contracts for Asia Pacific. Shopify International Limited contracts for all other jurisdictions. Q2-SH-K1243; Q2-SH-K1244; Q2-SH-K1246; Q2-SH-K1247; Q2-SH-K1248; Q2-SH-G0003; Q2-SH-G0004
- Shopify Payments names the Shopify entity by Designated Country. The P2B Terms are with Shopify International Limited under Irish law. The Terms of Service are governed by Ontario law. Q2-SH-K1544; Q2-SH-K1546; Q2-SH-K1547; Q2-SH-K1548; Q2-SH-K2298; Q2-SH-K2299; Q2-SH-K1253
- Shopify is not a party to the Payment Processor Terms, the Managed Markets Terms with Global-e, or the Issuing Bank Terms. Q2-SH-K1580; Q2-SH-K1962; Q2-SH-K2949; Q2-SH-P0008
- With Managed Markets, Global-e is merchant of record and holds the funds. Deactivating a market makes the seller responsible for its tax and regulatory requirements. Q2-SH-K1958; Q2-SH-K1960; Q2-SH-K1601; Q2-SH-K1961; Q2-SH-022; Q2-SH-023
- The billing country must match an active business entity. Entities are assigned to markets and each has its own Shopify Payments account. Q2-SH-K1259; Q2-SH-K1266; Q2-SH-K1268

**Where data is held.** New merchants in Europe have their data stored at rest in Europe by default. Data from the EEA, UK and Switzerland is first processed by Shopify International Limited. Transfers rely on BCRs and SCCs. Q2-SH-K2321; Q2-SH-K2330; Q2-SH-K2331; Q2-SH-K2341; Q2-SH-K2339; Q2-SH-K2327
- The admin shows the Customer data hosting location. Q2-SH-030
- Within 90 days of termination, EU merchants' personal information is deleted except as legally required. Q2-SH-K2356
