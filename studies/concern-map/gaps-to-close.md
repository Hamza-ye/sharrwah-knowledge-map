# Gaps (Q1): a reference

Each platform's re-pass records a gap wherever the platform's own sources, as held, state nothing for a concern and placement. Under `PLAN.md`, a gap is closed only inside a subject that needs it. **Nothing on this page is asked of the founder.** When a subject needs a gap that only the founder can close, the gate for that subject says so, with the reason.

On 12 October 2026, Stage 0 checked every gap against what observed-sa, observed-sh and this repository's capture runs hold. It re-routed the gaps whose page or corpus is held, without closing any. `held-check.tsv` lists each change and its rule. Generated by `method/q1_gaps_report.py`.

Gap kinds:

- **observation needed**: a journey, a store on a plan, a page saved from a browser, or a question to the platform would show it.
- **page not read**: a named page, structure or corpus would likely state it, and it has not been read yet.
- **stated only in general terms**: the platform's terms speak only of its services in general. Applying such a clause to one concern would be inference.
- **searched, not found**: the likely pages and structures were read, as the row lists, and none states it.
