About this app
arrow_forward
Revolutionizing Payments in Yemen
Meet ONE Cash, Yemen's first electronic wallet. Imagine carrying your money securely in your phone. With ONE Cash, simply top up your account at the nearest agent, and say goodbye to the hassle of carrying physical cash.
Effortless Transactions
Why stand in line to pay bills, top up your phone, or even shop? With ONE Cash, you can handle all your financial transactions directly from your phone. Whether you're in a supermarket, or clothing store, or paying through one of the many ONE Cash payment points spread across Yemen, just tap to pay. No more counting cash at the register.
Verify and top up your account to explore the seamless services offered by ONE Cash, all from the convenience of your phone.

Multiple Currencies:
You can deposit/Cash in money into your ONE Cash account or receive money in the following currencies (Yemeni Rial, Saudi Riyal, US Dollar).
You can also exchange from Saudi Riyal or US Dollar to Yemeni Rial within your One Cash account.
Not only that, but you can also Cashout your money in the currency of your choice.
Additionally, you can receive your international transfers from MoneyGram directly into your ONE Cash wallet and enjoy all our services.

Money Transfers:
With ONE Cash, you can transfer up to 3,000,000 Yemeni Riyals to any ONE Cash subscriber, no commission at all! It's completely free for ONE Cash users.
For transfers to non-ONE Cash subscribers, you can still send money through the app. If the recipient doesn’t claim the transfer within 14 days, the amount will be automatically refunded to your ONE Cash account. This ensures that your money is always safe and secure, making ONE Cash the most reliable financial application in Yemen.
Additionally, you can cancel any transfer within 24 hours of sending it. ONE Cash also allows you to transfer funds from your account to various local banks and other electronic wallets like Yemen Kuwait Bank, Tadhamon Bank, Shamil Bank of Yemen and Bahrain, Saba Islamic Bank, Yemen Bank for Reconstruction and Development, Islamic Bank of Yemen, Jawali, Floosak, and Mahfathati Wallets. You can also send and receive money from any electronic wallet using Wasel service, yes! Various options to send and receive money right from ONE Cash application.
Additionally, with ONE Cash, you can easily send and receive money through local networks, and HB Fast. For instance, if you receive a remittance from a local network, it can be deposited directly into your ONE Cash account. Likewise, you can send money to anyone using the same network.

Airtime & Bundles:
Easily top up airtime to your phone or a friend’s, whether prepaid or postpaid, regardless of the telecom provider. Choose from a variety of bundles available instantly in the app with a single tap.

Bill Payments:
Pay all your bills effortlessly, including:
- Private and commercial electricity
- Internet services (Yemen Wi-Fi, Yemen 4G, ADSL, and Fiber)
- Landline and water bills
- Microfinance company bills
- Traffic Fines and Fees
- University Fees

Cashout:
Your money is always safe with ONE Cash. Need cash? Cashout easily from the nearest ONE Cash Agent, any YKB ATM, or through various exchange networks.

Pay Merchants:
With One Cash Merchants widespread, you can pay for your purchases, whether it’s clothing, groceries, electronics, or more, with just a touch of your phone. No need to carry cash; everything is handled digitally.

Buy E-Cards:
For gaming and entertainment enthusiasts, ONE Cash has you covered. Purchase PUBG points, PC and Xbox game cards, and gems for various games, or subscribe to streaming platforms like Spacetoon Go, Shahid, OSN+, and Netflix, all from within the app. Enjoy endless entertainment with ease.

Donate:
Make a difference by donating directly to charitable organizations via ONE Cash, ensuring your contributions reach those in need without intermediaries.

ONE Cash: The ultimate financial solution and the perfect alternative to cash.
Control of all your financial transactions, payments, transfers with one tap.
