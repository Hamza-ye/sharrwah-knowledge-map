Getting Started with BigCommerce
CHAPTER 13:
Channels

To view this video in fullscreen, visit Channels.




Your business isn’t confined to just your website — it exists across multiple platforms and touchpoints. BigCommerce makes it simple to connect, manage, and optimize your sales and marketing channels, helping you to reach customers wherever they shop and engage with your brand.

 
Point of Sale

We support many of the most popular Point of Sale (POS) systems, making it easy to unify your in-store and online operations. By connecting your POS system to your store, you can seamlessly sync inventory and order data across both channels.

You can find supported POS solutions by:

Navigating to the Point of Sale section under Channels in your control panel
Exploring the POS and In-Store category in the BigCommerce App Store

See What is Point of Sale for a list of popular supported apps.

 
Marketplaces

Expand your reach by connecting your store to the world’s largest online marketplaces. By integrating these platforms with your BigCommerce store, you can list and sell your products while managing everything from one central location.

You can connect your store to leading marketplaces like:

Amazon
Facebook
Walmart
eBay

Once integrated, all marketplace orders are managed directly from your control panel, simplifying your workflow and saving you time.

To get started with a marketplace:

Visit the marketplace’s providers page in your control panel to see recommended apps for integration.
Browse the Sales Channels category in the BigCommerce App Store for a full list of marketplace solutions.
 
Advertising and Social Channels

BigCommerce makes it easy to connect your store to leading advertising platforms and social networks, allowing you to expand your reach and measure the effectiveness of your marketing efforts.

You can integrate with top online advertising platforms, including:

Google
Meta (Facebook and Instagram)
TikTok

With built-in support for Meta Pixel and Google Analytics event tracking, you’re already set up to monitor the performance and ROI of your ad campaigns, giving you the insights you need to refine your strategies even further.

To explore and connect advertising and social platforms:

Check out the Ads and Social section under Channels in your control panel for a list of supported platforms and apps.
Dig into the Marketing category in the BigCommerce App Store, which includes subcategories for tools like:
Affiliate marketing
Email marketing
Social media tools
 
Multiple Storefronts

BigCommerce enables you to create and manage multiple storefronts, giving you the flexibility to support your multi-channel strategy from a single control panel. Whether you’re using native storefronts or taking a headless approach, BigCommerce provides the tools to meet your business needs.

Additional storefronts can help you to tailor the shopping experience for different audiences or strategies, such as:

Localization — Create region-specific storefronts to accommodate different languages, currencies, or cultural preferences.
Brand Differentiation — Launch unique storefronts for individual brands under your business umbrella.
Customer Segmentation — Serve distinct customer cohorts, such as B2B, wholesale, or VIP customers, with dedicated storefronts.

See our Guide to Multi-Storefront for use cases, features, and setup.
