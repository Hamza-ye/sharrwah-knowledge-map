# Ensuring that your store complies with Shopify's policies

At Shopify, we believe in making commerce better for everyone. To help with this mission, we provide you with information to help you make informed decisions about how you sell your products using our platform.

Your products and use of Shopify must adhere to [Shopify’s Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms). Other Shopify products, such as Shopify Payments, have additional terms. For example, if you use the following products, then you need to also follow their terms:

* [Shopify Payments Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/legal)
* [Shop Pay Terms of Service](https://www.shopify.com/ca/legal/shop-pay-merchant-terms)
* [Shop App Content Policies](https://shop.app/content-policies)

You also need to consider the implications of selling in different jurisdictions, including researching the relevant import and export laws and regulations, and knowing the current legal status of the products you sell in every region you sell them.

Shopify can set you up for success, but there are also steps that you need to take, especially if you plan to sell regulated products. The information found in this section is intended as a starting point to help you learn about selling products in certain regulated areas. If you have questions about which laws apply to you, or questions specific to your business, then you need to consult with a lawyer.

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>This page provides general information to merchants, who are responsible for reviewing and using this information appropriately. This page doesn&#39;t contain and isn&#39;t meant to provide legal advice.</p>
<p>You can use the information found in this section to learn about some laws and regulations that you might need to follow, but you should consult your own legal counsel to determine how different laws might affect your business. To sell products using the Shopify platform, you must comply with the laws of the jurisdiction of your business and your customers, the <a href="https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms">Shopify Terms of Service</a>, the <a href="https://www.shopify.com/legal/aup">Shopify Acceptable Use Policy</a>, and any other applicable policies.</p>
</aside>
