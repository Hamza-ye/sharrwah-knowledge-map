Cash Pay – Electronic Payment Service
Cash Pay – Electronic Payment Service

Cash Wallet provides the Cash Pay electronic payment service for e-commerce stores and applications, enabling them to offer their customers an easy, fast, and secure payment method. Customers can complete their online purchases seamlessly, from selecting products to completing the payment. Cash Wallet also provides a dedicated technical support team to help store and app owners integrate the service step by step via API.

Get The Service Now:

Open a Cash Wallet account.

Add the Cash Pay payment option to your website or online store.

Complete the integration process with Cash Wallet’s technical support team.

Receive your sales funds and monitor sales reports electronically.

Withdraw your money in cash from any Cash Wallet service agent and enjoy all merchant features and benefits.

Features Of Cash Pay

Attract more customers: Enables them to pay anytime, anywhere.

Instant fund transfer: As soon as the customer clicks “Pay via Cash Pay”, the amount is deducted from their account and added to your Cash Wallet account. You can then withdraw the funds in cash from any Cash Wallet service agent.

Flexible API integration: Easily integrate Cash Pay with any online store, website, or mobile application with continuous direct support from Cash Wallet technical experts.

Real-time sales reports: Access all financial transactions and reports easily, with advanced technical tools to control sales, transfer, and refund money instantly.

How To Pay Via Cash Pay

After selecting your order, choose Pay via Cash Pay.

Enter your Cash Wallet account number (the phone number registered with your wallet).

Enter the Online Purchase Code, which can be obtained from the Cash Wallet app (Main Menu → Settings → Online Purchase Code).

Enter the verification code received via SMS to complete the payment.
