# Filing your taxes after automated filing ends

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>This guide is for informational purposes only, and isn&#39;t intended to replace professional tax advice. Tax laws change frequently. If you have further questions about your tax returns, then you should contact local tax authorities or a tax professional. It&#39;s your responsibility to consult with tax authorities or tax professionals to ensure that you file and remit your taxes correctly.</p>
</aside>

Automated filing with Shopify Tax is ending. Automated filing files your returns for sales made through December 31, 2026. In New York, automated filing also files the full December 2026–February 2027 quarterly return, which includes January and February 2027 sales.

After your last automated filing, you can continue to file your sales tax returns in either of the following ways:

* File yourself: Shopify Tax continues to calculate your sales tax and keep your [tax reports](/manual/taxes/tax-reports#united-states-sales-tax) current at no extra cost. You file directly with each state.
* Use a filing service: A third-party filing service files your returns for you, similar to automated filing. Two services, [Numeral](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#numeral) and [Zamp](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#zamp), offer promotions for merchants who are moving from automated filing.

The exact due date depends on the state. Starting with the return after your last automated filing, you need to either file your returns yourself or use a third-party filing service. You can [download your filing records](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#download-records) until April 30, 2027.

If your last automated return isn't filed, then its status changes to **Not filed**. This happens when the return is skipped, or when it has issues that aren't resolved by the filing date. If your last return isn't filed, then download the return report and file the return yourself.

You're charged for each return that's generated by automated filing, up to and including your last return. After your last return, you aren't charged for automated filing.

## Third-party filing services {#filing-partners}

Numeral and Zamp are third-party filing services with promotions for merchants who are moving from automated filing. Both services file sales tax returns in every US state. Pricing and services are set by each filing service, not by Shopify.

### Numeral {#numeral}

Numeral offers the following promotion for merchants who are moving from automated filing:

* You pay $50 USD for each return for 1 year.
* You can get a free consultation call instead of the standard onboarding.

Numeral provides Shopify migration support, concierge service, nexus monitoring, registrations, filings, a virtual mailbox for notice management, and an on-time filing guarantee.

To get the offer, sign up on [Numeral's page for Shopify merchants](https://www.numeral.com/partner/shopify).

### Zamp {#zamp}

Zamp offers the following promotion for merchants who are moving from automated filing:

* You get 3 free months of sales tax support, starting from the date that your Zamp service is activated. To get this offer, sign up by January 31, 2027. You can choose any onboarding date up to January 31, 2027.
* After the free period, you pay from $45 USD to $65 USD per state, per month, depending on the state tier and your payment terms.

Zamp's managed service includes onboarding and account management, phone and email support, a sales tax health check, state registrations, state and local filings, a free import of your historical data from Shopify, notice management, and a filing accuracy guarantee.

To get the offer, sign up on [Zamp's page for Shopify merchants](https://zamp.com/shopify-filing-partner).

## Considerations for switching to a filing service {#considerations}

Before you switch to a third-party filing service, review the following considerations:

* Pricing: Numeral charges for each return. Zamp charges per state, per month. Compare costs based on how many states you file in and how often you file.
* Included services: Some filing services include state registrations, local filings, and help with state notices. Other filing services charge separately for these services.
* Contracts: Neither Numeral nor Zamp requires a contract.
* Start date: Your filing service needs to start with the return after your last automated return, so that no return is filed twice or missed.
* Filing history: Your filing service might ask for your past returns. Download your filing records by April 30, 2027, and keep a copy.
* Other filing options: You don't need to use Numeral or Zamp. You can file yourself with your Shopify Tax reports, work with a tax professional, or use another filing service.

## Download your filing records {#download-records}

Download a copy of each automated return so that you have your records after automated filing ends. You can download your filing records until April 30, 2027.

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Finance** > [**Tax filing**](https://admin.shopify.com/taxes/filing).
2. Click the tax return that you want to download.
3. In the **Documents** section, click **Return report**.
4. Repeat these steps for each tax return that you want to keep.

## Switch to a third-party filing service {#switch}

After your last filing with Shopify, your filing service files your next return. Set up your account with the filing service before that return is due. You don't need to turn off automated filing. Automated filing ends after your last return is filed, and Shopify Tax continues to calculate sales tax for your store.

#### Steps:

1. In the [final filing dates table](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#key-dates), find your last automated return.
2. [Download your filing records](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#download-records).
3. Sign up with a [third-party filing service](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/filing-partners#filing-partners). To get the Numeral or Zamp promotion, use their page for Shopify merchants.
4. Tell your filing service which returns automated filing files, and which return they need to file first.
5. Authorize your filing service to file for you. Some states require a power of attorney or login credentials before a filing service can file for you. Your filing service can explain what each state requires.

## Support for switching to a filing service {#support}

[Shopify Support](/manual/your-account/contact-shopify-support) can help with your settings and reports in Shopify. For questions about a filing service's pricing, features, or the returns that it files, contact the filing service directly.
