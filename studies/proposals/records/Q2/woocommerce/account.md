- Section 18 of the Terms of Service, "US Economic Sanctions", makes the Services subject to US sanctions on who, or from where, they can be accessed. It excludes anyone located in Cuba, Crimea, Iran, North Korea, the Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics or Syria, and anyone on US restricted-party lists such as OFAC's SDN List. Automattic may block the seller or the seller's customers. Q2-WC-K0524; Q2-WC-K0525; Q2-WC-K0526; Q2-WC-K0528; Q2-WC-K0529
- Section 8.j bars Ecommerce Services for anyone embargoed or blocked by any government. It makes the seller solely responsible for export, import and sanctions compliance, including licences. Q2-WC-K0527; Q2-WC-K0532
- WooPayments is offered in a listed set of countries; for a business, the country is where it is registered. Some outlying territories of the U.S., UK, France and the Netherlands are excluded, and core keeps its own country list. Q2-WC-K0406; Q2-WC-K0402; Q2-WC-K0407; Q2-WC-K0408; Q2-WC-K0409; Q2-WC-P0168; Q2-WC-P0173; Q2-WC-A0002

- WooPay is offered only to U.S. merchants and U.S. customers. Its "Securely save my information" box is pre-checked only for U.S. stores. Q2-WC-K0447; Q2-WC-042; Q2-WC-043
- In-Person Payments, instant payouts and payout currencies each follow their own country lists. Fees depend on the business's and the customer's country. Q2-WC-K0439; Q2-WC-P0165; Q2-WC-K0379; Q2-WC-K0380; Q2-WC-K0427

Examples: GrabPay in Singapore only, Multibanco in Europe, the U.K. and the U.S., most others "except UAE", and WeChat Pay only for approved merchants in a listed set of countries. - iDEAL | Wero requires the KVK number for Dutch businesses, or the local equivalent for others. Q2-WC-018; Q2-WC-019

- Przelewy24 excludes a list of MCCs, some marked "(Germany only)", "(U.S. region only)" or "(Norway, Poland, Sweden only)". Q2-WC-023; Q2-WC-024; Q2-WC-025; Q2-WC-026; Q2-WC-027; Q2-WC-028
- Signing up for WooPayments creates a Stripe Express account. An existing Stripe account can't be used, and WooPayments accounts have no Stripe Dashboard. Q2-WC-K0371; Q2-WC-K0713; Q2-WC-K0373
- Stripe makes the final decision on reserves; Woo "cannot see why one was applied, nor appeal it". Most Stripe Radar rules are managed by Woo's team. Q2-WC-K0388; Q2-WC-K0390; Q2-WC-K0804
- The Terms allow payment method information to be shared with an alternative processor. Opting out of an updated Stripe agreement ends WooPayments use. Q2-WC-K0712; Q2-WC-K0714
- WooCommerce Shipping is for U.S. stores using U.S. dollars, through EasyPost and Automattic's servers. WooCommerce Tax covers named countries, with calculations by TaxJar. POS needs a supported store country and its native currency. Q2-WC-K0838; Q2-WC-K0842; Q2-WC-K0777; Q2-WC-K0776; Q2-WC-K1320; Q2-WC-K1321
- WooPayments issues Form 1099-K to U.S. persons and VAT or JCT invoices to EU, UK and Japan merchants. It adds VAT/GST/CT on its fees after September 2, 2025. Q2-WC-K0488; Q2-WC-K0486; Q2-WC-K0487; Q2-WC-K0792
- The EU VAT Number extension handles EU VAT on digital goods and stores location evidence in the order. Customers outside the EU see no VAT fields, and numbers are validated against VIES. Q2-WC-045; Q2-WC-046; Q2-WC-047; Q2-WC-K1500
- Core keeps customs fields on products; the seller is responsible for customs rules and duties. Q2-WC-K0797; Q2-WC-K0799; Q2-WC-K0800
- WooCommerce 11.2.1 shows the inbox note "Enable order withdrawal for EU regulatory requirements" to stores that sell to an EU country. The feature "does not guarantee compliance". Q2-WC-049; Q2-WC-050; Q2-WC-051; Q2-WC-K1337
- The Accounts & Privacy settings ask for retention periods that comply with laws such as the EU GDPR. WooCommerce may retain data when legal obligations require it. Q2-WC-052; Q2-WC-053
- Core's Payments settings suggest payment extensions per store country, in priority order, and tag some as preferred. The suggestions are fetched from WooCommerce.com unless the seller turns them off. Q2-WC-K0054; Q2-WC-K0055; Q2-WC-K0064; Q2-WC-K0331; Q2-WC-K0056; Q2-WC-K0335
- Third-party services "aren't vetted, endorsed, or controlled by Automattic". Geolocation uses MaxMind and needs a MaxMind account. AI features "may be powered by Anthropic and OpenAI". Q2-WC-K0352; Q2-WC-K0697; Q2-WC-K0701; Q2-WC-K1362
- Ecommerce Services on WordPress.com must follow the Store Guidelines. Q2-WC-K0493; Q2-WC-K0495

- The Terms are governed by California law, with San Francisco courts. Mandatory consumer rights in the country of residence are unaffected, but Woo use is "that of a representative of a business". Q2-WC-K0516; Q2-WC-K0517; Q2-WC-K0514; Q2-WC-K0501
- The data controller depends "on the particular service and the location of the individual". Q2-WC-K0502; Q2-WC-K0505; Q2-WC-K0506

- On data location: core keeps store data in the site's WordPress database; Jetpack syncs it to Automattic's servers; MailPoet keeps account data on U.S. servers; EU and UK data may be accessed outside the EEA or UK under standard contractual arrangements; saved-card tokens sit with the payment partner.
