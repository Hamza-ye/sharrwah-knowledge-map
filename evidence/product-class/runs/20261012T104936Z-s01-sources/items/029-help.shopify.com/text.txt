# Chargebacks and inquiries

If you accept credit cards on your store, then it's likely you need to deal with chargebacks or inquiries. When a cardholder has an issue with a charge on their credit card, they can contact their bank to dispute the charge. The bank then makes a chargeback or inquiry. The cardholder can be one of your customers or someone who believes that their card was used on your store without their permission.

If the cardholder's bank makes a _chargeback_, then the bank takes the disputed amount from you right away. The cardholder's bank also takes a [chargeback fee](/manual/payments/chargebacks/chargeback-process#chargeback-fee) from you. If the cardholder's bank makes an _inquiry_, then they don't take the disputed amount or a fee right away.

You can try to [resolve the chargeback or inquiry](/manual/payments/chargebacks/resolve-chargeback) in a few ways. Often, the company that issued the cardholder's credit card reviews any evidence, and then resolves the chargeback in either your favor or the cardholder's favor. If you win the chargeback, then you get the disputed amount back, and Shopify might refund the chargeback fee depending on your country or region. If the cardholder wins the chargeback, then the disputed amount is returned to the cardholder.

Some disputes are resolved automatically. If your store is enrolled in a network dispute resolution program, then disputes are
resolved automatically with funds reimbursed to customers before they become formal chargebacks, without an evidence-submission window. Learn more about
[network dispute resolution programs](/manual/payments/chargebacks/network-dispute-resolution-programs).

If Shopify's [fraud analysis](/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/protecting-orders/fraud-analysis) reports that an order is high risk, then you can review the order and choose to cancel it. This can help you avoid chargebacks and inquiries on fraudulent transactions. 

If you're using Shopify Payments and you're issued a chargeback, then the amount is deducted from your next available payout.

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>Shopify isn&#39;t liable for chargebacks that occur when using the Shopify platform. Shopify isn&#39;t involved in the decision making of chargeback outcomes.</p>
</aside>
