## 2. The note on open-source platforms (earlier the same evening)

** before i go read everything your wrote,one last note about medusa, saleor, and other open source platform mentioned on different places in the repository:**
Medusa, Saleor, and Vendure are closer to WooCommerce than Shopify in how they model stores; I beleive they all don't provide the same separation of each seller's store/customers/etc that Shopify and product family does

These platforms came up in earlier agent suggestions, but I think the underlying point they were mentioned was broader than commerce platforms: **we shouldn't invent mature architectural patterns from scratch when years of engineering experience are already available to learn from.** We can study how established platforms designed tenants, extensions, events, webhooks, and functional boundaries, including why they made particular choices and what their successes and failures taught them.

We're not copying their code; we're **buying architectural intelligence**. We study the topography they've refined over years and use it to make better decisions faster, avoiding choices that look right today but fail at scale later.

Shopify's engineering blog will also be a valuable source at the appropriate stages. We can explore other domains for relevant platforms and candidates as our questions evolve, rather than limiting our research to commerce or searching without a specific purpose. 

that's was part of the agent's talks at the time, how and when engineering put it in our path, i do not know.

**What the coordinating session found when it checked this note against the platforms' own pages, the same evening:**
- Saleor's channels stand for a region, a business division or a brand, and "the product catalog, warehouse network, and stock information remain shared" (docs.saleor.io/overview/why-saleor/multi-market).
- Medusa: "Medusa does not provide multi-tenancy natively" (medusajs.com/blog/multi-tenant-rigby/).
- Vendure's documentation lists multi-tenancy and multi-vendor among the uses of its channels. But "Every Vendure server always has a default Channel, which contains all entities" (docs.vendure.io/guides/core-concepts/channels/).
- None of the three keeps independent sellers' stores apart as Shopify does.
