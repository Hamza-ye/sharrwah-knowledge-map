# Fulfillment Network

<aside class="note note-app"><p><img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/app-store/listing_images/6cd1dee7b2d1b3fe679f9e3b19b1a9cf/icon/CLvcvvXXnIQDEAE=.png" alt="Shopify Fulfillment Network app icon"> <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/fulfillment-network">Shopify Fulfillment Network</a>: Install Shopify Fulfillment Network from the Shopify App Store.</p>
</aside>

You can use the Fulfillment Network to connect with third-party logistics providers (3PLs) that store your inventory and fulfill orders on your behalf. After you install the app and choose a 3PL partner, you can manage your entire fulfillment workflow from your Shopify admin.

When you receive an order, your 3PL picks, packs, and ships products to your customer. You can monitor inventory transfers, track order fulfillment, and manage your relationship with your 3PL partner without leaving Shopify.

You might use the Fulfillment Network when you want to scale without managing your own warehouse, need distributed inventory across multiple regions, or want to focus on growing your business instead of handling logistics.
