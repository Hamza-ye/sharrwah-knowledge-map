Main advantages
What is Qitaf?
Qitaf is a loyalty program from STC that offers rewards, discounts, and special offers for STC customers. When you activate the Qitaf service in your store, Qitaf customers can redeem their points for purchases from your store or earn new points when they shop.
How will your customer benefit from adding Qitaf to your store?
Your customers will benefit from redeeming Qitaf program points when they purchase from your online store or earning new points with every successful purchase from your store.
