# Q3, Shopify: account

## [1]

- **Opening an account.** To complete Account registration, the seller provides a full legal name, business address, phone number, a valid email address and any other information indicated as required. The person signing up is the Store Owner. Individuals and businesses within unsupported countries and regions are prohibited from creating a Shopify account. The seller must keep an accurate location in the admin. Q3-SH-K1238; Q3-SH-K1237; Q3-SH-K1239; Q3-SH-K1270; Q3-SH-K2276; Q3-SH-K1271
- **Launching the online store.** During the free trial the online store is in private mode. Before the store can be launched, three steps are listed as required: select a subscription plan, add a store address under Settings > General > Business details, and set up a payment method with test mode deactivated. Launch is done by deactivating Private mode under Online Store > Preferences > Store access. Q3-SH-027; Q3-SH-028; Q3-SH-031
- **Selling during and after the trial.** On a free trial the store is in private mode or its checkout is inactive. To let customers buy, the seller either deactivates private mode or chooses a paid plan, which activates the online store checkout and POS in-person sales. To sell and accept orders during the trial, the seller needs to set up a payment provider. Q3-SH-032; Q3-SH-033; Q3-SH-034; Q3-SH-035

- **Storefront and checkout.** The seller agrees to provide public-facing contact information, terms of service, a refund policy and shipping policies on the store, and to use Shopify Checkout for sales associated with the online store. Q3-SH-K1301; Q3-SH-K1453; Q3-SH-K1455
- **Choosing the store currency.** The store currency is chosen before the first sale; changing it after the first sale goes through Shopify Support. Q3-SH-002
- **Activating Shopify Payments: eligibility.** Only eligible merchants can use Shopify Payments. The business must be located in a supported country (its Designated Country). It must operate a business selling goods and services, and the seller must be at least 18. Certain business types aren't permitted, as listed for each Designated Country. Residents of sanctioned countries may not use it. Q3-SH-K1532; Q3-SH-K1535; Q3-SH-K1536; Q3-SH-P0043; Q3-SH-K1539; Q3-SH-K1540; Q3-SH-P0045; Q3-SH-P0046; Q3-SH-088; Q3-SH-K2268; Q3-SH-A0002

- **Activating Shopify Payments: the steps.** In Settings > Payments, the seller clicks Activate Shopify Payments, enters the required personal, address and identification information, enters the required information about the business and the phone number for customers' bank statements, then submits for verification, and clicks Activate once verification is complete. Only the store owner can activate it. Q3-SH-003; Q3-SH-004; Q3-SH-005; Q3-SH-006; Q3-SH-007; Q3-SH-008; Q3-SH-K1542
- **Two-step authentication.** To use Shopify Payments, the account must be secured with two-step authentication. Payouts might be held until it is activated. The admin's Two-Step authentication setup page under Payments, observed on one store on 5 October 2026, said that without it "You may also have your payouts paused." The saved HTML of the Payments page of another store, observed 30 September 2026, holds, in a dialog, "You need to set-up two-step authentication before adding a bank account." Q3-SH-K1274; Q3-SH-001; Q3-SH-P0158; Q3-SH-103; Q3-SH-107
- **Who and what must be identified.** Shopify and its banking partners collect and verify information about the seller and the business. Every Shopify Payments account must list one individual as the account representative; a company can't be listed. Owners of 25% or more must be listed as beneficial owners. Names must be full legal names as shown on a government-issued ID. PO Box and Private Mail Box addresses can't be used. In the United States, Shopify Balance and Shopify Credit require real home and business addresses. Q3-SH-012; Q3-SH-013; Q3-SH-020; Q3-SH-021; Q3-SH-022; Q3-SH-023; Q3-SH-026; Q3-SH-024; Q3-SH-025
- **Documents.** Documents are uploaded only through the secure banners in the admin. They must be photographed in colour, complete, readable, and match the account details. Q3-SH-015; Q3-SH-016; Q3-SH-019
- **Proof of Liveness.** Depending on the country, the account owner completes a selfie and photo ID check. It is necessary to complete setup and to process transactions with Shopify Payments. Q3-SH-041; Q3-SH-042; Q3-SH-043
- **United States.** For Shopify Payments in the United States, an external payout account must be a full checking account with a US bank, in USD, eligible for ACH transfers. The customer statement name must be 2 to 19 characters and include the shop name, legal entity name, store name or URL. The business type follows from whether the seller has an EIN. Q3-SH-088; Q3-SH-089; Q3-SH-090; Q3-SH-092
- **Europe and the United Kingdom.** Verification requirements are being updated throughout 2026, in line with requirements from the FCA and the Central Bank of Ireland. Anyone listed on the business registry as owning 25% or more must be listed with the Owner role. In the UK, directors listed on the registry must be listed with the Director role. Q3-SH-065; Q3-SH-067; Q3-SH-068
- **Payouts.** After the account is verified, the seller connects a payout bank account. It must be a checking or current account that can receive transfers in a supported payout currency, or a Shopify Balance account. Ownership of the bank account might need verification. That check compares the bank account name with the legal business name or the account representative's name, and may call for a bank statement issued within the last 6 months. Q3-SH-009; Q3-SH-010; Q3-SH-K1639; Q3-SH-P0061; Q3-SH-061; Q3-SH-062; Q3-SH-063; Q3-SH-099
- **Other payment methods.** Some local payment methods need additional approval after Shopify Payments is activated. Local payment methods require Shopify Payments. American Express sets its own address and name requirements on the payment account. For cryptocurrencies, the seller verifies that they are acceptable in their region, and the Shopify Payments account must be in an eligible region. Shop Pay Installments requires Shopify Payments, Shop Pay and a business entity in a listed country. The seller is responsible for activating the payment accounts Shopify creates and complying with their terms. - **Providers and entities.** Providers might enforce rules about which products can be sold. Selling with multiple entities requires Shopify Payments and the Shopify Plus or Shopify Enterprise Commerce plan. Q3-SH-K1530; Q3-SH-K1264; Q3-SH-K1265
- **Particular products.** To sell, the seller must comply with the laws of the jurisdictions of the business and its customers and with the Terms of Service. Alcohol in the United States calls for licenses and age verification for buyers under 21. Hemp and CBD in the US calls for confirmations about the seller and about each product. In Canada, selling cannabis calls for a federal or provincial license. Shopify Payments doesn't support hemp, CBD or THC sales; third-party providers vet stores for them. - **Displaying the store in Shop.** The requirements are: compliance with the Terms of Service and AUP, an active Starter plan or higher, Shopify Payments activated, a chargeback rate under 1% over six months, at least one eligible product, the Online Store channel out of private mode, and no password-control or age-verification apps. - **Financial products and other features.** Shopify Balance requires a US Social Security Number or ITIN and the same information as Shopify Payments. To be eligible for Shopify Capital in a country, the principal place of business and the bank account must be in that country; Capital is applied for by the store owner. Managed Markets requires the Basic plan or higher, an online store and Shopify Payments. Subscriptions on POS require Shopify Payments. - **Parties named.** Shopify and its banking partners collect the information. Payment Processors, Payment Network Rules, the law or Shopify set the Prohibited and Restricted Business categories. American Express sets its own requirements. In the EU/EEA and UK, approval rests with Shopify and, where required, the Acquirer and Payment Networks. Global-e and/or Shopify review Managed Markets eligibility. The Issuing Bank decides Shopify Credit eligibility. Third-party providers decide hemp approvals. - **Evaluation and re-verification.** The business is evaluated when it first applies for Shopify Payments, and risk is monitored throughout. Re-verification is periodic, and the seller may be asked for more information during the account's lifespan. Uploaded documents are reviewed within up to 24 hours. Bank account ownership is checked when a payout account is added or replaced, or when business details change. Q3-SH-K1617; Q3-SH-066; Q3-SH-012; Q3-SH-014; Q3-SH-061
- **What the data model records.** ShopifyPaymentsAccount has fields for whether Shopify Payments setup is completed and whether the account can be onboarded. Q3-SH-086; Q3-SH-087
- **Payout holds.** Payouts might be held until information and documents are verified, while the store can still be used. In most cases customers can still check out, but in some cases the store can't accept new orders. If the maximum number of upload attempts is reached, changes go through Shopify Support. Q3-SH-017; Q3-SH-P0085; Q3-SH-P0090; Q3-SH-018
- **Verification deadlines in Europe and the UK.** A missed deadline pauses payouts, and a second deadline may pause accepting orders through Shopify Payments. Both pauses lift once verification is complete. For accounts created in or after June 2026, payouts are paused at once, charges after 30 days, and payments are refunded after 90 days from the first Shopify Payments order. Payments through other providers aren't affected. Q3-SH-069; Q3-SH-070; Q3-SH-071; Q3-SH-072; Q3-SH-073; Q3-SH-074; Q3-SH-N01
