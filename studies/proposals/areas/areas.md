# The platforms' broad areas, for your marks

**Step R1, 18 October 2026.** This is the areas list your G1 answer asked for, before the detailed work. The files behind it are `areas.tsv`, `depth.tsv` and each platform's `areas-<platform>.tsv`, in this folder.

**What it is.** Each platform's own areas, named in its own words by one short run per platform, merged into 29 areas. Where a merged area's heading is one platform's own name, it says whose. Where no one name covers all the platforms, the heading is mine, and marked as mine.

**What your marks do.** With your marks, this list becomes Sharrwah's whole extent for the design of its first build.
- **Won't do:** the area is outside Sharrwah. The first build's design leaves it out, and no proposal provides for it. Its recorded evidence still serves the questions that draw on it.
- **Must do:** the area stays in. Where none of your eight items covers it, I bring you what reading it would need, and its cost, for you to decide.
- **Unmarked:** it stays in.

**Your eight items, as the questions the work asks:**
- **Q1:** places a store sells in or operates from.
- **Q2:** what a place's rules and the platform's own choices change.
- **Q3:** what a seller must do before doing particular things.
- **Q4:** orders paid outside the platform.
- **Q5:** payment methods: who decides, and where the money goes.
- **Q6:** who can register, and from where.
- **Q7:** currencies.
- **Q8:** how the platform is organized and grows.

## Which platforms each question reads in depth

For each question, some platforms are read in depth, and the others only checked for anything that differs from the in-depth platforms or goes beyond them. Every platform is read for every question.

### Q1: places a store sells in or operates from

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla, BigCommerce.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify's Markets give each country or region its own version of a store, with currencies, catalogs, domains and duties, and its contracting company changes with the seller's billing address: the most worked-out setup by place that is recorded.
  - **Salla:** Salla runs per-country versions of a store, with taxes, currencies, languages and shipping set per market, and the country a store registers from decides the documents it is set up under. It is the Gulf platform with most recorded on this.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce sets currencies, payment providers and tax providers per store and storefront, sells through several storefronts and channels, and changes its contracting company and law with the seller's country of residence: a different design from Shopify's and Salla's.
- **Checked:** Zid, WooCommerce.
  - **Zid:** Zid's recorded countries, payment networks, logistics and taxes resemble Salla's. A check records where Zid differs, such as its contracting company and its operations outside Saudi Arabia.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce is one store on the seller's own site. Its suggestions of payment providers per store country, WooPayments' countries, shipping zones and taxes are recorded; a check records what else it sets per place.

### Q2: what a place's rules and the platform's own choices change

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla, WooCommerce.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records the most of what changes by place for law and licensing: Shopify Payments' companies by country, Balance, Credit and Capital by country and lender, tax calculation, and sanctions and unsupported regions.
  - **Salla:** Salla records the Saudi and Gulf side: taxes per market, verification of the seller's legal entity, its legal entities and contracting company per country, licences and regulated activity, and personal-data rules.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce records restricted businesses and WooPayments' availability by country, personal-data rules, business services for compliance, and taxes the seller sets: the view from a platform that hosts nothing itself.
- **Checked:** Zid, BigCommerce.
  - **Zid:** Zid's contracting company, prohibited activities and sanctions, verification and privacy resemble Salla's. A check records where they differ.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce's contracting company and law by country, BigCommerce Payments, tax providers, data transfer and card security are recorded; a check records what goes beyond the in-depth platforms.

### Q3: what a seller must do before doing particular things

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla, Zid.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records what a seller must meet before payments, payouts, Balance, Credit and Capital: onboarding, identity checks and underwriting, and the countries and businesses it won't serve.
  - **Salla:** Salla records store verification of the seller's legal entity, what Salla Payments and pay-later providers require, and what a plan unlocks.
  - **Zid:** Zid records Zid Pay's activation, identity checks through Nafath, store verification, financing requirements and prohibited activities: requirements set by Zid and by its partners, in a market near Yemen's.
- **Checked:** WooCommerce, BigCommerce.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce's store launch (coming soon), WooPayments' identity checks and restricted businesses are recorded; a check records what differs.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce records little here beyond BigCommerce Payments, fees and the store's profile; a check records what it requires.

### Q4: orders paid outside the platform

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records manual payment methods, orders left unpaid until the seller marks them paid, its transaction fee on them, and cash at its point of sale.
  - **Salla:** Salla records cash on delivery both through its own shipping labels, with the cash remitted to the seller, and through the seller's own courier, and bank transfer shown to buyers: both sides of the contrast the question asks for.
- **Checked:** Zid, WooCommerce, BigCommerce.
  - **Zid:** Zid's cash on delivery, its collection through Zid's shipping, bank transfer and payment status resemble Salla's; a check records where they differ.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce's offline methods (bank transfer, check, cash on delivery) are core and simple; a check records how its orders and refunds treat them.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce's order transactions, payment methods and B2B invoices are recorded; a check records how it handles payment outside the checkout.

### Q5: payment methods: who decides, and where the money goes

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla, BigCommerce.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records its own payment service, third-party providers that come only through approved payments apps, Shop Pay, and where the money goes, through Balance and payouts.
  - **Salla:** Salla records Salla Payments, the outside gateways a seller connects, pay-later providers, the customer wallet, and the e-payments balance and its payouts to the seller's bank.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce keeps a list of embedded payment providers and charges a fee on others, with BigCommerce Payments for eligible sellers: the clearest record of a platform choosing which methods are offered.
- **Checked:** WooCommerce, Zid.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce suggests payment providers for each store country and adds methods through extensions, and holds no balance itself; a check records what goes beyond the in-depth platforms.
  - **Zid:** Zid Pay, Zid Wallet, other gateways and Mazeed's collections resemble Salla's; a check records where they differ.

### Q6: who can register, and from where

- **In depth:** Shopify, Salla.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records who may open an account and from where: business entities, the store owner, identity checks, its contracting company by billing address, and the countries and regions it doesn't support.
  - **Salla:** Salla records the seller's account, verification of the legal entity, its contracting company per country, sanctions and screening, and the countries where it offers its platform.
- **Checked:** Zid, WooCommerce, BigCommerce.
  - **Zid:** Zid serves sellers in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, with Nafath identity checks; a check records what differs from Salla.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce's software runs anywhere, while Woo's services depend on a WordPress.com connection, its contracting company and US sanctions; a check records how that answers who can register.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce's contracting company by country, sanctions rules and store terms are recorded; a check records what differs.

### Q7: currencies

- **In depth:** Shopify, Zid.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records a store currency, prices shown in local currencies through Markets and catalogs, payouts and their currencies, and the currency it bills in.
  - **Zid:** Zid sets a currency for each market, and records the currencies of Zid Pay's deposits and Zid Wallet: a different level from Shopify's store currency.
- **Checked:** Salla, WooCommerce, BigCommerce.
  - **Salla:** Salla's currencies shown to buyers, its markets, and the currency Salla Payments settles in are recorded; a check records what differs.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce states one base currency per store, with more through WooPayments and its payout currencies; a check records it.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce's currencies, price lists and billing currency are recorded; a check records what differs.

### Q8: how the platform is organized and grows

- **In depth:** Shopify, WooCommerce.
  - **Shopify:** Shopify records plans, sales channels, the App Store and its review, apps and point of sale as parts a store adds, and releases features in stages.
  - **WooCommerce:** WooCommerce grows almost entirely through extensions: block extensibility, payment and other extensions, the Woo Marketplace connection and business services. It is the clearest record of a platform built in parts.
- **Checked:** Salla, Zid, BigCommerce.
  - **Salla:** Salla's App Store, point of sale, store app, merchant services and Mahally are recorded; a check records what goes beyond, such as features released by plan and country.
  - **Zid:** Zid's logistics, sales channels, App Market, Mazeed and point of sale are recorded; a check records what differs.
  - **BigCommerce:** BigCommerce's unified billing for apps, channels, App Marketplace and B2B Edition are recorded; a check records what differs.
