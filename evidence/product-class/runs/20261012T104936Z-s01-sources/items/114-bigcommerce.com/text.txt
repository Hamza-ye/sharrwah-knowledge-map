What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is the next evolution of online shopping — AI acts on behalf of the shopper to find, recommend, and even purchase products across platforms. By connecting your catalog with PayPal’s AI chatbot agent, conversations become conversions with one-click checkout.

How does it work?
Sign up to be part of the beta.
Download the PayPal app and connect your product catalog to PayPal’s agentic commerce service, enabling your products to be recommended and purchased in conversational, AI-powered shopping experiences.
Once your catalog is integrated with PayPal’s agentic commerce service, your products are purchasable on emerging AI-powered surfaces (subject to availability within PayPal’s agentic commerce service).
You retain ownership of pricing, promotions, and fulfillment — orders flow directly into BigCommerce. PayPal simply powers the frictionless path to purchase.
Why join the beta?
Tap into PayPal’s new agentic commerce experience and put your products where shoppers are already browsing to unlock incremental revenue opportunities.
Turn intent into sales with conversational, AI-driven product recommendations that guide shoppers from discovery to a trusted, one-click PayPal checkout directly on AI surfaces.
Be a first-mover and adopt new technology with trusted partners by your side — the price of adoption will never be lower. 
Who can join?
US-based BigCommerce customers on an Enterprise Plan license using PayPal
