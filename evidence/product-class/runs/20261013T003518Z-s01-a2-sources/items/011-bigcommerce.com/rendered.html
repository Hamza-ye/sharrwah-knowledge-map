How the fee works

The Open Payment Provider Fee is a monthly fee charged by BigCommerce for orders processed through Open Payment Providers during the billing period.﻿1 It is calculated on GMV attributed to those providers at the order level and appears as a distinct line item on your monthly BigCommerce invoice.

﻿

✅ The fee applies only to orders processed through an Open Payment Provider.

﻿

✅ Orders processed through any of the 21 Embedded Payment Providers below are not subject to the fee.

﻿

✅ If your business uses a mix of providers, the fee applies only to the GMV attributed to Open Payment Providers.

﻿

✅ If your business uses offline orders, that offline GMV counts as Open Payment Provider volume.

﻿

✅ The fee is charged to your business by BigCommerce. It must not be passed through to shoppers at checkout.

﻿

Compliance note: Payment network rules and local laws restrict shopper-facing surcharges in many jurisdictions. Your business is responsible for its own compliance.

How to add or switch a payment provider

﻿Switching providers takes real evaluation. Expect two weeks to four months, end to end, depending on the provider, account underwriting, and reconciliation needs. The four-step process includes:﻿

1. Decide

Compare your current fee exposure to the cost of switching. For some customers the fee is smaller than the switching cost. Additional information can be found in the Help Center about Payment Gateways.﻿﻿
2. Choose﻿
Pick a provider from the list above based on your country, currency, payment methods, and integration needs. Each provider has its own application process.﻿
3. Apply and configure﻿
Apply with your chosen provider, complete underwriting, then add them in your BigCommerce dashboard under Store Setup, Payments.﻿
4. Test and launch﻿
Run test transactions before going live. Review your reconciliation flows. Monitor your first few weeks of orders to confirm everything is working.﻿
Need help along the way? BigCommerce support is available throughout the process. Detailed setup guides are available in the Help Center.﻿﻿
