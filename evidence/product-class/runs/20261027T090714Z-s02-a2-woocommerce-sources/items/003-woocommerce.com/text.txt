Minimum charge amounts

We enforce a minimum amount when creating a charge to ensure that you don’t lose money due to our fees. The minimum amount you can charge depends on the payout currency that the payment would be paid out to.

CURRENCY	MINIMUM CHARGE
AED	2.00
AUD	0.50
BRL	0.50
CAD	0.50
CHF	0.50
CZK	150
DKK	2.50
EUR	0.50
GBP	0.30
HKD	4.00
HRK	0.50
HUF	175.00
INR	0.50
JPY	50
MXN	10.00
MYR	2.00
NOK	3.00
NZD	0.50
PLN	2.00
RON	2.00
SEK	3.00
SGD	0.50
THB	10.00
USD	0.50

If you have a product that costs less than these amounts, and a customer attempts to purchase it with WooPayments, they will see an error message.

Maximum charge amounts

The maximum amount you can charge in a single transaction is 999999.99. For example, the USD maximum is 999,999.99, the EUR maximum is 999,999.99, and so on for every supported currency.

The one exception to this rule is the Indonesian rupiah (IDR). In that case, the maximum charge amount is 9,999,999,999.99.

Certain local payment methods might also have different maximums. If that’s the case, those will be noted on this page.
