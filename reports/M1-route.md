## 7. Why this is established practice

- **One baseline.**
  - **The definition:** a baseline is "A specification or product that has been formally reviewed and agreed upon, that thereafter serves as the basis for further development, and that can be changed only through formal change control procedures". Source: ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2008, as [SEBoK's glossary](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Baseline_%28glossary%29) gives it, read 25 October 2026.
  - **Here:** [`METHOD.md`:40](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/49fc0d31e125701126678f7c063c9b39edcdd2fd/METHOD.md?plain=1#L40) cites IEEE 828 for baselines.
  - *My reading:* a text whose changes sit in amendments beside the parts they change, and contradict them in one place ([item 1](#1-can-the-current-method-be-reconciled-as-it-stands)), is hard to read as one agreed text.
- **Management of change.**
  - **The rule:** OSHA's process safety standard requires written procedures "to manage changes" to "process chemicals, technology, equipment, and procedures". Before any change, they address "The technical basis for the proposed change" and its "Impact of change on safety and health". Source: 29 CFR 1910.119(l), read at [Cornell's Legal Information Institute](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/29/1910.119), 25 October 2026.
  - **Here:** applying it to a design method is my reading, as it was in part two ([`M1-plan-changes.md`:66](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/49fc0d31e125701126678f7c063c9b39edcdd2fd/reports/M1-plan-changes.md?plain=1#L66)). The list does this for every change at once.
- **What agents read.**
  - **Anthropic's guidance:** ["Effective context engineering for AI agents"](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) (29 September 2025) says that "good context engineering means finding the smallest possible set of high-signal tokens". It also says that "as the number of tokens in the context window increases", "the model's ability to accurately recall information from that context decreases".
  - *My reading:* a related failure happened here. Items of the plan of 24 October that weren't carried into the files the resume path reads were lost at restart ([`M1-plan-trace.md`:28](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/49fc0d31e125701126678f7c063c9b39edcdd2fd/reports/M1-plan-trace.md?plain=1#L28), [:58](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/49fc0d31e125701126678f7c063c9b39edcdd2fd/reports/M1-plan-trace.md?plain=1#L58)).
- **Try before scaling:** Boehm 1988, as [the first plan cites it](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/2842819db926841221d9efbd22e2f2c58db27208/PLAN.md?plain=1#L291), and the plan's [kill rule](https://github.com/Hamza-ye/sharrwah-knowledge-map/blob/2842819db926841221d9efbd22e2f2c58db27208/PLAN.md?plain=1#L130).
