# Q3 · Salla

## [1]

- Registering a store: the usage agreement (اتفاقية الاستخدام) asks that the merchant be at least eighteen, or bring a guardian's consent (صك الولاية). It asks for a commercial registration (سجل تجاري) or a freelance document (وثيقة عمل حر), an Absher account, a Saudi bank account and a Saudi mobile number. An individual provides national ID and freelance data; an establishment, company or charity provides its commercial registration and other documents Salla asks for. The merchant declares that it met the official requirements. Salla may refuse to register a store, and a merchant whose membership was cancelled may not use Salla. Q3-SA-043; Q3-SA-044; Q3-SA-P0075; Q3-SA-049; Q3-SA-048; Q3-SA-045; Q3-SA-046
- The help centre says a merchant can start on Salla with a Saudi commercial registration or a freelance certificate, and foreigners need an e-commerce licence from the Ministry of Investment. A foreign merchant's store is verified only with a Saudi commercial registration. Q3-SA-032; Q3-SA-031; Q3-SA-035; Q3-SA-005
- Store verification (توثيق المتجر, Store Verification, `/payments/kyc`) is called the last step in launching a store, and a basic condition for running it officially and for activating payment and shipping services. Q3-SA-026; Q3-SA-028; Q3-SA-017; Q3-SA-012; Q3-SA-K0076
- Verification asks for a valid ID or iqama matching Absher, the owner's Absher mobile, and the entity's document. A freelance document is for Saudi citizens only. A company or establishment uses a commercial registration, for citizens and residents; a charity uses its certificate. It also asks for a stamped IBAN certificate in a matching name, and beneficial-owner data for owners and managers. Q3-SA-013; Q3-SA-014; Q3-SA-027; Q3-SA-K0084; Q3-SA-K0086
- The dashboard schema `merchants_saudi_kyc` has the states `kyc_requirements_preview`, `store_owner_details`, `government_documents`, `bank_account`, `review_confirm` and `finish`. Its entity types are `single`, `company`, `institution`, `charity` and `government`, and it requires a `truth_declaration`. Q3-SA-055; Q3-SA-056; Q3-SA-057
- The payout bank account: an individual may use a personal or business account in the owner's name. An establishment or company needs a business account in its own name, linked to its commercial registration. Q3-SA-009; Q3-SA-010; Q3-SA-018; Q3-SA-025; Q3-SA-K1563; Q3-SA-K1568
- In the UAE, a store prepares a trade licence or commercial registration, a memorandum where ownership isn't shown, a tax certificate only if VAT-registered, an Emirates ID and passport per owner, and an IBAN certificate. The account must be in AED at a UAE bank. Q3-SA-072; Q3-SA-K1564; Q3-SA-P0160
- In one store's onboarding (store "Store 1", plan basic, observed 8 October 2026), the country question says the choice of country sets the store's legal requirements, and asks for the country whose government documents the store uses: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain or another country. The question is mandatory. Q3-SA-084; Q3-SA-085
- In the same store, the onboarding steps `kyc` and `launch` (تدشين المتجر) were pending and not optional, while `salla-point` was optional and skipped. Its store answer showed "plan": "basic", "status": "active", "verified": false, and null tax, commercial and freelance numbers. Q3-SA-086; Q3-SA-087
- The verification page of an unverified Basic store, observed 8 October 2026, listed two paths: a freelance document, for Saudi citizens only, and a company or establishment, for citizens and residents, with a commercial registration and a business bank account linked to it. Q3-SA-088
- In that store's dashboard, splitting the plan's price into 3 payments without fees showed "يجب توثيق متجرك لتتمكن من استخدام الدفع بالتقسيط." Q3-SA-083
- To comply with the e-commerce law, the help centre lists the registration or freelance number and tax number, the Business Platform (منصة الأعمال) link, address and contacts, terms, privacy and return policies on at least two active pages, payment methods through approved banks, and Arabic. A commercial registration holder adds the store to that registration within 30 days of creating it. The Business Platform certificate has its own requirements. Q3-SA-034; Q3-SA-036; Q3-SA-030; Q3-SA-038; Q3-SA-K0093

## [2]

- Salla Payments (مدفوعات سلة) requires a Plus, Pro or Special plan, a completed verification and activation by Salla's team, reviewed with the verification. The Basic plan offers cash on delivery and bank transfer. A store can prepare products before verification, but payment gateways and receiving funds need it. Activating e-payments includes accepting the selling policy (سياسة البيع). Q3-SA-P0060; Q3-SA-K1526; Q3-SA-K1527; Q3-SA-P0068; Q3-SA-P0099; Q3-SA-016; Q3-SA-K0091; Q3-SA-K1532
- In an unverified Basic store's payments module, observed 8 October 2026, verification was "uncompleted" with "blocks_payment_activation": true. Mada, credit card, Apple Pay and STC Pay were each "blocked_by": "requires_paid_plan", and the online gateway labelled "noon" gave the same upgrade message. Q3-SA-073; Q3-SA-074; Q3-SA-075
- In the same store's wallet, the store was neither documented nor verified and had no active bank account. Withdrawal was disabled for "store_documents_required", with a message that verification must be completed first, and the e-payments wallet showed "is_balance_frozen": true. Q3-SA-076; Q3-SA-077; Q3-SA-078
- In the store real-merchant (Pro), observed 12 October 2026, verification was "completed" with "blocks_payment_activation": false, "store_documented": true, "kyc_complete": false and "has_approved_epayment_request": true. The "noon" gateway's verification status was "completed". Its wallet showed the store documented and verified, with an active bank account. E-payments were paid out every 48 hours above 100 SAR, and cash on delivery every Sunday and Wednesday. Q3-SA-079; Q3-SA-080; Q3-SA-081; Q3-SA-082
- Salla Payments can't be activated while a Tap link is active; charities link an external gateway instead. Q3-SA-P0072; Q3-SA-P0073
- Payouts are settled under the payment services contract (عقد خدمات الدفع) signed during verification. Withdrawing the store balance and transferring the e-payments balance require verification, and payouts go to the account given at verification. The minimum is 100 riyals, one dinar in Kuwait, and none in another article. Q3-SA-K1533; Q3-SA-K0071; Q3-SA-P0162; Q3-SA-K1568; Q3-SA-K1544; Q3-SA-P0062; Q3-SA-P0063
- Tabby, Tamara and external gateways are flagged for Plus, Pro and Special. The UAE Tabby link needs verification, a Tabby merchant account, its keys and an eligible plan; Tabby's fees and payouts follow the merchant's agreement with Tabby. Q3-SA-K1617; Q3-SA-K1614; Q3-SA-K1642; Q3-SA-069; Q3-SA-070; Q3-SA-K1654
- Salla Point card payments require a Geidea account. Cash on delivery through the merchant's own carrier uses a commission agreed with the carrier. Q3-SA-K1436; Q3-SA-K1434; Q3-SA-K1599
- On the Basic plan only ready-made products (منتج جاهز) can be added; digital products, cards, food, made-to-order, bundles and bookings need Plus or Pro. Q3-SA-029
- The selling policy forbids gambling or random boxes, harmful products, pirated content, sexual content, fortune-telling, pre-selling unreleased products, selling without proof of sale and reselling shipping labels. Article 20 of the agreement gives a shorter list "على سبيل المثال لا الحصر". Q3-SA-054; Q3-SA-053
- Products needing an official licence must have it disclosed. Salla's blog names food, cosmetics, supplements, imported goods and pharmacy products, and says a store on Salla doesn't replace product licences. Q3-SA-047; Q3-SA-041; Q3-SA-042; Q3-SA-039; Q3-SA-040; Q3-SA-N02
- A freelance document must cover the products sold, and the KYC schema requires the registration's activity to match the store's. Q3-SA-022; Q3-SA-058
- Mahally (محلي) needs a paid plan, verification and acceptance of its terms. The wholesale market (سوق الجملة) needs Plus or Pro and verification, and admits a limited number. Salla Capital needs a passport copy and data-sharing consent. Q3-SA-061; Q3-SA-063; Q3-SA-064; Q3-SA-065; Q3-SA-066
- The ZATCA link needs ZATCA registration, an approved commercial registration, verification, a complete main-branch address and Pro or Special, and is for Saudi stores. VAT registration is mandatory from 375 thousand riyals a year. Q3-SA-071; Q3-SA-K1937; Q3-SA-K1940; Q3-SA-K1927

## [4]

- Who sets it: the help centre calls Salla verification Salla's own initiative, not a substitute for government verification. It calls it an internal step for gateways and the bank account, unlike the government Business Platform licence. Rejections serve payment gateways' and government requirements. The law-derived obligations come from the e-commerce law and the Ministry of Commerce. Q3-SA-004; Q3-SA-037; Q3-SA-015; Q3-SA-024; Q3-SA-033
- When it is checked: Salla's team reviews each request within 24 to 48 hours, and Absher allows three attempts a day. Repeated wrong data, or documents not tied to the owner, can lead to final rejection. An ID suspended for violations can't open new stores. A verified store shows "مكتمل" on payment settings. Q3-SA-021; Q3-SA-K0087; Q3-SA-003; Q3-SA-008; Q3-SA-019; Q3-SA-020; Q3-SA-060; Q3-SA-059; Q3-SA-011
- While it isn't met: on the Basic plan verification is a basic requirement to start selling. Payments and settlement are suspended after a period (المهلة) whose length wasn't found. Salla Payments receives payments only after verification. Q3-SA-001; Q3-SA-002; Q3-SA-P0136; Q3-SA-N01; Q3-SA-P0070
- A rejection leaves the store's settings and products intact and pauses payment activation. A verified store updating its data keeps taking orders and payments, and keeps its status if the update is rejected. Q3-SA-023; Q3-SA-006; Q3-SA-007
- Payouts may be delayed while a complaint is open. A violation (المخالفات) must be corrected within 24 hours to avoid suspension. Q3-SA-K0073; Q3-SA-K2530
- Under the agreement, Salla may ask for documents proving identity or ownership of funds at any time. For breaches it may warn, stop the service, close the store, block access, cancel unconfirmed accounts, or return pending e-payments to consumers. Q3-SA-051; Q3-SA-050; Q3-SA-052; Q3-SA-P0083
- Mahally shows a message when a condition isn't met. Salla Capital appears only to owners of eligible stores, and an offer isn't final approval. Q3-SA-062; Q3-SA-067; Q3-SA-068
- Still open: the agreement clause naming verification (Q3-SA-G0009); where the payment services contract is signed, with whom, and why (Q3-SA-G0176; Q3-SA-G0177; Q3-SA-G0179); who holds Salla Payments funds (Q3-SA-H0002); and when selling-policy compliance is checked (Q3-SA-N03).
