# Q2: What a place's rules and the platform's own choices change for a seller there, a draft proposal

## 1. How the platforms handle it

### [1]

- Shopify lists unsupported countries and regions, among them "The Crimea region of Ukraine, and the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People’s Republics in Ukraine": no individual or business there may create a Shopify account, and products originating there and orders shipping there are prohibited. Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-SH-K2276; Q2-SH-K2278; Q2-SH-K2279

The two match on the same term; Shopify's list matches theirs by role. Q2-WC-K0526; Q2-BC-K0430; Q2-BC-K0431; Q2-SH-K2275

Q2-ZD-K0252; Q2-ZD-K0254; Q2-ZD-N01; Q2-ZD-G0055; Q2-SH-K2268

## 2. Proposed: names, structure and behaviour

- Everything here is Sharrwah's proposed design, not what a platform does: one mechanism, a **Place rule** on a **Place**, at whatever level the evidence shows things differing. Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-WC-K0496; Q2-BC-K0425
- **Place**: a node in one tree, a group of countries, a country or an area within one, under a root standing for every place not set up otherwise. A node with no setting of its own takes its nearest ancestor's. Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-WC-K0407; Q2-WC-K0496; Q2-WC-055
  - Draws on: areas within a country (Shopify's Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk; WooPayments' states, Washington D.C. and Puerto Rico), a group (WooCommerce's Designated Countries), and defaults for unlisted places, by row (Shopify's all other jurisdictions; BigCommerce's "Any other jurisdiction") or by region (Stripe's Regional Terms), which the tree's inheritance combines. Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-WC-K0407; Q2-WC-K0496; Q2-SH-K1248; Q2-BC-K0425; Q2-WC-055
  - Why: rule 1, with no level fixed in advance. Not taken: a flat list of countries (Salla's onboarding options; Zid Pay's two countries), which holds neither areas nor groups. Q2-SA-063; Q2-ZD-K0005
- **Place reference**: which of a party's places a **Place rule** reads, each kept apart: a seller's registered country, location, document-issuing country, billing address and residence; a buyer's billing country and destination; a product's origin; a store's selling regions. Q2-WC-K0402; Q2-SH-K1243; Q2-SA-062; Q2-WC-029; Q2-SH-K2278
  - Draws on: WooPayments' registered country beside WooCommerce's contracting company by residence; Shopify's contracting party by billing address beside Shopify Payments' location in its Designated Country; Salla's issuing country (observed); the buyer's billing country (WooCommerce), and destinations, product origin and selling regions (Shopify). Q2-WC-K0402; Q2-WC-K0499; Q2-SH-K1243; Q2-SH-K1536; Q2-SA-062; Q2-WC-029; Q2-SH-K2279; Q2-SH-K2278; Q2-SH-K1745
  - Why: rule 2, the place a rule reads is likely to change, so it is a field of the rule. Not taken: one store country for every rule, since one platform reads two seller places for two services. Q2-WC-K0402; Q2-WC-K0499
- **Place rule**: one thing Sharrwah offers, restricts, withholds, requires or does differently at a **Place**, with its **Place reference**, the point where it acts (sign-up, verification, payment setup, storefront, checkout, shipping, payout, data storage), its source, its setter, its stated reason and its dates. Q2-SH-K2262; Q2-ZD-K0251; Q2-WC-021; Q2-SA-061
  - Draws on: the sources the platforms name (law, payment networks, the platform, processors and their financial service providers, the place of the activity), a provider's dated change (Przelewy24, not checked on Zid or BigCommerce), and a reason shown where the rule acts (Salla's onboarding, observed). Why: rule 3, the data model is expensive to change after launch, so these are fields from the first build; how reasons show is left to the build. Q2-SH-K2262; Q2-WC-K0416; Q2-ZD-K0251; Q2-WC-021; Q2-SA-061
- **Restricted places**: lists of places where Sharrwah refuses business, each entry with its regime and its effects (opening an account, reaching a storefront, a product's origin, an order's destination, using a service), held per **Contracting entity** and per service. Screening named persons is a step whose doer is set per company. Q2-SH-K2276; Q2-SH-K2279; Q2-ZD-K0254; Q2-BC-K0431
  - Draws on: Shopify's effects; Zid Pay's differing Saudi and Kuwaiti lists; Shopify Payments' own sanctions term beside Shopify's list; BigCommerce's screening by the merchant. Why: rules 3 and 4, as which regimes bind Sharrwah depends on where its company is based and on its providers. Not taken: one list for the whole platform (Shopify's; WooCommerce's), since Zid's lists differ by contracting place. Q2-SH-K2278; Q2-ZD-K0252; Q2-ZD-K0254; Q2-SH-K2268; Q2-WC-K0526
- **Contracting entity**: the Sharrwah company a seller contracts with, per **Place** on the **Place reference** its terms choose, and per service (the platform, a payment service, a regulated activity), each row with its governing law, forum and prevailing language. The root's row covers every other place. Q2-SH-K1243; Q2-SH-K1615; Q2-BC-K0419; Q2-BC-K0425
  - Draws on: Shopify's table by billing address and its Canadian company for regulated payments; BigCommerce's table with law per row; WooCommerce's split by residence; Salla's and Zid's single Saudi company, here a one-row table; the prevailing text (Arabic at Salla, English at BigCommerce). Q2-SH-K1248; Q2-SH-K1615; Q2-BC-K0425; Q2-WC-K0499; Q2-SA-033; Q2-ZD-K0212; Q2-SA-030; Q2-BC-015
  - Why: rule 3, legal structure is expensive to change, so the table exists from the first build; rule 1, a company for a new place is then set up, not built. Not taken: one company fixed for every place, as Salla's agreement and its en-ae page read. Q2-SA-033; Q2-SA-020; Q2-BC-K0425
- **Provider**: an outside company whose service plugs in (payments, instalments, shipping, tax, identity checks, financing, messaging, hosting), with its own **Availability**, **Place rules** on the seller's place, the buyer's place, the currency and the seller's legal form. The provider behind a service at a place can change without the service changing. Q2-SH-K1563; Q2-SH-K1561; Q2-WC-029; Q2-ZD-K0042
  - Draws on: Shopify's changeable processors per Designated Country; Stripe's entity per account country; availability by the buyer's place and currency (Shopify, WooCommerce), the store's country and currency (BigCommerce) and legal form (WooPayments in the UAE; Zid Pay's KNET); gateways by country (Zid). Why: rule 2, each provider behind its own part; rule 1. Q2-SH-K1563; Q2-SH-K1561; Q2-WC-P0108; Q2-SH-K1743; Q2-BC-038; Q2-WC-K0411; Q2-ZD-K0087
  - Sharrwah's own choices among providers, one turning another off (Zid Pay; Salla Payments, observed) or a fee on others (Shopify; BigCommerce), are **Place rules** whose source is Sharrwah; whether to have any is a company decision. Q2-ZD-K0088; Q2-SA-K1653; Q2-SH-K1337; Q2-BC-K0143
- **Provider agreement**: the seller's own agreement with a **Provider**, named per **Place**. Sharrwah is not a party by default: Shopify says it isn't party to its processors' terms, and the other four name the seller's agreement as one with the provider. Q2-SH-K1580; Q2-WC-P0103; Q2-BC-P0040; Q2-SA-004; Q2-ZD-K0347
  - Provided for, not by default: Sharrwah as a party, as a Shopify affiliate acts as Payment Facilitator in certain Designated Countries. If that would hold sellers' money through Sharrwah, by Sharrwah or by a payment company in an account opened through Sharrwah, it goes live only by the founder's decision. Why: rule 3, partner contracts; rule 4. Q2-SH-K1603; Q2-SH-K1580
- **Seller requirements**: documents and checks per **Place** on the seller's **Place reference**, per legal form and per service, each with its source; identity checks run through identity **Providers** per place. Q2-SA-K0083; Q2-ZD-K0003; Q2-ZD-K0263
  - Draws on: Salla's documents by country and its freelance document for Saudi citizens only; Zid Pay's documents by entity type, investment licence for foreign companies and Nafath check in the provider's name. They sit in the place's set, apart from the terms' text, where Salla's agreement and UAE help disagree. Why: rules 1 and 2. Q2-SA-K0084; Q2-ZD-017; Q2-ZD-K0263; Q2-SA-035; Q2-SA-K1964
- **Restricted goods**: what may not be sold, or only through named providers, per source and per **Place reference** (the seller's place, the buyer's destination), with its effect: refuse the store or a provider, send a category to providers that accept it, or hide a product in a destination. Q2-SH-K2262; Q2-SH-002; Q2-SH-019
  - Draws on: the sources Shopify, WooPayments and Zid Pay name; Shopify's list varying by region; Shopify's hemp providers and WooPayments' Square or Viva Wallet for CBD; Managed Markets' hiding (not checked on Zid). Why: rule 1. Q2-SH-K1541; Q2-WC-K0416; Q2-ZD-K0251; Q2-WC-K0417; Q2-SH-019
  - Not taken: leaving legality to the seller alone, as BigCommerce's Highly Regulated Products terms do, since rules Sharrwah knows from providers and places would then not act at sign-up or checkout. Q2-BC-K0460
- **Buyer-place rules**: **Place rules** on the buyer's place at storefront and checkout (the payment methods and products shown, required address fields, the consent banner, the message explaining a refusal), some fixed by Sharrwah from a place's law, some set by the seller per place. Q2-SH-032; Q2-SA-K0800; Q2-SH-K2134
  - Draws on: Shopify's address fields by the customer's country, which can't be changed; Salla's national address for customers in Saudi Arabia, which the seller may turn off at its own risk (observed); Zid's address fields set by the seller per country; privacy settings per country (Shopify) and per storefront (BigCommerce). Why: rule 1. Q2-SH-032; Q2-SA-K0798; Q2-ZD-022; Q2-SH-K2134; Q2-BC-K0205
- **Data location**: where a store's data, orders and customers' personal data are held, per **Place** on the seller's **Place reference**, the root's location by default, shown to the seller; with, per place, who is controller and who processor, and the basis for any transfer abroad. Q2-SH-K2321; Q2-SH-031; Q2-BC-031; Q2-BC-K0436
  - Draws on: Shopify's European storage by default, shown in its admin; BigCommerce's regional hosting and its processor and controller roles; WooCommerce's controller by service and location; Zid's transfers under safeguards. Why: rule 3, where data sits is part of the data model and infrastructure, expensive to change after launch, so data is placeable by region from the first build. Not taken: one fixed location, as Shopify and BigCommerce set it per place. Q2-SH-K2321; Q2-BC-031; Q2-BC-K0436; Q2-WC-K0502; Q2-ZD-K0283
- **Payout settings**: per payment **Provider** and **Place**, the bank account's country and currency, the schedule, the minimum and the holiday calendar. Q2-SA-K1964; Q2-ZD-P0034
  - Draws on: Salla's UAE bank in dirhams; Zid Pay's deposits stopping on official Saudi holidays; Salla's transfers subject to the Saudi Central Bank. Why: rule 3, money flows; rule 4. Q2-SA-K1964; Q2-ZD-P0034; Q2-SA-P0090
  - The guard: any arrangement holding sellers' money through Sharrwah, by Sharrwah or by a payment company in an account opened through Sharrwah, is provided for only as an option left off, going live at a place only by the founder's decision. Q2-SA-P0090
- **Tax service**: tax calculation and links to tax authorities as **Providers** per **Place**, on the seller's place or the order's destination. Q2-SH-K1865; Q2-SH-K1867; Q2-BC-K0381; Q2-SA-K1936
  - Why: rule 2; the rest is left to the build (rule 3). Not taken: one built-in engine for every place, as Shopify Tax's calculations cover only some destinations. Q2-BC-K0381; Q2-SH-K1867

## 3. What can plug in, what is set per what, and who sets it

- Plugs in: payment processors per place (Shopify's Payment Processors; Stripe's entities by account country) and gateways listed by country (Zid's Tap). Q2-SH-K1563; Q2-WC-P0108; Q2-ZD-K0087
- Plugs in: providers for categories the platform's own service refuses (Shopify's hemp providers; Square or Viva Wallet for CBD on WooPayments). Q2-SH-002; Q2-WC-K0417
- Plugs in: tax engines (BigCommerce's Tax Provider API), links to a tax authority (Salla's ZATCA link, Saudi stores only), identity checks in a provider's name (Zid Pay's Nafath check), hosting regions (BigCommerce's on Google Cloud), and logistics companies the seller contracts with (Zid). Q2-BC-K0381; Q2-SA-K1936; Q2-ZD-K0263; Q2-BC-032; Q2-ZD-K0347
- Set per **Place** at any level, none fixed in advance: an area within a country, a country, a group, and a default for every other place. Q2-SH-K2275; Q2-WC-K0496; Q2-BC-K0425
- Set per seller's **Place reference**: the contracting company (billing address at Shopify, residence at WooCommerce), the platform's payment service (located; registered), required documents (their issuing country), data location, tax services. Q2-SH-K1243; Q2-WC-K0499; Q2-WC-K0402; Q2-SA-K0083; Q2-SH-K2321; Q2-SH-K1865
- Set per buyer's place, with the currency: payment methods shown, products shown, address fields required; per store's country and currency together: BigCommerce's local methods. Q2-WC-029; Q2-SH-019; Q2-SH-032; Q2-BC-038
