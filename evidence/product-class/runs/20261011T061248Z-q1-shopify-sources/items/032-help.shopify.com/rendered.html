# International sales tools

International sales tools can help you identify, set up, launch, optimize, and manage your international markets - all from a single store.

International sales tools are centralized for your business to help you manage and expand your global sales. You can create customized online shopping experiences for different customer segments. You can also create markets in Shopify to target specific countries or regions, or you can group countries and regions together to simplify your expansion efforts.

For businesses with globally dispersed teams that independently manage operations in their local market, you might prefer to sell cross-border using multiple stores along with the ability to sell and get paid in different currencies. Learn more about [managing your store and payout currencies](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/store-currency/index).

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>The European Price Indication Directive (PID) establishes a number of requirements for merchants selling to customers in the European Economic Area (EEA). Among other things, when you announce any price reductions, this law requires you to display the lowest price previously charged for a product within the last 30 days, including any promotional prices during that period. If your primary market is in the EEA, then compare-at prices are always displayed. For non-EEA primary markets that use international sales tools to reach EEA customers, compare-at-prices are hidden by default, but can be displayed from the Shopify admin. This content provides general information to merchants regarding the European Price Indication Directive and is for informational purposes only. This content doesn&#39;t contain, and isn&#39;t meant to provide, legal advice. Learn more about the <a href="/en/manual/compliance/legal/pricing-indication-directive">European Price Indication Directive</a>.</p>
</aside>
