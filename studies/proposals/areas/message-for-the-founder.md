On how the recording runs work:

7. Runs get the questions without your items or expectations, and never open your messages or decisions.
8. A run first picks which recorded topics bear on its question. Only those are copied to it.
9. The pilot's statements are offered to every question's runs, not only to the payment methods question's.
10. Copied statements keep their numbers, so a run's summary never points at the wrong one.
11. Each question's sentences are numbered, so every run counts them alike.
12. Reading is capped for each sentence of a question: at most 10 help pages, 5 search results (10 where no help map is held) and 5 live pages. A run stops early once 5 pages in a row add nothing.
13. Detailed reading is kept for sentences about money flows, legal structure, the data model or partner contracts.
14. A provider's own contract, such as Tabby's or Moyasar's, is read only if the platform's terms make it part of the seller's agreement. Otherwise it is recorded as not read, with its address.
15. "Not found" rests on two searches, not one for each country, plan and arrangement it names.
16. Live pages a run reads are captured after the run, before anything cites them.
17. The search now reads Shopify's Help Center in both languages and WooCommerce's own code, and the checker reads your saved PDFs.
