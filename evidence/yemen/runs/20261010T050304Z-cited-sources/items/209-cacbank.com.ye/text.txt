Visa Net Cards

 

Visa Net is a prepaid card dedicated by CAC Bank for customers whom shop online; with a high security and flexibility, and many other additional features.

Service Features:

Issuing the card without the need to open an account.
Receipt the card at the same time.
Low fees.
You can request for obtaining the card from any branch or office of CAC bank.
Replenishing the card whenever balance over at any time.

Terms and Documents Required to Obtain the Service:

Providing the original ID card (valid electronic ID or passport for foreigners).
Fill and sign the application form.
Card issuance fee is only $10.
Visa Net Card is being used to buy from internet only, customer cannot use this card to buy from POS, or withdraw from ATM.
