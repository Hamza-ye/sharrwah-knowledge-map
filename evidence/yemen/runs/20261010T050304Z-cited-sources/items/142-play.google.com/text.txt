About this app
arrow_forward
The revolutionary ONE Cash app for agents and merchants is the safest and easiest solution available across Yemen where you can manage all your finances with a touch of a button through your smartphone.

Inspire change and positively impact the lives of those around you by registering with ONE Cash. Spare them and yourself the never-ending problems that come with cash.

As a merchant, ONE Cash empowers you to manage your money safely and effortlessly by the end of each business day while minimizing the risk of theft whether while counting or moving your money. The more you utilize the following services from ONE Cash, the closer you get to your own financial freedom:
- Receive payments for goods purchased
- Make payments to other merchants and business accounts
- Cash out
- Airtime and internet top up
- Transfer between wallets and bank accounts
- Full or partial goods refund
- Business cash in
- Transfer funds
- Transfer from business wallets to bank accounts and vice versa

As an agent, you can unlock your full potential and secure yourself additional income by offering the following services to ONE Cash users:
- Customer registration
- Customer account activation
- Customer cash in
- Customer cash out
- Money vouchers redeem

You can also experience the financial freedom that ONE Cash offers you as an agent through the following services:
- Bill payment: Pay utility bills including water bills, Electricity bills from public and private power station companies in addition to internet and landline bills.
- Buy Airtime & bundles: You can now buy Airtime from any major telecom network in Yemen (Yemen Mobile, MTN & Sabafon) from the convenience of your smartphone.
- Transfers from wallets to bank accounts and vice versa
- Purchasing e-money

At ONE Cash, we work day and night to ensure that the possibilities that come with such a revolutionary solution are endless. Our ONE and only goal is to empower Yemenis to enjoy their financial freedom by overcoming the obstacles that come with cash money.
