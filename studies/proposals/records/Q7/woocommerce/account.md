# Q7 on WooCommerce: what it shows, sorted against the Shopify and Zid accounts

## [1]

### Store currency
- **differs** (role): WooCommerce allows one base currency per store. The `woocommerce_currency` setting "controls what currency prices are listed at in the catalog and which currency gateways will take payments in", and WooCommerce uses the base currency to display product prices and record new orders. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1934 ("Shopify names a store currency"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-002 ("The help defines"). Q7-WC-K0745 Q7-WC-K0746 Q7-WC-K0747 Q7-WC-024
- **differs** (role): All data in the Analytics menu is converted into the store's default currency; Analytics > Orders can be filtered by currency and has a Customer currency column. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-074 ("Reports can be viewed"). Q7-WC-021 Q7-WC-022
- **differs** (identifier): The REST API has a `data-currencies` route, and an order's `currency` is "Currency the order was created with, in ISO format", one field per order; the order table holds `currency varchar(10)`. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K2893 ("In the Admin GraphQL API"), Q7-SH-124 ("An order records both"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-005 ("The Merchant API"), Q7-ZD-008 ("Each order records"). Q7-WC-K0750 Q7-WC-055 Q7-WC-056

### Currency shown to buyers and paid by them
- **differs** (term): WooPayments names a **customer currency**, "displayed to customers while they browse your store and the currency they are charged in", also called the "presentment currency". By default it is the store currency; WooPayments' multi-currency can change it. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1941 ("A market's currency"), Q7-SH-122 ("In the Admin GraphQL API"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0300 ("With مدفوعات زد active"). Q7-WC-002 Q7-WC-003 Q7-WC-K0754 Q7-WC-K0752
- **differs** (role): Core needs a multi-currency extension to show or charge in another currency. WooPayments gives free multi-currency, letting customers pick from a list the seller defines; enabling it changes only how prices are shown, not payouts. A Currency Converter Widget changes display only and still charges the base currency. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1937 ("Only stores whose primary gateway"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0300 ("With مدفوعات زد active"). Q7-WC-K0751 Q7-WC-010 Q7-WC-011 Q7-WC-028 Q7-WC-029
- **differs** (role): WooPayments can accept payments in over 100 currencies; no list was read. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-135 ("Shopify publishes a list"). Q7-WC-001
- **differs** (role): Local payment methods show only when the customer billing country and customer currency meet a table, from Alipay to WeChat Pay (Bancontact, EPS, iDEAL | Wero in EUR; GrabPay SGD; P24 EUR or PLN). Alipay's checkout currency must match the WooPayments account currency. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-089 ("Within Shopify Payments"). Q7-WC-036 Q7-WC-037 Q7-WC-038 Q7-WC-039 Q7-WC-040 Q7-WC-041 Q7-WC-042 Q7-WC-043 Q7-WC-044 Q7-WC-045 Q7-WC-046
- **differs** (term): PayPal Standard checks the store currency against a 27-currency list, and is disabled with "PayPal Standard does not support your store currency." Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-100 ("With Adyen"). Q7-WC-058 Q7-WC-059
- **differs** (role): Card readers and Tap to Pay collect only in each country's default currency; core treats in-person payments as eligible only for US stores in USD and Canadian stores in CAD. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-072 ("Shopify POS sells"). Q7-WC-047 Q7-WC-060
- **differs** (role): WooCommerce's locale data moves Bulgaria from BGN to EUR on 2026-01-01; nothing read shows both currencies displayed together. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-115 ("Between August 8, 2025"). Q7-WC-051
- **differs** (role): The Payments > Transactions page lists transactions regardless of currency, with amounts in the payout currency and a tooltip for the customer-currency amount. Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-023 ("The زد باي transactions table"). Q7-WC-007
- **not in what was read:** a service selling abroad as merchant of record in buyers' currencies. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-184 ("Under Managed Markets"). Q7-WC-N01
- **not in what was read:** cryptocurrency or stablecoin payment or settlement. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-P0016 ("Under the Shopify Payments terms"). Q7-WC-N03
- **not in what was read:** fixed-amount discounts limited to the store currency. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-111 ("Reports can be viewed"). Q7-WC-N09
- **not in what was read:** a currency field per product, and amounts passed to providers in a currency's smallest unit. Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-014 ("The Merchant API"), Q7-ZD-024 ("In the Zid Payments Open APIs"). Q7-WC-N07 Q7-WC-N08
- **not in what was read:** guidance to developers on converting order totals between currencies, and a cash on delivery limit stated in a currency. Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0304 ("Partners are told"), Q7-ZD-K0120 ("The cash on delivery limit"). Q7-WC-N10 Q7-WC-N11

### Currency paid out to the seller
- **differs** (term): WooPayments names a **payout currency**, "the currency in which funds are paid out into your bank account", also called the "settlement currency"; it may differ from the customer currency. The payout detail page, and each payout's listing, show the payout currency. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-002 ("Shopify names a payout currency"). Q7-WC-004 Q7-WC-005 Q7-WC-006 Q7-WC-009 Q7-WC-049
- **differs** (role): A payment in a currency matching none of the seller's bank accounts is converted to the default currency and paid into the default account, with a currency conversion fee; with bank accounts in other currencies, funds can be paid out separately. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-013 ("When a customer pays"), Q7-SH-P0155 ("With Multi-Currency Payouts"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-004 ("The help also says"). Q7-WC-K0737 Q7-WC-K0738 Q7-WC-K0742 Q7-WC-K0743
- **differs** (role): Fees are charged in the customer currency when it matches a bank account on file; otherwise the total is converted to the default currency first. Fees differ by country and by the currency the customer paid in. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-K1950 ("When a customer pays"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0156 ("Zid's fees"). Q7-WC-032 Q7-WC-K0740 Q7-WC-K0427
- **differs** (role): A chart lists each country's available payout currencies, from Australia (AUD) to the United States (USD); a country with one listed currency pays out only in it, to banks in that country. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-018 ("Shopify publishes, per store location"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0039 ("The bank list"). Q7-WC-P0051 Q7-WC-K0739
- **differs** (role): Minimum payouts depend on the payout currency; instant payouts run from $50 to $1,000 USD. Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0025 ("Zid Pay deposit minimums"). Q7-WC-P0039 Q7-WC-P0048
- **not in what was read:** an API field for a payout bank account's currency. Shopify [1]: Q7-SH-134 ("In the API, a payout bank account"). Q7-WC-N05

- **differs** (role): The seller sets the store currency under WooCommerce > Settings > General > Currency options; only one may be selected, from a drop-down defaulting to USD, and an unlisted one can be added as a custom currency. - **differs** (role): WooPayments multi-currency is enabled under Payments > Settings > Advanced settings; the Enabled currencies list starts with the store currency. It can switch customers to their local currency by geolocation, and customers can choose a currency under My Account. Shopify [2]: Q7-SH-076 ("The currency is set per market"), Q7-SH-083 ("If a country's local currency"); Zid [2]: Q7-ZD-016 ("The payments page"); Zid [1]: Q7-ZD-K0299 ("On the compare-plans page"). Shopify [2]: Q7-SH-011 ("The domestic currency"), Q7-SH-016 ("A US-based store"), Q7-SH-060 ("In Singapore"), Q7-SH-K1636 ("Multi-Currency Payouts is not available"), Q7-SH-K1946 ("The conversion fee depends"); Zid [2]: Q7-ZD-K0300 ("مدفوعات زد is available"). - **differs** (role): Changing the base currency changes what the store displays and charges in and converts nothing: product prices keep their numbers, and orders placed before keep their currency and totals. The seller needs access to WooCommerce settings and should confirm the gateway supports the currency. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-008 ("Only the store owner"), Q7-SH-006 ("Shopify lists the effects"), Q7-SH-007 ("After the change"); Zid [3]: Q7-ZD-K0302 ("The help says"). Q7-WC-K0748 Q7-WC-026 Q7-WC-027 Q7-WC-023
- **differs** (role): An upgrade routine rewrote `woocommerce_currency` and orders' `_order_currency` from KIP to LAK. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-106 ("After Bulgaria moved"). Q7-WC-057
- **differs** (role): Exchange rates come from the WooPayments server by default, or a manual rate; switching the store currency and back forces fresh rates. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-078 ("Under each market's"). Q7-WC-K0755 Q7-WC-K0753 Q7-WC-016
- **differs** (role): A refund repays the customer the exact amount in the currency paid; the amount deducted from the balance uses the rate at refund time. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-068 ("Refunds go out"). Q7-WC-019 Q7-WC-020
- **differs** (role): A dispute fee is charged in the customer currency when a bank account matches it, otherwise in the default payout currency. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-066 ("Under Multi-Currency Payouts"). Q7-WC-033 Q7-WC-034
- **differs** (role): A transaction's timeline shows the exchange rate and conversion fee; since June 7, 2021 the fee is a separate line item. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-014 ("Historical exchange rates"). Q7-WC-008 Q7-WC-035
- **differs** (role): With several bank accounts, one must be the default. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-015 ("A payout currency is added"). Q7-WC-K0741
- **not in what was read:** editing orders placed in another currency; moving the account's country; notice before fee changes. Shopify [3]: Q7-SH-073 ("Editing orders"); Zid [3]: Q7-ZD-K0230 ("For Gulf merchants"), Q7-ZD-K0157 ("Zid may change"). Q7-WC-N17 Q7-WC-N18 Q7-WC-N19
- **not in what was read:** copying or converting prices when a group of countries is created. Zid [3]: Q7-ZD-022 ("When a market is created"). Q7-WC-N16
