## 2. S-02's decisions (gate G3)

Each is in full in [`decisions.md`](../studies/subjects/S-02/decisions.md): what the five platforms do, the options, and what the recommendation rules out.
- **The recommendations** are engineering's. D1 to D7 come from the five platforms alone. D8 and D9 are needs only the Yemen row shows, weighed against established designs.
- **Yemen today** says what an option needs there now, from [the Yemen row](../studies/subjects/S-02/yemen-row.tsv). Yemen is one of your markets, never a reason for a recommendation.
- **The walk** tried each recommendation against a second market, the company based elsewhere, and the first market dropped. Of its 51 scenarios, 41 cost a setting, 9 one module, and 1 a redesign: a country requiring data held there, a hypothesis for S-08 ([`scenario-walk.tsv`](../studies/subjects/S-02/scenario-walk.tsv)).

**RD1. The store's own country**
- **D1. Where a store's country is held.**
  - **Recommended:** on a business record (a company, or a person trading alone), one per store at launch, with room for more.
  - **Changing later:** a redesign.
  - **Yemen today:** what it needs exists.
  - **The walk:** a second business behind the store is a setting.
- **D2. When a seller moves country.**
  - **Recommended:** the store takes a new business record, and the legal country isn't changed in place.
  - **Changing later:** one module.
- **D3. What follows the seller's country.**
  - **Recommended:** every rule tied to it reads one record's country through one entry point: the store's main business, or the business that served the order. Other places with rules of their own stay open: where stock is held, an owner's residence, a payment account's country, and an area within the country.
  - **Changing later:** one module.
  - **Yemen today:** some rules differ by area within the country, which D8 takes up.

**RD2. The markets a store sells into**
- **D4. What a market can be.**
  - **Recommended:** a named set of conditions. Places come first; channels, points of sale, business customers and storefronts come later, as new kinds. Markets may sit inside each other. Customer groups stay beside markets.
  - **Changing later:** a redesign.
  - **The walk:** one module for each new kind.
- **D5. How markets overlap.**
  - **Recommended:**
    - a market may hold several countries;
    - within one kind, markets nest;
    - across kinds, a single value comes from the most specific market, and a list may combine.
  - **Changing later:** one module.

**RD3. What is set per market**
- **D6. Settings that vary by market.**
  - **Recommended:** a store value that each market may change, through one entry point.
  - **Changing later:** a setting.
- **D7. Currency and prices per market,** answerable in three parts:
  - **Every order keeps both amounts,** the market's and the store's. Changing later: a redesign.
  - **The exchange rate is held per market.** Changing later: one module.
  - **Which markets carry their own currency.** Changing later: a setting.

  Gift cards default to the store's currency. Payouts are each provider's, under your Decision 1.

**Needs no platform meets.** These come from the Yemen row alone. Each recommends what established designs support now, and keeps room for the rest.
- **D8. A seller's area within a country.**
  - **Recommended:** tax rules may use an area within the country, as the platforms do. The area is held from the start as a dated field with a stable identifier.
  - **The room kept:** payment and currency rules on an area.
  - **Changing later:** one module.
  - **Against the room:** no platform or design read keys payment services or currency on an area.
- **D9. Two currency areas under one currency code.**
  - **Recommended:** what D7 already gives: a market per area, with its own rate. The two areas are then told apart only by market.
  - **The room kept:** naming the area in the currency itself, so that amounts and reports tell the two apart.
  - **Changing later:** one module, if S-09 has amounts refer to a currency record; a redesign if every amount stores a bare code.
  - **Against the room:** every standard and platform read identifies a currency by its code alone.
  - **Yemen today:** one code covers two currency areas, at rates about three to one apart.

**Answer:** accept all / accept all but … / discuss. Deferring D8 or D9 is always open: the point stays open, at the cost each states.
