# Brief: the independent review of the class's model's extent (Stage 4A)

**What this is.** `PLAN.md`, Stage 4A: "An independent review of the whole extent, by an agent that made none of it." You are one reviewer with one batch of one share of the model. Your message names the share, the batch and the commit of the selection. You made none of the model. Check it against the evidence, not against what its parts, its batches or its coordinator say.

**The review is by risk, not every row** (`studies/class-model/method-report.md`, sections 7, 13 and 18; `inspection-Q3R.md`, part 2; the design `briefs/R-extent-design.md`). `method/review_select.py` chose the items where an error would change what the model says. It ran once, after the last landing before this review: the held pass and its second stage, the qualities pass and the omission review. It wrote them to `studies/class-model/review/selection.tsv` at the commit your message names. Your items are the rows with your share and batch. Review every one. Where an item leads you to a row outside your batch that is wrong, say so in your findings.

**What the model now holds.** Since 30 October the model also cites the held pass: 24,704 placement lines in `held/<platform>/placements-<batch>.tsv`, each one entry of a platform's own list (a route, a setting, a plan line, a permission, a changelog post) placed on one capability. The coordinator amended them into the parts as evidence rows whose key is the entry's id (such as `ZD-E-29f102f6`). The second stage's and the omission review's passages are held entries of the same form (structure `second-stage`). Sixteen capabilities were made from the held pass's new things, in the `*-amend` parts.

## What each reason asks

An item is a cell (a capability on one platform), a capability, or a question that is neither, with every reason it was chosen and each reason's detail. Answer each reason the item carries. A held line in a detail is named by its file, its line and its entry: `held/zid/placements-ZD-4.tsv line 12 (ZD-4 ZD-E-29f102f6: capability CM-ACC-001 core)`.

**Placements: the supports the conventions give.** Every reason that asks whether a placement is right reads it by `B-extent.md`, "Cells and evidence" (lines 114 to 118):
- "`core`: the platform itself does it for the merchant. The statement names its own admin section, setting, route or built-in behaviour, a plan line that includes it, or its own API for its own feature";
- "`own-service`: the statement names the platform's company, or a company of its group, as the maker or provider of a separate product, app, extension or service that does it, whatever channel sells it, its own marketplace included. A product's name that carries the platform's brand doesn't name its maker. Without a statement naming the company, place the cell by what its statements show, and note the missing maker statement as a gap. Never decide the maker from what you know";
- "`extension-point`: the statement shows third parties doing it through something the platform provides: an app-store or marketplace category, an extension target, a provider interface, or an API whose statements say apps or partners use it to do the work";
- "`outside`: the statement names a third party, not the merchant, the buyer or the platform's company, as the one that does it, with no interface of the platform named";
- "`shows`: the statement shows the platform has the capability, or gives its terms, and supports no placement. A statement that makes the merchant or buyer responsible is `shows`, never `outside`."

And a held line by `H-held.md`, rule 4: "**`supports`** takes `B-extent.md`'s values, as 'Cells and evidence' and its conventions define them. This pass's entries are statements in that sense."

## Rules

- Every claim is one of three (`PLAN.md`, section 6): "a fact traced to its source at a commit, a reading marked as one, or an assumption marked as one."
- The founder's words are never evidence of what a platform does.
- A held line, a batch's notes, a digest and a coordinator's check are what the model says, not evidence. The evidence is the entry, its source, and the concern map's statements.
- Don't change the model's files. Write findings; the coordinator corrects.
- Nothing gets settled that the evidence didn't settle. Where you can't tell, say what would settle it.
- `PLAN.md`, section 6: "A 'searched, not found' lists what it read, at a commit."

## How to work, keeping your context small

- `python3 -I method/class_slice.py model --domain <D> --areas <AREA>` prints a capability's cells; `key <key>` reads one statement or held entry; `search`, `outscope`, `candidates` and `caps` look further; `q3.py where <key>` says which cells cite a statement. `held_entries.py cells --platform P CAP` prints a cell compactly, and `grep -n <entry id> studies/class-model/held/<platform>/placements-*.tsv` finds an entry's lines.
- Work through your items in order. Append your verdict on each to your coverage file as you go, a few at a time. Don't print files back. Make your lookups together: each turn reads your whole context again.
- **When your context passes about 200,000 tokens,** finish the item, write it, and stop. Report the items left: a fresh agent continues from your coverage file.

## Output, in `studies/class-model/review/`

- `coverage-<share>-<batch>.tsv`, tab-separated, a header row, no quoting: `item` · `verdict` (`holds`, `wrong` or `unclear`) · `severity` (for `wrong` only: `kind` where a kind or count changes, `structure` where a landing, a parent, a capability or a concern's domain changes, `placement` where a cell's placement or a line's `supports` changes, `reading` where only a reading, a scope or a note does) · `basis` (the keys and entry ids you read, `;`-separated; a held line as `<entry id>@<batch>`) · `correction` (for `wrong` only: the file, the row and the new value. A part's row: `<part>/<file>`, by its `cap_id` and `platform`, its `concern_id` or its `key`. A held line: `held/<platform>/placements-<batch>.tsv`, by its `entry_id` and `cap_id`.
