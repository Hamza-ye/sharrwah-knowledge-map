Global Shipping

Global Shipping

Pricing: Covers up to 0.5 kg. Extra charges for every additional 0.5 kg.

Additional Charges: Perfumes, dangerous goods, dry ice, lithium batteries.

Address Change: Extra fee.

Remote Area Fees: Apply for pickup/delivery.

Fuel Surcharge: 25.75% included in the price.

COD: Not available.

Pricing: Varies by country/zone.

Destruction Charges: Billed to merchant.

Volumetric Weight: (L × W × H) ÷ 5000.

Chargeable Weight: Higher of volumetric or gross.

Invoice Dispute: Within 5 days.

Storage: Liquidated after 60 days.

Damage Proof: Required (images/videos + packaging).

Compensation Request: Within 48h of receipt, or 30 days if lost.

Missing Docs: Must be submitted within 2 days.

Compensation: Invoice value, up to 1000 SAR.

Wallet Credit: Within 30 days of confirmation.

Not Covered: Same as domestic + glass/ceramic, car parts, plants, weapons, paints.

Note: Customs/government delays not covered.

Customs duties will be charged to the merchant if the end customer refuses to pay them, and the fees will be deducted from the Zid wallet.

In case the shipment delivery fails, additional return-to-origin fees may apply, and the charges will be deducted from the merchant’s wallet.
