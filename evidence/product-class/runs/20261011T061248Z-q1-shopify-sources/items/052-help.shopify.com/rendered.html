# Manual payment methods

Some customers don't want to pay for their orders using a credit card. These customers can still place their orders online if you set up a manual payment method. When your customers use a manual payment method, you can arrange to receive their payment in a way that works best for them. You can [send an invoice](/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/payments/deferred-payments#email-an-order-invoice-to-a-customer) to your customer. After you receive the payment, you can then manually approve the order.

Common types of manual payments include cash on delivery (COD), money orders, and bank transfers. If your store and your customers are based in Canada, then you can accept [email money transfers](/manual/payments/manual-payments/email-money-transfers).

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>You aren&#39;t charged <a href="/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/billing-charges">third-party transaction fees</a> for manual payments.</p>
</aside>

Orders that accept manual payments are marked as unpaid on the **Orders** page. After you receive payment for a customer's order, you can mark the order as paid on the order details page and fulfill the order as you would if they had made an online payment.

To begin using manual payments, you have two options: you can either utilize a suggested manual payment method or create a custom manual payment method.

## Use a suggested manual payment method

You can choose from a list of suggested manual payment methods on the **Payment providers** settings page in your Shopify admin.

#### Steps:

<div class="sections"><details open><summary id="desktop">Desktop</summary><ol>
<li><p>From your Shopify admin, go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <a href="https://admin.shopify.com/settings/payments"><strong>Payments</strong></a>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In the <strong>Payment providers</strong> section, select one of the available manual payment methods.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Additional details</strong>, enter any information that you want displayed next to the payment method in your checkout.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Payment instructions</strong>, enter the instructions that you want your customers to follow to pay for an order. These instructions display on the order confirmation page after a customer completes their order. When you enter this information, check with your bank or delivery service to find out exactly what a customer must do to complete the payment.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Click the activate payment method button.</p>
</li>
</ol>

</li>

</li>

<li><p>In <strong>Additional details</strong>, enter any information that you want displayed next to the payment method in your checkout.</p>

## Create a custom manual payment method

If your business has unique needs for collecting payment or if you want to offer your customers alternative ways to pay, such as booking a service and paying in person, then you can create a custom manual payment method.

#### Steps:

<div class="sections"><details open><summary id="desktop">Desktop</summary><ol>
<li><p>From your Shopify admin, go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <a href="https://admin.shopify.com/settings/payments"><strong>Payments</strong></a>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In the <strong>Manual payment methods</strong> section, select <strong>Create custom payment method</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Custom payment method name</strong>, enter the name of your payment method. The following names are reserved by Shopify and can&#39;t be used:</p>
<ul>
<li>Bank Deposit</li>
<li>Cash</li>
<li>Cash on Delivery (COD)</li>
<li>custom</li>
<li>External Credit</li>
<li>External Debit</li>
<li>Gift Card</li>
<li>Money Order</li>
<li>Store Credit</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Additional details</strong>, enter any information that you want displayed next to the payment method in your checkout.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Payment instructions</strong>, enter the instructions that you want your customer to follow to pay for the order. These instructions are displayed on the order confirmation page after the customer completes their order.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Click <strong>Activate</strong>.</p>
</li>
</ol>
</details><details><summary id="mobile">Mobile</summary><ol>
<li><p>From the <a href="https://www.shopify.com/install/detect">Shopify app</a>, tap <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg" alt="Menu" class="admin-icon" />, and then tap <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg" alt="Settings" class="admin-icon" /> <strong>Settings</strong>.</p>

<li><p>In the <strong>Manual payment methods</strong> section, select <strong>Create custom payment method</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Custom payment method name</strong>, enter the name of your payment method. The following names are reserved by Shopify and can&#39;t be used:</p>
<ul>
<li>Bank Deposit</li>
<li>Cash</li>
<li>Cash on Delivery (COD)</li>
<li>custom</li>
<li>External Credit</li>
<li>External Debit</li>
<li>Gift Card</li>
<li>Money Order</li>
<li>Store Credit</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><p>In <strong>Additional details</strong>, enter any information that you want displayed next to the payment method in your checkout.</p>
</li>

A customer using a manual payment method displays the name of the payment method and the instructions to complete the payment on the order confirmation screen.

## Multi-currency support

To accept manual payments in multiple currencies, you need to use [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/) and activate the [international sales tools](/manual/international/).

With the international sales tools, your manual payment methods display in the currency configured for each market. Customers will see their local currency when selecting a manual payment method at checkout.

Learn more about how [multi-currency works with Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/store-currency/multi-currency).


## Deactivate a manual payment method

If you don't want to support a certain type of manual payment any longer, then you can deactivate the manual payment method at any time.

#### Steps:

<div class="sections"><details open><summary id="desktop">Desktop</summary><ol>
<li><p>From your Shopify admin, go to <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <a href="https://admin.shopify.com/settings/payments"><strong>Payments</strong></a>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In the <strong>Manual payment methods</strong> box in the <strong>Payment providers</strong> area, click <strong>Manage</strong> next to the manual payment method that you want to deactivate.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Click <strong>Deactivate payment method</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Click <strong>Deactivate payment method</strong> to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
</details><details><summary id="mobile">Mobile</summary><ol>
<li><p>From the <a href="https://www.shopify.com/install/detect">Shopify app</a>, tap <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg" alt="Menu" class="admin-icon" />, and then tap <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg" alt="Settings" class="admin-icon" /> <strong>Settings</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In the <strong>Store settings</strong> section, tap <strong>Payments</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>In the <strong>Manual payment methods</strong> box in the <strong>Payment providers</strong> area, tap <strong>Manage</strong> next to the manual payment method that you want to deactivate.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Tap <strong>Deactivate payment method</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Tap <strong>Deactivate payment method</strong> to confirm.</p>
</li>
</ol>
</details></div>

After you deactivate a payment method, you can reactivate it at any time from the **Manual payment methods** drop-down menu in the **Manual payments** section.

## Mark a manual payment as paid

When a customer makes an order using a manual payment method, the order is marked as **Pending** on the **Orders** page. After you receive payment for the order, you need to manually mark the order as paid. Refer to [accepting payment](/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/payments/deferred-payments#accept-payment-for-an-order-pending-payment) for steps to mark an order as paid.
