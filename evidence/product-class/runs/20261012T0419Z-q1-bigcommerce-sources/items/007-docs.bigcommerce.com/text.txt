# Catalog - Brands

> The Catalog API manages products, categories, brands, bulk pricing rules, and more. To learn more about catalog resources, see the [Catalog Overview](/developer/docs/admin/catalog-and-inventory).

Our Catalog brands endpoints let you [create individual brands](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/create-brand) and [modify the brands](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/update-brand) associated with a storeʼs products, along with [deleting brands](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/delete-brand).

Brand images have their own dedicated [create a brand image](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/images/create-brand-image) and [delete a brand image](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/images/delete-brand-image) endpoints.

In addition, brands have metafields that you can use to store information structured in key-value pairs; learn more about [creating brand metafields](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/metafields/create-brand-metafield), [updating brand metafields](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/metafields/update-brand-metafield), and [deleting brand metafields](/developer/api-reference/rest/admin/catalog/brands/metafields/delete-brand-metafield).

> To learn more about authenticating Catalog endpoints, locate the **Authentication** section at the top of each endpoint, then click **Show Details**.
