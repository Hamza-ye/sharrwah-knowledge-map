What businesses and products are prohibited or restricted on WooPayments?

Due to restrictions put in place by card networks, our payment processors, and their financial service providers, some business types and product types are not allowed to transact using WooPayments. Those include, but are not limited to:

Virtual currency, including video game or virtual world credits
Counterfeit goods
Adult content and services
Drug paraphernalia
Tobacco products, including e-cigarettes and vapes
Supplements supported by unsubstantiated claims
Multi-level marketing
Pseudo-pharmaceuticals
Peptides and other research chemicals
Social media activity, like Twitter followers, Facebook likes, YouTube views
Substances designed to mimic illegal drugs
Firearms and ammunition
Proprietary trading firms offering access to funded accounts

The full list of prohibited and restricted businesses can be found here.

By signing up to use WooPayments, you agree not to accept payments in connection with these restricted activities, practices, or products.

We also work to ensure that no prohibited activity is conducted using WooPayments. If we become aware of prohibited activity, we may restrict or shut down the account.

Alcohol and CBD

Alcohol sales are permitted in some, but not all, instances. Please see this page for more information.

Merchants selling CBD must use Square or Viva Wallet. More info can be found here.
