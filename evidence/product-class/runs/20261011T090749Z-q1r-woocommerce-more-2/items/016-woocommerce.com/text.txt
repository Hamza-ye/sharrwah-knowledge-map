Back In Stock Notifications for WooCommerce
by Woo
Notify customers when your products are restocked.

Running out of stock is always frustrating. Can’t avoid it? Then turn it into something positive! Stop worrying about all the sales you are missing, and think about the customers you can bring back.

Turn your sold-out products into waitlists that anyone can join.
Send automated emails to your customers when their favorite products are restocked.
Recover lost sales, build customer loyalty, and gain deeper insights into your inventory.
Bring customers back to your store
Build waitlists and notify your customers when their favorite products are restocked.

 

Back In Stock Notifications gives your customers an effortless way to know when your products are restocked, and brings them back to your store. Here’s how it works:

Customers who land on a sold-out product page are invited to sign up with their email and get notified when the product becomes available.
When the product is back in stock, all customers who joined the waitlist receive an automated notification.
Customers return to your store and complete their purchase!
Did you know that up to 20% of the customers who sign up for stock alerts eventually return and complete their purchase?
Measure demand for out-of-stock products

Stop hiding sold-out products in your catalog! With Back In Stock Notifications, you can:

Measure purchase intent for out-of-stock products.
View your most wanted sold-out products, and restock them first.
Gain deeper insights into your inventory, and make more accurate demand forecasts.
Use the Stock Notifications Dashboard to check customer activity and view key product insights.
Stay connected with your most loyal customers

Customers who sign up for back-in-stock notifications send you a strong buying signal with high purchase intent. So why not take the opportunity to stay connected with them?

Back In Stock Notifications stores your waitlist contact details even after all notifications have been sent, making it easy to export and use them in your favorite email marketing platform.

Turn stock-outs into an opportunity to grow your audience and build customer loyalty.
Let your customers manage their preferences

Concerned about your customers’ privacy? The plugin supports double opt-in, and handles opt-ins and opt-outs gracefully, giving customers with or without an account the freedom to manage their own preferences:

Logged in customers can sign up without entering their email and can manage active notifications from their Account page.
Confirmation and notification emails include a handy “unsubscribe” link that gives your customers a frictionless way to opt-out of all alerts they have signed up for.
Enable your customers to manage their notifications directly from their My Account page.
Experience WooCommerce at its finest

Back In Stock Notifications has been optimized to leverage the latest technologies built into WooCommerce, while making the most of the features you love.

Running a high-traffic store? Thanks to its integration with Action Scheduler, the extension can send thousands of back-in-stock alerts without breaking a sweat.
Need full support for Variable products? The plugin stores the current variation selections when customers sign up, and restores their selections when they return.
Using Product Bundles? With Back In Stock Notifications, your customers can sign up and get notified when bundles with insufficient stock become available.

Settle for nothing but blazing-fast, battle-tested WooCommerce software, backed by a support team you can trust.

Customize and extend it

Looking for a way to limit notification sign-ups to logged in customers? Don’t want to create a new account for guests who sign up for stock alerts? No problem! Back In Stock Notifications comes with everything you need to make it work just how you want.

How to get started
Buy Back In Stock Notifications.
Download and install it.
Make sure that out-of-stock products are visible in your catalog.
Sit back and start recovering lost sales!
