General Terms and Conditions 

The maximum weight limit is the maximum weight allowed at the specified rates, and any additional kilogram will be charged according to the rates provided upon service activation.

The volumetric weight is calculated by multiplying the dimensions of the shipment (length x width x height) and dividing the result by 4000 (4000/ length * width * height).

The chargeable weight will be either the volumetric weight or the gross weight, whichever is higher.
