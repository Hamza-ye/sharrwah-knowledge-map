Checkout Field Editor for WooCommerce
by Woo
Optimize your checkout process by adding, removing, or editing fields to suit your needs.
This extension works with the legacy checkout only
The block-based checkout will soon include native field customization, so this extension isn’t needed there. We recommend migrating to the block-based checkout when this becomes available.
Features
Customizable checkout: Personalize fields to create a tailored checkout experience.
Streamlined information collection: Add custom fields to gather essential data (such as requested delivery date).
Simplified checkout: Remove unnecessary fields for a faster and smoother process.
Customize your checkout fields via your WP Admin dashboard

Checkout Field Editor provides you with an interface to add, edit, and remove fields shown on your WooCommerce checkout page. Fields can be added and removed from the billing and shipping sections, as well as inserted after these sections next to the standard order notes. Core fields can also be moved around, giving you more control over your checkout without touching any code.

The editor supports several custom field types, including:

Text fields
Select attributes
Checkboxes
Datepickers
Benefits
Maintain brand consistency: Edit fields to align with your brand identity, reinforcing brand recognition and building customer trust throughout the checkout experience.
Reduce cart abandonment: Optimize the checkout process to minimize obstacles and create a smooth user experience, decreasing the likelihood of customers abandoning their carts.
Enhance customer satisfaction: Offer a customized and user-friendly checkout process, demonstrating your commitment to meeting customer needs and increasing satisfaction levels.
Improve data collection: Add fields to gather valuable customer insights, empowering data-driven decision-making and enabling personalized marketing strategies.
Increase conversion rates: With an optimized checkout process, guide customers seamlessly from cart to completion, resulting in higher conversion rates and increased revenue.
Adaptability and control: Take advantage of WooCommerce’s flexibility to actively optimize and experiment with different field configurations, maintaining control over the checkout experience.
Gain a competitive edge: Stand out from competitors by providing an optimized checkout process that differentiates your online store, attracting more customers and driving business growth.
