# Requirements and considerations for using Managed Markets

Before you activate and set up Managed Markets, you need to review the following requirements and considerations. Managed Markets doesn't support some countries or regions, and currencies.

## Requirements for using Managed Markets {#requirements}

To use Managed Markets, your business needs to meet the following requirements:

* Your store must use [Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments).
* Your store currency must be the default currency of the country where your business is based. For example, a business that's based in the United States needs a store currency of USD. If your store currency is different, then you can't activate Managed Markets.
* Your store and product information need to be available in English. English can be your [default language](/manual/international/languages/manage-languages#change-default-language), or you can [add and publish English as an additional language](/manual/international/languages/manage-languages#add-new-language). Translate your product titles and descriptions into English.
* Your products need to meet the [prohibited or restricted items requirements](/manual/international/managed-markets/prohibited-items).
* Your products need to meet the [Managed Markets shipping requirements](/manual/international/managed-markets/shipping).
* If you sell to customers in the European Union, then compliant cancellation and return policies are automatically applied to your Managed Markets orders. Refer to [EU right of withdrawal](/manual/compliance/legal/eu-right-of-withdrawal) to learn more.

In addition to meeting these requirements, there are requirements for Managed Markets specific to where your business is based.

### Requirements for using Managed Markets in the United States {#us-reqs}

To use Managed Markets in the United States, you need to meet the following requirements:

* Your business needs to be based in the continental United States (exclusive of territories including Puerto Rico and Guam).
* Your business needs at least one non-PO box location in the United States.
* You need at least one fulfillment location in the continental United States.

### Requirements for using Managed Markets in Canada {#can-reqs}

To use Managed Markets in Canada, you need to meet the following requirements:

* Your business needs to be based in Canada.
* Your business needs at least one non-PO box location in Canada.
* You need at least one fulfillment location in Canada.
* If you have multiple fulfillment locations in Canada, then all your fulfillment locations must be located within the same province.
* Your store must use [Shopify Tax](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax) or [Basic Tax](/manual/taxes/basic-tax). Managed Markets can't be used if your store uses [manual tax settings](/manual/taxes/manual-tax-settings).

### Requirements for using Managed Markets in the United Kingdom {#uk-reqs}

To use Managed Markets in the United Kingdom, you need to meet the following requirements:

* Your business needs to be based in the United Kingdom.
* Your business needs at least one non-PO box location in the United Kingdom.
* You need at least one fulfillment location in the United Kingdom.
* If you have multiple fulfillment locations in the United Kingdom, then all your fulfillment locations must be located either within Great Britain or Northern Ireland. If you have fulfillment locations in both Great Britain and Northern Ireland, then you can't use Managed Markets.

## Considerations for using Managed Markets {#considerations}

Before you activate and start using Managed Markets, review the following considerations.

### Managed Markets country requirements

* Managed Markets doesn't work in some customers' countries. For more information, refer to [_Incompatible countries_](/manual/international/managed-markets/requirements-and-considerations#supported-countries).
* Managed Markets collects duties and import taxes at checkout using [delivered duty paid (DDP)](/manual/international/duties-and-import-taxes/carriers-and-shipping-terms#incoterms) in every country and region where DDP is supported. Your customers pay at checkout instead of paying the carrier or customs on delivery. Delivered duty unpaid (DDU) shipping remains available only in countries and regions where DDP isn't supported.
* To keep your customers in a DDP-supported market paying duties on delivery, [deactivate Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets/deactivation) for that market.
* It's your responsibility to ensure that your products have accurate [countries of origin](/manual/international/duties-and-import-taxes/charging-duties#add-hs-codes).
* Shopify Protect for Managed Markets is available only for stores in the United States.

### Managed Markets feature limitations

* Managed Markets doesn't support redirects at checkout.
* Managed Markets doesn't support subscriptions. Subscriptions work only for domestic orders.
* Managed Markets and the Global-e app can't be used at the same time.
* Managed Markets doesn't support multiple business entities in a single store.
* Managed Markets isn't compatible with [Shopify Collabs](/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs).
* [B2B orders aren't supported by Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets/fulfillment#b2b-orders-with-managed-markets). You need to act as [your own merchant of record](/manual/international/managed-markets/fulfillment#change-mor) for B2B orders.

### Customer requirements and bundles

* Managed Markets orders require your customer's phone number. A phone number is collected at checkout for Managed Markets orders.
* Managed Markets is compatible with bundles created with [Shopify Bundles](/manual/products/bundles/shopify-bundles), where multiple SKUs are combined to create a single product. Managed Markets isn't compatible with manual bundles that are created as a single product and added together as a single line item. Accurate customs forms can't be generated for manual bundles.

### Customizations and free orders

* Managed Markets orders can't be edited after a shipping label is printed, or if the order has been partially or fully fulfilled. [Certain aspects of Managed Markets orders can't be changed](/manual/international/managed-markets/fulfillment#edit-order).
* Free orders that have a value of zero aren't supported by Managed Markets. You need to act as [your own merchant of record](/manual/international/managed-markets/fulfillment#change-mor) for free orders.

### EU right of withdrawal in Managed Markets {#eu-right-of-withdrawal}

Managed Markets automatically applies compliant cancellation and return policies to orders from customers in the European Union. Managed Markets activates these policies, but the following are still your responsibility:

* Responding to customer return and cancellation requests.
* Purchasing return shipping labels.
* Managing the cost of returns.

Managed Markets doesn't support product-level exemptions from the [EU right of withdrawal](/manual/compliance/legal/eu-right-of-withdrawal). All Managed Markets orders into the EU are subject to the same cancellation and return policies.

If you already use a compliant third-party solution, then you can deactivate the Managed Markets right of withdrawal feature and manage your EU compliance independently. This setting is in your admin at **Settings** > [**Customer accounts**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/customer_accounts), and requires you to agree to use your own solution for returns and cancellations for Managed Markets in the European Union.

If you don't want to use Managed Markets cancellation and return policies or a compliant third-party solution, then you can [deactivate Managed Markets](/manual/international/managed-markets/activation#deactivate) for markets that include EU countries.
