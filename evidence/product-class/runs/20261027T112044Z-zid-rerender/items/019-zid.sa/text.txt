Service Activation:

The merchant must attach a front and back image of their national ID to use Zid Pay.

Upon activation of Zid Pay, the other payment gateway on the store will be deactivated.

Zid Pay fees are based on the package selected for the Zid store. If the merchant selects the basic package, the basic Zid Pay fee will apply. If the growth package is selected, the growth Zid Pay fee will apply. If the professional package is selected, the professional Zid Pay fee will apply.

Payments are transferred when the collected amounts exceed 1 Kuwaiti Dinar.
