Capabilities and information requirements
Required verification information
Identify current and future information requirements that connected accounts must provide based on their business, location, and requested capabilities.
Ask about this page
Set up agent
How to use this tool
To view the current requirements for your connected accounts, enter your configuration using the following form. Use the Requirement update dropdown to see updated requirements for a current or upcoming requirement update. The Capabilities dropdown only includes capabilities that are compatible with the selected configuration. To compare your current requirements with the requirements for a different configuration or upcoming update, select the Setup B tab and enter the different configuration or update. Then turn on the Compare toggle.
See the Connect integration guide to learn more about connected account configurations.
View upcoming changes needed in Europe
Configure Setup A to your current setup for accounts in Europe. Then repeat for Setup B, selecting Europe in the Requirement update field. Use the Compare toggle to view differences.
Updates for Europe are subject to change. Learn more about upcoming changes in Europe.

KYC verification
European regulations require Stripe to verify the identity of certain persons associated with connected accounts. The following entities must provide verifiable KYC information:
Account representative
UBOs (for accounts deemed high risk by the Stripe risk model)
Learn more about Identity verification for connected accounts
.
Stripe initially verifies identity using the information the account provides: first_name, last_name, dob, and address. In applicable countries, nationality is also required, and id_number is available as an optional field for verification.If verification fails, or if there are possible concerns about sanctions, you see the following additional requirement fields on the account:
FieldUsage
verification.documentA scan of an ID document (front and back).
See how to upload additional documents.
verification.additional_documentA scan of an address document (front and back).
See how to upload additional documents.
id_numberIf the person’s nationality is eligible, alternatives.alternative_fields_due contains their id_number. A connected account can also provide the representative’s national ID number, in addition to their name, birthdate, address, and nationality.
See a list of supported national ID numbers
and implementation guidelines.
proof_of_livenessIf initial verification fails, you can use proof_of_liveness as an alternative verification path . Fulfill this requirement using Stripe-hosted onboarding, embedded onboarding, or the Stripe Identity API.
See handling liveness requirements for complete implementation details.
To learn more, see compliance requirements for Europe
.
For more information on uploading official documents, see Additional information on the account.

If Stripe can’t verify the company, or if there are possible concerns about sanctions

to enable payouts
. Collect it using the

Additional information on the representative
A person known as a representative must activate this connected account. The representative must be a beneficial owner who is authorized to sign for the company. Indicate this relationship to Stripe by setting
relationship.executive
to true, or, if the representative owns more than 25% of the company, by setting
relationship.owner
to true.
You might need to collect a scan of an ID document
and an address document
to enable payouts. That can happen if Stripe can’t verify the representative’s provided information or if there are possible concerns about sanctions
. In some cases, depending on various calculated risk factors, Stripe can use Simplified Due Diligence and request only one document for verification at a later time.
Additionally, for partnerships you must provide a
relationship.percent_ownership
value.
You can collect ID information with the
verification.document.front
and
verification.document.back
parameters.
Relationship verification for UBOs and directors
European regulations require verification of the relationship of ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs) and directors to the legal entity. Stripe attempts to verify each person’s relationship using third-party sources. If automatic verification fails, the account can upload an official document
or sign a digital attestation.
Who needs to be verified
UBOs: Verify the relationship of ultimate beneficial owners—individuals who own or control (directly or indirectly) more than 25% of the legal entity. Provide each UBO’s first_name, last_name, dob, address, and email.
Directors: Verify the relationship of directors—board members or senior persons responsible for managing the business (for example, CEO, COO, managing director). Provide each director’s first_name, last_name, dob, address, email, and title. Provide information for all of your directors; if you have more than 5, you only need to provide 5.
If automatic verification fails
If Stripe can’t verify the provided information or if there are possible concerns about sanctions, you might need to provide additional information to enable payouts. The additional documentation can either be a digital attestation or an official document upload:
Digital attestation: Use the following document templates:
Ultimate beneficial owner digital attestation template—submit using documents.proof_of_ultimate_beneficial_ownership
Director digital attestation template—submit using documents.proof_of_registration
When submitting an attestation, you must also provide a signer.person parameter identifying the authorized person who signed the document. The signer must be an existing person associated with the account (for example, the account representative, a business owner, or a director).
For the full API implementation details (including digital attestation integration, signer validation, and error handling), see Relationship verification for UBOs and directors
.
Upload additional documents:
Directors: See Additional information on directors
UBOs: See Additional information on beneficial owners
Additional information on directors
For companies, excluding partnerships, you must collect information on all directors. Directors are members of the governing board of the company. If you have more than 5 directors, you only need to provide information on 5. When you’ve finished collecting the required information from all directors, or if your company doesn’t have any directors, you must inform Stripe by setting
company.directors_provided
to true.
You might need to collect a scan of an ID document
and an address document
to enable payouts. That can happen if Stripe can’t verify the director’s provided information or if there are possible concerns about sanctions
. In some cases, depending on various calculated risk factors, Stripe can use Simplified Due Diligence and request only one document for verification at a later time.
You can collect ID information using the
verification.document.front
and
verification.document.back
parameters, and address information using the
verification.additional_document.front
and
verification.additional_document.back
parameters.
Additional information on beneficial owners (for both executives and owners)
You must collect information on all beneficial owners. Beneficial owners are persons who exercise significant management control over the company (executives) or who own more than 25% of the company (owners). When you’ve finished collecting the required information from all beneficial owners, you must inform Stripe by setting both
company.owners_provided
and
company.executives_provided
to true.
You might need to collect a scan of an ID document
and an address document
to enable payouts. That can happen if Stripe can’t verify the beneficial owner’s provided information or if there are possible concerns about sanctions
. In some cases, depending on various calculated risk factors, Stripe can use Simplified Due Diligence and request only one document for verification at a later time.
You can collect ID information using the
verification.document.front
and
verification.document.back
parameters, and address information using the
verification.additional_document.front
and
verification.additional_document.back
parameters.
Optionally, you can collect ownership information on each person who owns more than 25% of the company with
relationship.owner
and
relationship.percent_ownership
.
Additionally, for partnerships you need to provide a
relationship.percent_ownership
value for any owners added to the account.
Onboarding as a government entity
Government entities follow a specialized onboarding and verification process that differs from standard company verification. For eligibility criteria, required documentation, and step-by-step onboarding instructions, see Business verification requirements for government entities.
Ultimate Beneficial Ownership Verification
This requirement no longer applies. Instead, fulfill the relationship verification for UBOs and directors.
Communicate rejection reasons
If your platform owns onboarding and has UK-based connected accounts, you must notify your UK-based connected accounts without delay when their payments capabilities are rejected for terms of service violations or credit risk reasons.
If the rejection reason is unacceptable credit risk, you must advise the connected account that they can continue accepting payments for 90 days and that we’ll hold funds in reserve to help cover any disputes or refunds.
Learn how to determine the rejection reason and communicate it
to the affected account promptly.
