WooCommerce Square Documentation

Welcome to the documentation for the WooCommerce Square extension. Here, you’ll find everything you need to know about managing your products, inventory, and taking payments on your WooCommerce site via Square.

This guide covers the essentials from setup to managing inventory sync and secure payment processing, ensuring you have all the tools and information at your fingertips for a seamless online selling experience. Please explore the sections below for further details.

What You Can Do with WooCommerce Square

The WooCommerce Square Extension bridges the gap between your WooCommerce store and Square. The extension serves a dual purpose, functioning seamlessly as both a product inventory sync and a payment gateway for customer payments.

Inventory sync: Keep products and stock levels aligned between WooCommerce and your Square account. Manage from either platform.
Payment processing: Accept cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Square Gift Cards, and CashApp Pay at checkout.
