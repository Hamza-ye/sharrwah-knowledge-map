Upgrades/Downgrades

Upgrade Payments. Should you elect to upgrade your Services, or if your Services are automatically upgraded, then the Billing Date of the next renewal period will remain the same, but you will be billed immediately the pro-rata upgraded Services Fee for the remainder of the billing period.
