# Shopify Collective

[Shopify Collective](https://www.shopify.com/collective) lets you connect with other Shopify stores and sell each other's products in Shopify.

Retailers can import complementary products directly from suppliers that use Shopify, and start selling the products without any inventory costs or commitments. Suppliers can get discovered by retailers, create price lists, and share products for retailers to sell.

Your business can be both a supplier and a retailer. Shopify Collective is free to use with all Shopify plans, subject to store eligibility. There is no minimum store sales requirement for using Shopify Collective.
