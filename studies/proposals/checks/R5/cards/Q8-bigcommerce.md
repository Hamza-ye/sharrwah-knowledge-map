## The points cited

- **section 1, line 7:** - Shopify's Terms call all it offers the "Service(s)", and "Any new features or tools which are added to the current Services will also be subject to the Terms of Service". BigCommerce's agreement defines "Services" as "all products, services, and materials offered by Commerce or its Affiliates". Same term (basis 1; official pages). Q8-SH-054; Q8-BC-018
- **section 1, line 8:** - Four grow by plan. Shopify has "several plans to help your business grow at every stage"; on Salla "تختلف باقات سلة لتناسب مراحل نمو متجرك"; Zid's merchants "start on a free-forever plan and upgrade as they grow"; on BigCommerce "moving into a higher-tier plan comes with access to additional features". They match on role, plans a store moves up as it grows (basis 4; BigCommerce's an official page, the others stated). Q8-SH-001; Q8-SA-001; Q8-ZD-001; Q8-BC-002
- **section 1, line 15:** - Units are sold on top of a plan. One Salla store's answer (store real-merchant, plan Pro, observed 12 October 2026) listed "Warehouses & branches" and "Staff accounts", each with `type` "addon". Zid's الإضافات let a merchant buy "ميزات جديدة". BigCommerce sells added storefronts by plan, as "Add storefront $30/mo (max. 3)". Same role (basis 4; mixed kinds: Salla's observed, Zid's stated, BigCommerce's an official page). Q8-SA-043; Q8-ZD-009; Q8-BC-K0217
- **section 1, line 16:** - Some units carry terms of their own. Shopify's "Additional Services" may be subject to "Additional Service Terms"; BigCommerce adds "additional guidelines or rules applicable to such Services or features"; Zid's زد باي has terms that form the contract "بين مستخدمي زد باي وشركة زد". Same role (basis 4; official pages). Q8-SH-058; Q8-BC-024; Q8-ZD-K0009
- **section 1, line 17:** - On BigCommerce a provider's service can be the platform's own: "an Extension is a Service and is not a Third-Party Product". Q8-BC-K0457
- **section 1, line 25:** - One Salla store's قائمة المتاجر (store merchant, observed 8 October 2026) let a user move between stores "دون الحاجة لتسجيل الدخول في كل مرة", each with its own الباقة. BigCommerce's "Account-level API credentials provide direct access to all the account's stores". Q8-SA-050; Q8-BC-K0292
- **section 1, line 26:** - A Shopify sales channel app "connects a merchant's online store to surfaces outside Shopify", and "Each sales channel connects to your Shopify admin"; on BigCommerce "A channel is anywhere a merchant sells their products". Same role (basis 4). Q8-SH-K2063; Q8-SH-014; Q8-BC-K0209
- **section 1, line 29:** - Shopify's custom apps and Zid's Private apps fill one role, an app for named stores only (basis 4). Shopify's unlisted public apps and BigCommerce's Unlisted apps, "submitted and approved but not listed", share the term (basis 1). Q8-SH-029; Q8-ZD-K0400; Q8-BC-075
- **section 1, line 33:** - A BigCommerce checkout extension "is a web app hosted outside of BigCommerce" that extends a checkout step; the Tax Provider API can "integrate a tax calculation engine". Q8-BC-042; Q8-BC-077
- **section 1, line 36:** - WooCommerce's Marketplace offers "Discover Extensions Themes Business services"; BigCommerce's App Marketplace, "Apps & Integrations Themes Partner Services". Both use the term Themes (basis 1). Q8-WC-038; Q8-BC-045
- **section 1, line 46:** - All five set a third party's unit apart under that party's terms. Shopify's Third Party Services are "solely between you and" their provider; WooCommerce's "Extensions may have their own requirements"; Salla's third-party services have "أحكامها المستقلة التي تتم بين التاجر وبين الطرف الثالث"; on Zid "الشريك هو المقدم للخدمة"; BigCommerce's Third-Party Products "are governed by their own terms of service". Same role (basis 4; all official pages). Q8-SH-K2169; Q8-WC-K0830; Q8-SA-035; Q8-ZD-K0366; Q8-BC-K0453
- **section 1, line 48:** - App makers join under an agreement. Shopify's register "for a Partner Account" under the "Partner Program Agreement"; Zid's sign a partnership agreement; BigCommerce's sign "the Commerce Partner Agreement". Same role (basis 4; mixed kinds: Shopify's an official page, the others stated). Q8-SH-K2183; Q8-ZD-018; Q8-BC-062
- **section 1, line 49:** - Apps are reviewed. Shopify's public apps "undergo Shopify's app review process"; WooCommerce's review runs from "Business review" to "Launch preparations"; on BigCommerce "The Marketplace team reviews all app submissions". Same role (basis 4). Q8-SH-029; Q8-WC-071; Q8-BC-K0316
- **section 1, line 50:** - Payment integrations are held apart. Shopify's payments extension needs review and approval; WooCommerce requires "a separate agreement" for a "Payments Solution"; BigCommerce has "paused all new externally built payment integrations"; Zid Payments assigns a provider's ID "upfront before starting the integration". Same role (basis 4; all stated). Q8-SH-095; Q8-WC-K0353; Q8-BC-058; Q8-ZD-K0079
