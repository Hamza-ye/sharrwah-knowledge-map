# Third-party payment providers

To accept credit cards through a provider other than Shopify Payments, choose from over 100 [credit card payment providers](localized-payment-gateway) for your Shopify store. You can use a third-party provider as your sole credit card processor, or you can [use it alongside Shopify Payments](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/using-shopify-payments-with-a-third-party-provider) to access additional payment methods such as Shop Pay and local payment methods.

Shopify supports two different types of credit card payment providers: **direct providers** and **external providers**. If you're using a **direct provider**, then your customers can complete their purchases directly on your online store without having to pay at a third-party checkout. If you're using an **external provider**, then your customers must complete their purchases on a checkout page that's hosted outside of your online store.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Third-party transaction fees apply to all transactions processed through third-party and alternate payment gateways, even when <a href="/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments">Shopify Payments</a> is activated. However, if you elect to use Shopify Payments as your primary payment provider, then PayPal transactions are exempt from third-party transaction fees. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/billing-charges/types-of-charges/third-party-charges/third-party-transaction-fees">third-party transaction fees</a>.</p>
<p>To check the transaction fee rates for your location, refer to the <a href="https://www.shopify.com/pricing">Shopify pricing page</a> or <a href="/en/manual/your-account/contact-shopify-support">contact Shopify Support</a>.</p>
</aside>

You can activate or change a payment provider from the **Payments** page of your Shopify admin. If a provider you want to use isn't listed where you expect it, then refer to [troubleshooting a missing payment provider](/manual/payments/third-party-providers/configuring-providers#troubleshoot-missing-provider).
