# To fetch from inside Yemen

Sources this repository needs but could not fetch from outside Yemen. Open each address from inside Yemen without a VPN. Save the result exactly as received, into a founder run as `evidence/yemen/README.md` describes: the file as downloaded, or for a web page an `.mhtml` copy plus a full-page screenshot, and a one-line entry in `sources.tsv`. Tick the box when done.

Where a page is an index, such as a list of decisions or circulars, save the index first. Then save the documents the note names.

Items are ranked within each section. Section 1 matters most.

## 1. Sana'a official sources

These come from the Sana'a rules search (`finding-aids/20261010-sanaa-rules-search.tsv`). From outside Yemen, `centralbank.gov.ye` resets the connection or answers 503, and `saba.ye` answers 403.

1. [ ] **Central bank: "Electronic payment service companies"**, the list of licensed wallets and payment providers. Bears on PAY-60 and PAY-70.
   - https://www.centralbank.gov.ye/Home/payment
   - Alternate addresses: https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/payment?lang=en and https://ftp.centralbank.gov.ye/Home/ChangeLanguage?lang=ar&act=payment
2. [ ] **Central bank: "Payment Systems" section.** Look for Decision No. 1 of 2020, Governor Resolution 4/1445 and any later e-payment resolutions. Bears on PAY-58 and PAY-60.
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/141-43104/%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA?lang=en
3. [ ] **Central bank: decisions index.** Bears on PAY-58 and PAY-60.
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/143-00124/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA?lang=en
4. [ ] **Central bank: circulars to banks and financial institutions.** Look for Circular 11/2014, Periodic Circular 6/2021, Circulars 17, 18 and 40 of 1447 AH, and the 22 June 2025 circular on Al-Kuraimi. That circular has only been seen in news reports, and nothing found shows whether it was lifted. Bears on PAY-60 and PAY-61; the Al-Kuraimi circular also bears on PAY-59 and PAY-70.
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/1186-20312/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA?lang=en
5. [ ] **Saba (English): Central bank Decision No. 4 of 1445 AH**, the list of banned banks. Bears on PAY-59 and PAY-60.
   - https://www.saba.ye/en/news3335749.htm
6. [ ] **Saba: Law No. 4 of 1444 AH prohibiting usurious transactions** (23 March 2023). Bears on PAY-48 and PAY-57.
   - https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3230501.htm
7. [ ] **Central bank copy of Law 40/2006** on payment systems and electronic financial and banking operations, and the Financial Laws index. Bears on PAY-57, PAY-58, PAY-60 and PAY-61.
   - http://centralbank.gov.ye/ar/CBY.aspx?cattype=1&keyid=80&lang=2&pid=74
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/1254-22242/Financial%20Laws
8. [ ] **Central bank: money-changer circulars, and exchanger licensing requirements and forms.** Bears on PAY-59, PAY-60 and PAY-61.
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/160-41024/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA?lang=en
   - https://www.centralbank.gov.ye/Home/Detail/1264-13344/Exchangers-requirements-and-forms
9. [ ] **Central bank: official exchange-rate report.** Bears on PAY-48.
   - https://centralbank.gov.ye/Home/ExchangeRateReport
10. [ ] **Central bank: licensed banks list**, including the two Al-Kuraimi entries. Bears on PAY-60 and PAY-70.
    - https://www.centralbank.gov.ye/Home/banks
11. [ ] **Saba: currency issues and compensation.** The first two links are the July 2025 currency issues (a second issue of the 200-rial note, and a 50-rial coin); the third is the central bank's mechanism to compensate "legal currency" amounts. Bears on PAY-48, and the third link also on PAY-59.
    - https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3516903.htm
    - https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3515394.htm
    - https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3337222.htm
12. [ ] **Saba: payment rails and e-commerce policy.** Bears on PAY-58 and PAY-60.
    - The Sadad platform (3 March 2025): https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3445820.htm
    - The e-commerce law listed for Cabinet (October 2024): https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3389327.htm
    - The productive-families e-marketing platform (May 2025): https://www.saba.ye/ar/news3474361.htm
13. [ ] **Ministry e-services portal**: the commercial-registration activity categories and fees, as shown online. Bears on PAY-58.
    - https://e-moit.gov.ye/
14. [ ] **General Zakat Authority website.** Its address wasn't confirmed (possibly `zakat.gov.ye`); save whatever page sets out the obligations on businesses. Bears on PAY-62.

The Wayback Machine holds older copies of some of these, for example the payment companies page (April 2025) and the banks list (March 2025). Those show what was published on that date, not what is in force now.
