# Q1 on Shopify: what its material shows

## [1]

- Shopify's name for what a seller sets up per place is a "market", in the admin's **Markets** section. A market is "a set of customers that you define in your store". It can be made of "countries or regions for cross-border sales, company locations for B2B sales, retail locations for in-person sales, or sales channels". Its "Conditions" set who it applies to. Q1-SH-011, Q1-SH-014, Q1-SH-K1930, Q1-SH-K1225
- A country or region market holds one country or several. A sub-region market (marked "Early access") targets a state or province. On a sub-region market only the shipping options can be customized. Q1-SH-017, Q1-SH-012, Q1-SH-021, Q1-SH-022
- A new market "inherits your store's default settings", and the seller can override them with customizations. Shopify's table lists six: Catalogs (which products, at what price), Local currencies, Online store (theme sections, blocks and content), Domains and languages, Duties and taxes, and Business entities ("Shopify Plus only"). Its other page lists "currency, product availability, theme content, domains, languages, and taxes". Q1-SH-001, Q1-SH-002, Q1-SH-003, Q1-SH-004, Q1-SH-005, Q1-SH-006, Q1-SH-007, Q1-SH-K1931
- A customer is matched to a market by IP location, by a market-specific domain, subdomain or subfolder, or by a choice made in the store's selectors. A customer whose country is in no active market "can browse your store, but can't complete a purchase", and gets the backup region experience. Every store must have a backup region. Q1-SH-060, Q1-SH-061, Q1-SH-016, Q1-SH-018, Q1-SH-039, Q1-SH-K1972, Q1-SH-K1973
- Per place, a market can carry its own currency and exchange rate, its own catalogs, languages and local domains (the `MarketWebPresence` object), its own tax display and duty collection, and its own local payment methods. Q1-SH-055, Q1-SH-K1941, Q1-SH-K0091, Q1-SH-K3003, Q1-SH-K2894, Q1-SH-006, Q1-SH-051, Q1-SH-K1743, Q1-SH-K1744
- In one store's admin (capture-plus, observed 5 October 2026), the **New market** page lists under "Customized" and "Inherited": Currency, Catalogs, Discounts, Online Store, Checkout and accounts, and Domain / language, each inherited from the store default. Once the market includes United Kingdom, its Currency shows as customized (British Pound, Dynamic FX). The page then also lists Taxes and duties ("Not collecting"), Returns and cancellations, a Parent market ("Store default"), and, under "More settings", Shipping and Customer privacy. Q1-SH-106, Q1-SH-107
- In one saved Markets graph view (demo-fake-dev-store, 30 September 2026), "Store defaults" includes the business entity, beside currency, domain, taxes and duties, and returns. After duty collection was turned on for a market, the saved page held: "Shopify partners with Avalara to provide duty calculation for a 0.5% transaction fee". Q1-SH-113, Q1-SH-112
- A store's General settings page shows its "Backup Region", which "Determines settings for customers outside of your markets" (Yemen, for capture-gb-plus on 5 October 2026), and its currency display, managed from Markets. In capture-advanced's saved General page, the Backup Region field's options are Canada and Yemen. Q1-SH-102, Q1-SH-122
- What a market can't change, as the pages put it: staff permissions "can't be assigned per market"; "Only one theme can be published at a time"; fixed amount discounts "can be created only in your store's default currency". Q1-SH-035, Q1-SH-036, Q1-SH-038
- Some per-market settings depend on the plan. Per-market theme customization needs "the Advanced plan or higher". Business entities per market need the Plus plan. Regional markets are "free for all Shopify plans". Q1-SH-037, Q1-SH-013, Q1-SH-040, Q1-SH-023
- A business entity is "the legal entity that processes payments for orders in a market". Each entity has its own Shopify Payments account, payout currency and bank account. Shopify adds that "Some regions have regulations about which entity can process payments for customers in that region". Q1-SH-041, Q1-SH-K1266, Q1-SH-K1268, Q1-SH-044, Q1-SH-K1265
- Where a market's buyers belong to several market types, "the more specific market type takes priority": B2B over retail, and retail over country or region. A retail transaction can belong to a retail market and a regional market at once. Q1-SH-025, Q1-SH-026, Q1-SH-024
- Managed Markets makes Global-e the merchant of record. It "supports selling to 150+ regions", with some high-risk currency markets not supported. Q1-SH-K1958, Q1-SH-073, Q1-SH-K1966
- The admin's RequestDetails answer gives each store feature flags named for markets. `subCountryMarketsEnabled` is true on some captured stores and false on others. `perMarketIncoterms` and `landedCostIncoterms` are true on the stores that carry them. `marketDeliveryProfiles`, `managedMarkets` and `usingMarketsPro` are false on every store that has them. `marketDrivenShipping` varies by store. ShopFeatures describes `subCountryMarketsEnabled` as "Whether a shop is eligible for Sub Country Markets". It describes `unifiedMarkets` as "Whether a shop is eligible for Unified Markets". What the other keys govern was not in what was read. Q1-SH-088, Q1-SH-089, Q1-SH-087, Q1-SH-082, Q1-SH-084, Q1-SH-083, Q1-SH-091, Q1-SH-K2594, Q1-SH-K2595, Q1-SH-079, Q1-SH-080, Q1-SH-N01
- Taxes, shipping and duties per place: the tax services available "depend on the location of your business". Shipping label eligibility "varies by carrier and country". Duties are set by "the destination's rules". The Terms leave tax compliance with the merchant. Q1-SH-K1865, Q1-SH-K1866, Q1-SH-K1987, Q1-SH-K1921, Q1-SH-K1871, Q1-SH-K1891

## [2]

- Customizations are set in Shopify admin > **Markets**, per market, by clicking the plus icon next to a setting. They apply when saved. A rollout can schedule a catalog's status change. Q1-SH-010, Q1-SH-K1969, Q1-SH-K0209
- If a market has no customization of its own, it inherits from its parent market, or else from the store default. Rules differ by customization: some are overridden by the submarket, while catalogs are combined. Currency and business entity each have their own tie-break rule for multiple parents. Q1-SH-008, Q1-SH-009, Q1-SH-056, Q1-SH-045
- The staff who can reach Markets are set by permissions such as `VIEW_MARKETS` and `CREATE_AND_EDIT_MARKETS`. Managed Markets settings need `MANAGE_PAYMENTS_SETTINGS`. Apps use the `read_markets` and `write_markets` access scopes and the `markets/create`, `markets/update` and `markets/delete` webhook topics. Q1-SH-K0175, Q1-SH-K0176, Q1-SH-K2463, Q1-SH-K0862, Q1-SH-K0856, Q1-SH-K0857, Q1-SH-K0858
- A business entity change for a market "affects only new orders". Q1-SH-043
- A store's General settings page names the "Business entity used for financial products, markets, apps, and taxes in this shop", with its country, and points to Organization settings to manage entities (capture-gb-plus, observed 5 October 2026). Q1-SH-100
- In one run (capture-advanced, 4 October 2026), the saved General page lists the organization's entities to switch to, four in Yemen and one added in the United States. Switching "will update this store's business details", with "multiple tax forms". After the United States entity was chosen, the saved page held "Store address will update to match new legal entity", which "may affect subscription fees, billing currency and apps". The page's markup also holds: "To switch your legal entity, contact support to deactivate active Shopify Payments, Balance, and Capital accounts." Q1-SH-114, Q1-SH-115, Q1-SH-116, Q1-SH-118, Q1-SH-119
- Automatic redirection is on by default in new stores. It is set at Online Store > Preferences. Q1-SH-062
- No Shopify term or legal record was found naming the party responsible for markets, rollouts, backup region, domains or currency. Q1-SH-G0132, Q1-SH-G0133, Q1-SH-G0135, Q1-SH-G0138, Q1-SH-G0139, Q1-SH-G0141

## [3]

- The Shopify Contracting Party depends on the billing address of the store: Shopify Inc. and Shopify Technology (USA) Inc. for the United States or Canada; Shopify Commerce Singapore Pte. Ltd for Asia Pacific; Shopify International Limited for all other jurisdictions. Shopify's own taxes on its bills follow the billing address. Q1-SH-K1243, Q1-SH-K1244, Q1-SH-K1246, Q1-SH-K1247, Q1-SH-K1248, Q1-SH-K1328, Q1-SH-K1329
- The merchant must "maintain an accurate location in the administrative console". Merchants in unsupported countries and regions can't create an account. Q1-SH-K1271, Q1-SH-K1270, Q1-SH-K2271, Q1-SH-K2272, Q1-SH-K2273, Q1-SH-K2274, Q1-SH-K2275
- At store creation, the domestic market is "Your store's home country or region, as determined by your store's address and currency". It also becomes the backup region. The international market is "A curated list of countries or regions where merchants similar to you also sell". Q1-SH-028, Q1-SH-029, Q1-SH-K1974
- Shopify Payments is used "only in the Designated Country", with a different Shopify entity per country group. In the EU/EEA and UK a payment facilitator works with the acquirer Adyen N.V. Canada has its own regulated payment services entity. Payment Processor Terms are named per Designated Country. Q1-SH-K1535, Q1-SH-K1536, Q1-SH-K1537, Q1-SH-K1544, Q1-SH-K1546, Q1-SH-K1547, Q1-SH-K1548, Q1-SH-K1607, Q1-SH-K1608, Q1-SH-K1611, Q1-SH-K1614, Q1-SH-K1550, Q1-SH-K1566, Q1-SH-K1569
- By default, orders go through "your primary Shopify Payments account, which is associated with your store's home country". Q1-SH-042
- Where Shopify Payments isn't available, the seller picks a provider under "Choose a provider". Where it is available, standalone Stripe isn't offered. The saved Payments page of one Yemen-address store (ibx5bt-xy, 30 September 2026) shows "Payment providers" with "Choose a provider". Q1-SH-065, Q1-SH-K1528, Q1-SH-K1533, Q1-SH-123
- Fees by location: the currency conversion fee "is either 1.5% or 2%, depending on your store's location". Transaction fee rates are given "for your location". Q1-SH-057, Q1-SH-K1946, Q1-SH-070
- Some services are offered only where the business is based. Managed Markets needs a business based in the United States, Canada or the United Kingdom. Shopify Balance is for merchants with a residential address in the United States or Puerto Rico. Shopify Protect is for businesses in the United States. Shopify Capital lists nine countries, each with its own lender. Multi-currency payouts were extended to Australia and France, with different currency lists. Some local currencies "aren't available to merchants in France". Q1-SH-071, Q1-SH-K1956, Q1-SH-K1793, Q1-SH-K0041, Q1-SH-K1671, Q1-SH-K1815, Q1-SH-K1816, Q1-SH-K1819, Q1-SH-K1824, Q1-SH-K1838, Q1-SH-K1843, Q1-SH-075, Q1-SH-076, Q1-SH-077, Q1-SH-078, Q1-SH-059
- New merchants in Europe have their store data stored at rest in Europe by default. Q1-SH-K2321
- `threeDSecureEligibilityForCountry` is false on every captured store that carries it. The admin's RequestDetails answer gives each captured store's billing address country. It is `"countryCodeV2":"YE"` for 13 of the 14 stores and `"US"` for capture-plus-dev. The General settings page of capture-gb-plus shows "Store address Yemen". Q1-SH-090, Q1-SH-092, Q1-SH-093, Q1-SH-101
- In that switch, the saved page held "Store address must be in the same country as the business entity". The form for a United States individual entity asks for a "Social Security Number (SSN)", "Used for tax filing and verification". Q1-SH-117, Q1-SH-120
- For a store whose business entity address is `"countryCode":"YE"` (capture-gb-plus, observed 5 October 2026), the admin's answers give Shopify Payments `"eligible":false`, with `"localPaymentMethodsForMerchantCountry":[]`; Stripe `"available":false,"eligible":false`; Amazon Pay and PayPal Express Checkout `"eligible":false`; and `"flexibleGatewaysEligible":true,"thirdPartyDirectEligible":true`. Q1-SH-094, Q1-SH-095, Q1-SH-096, Q1-SH-097, Q1-SH-098, Q1-SH-099
- The third-party providers the admin listed for one Yemen-address store were (for testing) Bogus Gateway, Tap Payments, ONERWAY (Direct), Integration-UseePay, PagBrasil - Cartão de crédito and Cybersource (fake-demo-sharrwah, observed 5 October 2026). Q1-SH-103
- The admin's currency options for one Yemen-rial store give `"foreignExchangeFee":"0.02"` (capture-grow, observed 2 October 2026). Q1-SH-104

## [4]

- At store creation, Shopify creates a domestic market and an inactive international market for the seller. Q1-SH-027, Q1-SH-028, Q1-SH-029
- To add a place, the seller goes to Markets > **Create market** and, under **Includes**, adds a "Regions" condition. Regional markets have no limit and are free on all plans. In one run, a market whose Includes was United Kingdom was saved with "3 rates • Shipping to United Kingdom" under Shipping, and "Store default" as its parent market. Q1-SH-030, Q1-SH-031, Q1-SH-023, Q1-SH-040, Q1-SH-107
- A market can be activated only for countries that have shipping rates. A draft market, or one whose countries aren't all shipped to, stays inactive. In one run, the saved page of a Saudi Arabia market with no shipping rates held "Region Active", and that its customers "won’t be able to check out". A shipping zone's page says "To start selling to 27 countries/regions in this zone, include them in a market". Q1-SH-034, Q1-SH-032, Q1-SH-033, Q1-SH-108, Q1-SH-109, Q1-SH-110
- With business entities, the seller must set up an extra Shopify Payments account per legal entity first, and add a market for each entity. Q1-SH-046, Q1-SH-K1261
- When Managed Markets is activated, Shopify "migrates any existing international market settings". Q1-SH-074
- On the platform's side, Shopify says Managed Markets "will be expanding to other regions". It announced Multi-Currency Payouts in Australia and France. It "consistently updates its list of available payment gateways to include new options and geographic expansions". The Shopify Payments terms let Shopify add a new Designated Country or replace an acquirer. What adding a country involves on its side was not in what was read. Q1-SH-072, Q1-SH-075, Q1-SH-068, Q1-SH-K1604, Q1-SH-N02

## [5]

- Currency: the store currency is used for pricing and reports. Changing it after the first sale goes through Shopify Support. A market's currency is set at Markets > Currency, with Dynamic or Manual exchange rates and rounding. A single-country market takes that country's currency. A multi-country market uses local currencies by default. Q1-SH-K1935, Q1-SH-K1936, Q1-SH-053, Q1-SH-054, Q1-SH-019, Q1-SH-020, Q1-SH-058
- For a market including Saudi Arabia, the currency picker listed Saudi Riyal (SAR) under "Recommended". The store currency dialog's markup offers reasons for changing, among them "Testing payment processors not available in current currency" and "Experimenting with different markets". Q1-SH-111, Q1-SH-121
- In one run, a new market that included United Kingdom showed Currency "British Pound", "Dynamic FX", with a rate from YER. In the same admin, the store's own currency YER is among the supported presentment currencies with `"automaticRate":null`. Q1-SH-107, Q1-SH-105
- To accept several currencies, the store must use Shopify Payments or Adyen. Only Shopify Payments cards, Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and PayPal Express are listed as multi-currency compatible. With other providers, local currency affects display only. The store feature `multiCurrencyGatewayEnabled` is false or true depending on the store and set. Q1-SH-K1937, Q1-SH-K1940, Q1-SH-048, Q1-SH-049, Q1-SH-050, Q1-SH-085, Q1-SH-086
- Payment provider: it is added from Settings > Payments, and only the store owner can add one. One credit card provider can be active at a time. The provider may require bank account verification. Availability by country is shown on Shopify's payment gateways page filter. The held capture doesn't hold the filter's results per country. The runs show the providers listed to Yemen-address stores only. Q1-SH-K1523, Q1-SH-064, Q1-SH-063, Q1-SH-066, Q1-SH-067, Q1-SH-069, Q1-SH-103, Q1-SH-N03
- Local payment methods appear on a market's payments card when the market holds an eligible country, and are activated there. Managed payment methods are activated "based on the regions where you sell". Q1-SH-047, Q1-SH-051, Q1-SH-052, Q1-SH-K1745
- Payments apps add providers through the Payments Apps API, after Shopify's approval process. Q1-SH-K1695, Q1-SH-K1697
