# Eligibility and considerations for using automated filing with Shopify Tax

If you have further questions about your tax returns, then you should contact local tax authorities or a tax professional. It&#39;s your responsibility to consult with tax authorities or tax professionals to ensure that you file and remit your taxes correctly.</p>
</aside>

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Automated filing is closed to new enrollment. The pages in this section apply only to stores that are already enrolled in automated filing. If your store isn&#39;t already enrolled, automated filing isn&#39;t available and can&#39;t be turned on.</p>
</aside>


## Eligibility for automated filing with Shopify Tax

**Automated filing is closed to new enrollment.** Only stores that are already enrolled can use it. If automated filing isn't already active on your store, you can't enroll — the feature isn't available to stores that haven't previously been granted access, regardless of plan, location, or tax-registration status.

If you're already enrolled, your continued eligibility is based on your existing shop data, and the following considerations apply. These parameters can't be adjusted, and eligibility isn't being reopened to new stores.

For an enrolled store, automated filing has the following requirements and limitations:

* Automated filing is available only for stores in the United States that use Shopify Tax.
* Automated filing is not available if you used a tax service other than Shopify Tax in 2025, such as Basic Tax, Manual Tax, or third-party tax apps.
  * Orders that had tax collected by a tax service other than Shopify Tax at the time of the transaction, will not be included in tax filing reports.
* Automated filing is not available if your store has customers who've requested [data erasure](/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/processing-customer-data-requests#erase-customer-personal-data).
* To file your tax returns, you must complete your setup for each state where you collect sales tax before the [filing deadline](/manual/taxes/shopify-tax/automated-filing/timeline#tax-return-timeline).
* Taxes are filed only for sales that were made on the Shopify platform where you are the merchant of record. Automated filing can't be performed in stores with the following type of sales:
  * Imported orders.
  * Sales channels that aren't connected to Shopify.
  * Marketplaces where you aren't the merchant of record, such as Amazon, Meta, or TikTok. If you're not sure whether you're the merchant of record for a sale, then refer to the **Filed by channel** column of the [taxes finance report](/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/finances-report#taxes-finance-report). If the entry for a sale is marked as **Filed**, then the marketplace has indicated that it files taxes on your behalf.

## Considerations for using automated filing with Shopify Tax {#considerations}

The amount of tax that's due on your return should generally equal the amount of tax collected during the return period. There might be differences in US states that impose additional gross receipts taxes or that allow filing discounts.

Before you use automated filing, review the following considerations.

### Filing scope and tax service limitations

* Automated filing is performed only for current or upcoming filings. If you need help with past or amended returns, then contact a tax professional.
* Automated filing is performed only for a single store at one time, and can't be performed for multiple stores at one time within an organization.
* Automated filing doesn't support Streamlined Sales Tax (SST).
* Automated filing can't support prepayments in any state.

### Order and app compatibility

* Automated filing doesn't include orders that had tax calculated by a tax service other than Shopify Tax. 
* Automated filing isn't compatible with the [Loop Returns & Exchanges app](https://apps.shopify.com/loop-returns).
* As of July 1, 2024, product tax codes and categories are recorded as they existed at the time that the products were ordered. Destination and origin addresses are matched for these orders. From January 1, 2024 to June 30, 2024, if multiple order edits with address changes occurred, then the initial order addresses are used. 
* Refunds are included in automated filing calculations when they can be attributed to a specific line item on the invoice. [Learn more about issuing refunds](/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/refunding-orders). 

### Store settings for automated filing accuracy

To ensure accuracy in returns that are generated by automated filing, the following must be true for the entire period that you file for:

* [Tax-inclusive pricing](/manual/taxes/include-exclude-taxes) is deactivated for your store.
* USD is set as your [store currency](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/store-currency/index).

## Marketplace sales reporting for automated filing

Marketplace facilitators, such as Amazon, Etsy, and TikTok, are responsible for the collection and remittance of sales tax on behalf of merchants that use their platforms. However, even though these marketplaces frequently file and remit taxes, certain states and localities require that you report those sales in your gross sales totals.

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Meta (Facebook and Instagram) has transitioned away from acting as a marketplace facilitator for all new orders created as of August 26, 2025. Merchants are responsible for tax collection and compliance on these orders. Facebook orders created before August 26, 2025 remain Meta&#39;s responsibility for tax filing and remittance.</p>
</aside>

### Channels supported for automated filing

Shopify Tax automated filing includes sales that were made on [Shop](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop) and on [Meta](/manual/online-sales-channels/social-commerce/facebook-instagram-by-meta) channels orders made before August 26th, 2025 (Facebook and Instagram) in your gross sales totals, where required. 

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>As of August 26, 2025, Meta no longer acts as a marketplace facilitator. This means:</p>
<ul>
<li>You&#39;re responsible for tax compliance on these orders.</li>
<li>Orders created through the Facebook &amp; Instagram sales channel before August 26, 2025 remain Meta&#39;s responsibility for tax filing and remittance.</li>
</ul>
</aside>

If you sell with sales channels that aren't supported, then you can still use automated filing for states where there are no marketplace reporting requirements. However, Shopify doesn't check your marketplace settings when determining eligibility for automated filing. If you have questions about your specific tax liabilities, then consult a tax professional.
