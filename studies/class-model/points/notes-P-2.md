## Points that were hard to land, and why

- **S01-VP-11, "account".** The platforms name a dozen kinds of account. A reading: they are of two kinds. One is the account with the party that processes and holds the funds behind the platform's own service (Shopify's Payment Processor Accounts, the Global-e account, WooCommerce's Stripe Express account and Stripe's pooled accounts, BigCommerce's PayPal platform account). That is CM-PAY-004's. The other is the seller's own account with another gateway (Salla's gateway credentials SA-C-120/boundary/SA-S-0205, Al Rajhi Mokafaa, Zid's Tap, BigCommerce's Stripe and Braintree). That is CM-PAY-011's. These were not landed: the instalment providers' accounts (Zid's Tabby, Madfu, Emkan and MIS Pay, S01-V-196 to S01-V-199; Salla's Tamara, S01-V-185), which are CM-PAY-015's; Zid Pay's account balance (S01-V-190), which is CM-PAY-005's; and Zid's Trendyol and Amazon seller accounts (S01-V-200, S01-V-201), which are CM-CHN-003's and are no money account. S01-D1.3 (no balance sellers pay into) rests on Salla's wallet. The model holds that wallet as CM-PLN-006, core on Salla and Zid, which agrees with it, so there is no `premise` conflict.
- **S01-VP-13, "terms of service".** Two things the model keeps apart: the processor's or provider's terms behind the payment service (CM-PAY-004), and the platform company's own agreement, with its entity and law (CM-ACC-002, an `open` capability). Every variant is a term of an agreement, so none supports a placement and none makes a `count`.
- **S01-VP-18, "held".** The model puts payout holds in two places. CM-PAY-006 holds reserves and processors' holds. CM-PAY-005 holds the pauses tied to the payout account: Shopify's pause on a new payout destination (SH-C-069/decides/SH-S-0345, shows), WooPayments' balance held until a bank account is added (WC-C-007/decides/WC-S-0530) and its suspended or paused payouts (WC-C-007/decides/WC-S-0533, WC-C-007/reason/WC-S-5289). So the point lands on both, CM-PAY-006 first. The coordinator may want to choose one home for holds. I did not record that as a `structure` conflict: payouts and reserves are two things, and only the holds straddle them.
- **S01-VP-19, "deducted".** The platforms answer in four capabilities: the payout netted of refunds, chargebacks, reserves and set-off (CM-PAY-005); the payment service's processing fees (CM-PAY-007); the platform's own charges taken from the seller's balance (CM-PLN-005: Salla's waybills and dues from the wallet or e-payments, S01-V-473, S01-V-474, S01-V-601; Zid's dues from wallet deposits, S01-V-481, S01-V-482); and financing repaid from sales (CM-PAY-026). Fees that third-party providers charge (BigCommerce's PayPal, Stripe and other processors, S01-V-650 to S01-V-652) are CM-PAY-011's; they were not landed and make no conflict. The rows: S01-D1.2 and S01-E3 rest on Shopify billing third-party transaction fees and deducting Shopify Payments fees, and on Salla collecting financing from the sales it holds. The model agrees: CM-PLN-004 and CM-PLN-005 are separate from CM-PAY-005 and CM-PAY-007, and Salla's CM-PAY-026 is core. So there is no `premise` conflict.
- **S01-VP-21, "refunded to your customers".** No capability names "funds returned to their source". A reading: it is the end of a hold (CM-PAY-006, where Zid's ZD-C-037/responsible/ZD-S-0772 is cited) or a sanction of the platform's enforcement (CM-TRU-009, which cites the same Zid statement as core, and holds Salla and WooCommerce). WooCommerce's statement (S01-WC-010, from WC-C-048/decides/WC-S-0472) is cited in CM-PAY-002 as shows, not in either landing capability. CM-TRU-009's WooCommerce cell is own-service, so nothing conflicts.
- **S01-VP-23, "currency".** Reach `cross-cutting: money records and currency`, a reading: the value asked for is the currency the funds settle and pay out in, which every amount carries. The main capability is still the payout (CM-PAY-005). With it come conversion (CM-MKS-007) and Zid's wallet currencies that can't be withdrawn (S01-V-528, CM-PLN-006).
- **S01-VP-14 and S01-VP-15.** Beside the payout (CM-PAY-005), Salla and Zid time cash on delivery remitted by their shipping services (CM-PAY-029) and wallet withdrawals (CM-PLN-006), so both points also land there. These were not landed: third-party providers' own schedules (Shopify's S01-V-288; BigCommerce's Stripe, S01-V-629, S01-V-636, and the standalone PayPal gateway, S01-V-630, S01-V-348), which are CM-PAY-011's, and Zid's Trendyol and Amazon deposits (S01-V-324, S01-V-325), which are CM-CHN-003's. BigCommerce's 21-day hold (S01-V-635) is under S01-VP-18's conflict.

## Each conflict, with both sides' evidence

1. **S01-VP-18, `count`, Salla (rows: S01-VP-18, model).**
   - The subject: S01-SA-160 says "Store verification is required to complete transfers. Unverified stores will have funds pending until verification is complete" (Saudi Arabia). S01-SA-161 says verification is required for "Transferring Electronic Payments Balance". S01-SA-135 says "يتم تعليق عمليات الدفع والسداد إلى أن يتم إتمام خطوات توثيق المتجر" (KSA). These are Salla's own payment service holding funds, its built-in behaviour: `core` by B-extent's rule.
   - The model: CM-PAY-006's Salla cell is none-held. Its search covered معلق and تعليق التحويل and cites nothing. CM-PAY-005's Salla cell cites only the bank-account name match, SA-C-112/decides/SA-S-0098, as shows.
   - A reading: the statements bear out core.
   - Not a placement: S01-SA-089 (the Saudi Central Bank's and the Ministry of Commerce and Investment's restrictions may delay transfers) is a term of Salla's agreement.
   - Not a conflict type: S01-SA-180 and S01-SA-181 (payments held by the issuing bank over disputes, observed) show a third party, which supports `outside`, and outside on a none-held cell is no conflict type. Both go below as slot-filler leads.
2. **S01-VP-18, `count`, BigCommerce (rows: S01-VP-18, model).**
   - The subject: S01-BC-082 says "For new BigCommerce Payments accounts, initial payments are held for up to 21 days before being available for payout", and points to PayPal's page on payments on hold. S01-BC-083 says "Funds may be held or blocked for several reasons ... Check the Actions section". This is a hold on the platform's own service: core by built-in behaviour (BigCommerce Payments is core in CM-PAY-002).
   - The model: CM-PAY-006's BigCommerce cell is none-held (searched hold, held).
   - A reading: PayPal may place the hold, which would make it outside. What would settle it: the PayPal page S01-BC-082 cites, or BigCommerce Payments' terms with PayPal.
3. **S01-VP-22, `placement`, Salla (rows: S01-D13, model).**
   - The model: CM-PAY-015's Salla cell is core only (SA-C-118/where/SA-S-0070, SA-C-118/decides/SA-S-1965, flags SA-S-3125 and SA-S-3128). Its reading says who lends isn't stated. Tabby and Tamara are listed as slot fillers, but no cell places them.
   - The subject: S01-SA-102 (Tabby pays the store first and collects from the buyers later), S01-SA-104 (Tamara settles all payments on the agreed schedule) and S01-SA-101 (payments through Tabby are collected through Tabby, not Salla Payments, on the merchant's agreement with Tabby, UAE). These show third parties doing the lending and the settlement.
   - A reading: outside beside core.
   - S01-D13 is a moved row (to the installments subject), so under the brief this conflict is carried there.
4. **S01-VP-22, `count`, BigCommerce (rows: S01-D13, model).**
   - The model: CM-PAY-015's BigCommerce cell is not-stated (BC-C-027/where/BC-S-0161, BC-C-025/where/BC-S-0142, BC-I-0438, BC-I-0442, BC-I-0445). Its reading says no statement names instalments.
   - The subject: S01-BC-024, on bigcommerce.com/payments/paypal/, reads "Allow customers to pay in installments with Pay in 4 and Pay Monthly while you get paid up front". S01-BC-022, on BigCommerce's product page Consumer financing, reads "Our consumer financing integrations guarantee you get paid". By the model's convention for a product line (CM-RET-001's BigCommerce cell is core on a product line, BC-S-0117), these support core. S01-BC-094 (the shopper owes PayPal) supports outside.
   - What would settle it: whether a marketing product page's line counts as a product line in B-extent's sense.

## Problems with the brief or the script

- **The own-service rule.** The brief's rule for a `count` on `own-service` ("only on a statement naming the company as the maker or provider") is stricter than the model's practice of joining a maker statement held in another cell. It decides S01-VP-22 on WooCommerce, above. The coordinator may want one rule for both.
- **The `concern` basis.** The checker accepts `concern:<id>` only for a concern landed in a landing capability. Zid's ZD-C-007, whose statements CM-PAY-006 cites (ZD-C-007/decides/ZD-S-0582), lands in CM-PAY-002. So S01-VP-21 records only the `cited` route, though the `show` view's leads came by concern too. This is not a problem with the landing, only with how its route can be written.
