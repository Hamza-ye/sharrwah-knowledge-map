## Problems with the brief or the tools

- **Working copies.** The harness refused a working file named `.md` in the scratch directory as a report file, so the per-sentence working copy is `analysis.txt` there.
- **`record_ids.py` on long axes** printed only the first citations and then "... and N more citations" (YA-62 showed 59 more), so when the draft was first written the quotes behind YA-62's rows for Stripe, WooPayments and Zid Pay Kuwait weren't readable, and the draft rested on the digest's state and date for Stripe's row. `--all`, added since, prints them; the revision read Stripe's two (its list of supported countries, and "Is your business outside of a supported country/region?") and rewrote section 6's sentence to say only what they and the state give.
- **The checker and Yemen ids.** It reads every mention of a Yemen id as a fact needing its state and date, and counts it again; a bare id repeated at the end of a point is flagged. The draft writes each Yemen fact once per point, in full, at the point's end.
- **WooCommerce's policy pages.** The brief's open question, whether they count among WooCommerce's terms, is set under section 8 (Q5 and Q7). The draft treats them as help pages (stated), as `source_kinds.py` gives them, and never calls them terms.
- **A digest date after today.** YA-29 gives its state at the UN Security Council's area as of 2026-11-14, after today (24 October 2026); its quotes are dated 2025-11-14 and say the measures were renewed "until 14 November 2026". The draft doesn't use YA-29.
