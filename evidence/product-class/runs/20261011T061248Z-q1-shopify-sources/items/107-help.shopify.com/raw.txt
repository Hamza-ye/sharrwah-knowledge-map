# Subprocessors

All personal data from customers in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (U.K.), and Switzerland is initially processed by Shopify International Limited (Ireland). Shopify affiliated entities and third-party subprocessors help Shopify process personal data for merchants in accordance with our [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.shopify.com/legal/dpa). If your store is on the Shopify Plus plan, then you can contact Shopify Plus Support for more information.

The Subprocessors used by Shopify for the performance of the Services under the Terms are listed below. To receive notifications about new subprocessors, click **Sign up for notifications** and complete the registration. After registration, a confirmation email is sent to verify the subscription for notifications.
