Main advantages
What is Mokafaa?
Mokafaa is a loyalty program from Al Rajhi Bank that rewards customers with points for every purchase. By activating Mokafaa in your store, your customers can use their existing Mokafaa points to pay, or earn new points every time they shop.
How will your customers benefit from Mokafaa in your store?
Your customers can redeem their Mokafaa points at checkout to get discounts, or earn new points that increase their balance with every purchase. This makes shopping at your store more rewarding and keeps them coming back.
