## How the structure reads

- **An entry stands for every item with its words.** The structure holds 699 rows over 21 steps, and many steps repeat a screen (`meta.json`, `dumps_sha256`: steps 15, 16, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26 and 30 are one dump). The build made one entry of the items that agree, and its locator names the first. So `SH-E-7d092ea3`, 'Changes will only affect this device', stands for lines 131, 416, 445 and 473 (steps 17, 27, 28 and 29): the Payments page's subtitle and the Payment options page's. The Back button (`SH-E-e5c9d6d0`) and the heading wrappers stand for every settings page's.
- **`parent` is an index within the entry's step**, not a `position` and not an entry: step 14's `parent: 4` is the Settings scroll view, step 17's `parent: 7` the Payments page's. I read each entry's item at its locator's line for its step (the brief's known case for a numeric `parent` speaks of a `position`; here it is the step's `index`).
- **A row is read with its parents** (rule 1): a button or switch, its label and its description are one setting; the value a setting shows (Off, 'Use device settings', 'Lock after 2 minutes of inactivity') is this device's own and goes in `reading`; a switch's state (`checked`) goes in `reading`.

## The rules I followed (for the coordinator to check, and SH-97 to keep)

1. **The frame is `out`** (the ruling after SH-37). A layout node with no text and no description whose id names a frame part, not a thing: Android's `action_bar_root`, `content` and `navigationBarBackground`, `TabNavigator/AccessibilityBoundary`, `Component.Heading`, `Component.Heading.Default`, a screen's `…ActionBar` and `Component.ActionBar`, the tab bar's `activeIndicator`. The Back button (`Component.ActionBar.PrimaryActionButton`) is the frame's navigation, `out`.
2. **A screen is a container** (M-13): its view and scroll view (`Screen.Settings`, `Screen.PaymentTypesSettings.screen`, `Screen.TipSettings`, `Component.LocationSelection`, `Screen.PaymentSettingsOptions.screen`, `Screen.ScreenLockScreen`) and its heading (`Component.Heading.Title`) take the capability of the screen's own thing, `core`, the id or the heading as the term. A screen with no `Screen.*` id (Apps, Dark/light mode) is placed by its heading.
   - Settings: CM-POS-001, the POS app's own settings, placed once. Its sections and settings take no CM-POS-001 line for sitting under it (M-6 refined): each is placed on what it sets.
   - Payments and Payment options: CM-PAY-001. Tips: `new:Tipping`. Locations: CM-INV-005. Apps: CM-POS-004, `extension-point` (rule 7). Dark/light mode and Screen lock: CM-POS-001.
3. **A section header** (`undefined.SectionHeader.Title`) is a container on the capability its title names (M-13): Display, Checkout and Devices on CM-POS-001 (parts of the POS app, rule 5); Orders on CM-ORD-001; Apps on CM-POS-004; Location on CM-INV-005; Account on CM-ACC-001. A group title that names only a grouping ('Other' on the Payments page, the tab 'More') is `out` (the ruling after SA-28).
4. **A setting is placed on the capability of what it sets, `core`** (the coordinator's message: POS settings are the platform's own channel, `core`, as CM-POS-001's cell holds them). One line per thing the setting names as its own; the capability agrees with SH-6's and SH-7's POS role permissions where both name the same thing (fulfilment, CM-SHP-009; location, CM-INV-005; gift cards, CM-LOY-002; store credit, CM-LOY-001; apps, CM-POS-004; log out, CM-POS-001).
5. **A setting of the POS app alone goes on CM-POS-001 only:** its look (Dark/light mode), its screen lock, the devices it works with (HID mode for barcode scanners), its log out. This keeps to SH-34's rule 8 ('A screen or setting of POS alone takes CM-POS-001 only (connectivity, devices, cart button, editor).') and to the changelog lines on the same things: dark mode on POS Terminal (SH-E-b1c80711), the POS lock screen (SH-E-37f4156a), HID scanner support (SH-E-6a0173b7), and SH-6's LOG_OUT (SH-E-098d07ef).
6. **A payment type on the Payments page takes two lines** (M-6 refined: a payment type is a kind of what the page's title, Payments, names): its own method's capability, and CM-PAY-001. Cash, CM-PAY-016 (Shopify's own words list cash among manual payment methods, CM-PAY-016's note, SH-S-0322); Credit/debit, CM-PAY-021; Gift cards, CM-LOY-002; Store credit, CM-LOY-001.
7. **The POS's list of apps is `extension-point` on CM-POS-004** (M-7, the platform's own list of third parties' things): the section Apps, All apps, the page Apps and 'Apps give you additional features in POS.'. 'Manage apps in Shopify admin' and its button Visit admin name the admin's apps: CM-APP-001, `core`.
8. **An option of a setting** (a radio button's '2 minutes') is a kind of the setting's thing (M-6) and takes its line, its period a quality: `lock after inactivity: 2 minutes`. The value the Settings list shows for this device ('Lock after 2 minutes of inactivity') is the device's own: `reading`, never a quality (rule 5). **SH-97:** '5 minutes' and '10 minutes' take CM-POS-001 `core` with `lock after inactivity: 5 minutes` and `lock after inactivity: 10 minutes`; 'Lock screen after sale' (`LockSale`, off) with its label and its description 'Screen will lock after an order is completed.' takes CM-POS-001 `core`, off in `reading`.
9. **One store's or one account's own data is `out`:** the signed-in user's name and email, the store's location named 'Shop location' and its country 'Yemen', and the Apps page's 'Last updated just now'. The location setting itself (`Screen.Settings.LocationsItem`) is placed (CM-INV-005); the name it shows is not.
10. **Grouping:** a switch or button and its label share a line where their terms are the same (the label's words); a value or a description, whose words differ, takes its own line.

## Problems with this brief or the script

- `show` prints an entry's `parent` without its step, and for this structure the parent is an index within the step. I mapped each entry to its item by the locator's line (`entries.tsv`) to read its step. Printing the step would save that.
- The checker printed no `CHANGES` or `NOTE` line for this batch: no line changes a cell's supports, and the containers of this structure aren't noted (see the report).
- No rule of the brief read differently from B-extent.md here.
- **Scope:** every line's `scope` is empty. The structure is one store's POS device (fake-demo-sharrwah), but no entry states a plan, store, market or listing condition for a setting; as SH-1 and SH-2 did for one store's screens, the store isn't written as scope. 'Changes will only affect this device' is the settings' own reach, in `reading`.
