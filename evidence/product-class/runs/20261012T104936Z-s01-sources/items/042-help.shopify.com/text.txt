# Viewing and exporting your Shopify Payments payout details

The [**Payouts**](https://admin.shopify.com/payments/payouts) page of your Shopify admin displays your previous payouts, any scheduled payouts, and the **Payout balance** amount. The **Payout balance** amount represents the funds that have been captured but not yet added to a scheduled payout, not including reserved funds. You can also export your transactions as a CSV file to view the total fees for each payout and pending transaction, or [export the transactions for a specific payout](#export-a-payout).

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Your payouts page might differ from the guidance on this page during the release of a new payouts view in the Shopify admin. If you need help managing payouts in the new view, then <a href="/en/manual/your-account/contact-shopify-support">contact Shopify Support</a>.</p>
</aside>

To reconcile balance activity across a date range, you can use the [Shopify Payments activity report](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/payouts-activity-report) from **Finance** > [**Documents**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance/documents) in your Shopify admin.

## Using the new payouts view

<aside class="note note-beta"><h4>Early access</h4><p>The new payouts view is in early access and is available only to some merchants.</p>
</aside>

In the new payouts view, payouts are grouped by payout date. For each payout date, you can review the total amount paid or expected, individual payouts for that date, and the transactions and adjustments that make up the amount.

The new payouts view can include the following details:

* Payout status
* Payout ID
* Payout amount
* The bank account where the payout is being sent
* The transfer reference number, when available
* A breakdown of charges, refunds, adjustments, fees, and reserves
* Transactions included in the payout

Pending payout amounts and payout dates are estimates. The final payout amount can change before funds are sent to your bank account because of refunds, chargebacks, reserves, adjustments, or processing updates.

If your store receives payouts in multiple currencies, then payout and transaction details can be separated by currency. To learn more, refer to [payouts in multiple currencies](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/store-currency/payouts-in-multiple-currencies).

## Filtering transactions

You can use the following filters to review your transactions:

* **Transaction type**: Filter by specific transaction types.
* **Payout status**: View transactions by their payout status.
* **Payment method**: Filter by payment method used.
* **Credit card**: Filter by specific credit card types.
* **Transaction date**: Filter by date range when transactions occurred.
* **Payout date**: Filter by when transactions were paid out.
* **Currency**: Filter by transaction currency.

You can save filtered searches for future use. For example, you can create a saved view that displays only transactions in CAD currency, which is useful if you're selling in multiple currencies.

## View a specific payout {#view-a-payout}

You can navigate to the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance) > [**Payouts**](https://admin.shopify.com/payments/payouts) page of your Shopify admin to review the details of a specific payout by clicking the Payout date in the Payout transactions table.

The **Overview** section displays the following information about the payout:

* The total amount of the payout.
* The payout status.
* The bank account that the payout is transferring to.
* The transfer reference number (where available).
* A breakdown of the charges, refunds, adjustments, and reserves.

The **Transactions** section shows the details of each transaction included in the payout.

For more information about the status of a specific payout, you can reach out to the bank connected to your Shopify Payments account and provide them with the payout's transfer reference number.

## View all transactions

Clicking **View order transactions** on the **Payouts** page lists all transactions associated with all of your payouts.

Transactions share the same [status](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts/view-details) as payouts with the addition of the **Pending** status. Transactions marked as **Pending** are for sales that you've made, but that haven't been added to a payout and sent to your bank account yet. The Payout Date for pending transactions is an estimate only.

## Export your transactions {#export-transactions}

The total fees for each payout and pending transaction are displayed in the **Transactions** table. You can export the transactions for a specific payout, or export all of your transactions, to a CSV file. Both exports contain the same [CSV file columns](#csv-file-columns).

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Exported CSV files are emailed to you and the Shopify store owner.</p>
</aside>

### Export a specific payout {#export-a-payout}

You can export only the transactions that are included in a specific payout. Use this export when you need to reconcile one payout or bank deposit.

<div class="sections"><details open><summary id="new-payouts-view">New payouts view</summary><ol>

<li>Click the payout date, and then click the payout that you want to export.</li>

### Export all transactions {#export-all-transactions}

You can export all of your transactions, or the transactions within a date range.

#### Steps:

1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Settings** > [**Payments**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/payments).


2. Click **View payouts**.
3. Click **View Transactions**.
4. Click **Export**.
5. Select the range of transactions that you want to export and the type of CSV file, and then click **Export balance transactions**.
