# AI-powered tools

You can use AI-powered tools to help build, manage, and grow your Shopify store. Some tools are part of Shopify, such as Sidekick and other AI features in your Shopify admin.

Other tools connect your store to third-party AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Muse, or Perplexity. You control what each tool can access and do in your store.

<aside class="note note-caution"><h4>Caution</h4><p>AI outputs can contain errors, so review anything a tool creates or changes.</p>
</aside>

### Getting better results from AI-powered tools
Whatever tool you use, following [best practices](/manual/ai-powered-tools/best-practices) helps you get better results. You should give clear instructions, add context about your store, refine results through conversation, and review changes before you apply them.

### Getting help and completing tasks with Sidekick
[Sidekick](/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick) is an AI assistant in your Shopify admin. You can chat with Sidekick to get guidance, [generate content](/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick/generate-content), [build custom apps](/manual/ai-powered-tools/sidekick/generate-apps), and complete tasks using everyday language.

Sidekick also works inside the pages that you edit. When you click the <img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MagicIcon.svg" alt="Generate text" class="admin-icon" /> icon, Sidekick writes text for the following:

* [Blog posts](/manual/online-store/blogs/writing-blogs/generate-blog-posts)
* [Online store pages](/manual/online-store/add-edit-pages#shopify-magic)
* [Product descriptions](/manual/products/details/product-descriptions/generate)
* [Shopify Messaging emails](/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/email/create-email/generate-text)
* [Content in the theme editor](/manual/online-store/themes/customizing-themes/theme-editor/sidekick/generate-content)

In the theme editor, Sidekick can also [generate custom theme blocks](/manual/online-store/themes/customizing-themes/theme-editor/sidekick/generate-blocks) from a text prompt, without writing code. In the file editor, Sidekick can [change an image from a text prompt](/manual/ai-powered-tools/media-generation#generate).

### Creating images and videos with Tinker
[Tinker](/manual/ai-powered-tools/tinker) is a free mobile app with more than 100 AI tools for creating images, videos, and brand assets for your store, such as product photography and marketing videos.

### Connecting third-party AI tools
You can [connect your store to third-party AI tools](/manual/ai-powered-tools/connecting-ai-tools), such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Muse, and Perplexity. From within those tools, you can ask questions and manage store data such as products, orders, and customers.

You control what data is shared and what access each tool has. If you're a developer connecting an AI tool from your own machine instead, then refer to the [Shopify CLI connector](/manual/ai-powered-tools/shopify-cli-connector) page.
