# Q6 on Zid: account

## [1]

### Where Zid says it serves merchants

- Zid's help centre FAQ "زد يخدم التجار في السعودية فقط؟ هل يخدم الخليج؟" says: "حاليًا زد يخدم التجار المتواجدين في السعودية ودول الخليج فقط من جميع الجنسيات". Q6-ZD-K0226
- zid.sa lists other country pages: Egypt, UAE, Kuwait, Oman and Syria. Q6-ZD-K0225
- The help centre has two store-creation articles, one for "مستخدمي دول الخليج" and one for "مستخدمي السعودية". Q6-ZD-001; Q6-ZD-002
- The Egypt page says "Join thousands of merchants in Egypt and launch your online store easily". Zid Egypt states its own commercial registration, tax number and Cairo address. Q6-ZD-K0220; Q6-ZD-K0221; Q6-ZD-K0222; Q6-ZD-K0223; Q6-ZD-K0219
- The UAE page says "Join thousands of merchants in the UAE and launch your online store easily." Q6-ZD-029
- The Syria page calls Zid "The best and easiest e-commerce platform in Syria". Its FAQ says a merchant can "create your online store through Zid Syria". Q6-ZD-026; Q6-ZD-028
- The Syria page's FAQ answers were not in what was read, including the answer to "Do I need a commercial registration to sign up with Zid Syria?". Q6-ZD-N02

### Who may register, by Zid's terms

- The terms' section الحسابات requires legal capacity and an age of at least 18, "أو قد بلغت سن الرشد حسب الأنظمة واللوائح ذات العلاقة الخاضعة لها الدولة التي تستخدم منها الخدمة". Q6-ZD-009
- An institution, company or other legal form that registers must itself have the required legal capacity. Q6-ZD-K0242
- Registration requires a name as in official papers, the current address, a mobile number, an email address and whatever else the registration form asks. Q6-ZD-010
- Information must be accurate, complete and up to date. Failing this may lead to immediate termination of the account. Q6-ZD-012
- "يحق لزد رفض طلب إنشاء أي حساب". Q6-ZD-011
- The terms belong to شركة القدرة التقنية لتقنية المعلومات والاتصالات ذ.م.م, CR 1010365366, and are governed by Saudi law. Q6-ZD-K0214; Q6-ZD-K0211
- Whether Zid has separate entities for the Gulf countries, and which terms and entity apply in Egypt, remain open. Q6-ZD-G0041; Q6-ZD-G0044; Q6-ZD-G0047

### The registration flow

- The account is created with an email address or a Google account. Q6-ZD-003
- The live registration page asks for an email first. Its next steps are "معلوماتك" ("أضف بياناتك الشخصية") and "تفاصيل المتجر" ("معلومات نشاطك التجاري"). Continuing means agreeing to the terms and privacy policy. Q6-ZD-031; Q6-ZD-032; Q6-ZD-033
- The flow asks 5 questions about the business. Q6-ZD-004
- One question is the seller's location or the inventory's location ("من وين تبيع ؟"), given through the browser or by hand as country, city, district and street. Q6-ZD-K0231; Q6-ZD-005; Q6-ZD-006
- The countries this question offers were not in what was read. Q6-ZD-N01
- The seller then chooses a plan: زد لايت, النمو or الاحترافية. Q6-ZD-007
- Fees are in Saudi riyals or the merchant's local currency. Q6-ZD-K0156

### Business details and documents

- In store data, the seller chooses a business type and attaches official documents "حسب دولتك". Q6-ZD-022
- Requirements differ by business type and country. Q6-ZD-K0240
- For Saudi Arabia the types are فرد، مؤسسة، شركة، جمعية خيرية. Q6-ZD-K0239
- For Saudi merchants there is documentation on the Saudi Business Center platform. Q6-ZD-K0238
- Saudi stores upload a national address proof from SPL, "مطلوبة لتفعيل بعد الخدمات مثل المدفوعات". Q6-ZD-023
- The requirements for stores outside Saudi Arabia were not in what was read. Q6-ZD-N03
- A commercial registration is not required unless the seller wants to connect payment gateways. Q6-ZD-K0241; Q6-ZD-K0244
- Stores must show their name, address, CR and tax number or freelance document, and official contact details. Q6-ZD-K0245; Q6-ZD-K0247

### Requirements to take payments through Zid Pay

- Requirements depend on the business entity, and the payment gateway approves the account. Q6-ZD-K0003
- Only those officially permitted to make and receive payments in their own country may subscribe to Zid Pay. Q6-ZD-K0004
- For Saudi Arabia the supported entities are فرد، مؤسسة، شركة. Q6-ZD-013
- Individuals need وثيقة عمل حر، الهوية الوطنية، حساب بنكي تجاري، شهادة العنوان الوطني. Q6-ZD-014
- Institutions need CR, national ID, a commercial bank account and national address. Q6-ZD-019
- Companies need the same, plus the articles of association and "ترخيص الاستثمار (للشركات الأجنبية)". Q6-ZD-020
- Some activities need licences, decided after review. Q6-ZD-021
- In Kuwait the entities are فرد and شركة. Separate document lists are given for فرد, مؤسسة and شركة. Q6-ZD-015; Q6-ZD-016; Q6-ZD-017; Q6-ZD-018
- The store owner must pass Nafath verification, using the store owner's ID. Q6-ZD-K0261; Q6-ZD-K0265; Q6-ZD-K0262
- No stated reason for the Nafath requirement was found. Q6-ZD-G0058
- One published product matching the activity is needed before the activation request. Q6-ZD-K0007

### Sanctions

- Zid Pay's Saudi terms bar a merchant or end beneficiary "الذي يقطن في البلدان الخاضعة للعقوبات الدولية تتضمن (إيران، سوريا، جنوب السودان، كوبا، ارتريا، جمهورية أفريقيا الوسطى)". Q6-ZD-034
- Its Kuwait terms give a longer list, which adds روسيا، اليمن، العراق، اوكرانيا among others. Q6-ZD-035
- The same terms bar dealings with sanctioned countries, and merchants named on UN, UK Treasury, EU or OFAC lists. Q6-ZD-K0251; Q6-ZD-K0252; Q6-ZD-K0254; Q6-ZD-K0253; Q6-ZD-K0257
- Who screens, and why the two lists differ, remain open. Q6-ZD-G0055; Q6-ZD-G0057

## [2]

### Zid Pay and services tied to it

- "في الوقت الحالي خيار زد باي متاح لتجار المملكة العربية السعودية و دولة الكويت." Q6-ZD-K0005; Q6-ZD-K0227; Q6-ZD-K0300
- Wallet deduction from Zid Pay is only for Saudi and Kuwaiti merchants with Zid Pay. Q6-ZD-K0143
- Zid Financing is "متاح لتجار المملكة العربية السعودية فقط" and requires Zid Pay. Q6-ZD-K0177; Q6-ZD-K0178

### What a seller elsewhere can use

- The GCC store-creation article directs the seller to the Tap gateway under الاعدادات > طرق الدفع. Q6-ZD-K0090
- The same article directs the seller to the shipping company اوتو. Q6-ZD-008
- Tap is available to merchants in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, the UAE, Qatar and Oman. Paymob is available in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Oman, and amwal pay in Oman. Q6-ZD-K0087; Q6-ZD-K0085; Q6-ZD-K0086
- Merchants in Saudi Arabia or any Gulf country can turn on shipping through licensed logistics companies. Q6-ZD-K0345
- Mazeed is "متاح لجميع متاجر زد" to Gulf merchants. Its terms bar activities prohibited under Saudi regulations. Q6-ZD-K0547; Q6-ZD-K0546
- Amazon is available "في السعودية والإمارات". Jahez requires the store or inventory to be in a city of the Kingdom. Q6-ZD-K0631; Q6-ZD-K0632
- The UAE page names Tabby and Tamara as instalment options. Q6-ZD-030
- The Syria page names "ROVO" as "Our exclusive partner in Syria". Q6-ZD-027
- Which services a store registered in Egypt can turn on remains open. Q6-ZD-G0045

### Wallet and currency limits by place

- The Zid wallet is available to all merchants outside the Kingdom with the Saudi riyal as base currency, except merchants in GCC countries. Q6-ZD-K0136
- Balances in Omani rial, UAE dirham, Kuwaiti dinar and Bahraini dinar cannot be withdrawn. Q6-ZD-K0137
- Customer Wallet APIs are available only for stores using SAR, AED, QAR, KWD, BHD or OMR. Other stores receive "Customer wallets is only available for GCC countries." Q6-ZD-025
- Changing the store's country requires the wallet balance to be zeroed first. Q6-ZD-K0230

### How Zid says so

- The FAQ that limits service to Saudi Arabia and the Gulf adds: "في المستقبل باذن الله نتوسع للعالم العربي." Q6-ZD-024
- No reason is stated for the countries served. Q6-ZD-G0049
