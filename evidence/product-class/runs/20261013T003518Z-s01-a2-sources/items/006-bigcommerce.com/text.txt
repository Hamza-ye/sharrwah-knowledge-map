Introducing BigCommerce Payments: Manage Payments Seamlessly

Get The Print Version

Tired of scrolling? Download a PDF version for easier offline reading and sharing with coworkers.

DOWNLOAD PDF
Key highlights:

BigCommerce Payments, powered by PayPal, is built directly into your store, so you can start accepting payments without adding another tool.

Manage your balances, payouts, and bank connections with PayPal from a single dashboard inside BigCommerce.

Give customers more ways to pay with cards: PayPal and Venmo ready at checkout.

Offer flexible Buy Now, Pay Later options with PayPal Pay Later, Klarna, and Afterpay, built right in.

Let shoppers check out faster with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Fastlane by PayPal included by default.

BigCommerce Payments is here: an embedded payment solution, powered by PayPal, now available directly in your BigCommerce control panel for US customers on Core, Growth, and Scale plans. For the first time, manage payments natively in BigCommerce. Accept payments, manage payouts, track your balance, and handle bank connections with PayPal without ever leaving your store.

Payments and your store, finally in one place 

From the perspective of most customers, payments live outside your storefront. You process an order in BigCommerce, then open a separate tab to check your balance, issue a payout, or investigate a transaction. Context switching adds up quickly and can create blind spots in knowing your business. 

BigCommerce Payments is built to close that gap. Powered by PayPal, it brings payment processing directly into the BigCommerce control panel through a co-branded integration that builds on more than a decade of partnership between BigCommerce and PayPal. When you connect to BigCommerce Payments, you create a new BigCommerce Payments account. Your all-in-one Money Dashboard. PayPal powers the payment processing behind the scenes through your linked PayPal account, but all transactions made through BigCommerce Payments flow through your new BigCommerce Payments account for simplified management. The centerpiece of that experience is a new Money dashboard, which gives you real-time access to balance insights, top-ups, payouts, bank connections, and currency management.

Everything included in BigCommerce Payments

BigCommerce Payments is designed to match the full capabilities customers already rely on with PayPal — everything accessible from a single interface. Here is what’s included at launch:

Supported payment methods.

Credit and debit cards  

Full suite of PayPal-branded payment methods at checkout: PayPal Checkout, and Venmo

Buy Now, Pay Later (PayPal Pay Later, Klarna, and Afterpay)

Digital wallets (Apple Pay and Google Pay)

One-click, express checkout solution (Fastlane by PayPal)

More ways to get paid.

Start simple today, with room to add more payment options as you scale.

The final word

BigCommerce Payments brings your payment operations into the same place you run your business. Whether you are setting up payments for the first time or migrating from an existing payments integration, the experience is designed to be fast to enable, easy to manage, and built on the payment infrastructure you already trust.

This will launch in the US first, with global expansion already in motion.  

Ready to get started? Turn on BigCommerce Payments, powered by PayPal, today and start managing everything from your store. Want the details? Explore our Knowledge Base.
