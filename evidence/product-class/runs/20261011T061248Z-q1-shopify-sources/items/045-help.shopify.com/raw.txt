# Shop Campaigns

[Shop Campaigns](https://www.shopify.com/shop-campaigns?utm_source=web&utm_medium=help_center&utm_campaign=shop_campaigns_overview) is an ads program that helps you acquire and convert customers at an average customer acquisition cost that you set. You're charged only when a customer converts, with no upfront budget or subscription fee.

Your ads can run across the [Shop app](https://shop.app/download), [Shop website](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/web), [Shopify Product Network](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns/understanding-campaigns#shopify-stores), and optional [third-party placements](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns/understanding-campaigns#other-platforms). How often your ads are recommended depends on the [customer experience that your store provides](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns/understanding-campaigns#ad-recommendation-status).

Before you start, check that your store meets the [requirements](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns/eligibility), then learn [how Shop Campaigns works](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/shop-campaigns/understanding-campaigns).

Learn how approvals and eligibility work in <a href="/en/manual/promoting-marketing/autopilot/shop-campaigns">Shop Campaigns with Autopilot</a>.</p>
</aside>
