Experience financial freedom with the ONE Cash app. This revolutionary solution is designed to empower you and every ONE around you to spare you
the problems that come with cash and take control over your financials seamlessly and securely. 

Break free from the traditional way of handling your financial transactions By benefiting from ONE Cash’s revolutionary solutions

•	Bill payment
Pay all your utility bills including Water bills, Electricity bills from public and private power station companies in addition to internet and landline bills.

•	Paying for purchases
Enjoy a seamless shopping experience by paying for your purchases via ONE Cash at our wide network of merchants including grocery stores, gas stations, restaurants, supermarkets, malls and many more.
 
•	Buy Airtime & bundles
You can now buy Airtime from any major telecom network in Yemen (Yemen Mobile, YOU & Sabafon) from the convenience of your smartphone.

•	Send & receive money
Send and receive money quickly and easily from your ONE Cash account via your smartphone

•	Online Payment:
Make payments and purchases from your favorite apps and sites with ease and security

• Quick Payment:
No need to inquire about your bills!
Just add your important bills to the Quick Pay list and the system will remind you of your financial obligations to pay them

•	Money cash out
Withdraw cash from your ONE Cash account through our wide network of agents all around Yemen.

•	Transfer to a bank account or e-wallet:
Send money from your ONE Cash wallet to any e-wallet or any bank account linked to WeNet

•	Send money vouchers
Transfer money to any ONE Cash Subscriber or non-subscriber any time from wherever you are with just a tap of a button

ONE Cash is an opportunity for every ONE. Regardless where you are, you can start taking charge of your financial future with this revolutionary solution as it is widely accepted among agents and merchants all across Yemen.
