## How the structure reads

- **An entry stands for every node with its words, apart from its parent's index** (the script's help). So one entry can be two places of the app: `SH-E-3a6b17b3` (Home), `SH-E-fa806128` (Orders) and `SH-E-c9417162` (Products) are a tab of the tab bar on Home (step 2, parent 42) and a row of the More navigation (steps 8 and 3, parent 0). `SH-E-fc2f9230`, 'Toggle sub navigation', stands for 128 nodes, every section's toggle button. Their lines fit both places.
- **`parent` is an index within the entry's step,** as SH-95 and SH-96 found, and the entries' text drops ids of the form `android:id/…` and `com.shopify.mobile:id/…`. I read each entry's item at its locator.
- **The More navigation's rows:** a section is a button (`TabNavigationScreen.<X>Tab.tab`, or no id for Balance and Credit), its label, and, where it has a sub navigation, a toggle node (`TabNavigationScreen.MoreTab.navOverlay.<X>.toggle`) beside a button 'Toggle sub navigation'. A sub navigation item's id is `TabNavigationScreen.<X>Tab.secondaryTabs.<item>`. The sales channels are rows of an `AppSection`, each id ending in the channel's app gid. Sales channels and Apps are header cells (`TabScreen.MoreTab.OverflowBottomSheet.Header.Cell.Channels`, `…Apps`). Settings, Quick Sale and Close sit in the overlay's bottom bar (`TabNavigationScreen.FloatingTabBar`), the node that on Home holds the six tabs.
- **Step 8 opens the More navigation with Products expanded,** a reading: step 7 had opened the Products tab (`actions.jsonl`). So Products' sub navigation first occurs at step 8, and the other sections' at the steps that expanded them: Online Store 11, Analytics 13, Balance 15, Markets 17, Content 19, Growth 21, Customers 23, and Orders in the second run's step 3.
