# G2: what Sharrwah won't do, and what it must

**What this file is:** the founder's page for gate G2 (`PLAN.md`, Stage 3), written on 26 October 2026. It isn't urgent: the S-01 review comes first.

**The list.** Stage 3 drew the topics from the evidence only:
- the seven concerns Q1 names;
- the 96 concerns that the platforms limit by country or region.

Each topic is a decision that would be expensive to change after launch ([`subjects.tsv`](../studies/subjects/subjects.tsv), with the concern map's evidence for each). How the concerns are grouped into topics, and the order, are my reading.

**What you mark,** for each topic, if you already know:
- **must:** Sharrwah certainly does this, so the topic runs first;
- **won't:** Sharrwah certainly won't, so the topic is dropped, and its decisions don't come to you;
- **evidence:** neither, so the topic runs on the evidence, in the order of the list.

Nothing else is asked.

**Not topics now:**
- **Return with A or B:** financing repaid through the platform's own payment service, as Zid's is through Zid Pay; a platform balance or bank account; platform reserves and holds. Each needs Sharrwah or a processor to hold sellers' money.
- **Left to the build:** apps, sales channels, point of sale, marketing tools, and financing from a lender that collects by its own arrangement: Shopify Capital is open to sellers on approved outside providers too. These are cheap to change. Before close-out, a last sweep re-checks this list (`PLAN.md`, Stage 5).

*Corrected on 26 October 2026:* financing from a lender that collects by its own arrangement moved from "Return with A or B" to "Left to the build". Shopify Capital is open to sellers on approved outside providers ([S-01's review, section 4](S-01-review.md#4-premature-constraints)).

**My recommendation:** mark "evidence" for all, unless you already know Sharrwah won't do something. For example, if Sharrwah won't sell across borders at launch, that narrows S-05 and S-08. A mark you're unsure of would cut the topic before its evidence is read.
