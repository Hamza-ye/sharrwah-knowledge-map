Why are payouts to my bank account paused or suspended?

On occasion, automatic payouts to your bank account may be paused or suspended. Depending on the reason for the pause or suspension, you will see a different alert in the dashboard. It will either say:

“Payouts are temporarily suspended” or…
“Payouts are currently paused because a recent payout failed.”

Please see the correct section below for more details.

“Payouts are temporarily suspended”

Payouts can be suspended for several reasons, including:

Disputes from your customers have seen a rapid or substantial increase.
Your account was flagged for manual review due to some irregular activity.
Stripe, our payments partner, has found other issues with your account.

When a suspension occurs on an active account, you should receive an email with information about the issue, either from the WooPayments team or Stripe. Please respond to this email with the required information to resume payouts.

If you did not receive an email, or have other questions, please contact support. (Note that our support staff will not be able to disclose exactly why your account was suspended or what you must do to lift the suspension.)

It might also help to make sure that your account isn’t restricted or missing any information, since that can affect payout availability as well.

Lastly, make sure your site follows our suggestions for preventing disputes. This can help avoid additional payout suspensions in the future.

“Payouts are currently paused…”

In most cases, payouts are usually paused because there’s problem with your bank account that prevents us from issuing a payout. If this is the case, you’ll see a notice about it in a few different places in the dashboard:

The Payments > Overview page, in the Payouts box
The Payments > Settings page, in the Payouts section
The Payments > Payouts page, at the top of the list

The cause of the issue could be a number of things:

Your bank account number and/or routing number is incorrect.
Your bank has reported that your account has been closed.
Your account has insufficient funds, in the case of a debit.

To correct the issue, you’ll need to update your bank account details. You can also contact support to find out the exact issue with your bank account.

Paused to recover a negative balance

Payouts can also pause if your account balance becomes negative. To try to recover the negative balance, WooPayments may debit your bank account. While that withdrawal is in progress, your automatic payouts may be paused temporarily.

For more details, please see our docs page about bank account debits.
