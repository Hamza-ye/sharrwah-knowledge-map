# Q5: Payment methods: which a seller can offer, who decides, and where the money goes, a draft proposal

## 1. How the platforms handle it

### [1] [2]

- The merchant turns methods on, from those offered to its store. Shopify's buyers pay with "any of the methods that you've activated"; Salla's merchant picks a method on its طرق الدفع page and presses (تفعيل); BigCommerce's merchant sets methods under Settings › Payments; WooCommerce's core offline gateways each carry an "Enable/Disable" box, off by default; Zid's merchant turns networks on in إدارة زد باي. - Through the platform's own service, money goes into a balance, then to the merchant's bank account. Salla: "دفع العميل إلكترونيًا ← رصيد المدفوعات الإلكترونية ← التحويل إلى حسابك البنكي"; Zid: زد باي and its provider take their fees from the amounts collected and add "المبلغ المستحق لرصيد التاجر", then deposit it in the bank account; WooPayments: "the payment amount (minus our fees) will be added to your account balance", paid out "to a bank account (all countries) or a debit card (U.S. only)"; BigCommerce Payments pays out from its balance "to a linked bank account or PayPal account"; at Shopify Payments, "the funds are transferred to the payout bank account that you set up with Shopify Payments". They match by role, on stated pages and Zid's terms (official). Q5-SA-P0006, Q5-ZD-P0024, Q5-ZD-P0041, Q5-WC-P0024, Q5-WC-P0027, Q5-BC-P0074, Q5-SH-P0060
- Who holds the money in between, where the records say. Under Shopify Payments the merchant opens "financial accounts and/or cryptocurrency wallets with one or more Payment Processors", and "The Payment Processor retains sole and exclusive responsibility for Payment Processing, including the settlement of funds"; under Stripe's terms for WooPayments, funds are "held in one or more Pooled Accounts at one or more Financial Providers", with Stripe appointed as the merchant's agent; for BigCommerce Payments, "a brand new PayPal platform account dedicated to your BigCommerce store is created". For Salla and Zid, the entity, licence or account under which the balance is held was not in what was read; Salla's Kuwait help says payments are settled under "عقد خدمات الدفع الذي تم توقيعه أثناء توثيق متجرك". They match by role, on official pages for Shopify and Stripe and stated pages for BigCommerce and Salla. Q5-SH-P0010, Q5-SH-K1596, Q5-WC-P0121, Q5-BC-P0069, Q5-SA-H0002, Q5-ZD-H0002, Q5-SA-P0064
- When the balance is paid out, and at what level. They match by role; the level differs: plan, country or account. Q5-SA-P0020, Q5-SA-P0021, Q5-SA-P0022, Q5-SA-P0008, Q5-ZD-P0041, Q5-WC-P0036, Q5-WC-P0034, Q5-WC-P0038, Q5-BC-P0083

## 4. Parts, in the order they can be built

- Part 1: **Payment method**, manual methods first, with the **Payment record**. It needs nothing else from this question.
- Part 2: the **Place payment list** and **Store payment settings**. It needs part 1.
- Part 3: the payments interface of **Provider integration**, the first **built-in integration**, and the **Provider account**, all on the **direct route** of the **Money route**. It needs parts 1 and 2.
- Part 4: **Provider withdrawal**. It needs part 3.
- Part 5: **Checkout payment rules**. It needs part 2.
- Part 6: the **payments add-on**, with its review, readiness and wind-down. It needs parts 3 and 4.
- Part 7: the **Merchant balance** and its payouts, on the **held route**. It needs parts 3 and 4, is built only if the founder decides to provide for it, and goes live only by the founder's decision.
