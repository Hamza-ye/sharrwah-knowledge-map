# Shopify Capital

Shopify Capital is a financing program that offers merchant cash advances and loans to eligible businesses based on the store's location, history, use and interaction with the Shopify platform. 

You might use Shopify Capital when you need funding to invest in inventory, launch marketing campaigns, rent a physical retail space, or hire staff.

Eligible businesses based in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain, the United Kingdom, and the United States can apply for Shopify Capital. If you meet the eligibility criteria, then you might receive an offer to apply for funding. You can also check for offers in the [**Finance**](https://admin.shopify.com/finance) page of your Shopify admin. Before you explore funding options, consider reviewing the [Shopify Capital glossary of terms](/manual/finance/shopify-capital/shopify-capital-glossary).

Business entities operating as Trusts and Partnerships aren't supported.
