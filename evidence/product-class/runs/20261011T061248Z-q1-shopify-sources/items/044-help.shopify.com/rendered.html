# Shop

<aside class="note note-app"><p><img src="https://cdn.shopify.com/app-store/listing_images/532861601aa89a5e70f5d56d075e82ac/icon/CLq7q92-4_0CEAE=.png" alt="Shop icon"> <a href="https://apps.shopify.com/shop">Shop</a>: Install the Shop channel from the Shopify App Store.</p>
</aside>

<aside class="note"><h4>Note</h4><p>Using this feature requires Shopify Network Intelligence to be enabled in your privacy settings. Learn more about <a href="/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/customer-privacy-settings/privacy-settings#network-intelligence">Shopify Network Intelligence</a>.</p>
</aside>


Shop is a shopping destination and delivery tracking app that's available to customers on iOS, Android, [on the web](https://shop.app/), and through [agentic shopping experiences](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/customer-experience#agentic-shopping). Shop is available in [countries where Shopify Payments is supported](/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries) in the following languages:

* Czech
* Danish
* Dutch
* English
* Finnish
* French
* German
* Italian
* Japanese
* Lithuanian
* Norwegian
* Polish
* Portuguese
* Romanian
* Spanish
* Swedish

Customers can use Shop to track packages, discover new stores and products, make purchases using [Shop Pay](/manual/payments/shop-pay), earn and redeem [Shop Cash](/manual/payments/shop-cash) on eligible purchases, and engage with your brand. If a customer uses [Shop Pay Installments](/manual/payments/shop-pay-installments), then they can also use the Shop app to manage their payments.

If your store meets the eligibility requirements outlined in the [Shop Merchant Guidelines](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility), then your store and products are automatically listed in Shop, and the Shop sales channel appears in your Shopify admin.

You can [activate Track with Shop](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/delivery-tracking) so customers can install the app from your order status page, or use [posts and post-purchase offers](/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/marketing) to stay connected with customers on Shop.
